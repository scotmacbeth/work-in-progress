## The mathematics I am re-packaging (all already proved; identifications are routine XM bookkeeping)

**Crossed module** `∂=conj: K → Aut K`, Aut K acting on K.
- Peiffer + equivariance check: both immediate.
- π₀ = coker ∂ = Out K  (band target, Giraud stratum 2)
- π₁ = ker ∂ = Z(K)      (inventory coefficient, Giraud stratum 3)
- im ∂ = Inn K           (the HINGE)
- Giraud ext (I) `1→Inn K→Aut K→Out K→1` = the **cokernel extension** of ∂ (quotient by im).
- Giraud ext (II) `1→Z(K)→K→Inn K→1`    = the **kernel extension** of ∂ (im as quotient).
  ⟹ the two extensions are the two "edges" of ONE crossed module; shared hinge Inn K visually obvious.

**Band** ρ: ℤ² → Out K = π₀. Strict lift = homomorphism ℤ²→Aut K (a STRICT map of XMs [1→ℤ²]→[K→Aut K]).
Usually no strict lift — that's the obstruction.

**Butterfly** (Aldrovandi–Noohi) source [1→ℤ²], target [K→Aut K]: central group E,
- NE–SW SES: `1 → K →^ι E →^p ℤ² → 1`  (the WAIST; E = extension of ℤ² by K)
- q: E → Aut K with q|_K = ∂ = conj; NW–SE complex.
- induced π₀ map ℤ² → Out K is exactly the band ρ.

**Link to the proof's pullback extension** `E_ρ = Aut K ×_{Out K} ℤ²` (ext of ℤ² by Inn K):
- (q,p): E → E_ρ; ker = ker(K→Inn K) = Z(K). So **E_ρ = E / Z(K)**. (routine)
- δ(ρ) = [E_ρ] ∈ H²(T²; Inn K). Splits ⟺ band lifts strictly ⟺ butterfly degenerates to a strict map.
