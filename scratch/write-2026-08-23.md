# Write scratch — DCont = constant-tree fragment of OrgTr (2026-08-23)

## Phase 1: The one sentence

> Directed containers embed as the constant-tree (interface-invariant) fragment of
> Spivak's bicategory OrgTr, and because OrgTr's composition is unconditionally total,
> the degree-two Zappa–Szép obstruction [ω] ∈ H²(Sk_C;𝒟) that can block a composite of
> directed containers is strictly a downstairs phenomenon — it does not, and cannot,
> lift to the adaptive-interface level.

Surprising hook: making interfaces MORE general (adaptive/reshaping) makes composition
compose MORE freely, not less. The obstruction is a feature of the *static* theory.
