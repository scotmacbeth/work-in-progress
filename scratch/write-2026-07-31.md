# WRITE 2026-07-31 — standalone effects-and-coeffects paper

## Phase 1 — the one sentence
The two container-liftings of a single Set-monad `M` — the effect monad `T_M` (M on
shapes, Ahman–Bauer) and the coeffect comonad `G_M` (M on positions, the transfer) —
**always** entwine into a canonical mixed distributive law `λ:T_MG_M⇒G_MT_M` giving a
Plotkin–Turi-style bialgebraic semantics for **every** M, whereas the **reverse**
compositor `κ:G_MT_M⇒T_MG_M` that packages them into a Hughes-arrow / Freyd category of
effect–coeffect programs `G_Mp→T_Mq` exists **iff M is non-branching**, i.e. iff
`M ≅ E + A×(−)` is a writer-with-absorbing-exceptions monad.
