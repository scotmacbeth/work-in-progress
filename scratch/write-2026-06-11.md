## The one sentence (Phase 1)
A small category K factors strictly over a prescribed wide subcategory D — i.e. K = C ⋈ D —
**iff** a cheap fibrewise freeness test (each hom-presheaf Hom_K(−,b) is a free right D-set)
and a global closure search (the free bases assemble into a wide subcategory C) succeed: the
same local-freeness ∧ global-holonomy shape as the single-category case, recovering Brin's
classical Zappa–Szép axioms for monoids at the one-object special case.
