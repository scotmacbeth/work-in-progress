# WRITE scratch — M-containers: codensity & polynomiality — 2026-09-05

## Phase 1 — The one sentence

> For a monad M on Set, the effectful-container extension ⟦S,P⟧_M(X)=Σ_s Kl(M)(P_s,X) is the
> ordinary polynomial functor ⟦S,P⟧ post-composed with M; it is always faithful, is *full* iff M is
> **codense** (M ⇒ Ran_M M iso), and its extensions are closed under composition iff M is
> **polynomial** — two independent invariants of M that trade oppositely: probability keeps
> faithfulness but loses composability, error keeps composability but loses faithfulness.

Surprising payload: fullness and composition, which one instinctively expects to rise and fall
together, are governed by *different* Kan-theoretic invariants of the effect monad and are in fact
in tension across the standard examples.
