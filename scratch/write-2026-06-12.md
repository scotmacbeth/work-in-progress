## Phase 1 — the one sentence
Each of the four products on Cont≅Poly carries its own closure: × is cartesian closed and
⊗ is monoidal closed, but the non-symmetric composition ◁ is *not* closed — only co-closed —
and it is exactly the right co-closure of ◁, not any internal hom, that houses the morphisms
of directed containers, which is why those morphisms are cofunctors (reversed) rather than functors.
