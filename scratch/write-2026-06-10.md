# Write session 2026-06-10 — DCont ≅ Cof note

## Phase 1: The one sentence
> The morphisms native to directed containers are not functors but **cofunctors**:
> writing out the directed-structure compatibility conditions shows a directed-container
> morphism is *exactly* a cofunctor of the associated small categories, giving an
> isomorphism of categories **DCont ≅ Cof** — and this contravariant variance is
> precisely the Put-half of a delta lens, which is why the bidirectional-update
> applications land.

## Framing (from WRITE.md)
CORRECTION + bridge. The roadmap/literature slogan "directed-container morphisms ↔ functors"
is WRONG on variance. Right statement: cofunctors. Payoff: that is exactly what makes
the lens applications work. Self-contained, example-driven. ~6–8pp, ACT extended-abstract
register.

## Audience
Applied category theorists who know containers/Poly + lenses but may carry "morphisms =
functors" intuition. Background assumed: Ahman–Chapman–Uustalu 2014, Ahman–Uustalu 2016,
Clarke 2020/2022.
