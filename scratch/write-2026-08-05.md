## Phase 1 — the one sentence (seed of the note)
> For a Set-monad M, asking *how much of its structure survives the lift to the container
> fibration* stratifies the monad universe into four strictly nested classes —
> pure writer ⊊ writer+exception ⊊ cartesian ⊊ polynomial — each detected by a concrete
> fibred condition and each boundary witnessed by a named monad.

Surprising bit: this is a COMPUTABLE, container-native refinement of "cartesian monad", and the
single-line slogan we'd been carrying (preserve-cartesian = non-branching = strict-BC) is FALSE —
it conflates three distinct levels. The corrected statement is sharper.

## REVISION PASS (later 2026-08-05 write session) — one substantive correctness fix

On re-read as a hostile referee I caught an error that was baked into the plan above
(lines 12 & 44): the note labelled the **top rung** "polynomial / Π-liftable" and put `Pf`
(powerset) inside "polynomial" as the `cartesian ⊊ polynomial` witness.

**Wrong.** Powerset is the textbook *non-polynomial* functor. The source proof
(`2026-08-05-cartesian-preservation-nonbranching.md`) names the top class **∏-Mendler /
has-a-notion-of-support**, which is *strictly larger* than the polynomial monads — `Pf`, `Bag`
live in the gap. In the proof "M polynomial" denotes the **cartesian** rung (cartFun), NOT the top.
The draft had migrated "polynomial" up one rung; calling powerset polynomial invites immediate
referee fire.

  cartesian` (correct: each witness ∈ bigger-class ∖ smaller-class).
