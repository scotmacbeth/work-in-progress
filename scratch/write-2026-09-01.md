# WRITE session 2026-09-01 — "Admissibility of the composition product over a base"

## One sentence (the seed)
Whether the composition product `◁` (polynomial substitution) exists on `Fam(C^op)` is
not a graded question but a near-dichotomy governed by a single necessary condition (the
Shape Lemma): on extensive bases the mere existence of `◁` already forces the monoidal
unit to be connected — so substitution is canonical and its slot-reindexing left adjoint
exists — the sole remaining obstruction being multiplicativity of the connected-components
functor; off the two poles the region is thin but non-empty (`Set × Vec_fd`), and it is
non-empty precisely by combining a rigid and a flexible factor.

## Scope (abstract, up front, not footnote)
All theorems concern the EXTERNAL-shape construction `Fam(C^op)` (shape = external set,
positions in C). Internal (Gambino–Kock) polynomials in a topos ARE closed under composition;
the external-shape functors need not be. Theorem G is exactly that gap, localized.
