\begin{abstract}
Composition fails silently. A smart contract that is correct in isolation drains its
balance when re-entered; two agents each individually well-behaved deadlock when
orchestrated; a supply chain reconciles locally but not globally. This document reports
a single mathematical program that gives these failures one home. We work in the category
$\DCont$ of directed containers, which is equivalent to the category $\Cat$ of small
categories; in this setting composing two directed systems is a \emph{Zappa--Sz\'ep
product}, and it exists and is correct precisely when a lifting condition $(L)$ holds and
a second cohomology class $\omegacl \in \HH{2}$ vanishes. The slogan
\[
  \text{compositional correctness} \;=\; (L)\ \wedge\ \omegacl = 0 \in \HH{2}
\]
organises the whole program. We map the program's artifacts onto the grant's three
pillars --- theory, applications, formalisation --- state each result at its honest
grade (peer-refereed, Lean-verified, proved, computed, or open), and flag the three
places where the story is genuinely incomplete: tax computation has no artifact yet, the
representability wall $\mathcal{E}=0$ (Conjecture~6.2 / Gap-$S$) remains open, and nothing
here has been published at an external venue. This is a map of what exists, drawn to
scale.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The problem: composition fails silently}

The recurring and expensive failure mode of engineered systems is not that a component
is wrong, but that two correct components are wrong \emph{together}. The 2016 DAO
re-entrancy exploit drained roughly \$60M from a contract whose withdrawal function was
correct in isolation; the fault lived in the \emph{interleaving} of a call and a state
update. Multi-agent orchestration exhibits the same shape: two agents, each sound, can
deadlock or corrupt shared state when their control is welded together. Supply chains
reconcile locally at every node yet fail to reconcile globally. In each case the defect
is a property of the \emph{composite}, invisible to any analysis of the parts.

Category theory is the mathematics of composition, so it should have something precise to
say here. The thesis of this program is that it does: that there is one obstruction, living
in one cohomology group, that governs whether a directed system composes correctly, and
that it specialises --- with real content --- to smart contracts, to agent orchestration,
and to supply chains.

\subsection{The gap}

The pieces have existed separately. Directed containers are known to model stateful,
navigable structures and to be equivalent to small categories (Ahman--Chapman--Uustalu;
Shapiro--Spivak in the language of $\Poly$). Zappa--Sz\'ep products are the classical
algebraic device for gluing two structures that act on each other. Group cohomology
supplies obstruction classes. What has been missing is the \emph{fusion}: a single frame
in which ``these two directed systems compose'' is literally a Zappa--Sz\'ep product in
$\DCont$, in which the obstruction to that product is a specific $\HH{2}$ class, and in
which the same class, read in an application, is the re-entrancy bug or the orchestration
deadlock. That fusion, and its consequences, is what this program supplies.

\subsection{The result, in one line}

Throughout, ``correctness'' of a composition means: the composite directed container
exists (its structure maps are well-defined and satisfy the directed-container laws) and
computes what the two factors jointly specify. Our organising claim is:

\begin{quote}
\emph{A directed system composes correctly if and only if a lifting condition $(L)$ holds
and an obstruction class $\omegacl \in \HH{2}$ vanishes.}
\end{quote}

The two halves are independent and have different characters. $(L)$ is a first-order,
degree-one ``the quotient exists'' condition; $\omegacl=0$ is a degree-two ``the pieces
reassemble'' condition. The program's theory pillar (\S\ref{sec:theory}) makes both
precise and proves the biconditional in the cases we have closed; the applications pillar
(\S\ref{sec:applications}) exhibits $\omegacl$ as a concrete, sometimes machine-checked,
invariant of real systems; the formalisation pillar (\S\ref{sec:formalisation}) verifies
the arithmetic core of $\omegacl$ in Lean.

\subsection{Method}

The engine is the equivalence $\DCont \simeq \Cat$ (\S\ref{sec:chain}). It lets us move a
question about composing directed systems into a question about welding two categories,
where the classical apparatus --- Zappa--Sz\'ep products, the Lyndon--Hochschild--Serre
spectral sequence, group cohomology --- is available and sharp. The obstruction $\omegacl$
is the class of a non-split extension; the lifting condition $(L)$ is the existence of a
suitable transversal. Applications are obtained by \emph{specialising the base}: an agent
orchestration, a contract's storage, a supply graph each present as a particular directed
container, and the general obstruction computes to the domain's known failure mode.

\subsection{Consequences and scope}

The payoff is a design principle: to certify a composite directed system, check $(L)$ and
compute $\omegacl$. Where $\omegacl$ lands in a finite group --- as it does for the
re-entrancy model, where $\omegacl = \varepsilon \in \mathbb{F}_2$ --- this is a decidable
check, and we have machine-checked the arithmetic. This is what ``compositional
correctness has economic consequences'' means concretely (\S\ref{sec:impact}).

\section{Theory}\label{sec:theory}

\subsection{The equivalence chain}\label{sec:chain}

\begin{definition}
A \emph{container} is a pair $(S, P)$ with $S$ a set of \emph{shapes} and
$P \colon S \to \Set$ an $S$-indexed family of \emph{positions}; it denotes the functor
$\llbracket S,P\rrbracket X = \sum_{s\in S} X^{P(s)}$. A \emph{directed container} adds
to $(S,P)$ operations making each fibre navigable --- a ``root'' position, a way to move
along a position, and a way to compose moves --- subject to laws.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Equivalence chain]\label{thm:chain}
There is a chain of equivalences
\[
  \DCont \;\simeq\; \PolyComon \;\simeq\; \Cat,
\]
identifying directed containers with comonoids in $(\Poly,\triangleleft)$ and with small
categories. Under it a directed container \emph{is} a small category: shapes are objects,
positions are morphisms out of an object, the root is the identity, and composition of
positions is composition of morphisms.
\grade{external identification established; assembled chain in-progress}
\end{theorem}

The equivalence $\PolyComon \simeq \Cat$ is Shapiro--Spivak's established $\Cat^\# =
\DCont$ identification in $\Poly$; we take it as given and build on it. The chain is the
program's convergence hub: four independent lines of work (state liftings, holonomy,
orchestration, obstruction theory) all land on the single statement $\DCont \simeq \Cat$.
It is the reason a theorem proved about categories transports to a statement about
directed systems, and conversely. The convergence fronts are individually \gProved{} (for
instance, state liftings $\cong$ categorical completeness); the fully assembled and
formalised chain as our own artifact is recorded \gWIP{} in the registry, and is the
natural target for the next formalisation push.

\subsection{Zappa--Sz\'ep composition and the condition $(L)\wedge(G)$}\label{sec:zs}

\begin{definition}
Given two categories (equivalently, two directed containers) $C$ and $D$ that act on each
other, their \emph{Zappa--Sz\'ep product} $C \bowtiemap D$ is the category whose morphisms
are pairs composed by a mutual ``straightening'' rule: $D$ acts on $C$ and $C$ acts on
$D$, and the two actions interleave. It generalises both the direct product (no
interaction) and the semidirect product (one-sided action).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pairwise Zappa--Sz\'ep criterion]\label{thm:zs}
A category $K$ decomposes as a Zappa--Sz\'ep product $K \cong C \bowtiemap D$ of two
subcategories if and only if two conditions hold:
\[
  (L)\ \text{a lifting/factorisation condition, and}\quad
  (G)\ \text{a global cocycle condition.}
\]
\gProved{}
\end{theorem}

This is the composition theorem in categorical form. Because $\DCont \simeq \Cat$, it is
simultaneously a theorem about welding two directed systems. $(L)$ says every morphism of
$K$ factors uniquely through the two factors (the ``straightening'' is well-defined);
$(G)$ says the resulting factorisation is globally coherent. The name of the game in the
rest of the theory is: $(L)$ is usually easy to arrange, and $(G)$ is where the content
lives --- so we ask what $(G)$ \emph{is}.

\subsection{The obstruction $\omegacl \in \HH{2}$}\label{sec:omega}

\begin{theorem}[The global condition is a cohomology class]\label{thm:omega}
The global condition $(G)$ is the vanishing of an obstruction class:
\[
  (G) \iff \omegacl = 0 \in \HH{2},
\]
where $\omegacl$ is the class of the extension welding the two factors. Holonomy
composition realises the Zappa--Sz\'ep bridge, and trivial holonomy forces collapse (the
State $\leftrightarrow$ Belief proposition). \grade{peer-reviewed (holonomy--ZS bridge);
proved (obstruction is $\HH{2}$)}
\end{theorem}

This is the technical heart. Combining Theorems~\ref{thm:zs} and \ref{thm:omega} yields
the program's slogan,
\[
  \boxed{\ \text{correct composition}\ \iff\ (L)\ \wedge\ \omegacl = 0 \in \HH{2}\ }
\]
The holonomy--Zappa--Sz\'ep bridge --- that the composite's holonomy is exactly the
obstruction to a splitting --- is \gReviewed{}. Two further structural facts sharpen the
picture: the emergent-holonomy count is a degree-zero invariant ($\HH{0}$), with the
higher Mackey/Shapiro data in $\HH{n}$ \gProved{}; and there are genuinely two distinct
$\omegacl$-sites that are \emph{not} related by isotropy restriction \gProved{}, so the
obstruction is not an artifact of one presentation.

\subsection{Change of base: when enrichment is free}\label{sec:cob}

\begin{theorem}[Self-enriched change of base]\label{thm:cob}
For a monad/comonad $M$ used to change the base of the container construction, the induced
functor is full and faithful if and only if $M = \Id$. \gProved{} \gLean{}
\end{theorem}

The lesson is a negative one worth stating: enriching the base --- carrying extra effect
structure $M$ along the container construction --- buys expressive power but, for $M\neq
\Id$, \emph{costs} full-faithfulness; the extension is no longer a conservative embedding.
This delimits how far the clean theory travels when one moves from $\Set$ to a richer
base such as $\Vec$, and it was independently machine-checked.

\subsection{Full-faithfulness is unit-connectedness}\label{sec:t1}

\begin{theorem}[Flagship, T1]\label{thm:t1}
The container denotation functor $\llbracket-\rrbracket$ is full and faithful if and only
if the monoidal unit is \emph{connected} --- not, as one might guess, if and only if the
base is extensive. \gProved{}
\end{theorem}

T1 is the program's cleanest ``the obvious guess is wrong'' result. It was natural to
expect that full-faithfulness of the container semantics tracked extensivity of the base;
it does not. It tracks connectedness of the monoidal unit, a strictly different and
sharper condition. This is the kind of result that a formalisation makes trustworthy and a
synthesis exists to highlight.

\subsection{The $\HH{2}$-cluster: holonomy and prunability}\label{sec:cluster}

\begin{theorem}[Two edges of one spectral sequence]\label{thm:cluster}
On $\DCont \simeq \Cat$ there are two a priori different degree-two obstructions --- the
Zappa--Sz\'ep holonomy $\omegacl$ and Ferri \emph{prunability} --- and they do \emph{not}
decompose as a direct sum. Instead they are the two edges of the Lyndon--Hochschild--Serre
spectral sequence of the welding extension: $\omegacl$ is a base class and prunability the
transgression $d_2$, coupled by $d_2(\varphi) = \varphi_*\omegacl$. A clean splitting
$\HH{2} = (\text{base}) \oplus (\text{fibre})$ holds if and only if $\omegacl = 0$.
\grade{proved; supporting fibre-edge kernel Lean-verified}
\end{theorem}

An earlier conjecture that the cluster splits as a product was \emph{refuted}; the correct
statement is the spectral-sequence coupling above. We record this because the refutation is
itself informative --- it says $\omegacl$ and prunability are not independent knobs, they
are linked by a differential, and the link degenerates exactly when the base obstruction
vanishes. A third invariant, the direction-functor $\HH{2}_{\Cat_B}$, was placed in the
cluster: for a connected groupoid it reduces to $\HH{2}(\pi; M)$ of the vertex group, and
it is orthogonal to prunability \gProved{}. Prunability separates from $\omegacl$ in
general (the full loop bundle always has $\omegacl = 0$) \gProved{}.

\subsection{The open wall: representability, Conjecture 6.2 / Gap-$S$}\label{sec:wall}

One question is genuinely open, and the program's honesty depends on saying so clearly.

\begin{conjecture}[6.2 / Gap-$S$]\label{conj:62}
For the $\Vec$-admissibility question, the natural-transformation functor
$F = \mathrm{Hom}(E, \bigoplus_{\mathbb{N}} -)$ is projective in the relevant additive
functor category (equivalently, a certain comparison map $\iota_*$ is injective).
\gOpen{}
\end{conjecture}

The September 2026 module reduction is real progress that stops short of a proof. It
transfers the question to modules over $S = \mathrm{End}_k(E)$, computes the residual
group $\mathrm{Nat}(Q,Q)$ up to an $\mathrm{Ext}^1$ wall, and --- decisively --- shows the
\emph{Baer--Specker obstruction is void}: the Specker identity is consistent with freeness,
so no product-vs-sum argument can manufacture an exotic map. That explains why every
attempted exotic construction failed and reframes the residual question ($\mathcal{E}=0$)
as one about a countable product of non-finitely-generated projectives over $S$. It is
\gProved{} \emph{as a reduction}; the wall itself, $\mathcal{E}=0$, is \gOpen{}. We flag
it here so no reader mistakes the reduction for a resolution.

\section{Applications}\label{sec:applications}

Each application is obtained by specialising the base of the general theory. The pattern
is always the same: present the domain object as a directed container, and the general
obstruction $\omegacl$ computes to the domain's known failure mode.

\subsection{Agent orchestration is a Zappa--Sz\'ep product}\label{sec:orch}
Composing two agent orchestrations is literally the Zappa--Sz\'ep weld of
\S\ref{sec:zs}: each agent is a directed container, and orchestrating them interleaves
their control exactly as the mutual action of $C \bowtiemap D$. Correct orchestration is
then $(L) \wedge \omegacl=0$; a deadlock or a shared-state corruption is a non-vanishing
$\omegacl$. \gProved{}

\subsection{Re-entrancy is a failed distributive law, $\omegacl = \varepsilon \in
\mathbb{F}_2$}\label{sec:reentrancy}
A smart contract's storage and its call structure are two directed systems that must
compose via a distributive law. Re-entrancy is precisely the failure of that law, and the
obstruction lands in a two-element group: $\omegacl = \varepsilon \in \mathbb{F}_2$, with
$\omegacl = 0$ the safe contract and $\omegacl = 1$ the exploitable one. This is the
sharpest specialisation in the program --- a finite, decidable obstruction --- and its
arithmetic is \gLean{}. The standalone note packaging this result is \gReviewed{} (Rick,
2026-09-03). Security audits are, in this language, the search for a failed distributive
law.

\subsection{Tool-calling protocols are directed containers}\label{sec:mcp}
A tool-calling protocol (of the MCP kind used by agent harnesses) is a directed container:
the protocol is the container, the harness is its comonad, the cofree construction is the
execution trace, and multi-agent interleaving is the Zappa--Sz\'ep/distributive-law weld.
Protocol correctness is again $(L) \wedge \omegacl = 0$, with $\omegacl = \varepsilon \in
\mathbb{Z}/2$ (Lean). This is the most recent application and is written for an external
audience (ACT/$\Poly$); it imports Theorem~\ref{thm:omega} rather than reproving it.
\gProved{} \grade{$\omegacl=\varepsilon$ Lean-verified}

\subsection{Supply chains}\label{sec:supply}
A supply graph with local reconciliation at each node is a directed container; global
reconciliation is the vanishing of the composite obstruction. This is established on
worked examples. \gComputed{}

\subsection{Genetic-algorithm and linear-attention composition}\label{sec:ga}
The container framework analyses compositional structure in learned systems: linear
attention is a $\odot$ operation at fixed context, and the depth of a stack is not
concentrated in the attention layer (the ``stack = one matrix'' guess is refuted). The
$\Vec$-based container theory (biproduct collapse, extensivity crux) is the setting.
\gProved{} for the structural claims.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Gap.} \emph{Tax computation} is named in the grant as a target
application. It has \emph{no artifact} in the program to date. It is a genuine opportunity
--- the compositional structure of a tax code (rules composing, with global consistency
conditions) fits the frame --- but nothing has been built, and this document does not
pretend otherwise.

\section{Formalisation}\label{sec:formalisation}

The program's Lean~4 development comprises on the order of two dozen registry nodes,
mostly Mathlib-free core constructions, verifying the load-bearing arithmetic of the
theory. The most important cluster is the $\HH{2}$ engine trio, which mechanises the
computation of the obstruction class:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \texttt{cyclic-h2-gcd-engine} --- the cyclic $\HH{2}$ gcd computation. \gLean{}
  \item \texttt{cyclic-h2-norm-engine} --- the cyclic norm/kernel computation. \gLean{}
  \item \texttt{fibre-h2-z2-engine} --- the fibre-edge kernel, $\HH{2}(\mathbb{Z}/2;
    \{0,2\}) = \mathbb{Z}/2$, sorry-free. \gLean{}
\end{itemize}
Alongside these: the re-entrancy witness $\omegacl = \varepsilon$ is machine-checked
(\S\ref{sec:reentrancy}); change of base ($M=\Id$, Theorem~\ref{thm:cob}) is
Lean-verified; the free-monad grafting/unit laws are verified sorry-free; and the
first composition adjunction $F_q \dashv (-)\triangleleft q$ over $\Vec$ is verified with
its naturality as an independent probe. These are the pieces of the theory whose
correctness is not a matter of my say-so.

\section{Impact}\label{sec:impact}

The unifying claim of the program is economically legible: \emph{compositional
correctness has economic consequences exactly where composition fails silently}, and the
program pinpoints that failure as a single, often-computable obstruction.

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Smart contracts.} The DAO-class re-entrancy bug is $\omegacl = 1 \in
    \mathbb{F}_2$. The obstruction is finite and decidable; auditing is checking
    $\omegacl$. (\S\ref{sec:reentrancy}, peer-reviewed and Lean-verified.)
  \item \textbf{Agent orchestration.} Deadlock and shared-state corruption in a
    multi-agent system are non-vanishing $\omegacl$; correct orchestration is
    $(L)\wedge\omegacl=0$. (\S\ref{sec:orch}, proved.)
  \item \textbf{Supply chains.} Global reconciliation failure despite local consistency
    is a non-vanishing composite obstruction. (\S\ref{sec:supply}, computed.)
\end{itemize}

The design principle for a practitioner is compact: \textbf{to certify a composite, check
$(L)$ and compute $\omegacl$.} Where $\omegacl$ lives in a finite group this is a
decidable, and now machine-checked, procedure.

\section{Conclusion and roadmap}\label{sec:roadmap}

The program has a spine and it holds: composition of directed systems is Zappa--Sz\'ep
composition in $\DCont \simeq \Cat$, its correctness is $(L) \wedge \omegacl = 0 \in
\HH{2}$, the obstruction specialises to re-entrancy, orchestration, and supply
reconciliation, and its arithmetic core is machine-checked. The slogan is not a
simplification imposed after the fact; it is what the theorems say.

Three moves would most strengthen the grant narrative, in priority order:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Close or cleanly frame the wall.} Either resolve $\mathcal{E}=0$
    (Conjecture~\ref{conj:62}) or write it up as a precise open problem about countable
    products of non-finitely-generated projectives over $S=\mathrm{End}_k(E)$ --- the
    reduction is already \gProved{} and publishable as such.
  \item \textbf{Formalise the assembled equivalence chain.} Theorem~\ref{thm:chain} is the
    hub; upgrading it from \gWIP{} to \gLean{} would anchor the whole theory pillar.
  \item \textbf{Build the tax application, or drop it.} It is the one grant-named
    application with no artifact. The compositional structure of a rule system with global
    consistency conditions fits the frame; a worked example would close the gap, and if it
    does not fit, saying so is also progress.
\end{enumerate}

Finally, the program has produced no externally published paper. The re-entrancy
obstruction (finite, decidable, Lean-verified, economically vivid) and the tool-calling
protocols note (written for ACT/$\Poly$) are the two most venue-ready artifacts, and are
the natural candidates for the first submission.
