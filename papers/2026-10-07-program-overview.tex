\section{Orientation}\label{sec:orient}

The spine of the whole program is a single equivalence, read in four dialects:
\[
\Cont \;\simeq\; \DCont \;\simeq\; \text{Polynomial comonoids in }(\Poly,\lhd)\;\simeq\; \Cat\;(\text{small categories}).
\]
Containers $(S\lhd P)$ (Abbott--Altenkirch--Ghani) are shapes-and-positions; adding the
Ahman--Chapman--Uustalu directed structure (D1--D5) makes the extension a comonad; a
comonad for substitution $\lhd$ is a comonoid in $\Poly$ (Spivak--Garner--Fairbanks); and
such a comonoid is exactly a small category. $\DCont\simeq\Cat$ is the convergence hub
where Nottingham, Tallinn and the Topos Institute meet. The central technical tool for
\emph{composition} is the Zappa--Sz\'ep product $K=C\bowtie D$ / distributive laws: it
says when two directed containers (categories, update monads, orchestrations) can be
welded into one, and the obstruction to welding is cohomological. Everything below hangs
off these two facts: the chain, and the ZS composition tool.

\subsection{(a) The equivalence chain and the \texorpdfstring{$\DCont\simeq\Cat$}{DCont=Cat} hub}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{The chain itself} is tracked as an \emph{open umbrella node}, status
\grade{in-progress} (\reg{equivalence-chain}). The classical equivalences
($\Cont\simeq\DCont\simeq$ poly-comonoid $\simeq\Cat$) are each established and

milestones M5 (bridge to $\Poly$'s four tensors) and M6 (double category \textsf{Org})

rungs}, not a single closed theorem.
\item \textbf{$\DCont\cong\mathrm{Cof}(\Poly)$} (directed containers = cofree comonoids /
small categories) is the hub result reused everywhere; the D1--D5 comonad laws are
\grade{lean-verified} (\reg{cofree-comonad}, leaf \texttt{dcont-laws-D1-D5}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{(b) Monoidal structures on containers / \texorpdfstring{$\Poly$}{Poly}: the four tensors}

$\Cont=\Fam(\Set^{\mathrm{op}})=\Poly$ carries four monoidal products, each answering a
different compositional question:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item[$\otimes$] the \textbf{Dirichlet / Day convolution} tensor (parallel, symmetric) --- see \S\ref{sec:day};
\item[$\lhd$] the \textbf{substitution} product (sequential composition of functors; its comonoids are the small categories);
\item[$\times$] the categorical product (independent choice);
\item[$\ltimes/\rtimes$] the two \textbf{Dialectica} tensors (one-sided, non-convolutional).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Closedness / closed structures.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item $(\Poly,\times)$ is \emph{cartesian closed}; $\Poly$ has \textbf{three} closed
structures but is \emph{not} locally cartesian closed. (\reg{closed-day-structures},
\grade{proved}.)
\item \textbf{T4 left $\lhd$-closedness}: the non-symmetric $\lhd$ admits a \emph{left}
internal hom (distinct from the known $\lhd$-coclosure = directed containers) over a
rigid/tiny base, via the collapse $\lhd=\otimes$ there. \grade{proved}
(\reg{t4-left-closedness-lhd-famcop}).
\item \textbf{$\ltimes/\rtimes$ are the Dialectica tensors}: $\ltimes$ restricts on the
homogeneous subcategory to de Paiva's Dialectica $\otimes$; they are non-convolutional,
non-closed; non-closure is one-sided. Status \grade{computed}
(\reg{other-cont-monoidal-tensors}).
\item \textbf{Bare Dirichlet comonoids} $=\Fam(\mathsf{Mon}^{\mathrm{op}})$: a
$\otimes$-comonoid in $\Cont$ with no $\lhd$ structure is exactly a family of monoids
(cocommutative ones: $\Fam(\mathsf{CMon}^{\mathrm{op}})$). \grade{proved} and
\grade{lean-verified} forward+converse (\reg{bare-dirichlet-comonoid}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{(b$'$) Day convolution}\label{sec:day}

The symmetric tensor on $\Fam(C^{\mathrm{op}})$ is a Day convolution: for
$(S,P)$ and $(T,Q)$,
\[
(S,P)\otimes(T,Q) \;=\; \bigl(S\times T,\ (s,t)\mapsto P_s(x)\,Q_t\bigr),
\]
convolving shapes by product and positions pointwise. Over $\Set$ this is Dirichlet
multiplication of species; over a general closed symmetric monoidal base it is the Day
convolution for the pointwise tensor of $C$.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{T2 (Day-)closedness criterion} (Neil's UID~124, ``do T2 generally''): for a
closed symmetric monoidal $C$ with small coproducts, $(-)\otimes(T,Q)$ has a right adjoint
(internal hom) inside $\Fam(C^{\mathrm{op}})$ \emph{iff} the functor
$\Phi(Z)=\prod_{t\in T}\coprod_{r\in R} C(M_r,\,Z(x)Q_t):C\to\Set$ is
\emph{familially representable}. Over $\Vecc$ this gives a clean dichotomy. \grade{proved}
(\reg{t2-day-closedness-famcop}).
\item The general \textbf{Day-convolutional tensor} $(\Cont,\odot_\star)$ is left closed
\emph{iff} $(-)\star B$ is polynomial for every set $B$, with a uniform
$\Pi$-formula internal hom; this and ``poly closed under composition'' are
\grade{lean-verified} (\reg{closed-day-structures}).
\item The underlying full/faithfulness of container extension (the reason the Day picture
is honest at all): \textbf{$\langle\!\langle-\rangle\!\rangle:\Fam(C^{\mathrm{op}})\to[C,C]$
is fully faithful iff the monoidal unit $I$ is connected} (i.e.\ $C(I,-)$ preserves
coproducts) --- Neil's flagship T1, \emph{not} an extensivity condition. \grade{proved}
(\reg{fullness-unit-connectedness}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{(c) Zappa--Szép / orchestration}\label{sec:zs}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{Pairwise ZS criterion}: a pairwise-matched structure extends to a global
Zappa--Szép product $K=C\bowtie D$ \emph{iff} (L) freeness \emph{and} (G) global closure
hold. \grade{proved} (\reg{pairwise-zs}); the Lean ZS-associativity input is
\grade{lean-verified}.
\item \textbf{The (G) obstruction is an $H^2$ class}: $(G)\Leftrightarrow[\omega]=0$. In
the orchestration model, parametrising supervisor--worker by a token-mutation bit
$\varepsilon\in\mathbb{Z}/2$, $C\bowtie D$ exists iff $\varepsilon=0$ and
$[\omega(K_\varepsilon)]=\varepsilon\cdot(\text{gen})$ in $H^2(\mathrm{Sk};\mathbb{Z}/2)\cong\mathbb{Z}/2$.
An unprotected re-entrancy pattern \emph{is} a $(G)$-failure. \grade{proved}
(\reg{orchestration-zs}); the leaf $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ is \grade{lean-verified}.
\item \textbf{Holonomy / composition bridge}: composing two update monads sharing a state
set is a ZS product welding the state/isotropy-holonomy mode to the directed/re-entrancy
mode. \grade{peer-reviewed} (\reg{holonomy-composition-zs-bridge}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{(d) The tangent / Cartan-calculus arc}\label{sec:tangent}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{$\partial$ is a differential combinator}: the AAGM one-hole container
derivative is the differential combinator of a Cartesian differential category $\mathsf{SPoly}$
of polynomial functors --- equivalently the first-order tangent structure realised by
dual-number substitution $T(p)=p(y+\varepsilon v)\bmod\varepsilon^2$. \grade{proved}
(\reg{tangent-container-derivative}); arithmetic and the CDC bridge are \grade{lean-verified}.
\item \textbf{Uniqueness of the representable tangent structure}: among representable
first-order tangent structures, in the \emph{corrected} ambient category --- base change by
the Weil algebra $W=\mathbb{N}[\varepsilon]/\varepsilon^2$, $T=(-)\otimes W$, on finite-rank
Weil/$\mathbb{N}$-algebra objects --- the only nontrivial one is $\partial$. Status
\grade{proved}, specifically \emph{accept-with-required-revisions} from Rick's 2026-10-07
referee report, which \emph{supersedes} the earlier clean peer-review stamp; so this is
\textbf{not} a clean peer-reviewed result (\reg{tangent-uniqueness-spoly}). Note the ambient
category is $T=(-)\otimes W$, \emph{not} full substitution $(\Poly,\lhd,y)$.
\item \textbf{Negation obstruction}: on finite-support polynomial functors the additive
bundle of $T=(-)\lhd D$ is a bundle of commutative \emph{monoids} with no fiberwise
inverses, so $\mathsf{SPoly}$ carries no scalar ring object and the full Cartan calculus
($d,\iota,\mathcal{L}$) does \emph{not} descend --- Cartan fails over $\mathbb{N}$.
\grade{proved}, with \grade{lean-verified} arithmetic (\reg{cartan-scalar-object-spoly}).
\item \textbf{The $\mathbb{Z}$-completion far side}: completing the rig
$\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{Z}$ (virtual containers) switches Cartan back \emph{on}. \grade{proved}
(\reg{virtual-container-cartan}), $\mathbb{Z}$-mirror \grade{lean-verified}.
\item \textbf{Parallelizability does not port}: the attempt to port Lanfranchi's
``parallelizable $\Leftrightarrow$ Lie group object'' is a \emph{refutation with salvage} ---
the literal port is ill-posed ($T\not\prec\times$); what survives is
``tangent-regular $\Leftrightarrow$ out-degree homogeneous'', which is
composition-\emph{blind}. \grade{proved} (\reg{lie-parallelizable-poly-comonoid}), leaf
\texttt{tangentRegular-outdegree} \grade{lean-verified}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{(e) The degree ladder / composition-blindness (a PROGRAM, not a theorem)}\label{sec:ladder}

\begin{remark}
The following is a \textbf{conjectural organising program}, not a proved theorem. The
meta-claim ``cohomological degree classifies compositional invariants'' is a conjecture;
the individual rungs below have their own (varying) grades. It is the ``composition is a
degree-two datum'' synthesis, shipped 2026-10-07 as a \emph{program}.
\end{remark}

The slogan: compositional invariants of a directed container stratify by cohomological
degree.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{Degree 0}: out-degree / tangent-regularity --- composition-blind, sees only
local fibre rank (\reg{lie-parallelizable-poly-comonoid}, \grade{proved}).
\item \textbf{Degree 1}: supply-chain inventory consistency as a class
$[\theta_R]\in H^1$, \emph{independent} of the degree-2 weld (see \S\ref{sec:apps},
\reg{supply-chain-zs}, \grade{computed}).
\item \textbf{Degree 2}: the ZS weld $[\omega]\in H^2$ (\S\ref{sec:zs}); plus the
observation that $\delta$-left-translation trivializability over a component sees exactly
the \emph{groupoids}, and the three-level monodromy tower
(generic $\subsetneq$ groupoid $\subsetneq$ thin), separated by the vertex group. Both are
registered as \grade{proved}: ``$\delta$-left-translation trivializable over a component
$\Leftrightarrow$ groupoid'' is node \reg{delta-left-translation-trivialization}
(\grade{proved}), with crux child
\reg{arrows-out-presheaf-local-system-iff-groupoid} (\grade{proved}); the
constant-trivializable $\Leftrightarrow$ thin-groupoid refinement (the monodromy tower) is
node \reg{arrows-out-presheaf-constant-iff-thin-groupoid} (\grade{proved}). Both live under
the \grade{proved} root \reg{lie-parallelizable-poly-comonoid.json}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{(f) The $H^2$-cluster and the skew-brace obstruction}\label{sec:h2}

Three genuinely different $H^2$ obstruction theories live on $\DCont\simeq\Cat$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{ZS holonomy} $[\omega]$ (\S\ref{sec:zs}, \reg{orchestration-zs}).
\item \textbf{Ferri prunability} --- shown to be a degree-1 descent/subfunctor condition,
orthogonal to $[\omega]$ (memory \texttt{prunability-is-descent-not-schreier};
\texttt{ferri-prunability-separates-from-zs-holonomy}). The groupoid case: when the base is
a connected groupoid the merge class is a Schreier extension class $H^2(\Gamma;M)$, which
need \emph{not} vanish --- the seed ``groupoid $\Rightarrow$ contractible $\Rightarrow$
$H^2=0$'' conjecture is \textbf{refuted}. Status \grade{computed}
(\reg{groupoid-zs-obstruction}).
\item \textbf{$H^2_{\mathrm{CatB}}$} --- the two $[\omega]$ sites are irreducibly distinct:
the handoff class and the stabiliser class are both $\cong\mathbb{F}_2$ with generator
$\varepsilon$ but are \emph{not} the same class (isotropy-restriction kills one).
\grade{proved} (\reg{two-omega-sites}).
\item \textbf{$o_\nu$ skew-brace solvability obstruction}: a group homomorphism
$o_\nu:H^2_+\to C^3/A(\mathbb{Z}^2_\circ)$ with
$\operatorname{im}\varphi^+=\ker o_\nu$ --- an additive class extends to a full skew-brace
cocycle iff $o_\nu$ kills it. \grade{proved} (\reg{solvability-obstruction-o-nu}); the
$|G|=16$ lock and torsor slice are \grade{lean-verified} (\reg{quiver-skew-brace-zs}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Applications}\label{sec:apps}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{GA composition / diversity dynamics}: island-model migration topology
determines diversity dynamics; categorical composition (ZS / distributive laws, the
laxator as obstruction) is the invariant. Empirical (Langer--Vega GECCO 2026); the
categorical model is the orchestration/ZS machinery above.
\item \textbf{Supply-chain inventory consistency}: a supply chain is a directed container;
composing two flows sharing a warehouse is a ZS product; inventory inconsistency between
two routes is a class $[\omega]\in H^2(\mathrm{Sk};\mathbb{Z}/n)\cong\mathbb{Z}/n$
(measuring the discrepancy in \emph{units}). The consistency datum is degree-1
$[\theta_R]\in H^1$ and is \emph{independent} of the weld $[\omega]\in H^2$. Status
\grade{computed} (\reg{supply-chain-zs}); $H^1$ arithmetic and the $W_1/W_2$ separation are
\grade{lean-verified}.
\item \textbf{Blockchain re-entrancy = failed distributive law}: a smart contract's
re-entrancy bug is a nonzero $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ in $H^2\cong\mathbb{F}_2$ --- the same
$(G)$-failure as orchestration. \grade{proved} / $[\omega]=\varepsilon$
\grade{lean-verified} (\reg{orchestration-zs}).
\item \textbf{AI agent orchestration / tool-calling protocols as directed containers}:
agents are containers ($S=$ request types, $P(s)=$ responses); tool-calling protocols are
directed containers; composing supervisors is a ZS weld. \grade{proved}
(\reg{orchestration-zs}); write-up ``Tool-calling protocols are directed containers''
shipped 2026-09-19.
\item \textbf{M-containers (monad-lifted)}: $M\text{-}\Cont=\Fam(\mathrm{Kl}(M)^{\mathrm{op}})$;
$\langle\!\langle-\rangle\!\rangle_M$ is always faithful, and full iff $M$ is codense and
every $(M-)^A$ is connected (affine $\Rightarrow$ connected). \grade{proved}
(\reg{m-containers}); the composition criterion (M-containers compose iff $M$ is
polynomial) is \grade{computed}.
\end{itemize}
