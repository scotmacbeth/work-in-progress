\chapter{Milestones M1--M6: status}
\label{ch:milestones}
This chapter is the authoritative formalisation ledger for the equivalence
programme. It records, milestone by milestone, exactly what has been
machine-checked in Lean~4, what is proved only on paper or in the literature, and
what remains open or conjectural. The single governing rule is honesty against the
proof registry (\texttt{registry/equivalence-chain.json}): where the roadmap prose
and the registry disagree on a grade, the registry wins and the discrepancy is
flagged in place. Milestones \emph{M1}--\emph{M4} (with the converses \emph{M2b},
\emph{M3b}) are \emph{lean-verified}; milestones \emph{M5} and \emph{M6} are
\emph{not} formalised and are not even uniformly proved on paper --- this is
Honest~Ledger item~3, made explicit in \S\ref{sec:mile-open}.

\section{The verified core: M1--M4}\label{sec:mile-core}
%============================================================

The four load-bearing milestones, together with the two converses, are all graded
\emph{lean-verified} in the registry, with zero \texttt{sorry} and axiom
dependencies restricted to \texttt{[Quot.sound]} (via \texttt{funext}); the
Dirichlet-coherence sub-file depends on no axioms at all.

\paragraph{M1 --- representation.}
Registry node \texttt{m1-representation}, Lean
\texttt{Container.repr\_full\_faithful}, graded \emph{lean-verified}. This is the
statement that the extension $\den{-}\colon\Cont\to\End(\Set)$ is full and faithful
onto the polynomial functors --- the foundation the whole chain stands on, so that
every downstream identification may be read either in $\Cont$ or in $[\Set,\Set]$.

\paragraph{M2 / M2b --- directed containers are comonads.}
Node \texttt{m2-comonad-laws} (Lean \texttt{DirectedContainer.comonad\_laws}) proves
the forward direction: the comonad laws on $\den{S,P}$ are exactly (D1)--(D5), the
left counit $=$ (D1)+(D2), the right counit $=$ (D3), coassociativity $=$
(D4)+(D5). Node \texttt{m2b-converse} (Lean
\texttt{ComonadConverse.recover\_forward\_eq\_id}) proves the converse and certifies
\emph{both} round-trips by \texttt{rfl}. Both are \emph{lean-verified}; the
mathematics is Ahman--Chapman--Uustalu \cite{ACU14}.

\paragraph{M3 / M3b --- the $\tri$-comonoid packaging.}
Node \texttt{m3-comonoid-forward} (Lean
\texttt{Containers.DirectedContainer.toComonoid}, file \texttt{Comonoid.lean})
states the same data \emph{internally} to $(\Cont,\tri,\Id)$ --- no $\Ext$, no
$[\Set,\Set]$: right counit $=$ (D3), left counit $=$ (D1)+(D2), coassociativity
$=$ (D4)+(D5). Its converse \texttt{m3b-comonoid-converse} (Lean
\texttt{Containers.Container.Comonoid.toDirectedContainer\_toComonoid}, file
\texttt{ComonoidConverse.lean}) is fully verified with \emph{both} round-trips
certified (counit by \texttt{rfl}, comultiplication via \texttt{ContainerMorphism.}
\texttt{ext\_eq}), upgrading M3/M3b to a genuine isomorphism of packagings
$\mathrm{DirectedContainer}\;C=\mathrm{Comonoid}\;C$. Both \emph{lean-verified}.

\paragraph{M4 --- the morphism-level isomorphism $\DCont=\mathbf{Cof}$.}
Node \texttt{m4-dcont-cof}, Lean \texttt{Containers.DContMorphism.toCofunctor},
graded \emph{lean-verified}. This is the delicate milestone: the correct morphisms
are \emph{cofunctors}, not functors, because the position map
$f^{\sharp}_{s}\colon P'(fs)\to P(s)$ is contravariant. The registry preserves the
refuted naive reading as an explicit dead-end (node \texttt{morphisms-are-functors},
grade \emph{dead-end}, refutation \emph{computed}: ``\#functors $\neq$ \#cofunctors
in 17 of 36 test pairs''), and carries a provenance note (2026-07-14) recording
that the original \texttt{Cofunctor.lean} was orphaned by a PR-close during a repo
reorg, not lost to an out-of-memory event, and that \texttt{DContCat.lean} is a
rebuild of an artifact that had been on a feature branch all along --- lesson:
\emph{merge or back up before stacking}.

\begin{remark}[Axiom hygiene]\label{rem:mile-axioms}
The registry records the \texttt{\#print axioms} audit for the comonoid layer:
\texttt{toComonoid} and its relatives depend on \texttt{[Quot.sound]} only, while
the Dirichlet monoidal structure (\texttt{contDirichletMonoidal}, node
\texttt{four-monoidal-lean}) depends on \emph{no} axioms --- every coherence is
\texttt{rfl}. This is the sense in which ``\emph{lean-verified}'' in
Table~\ref{tab:milestones} is a strong grade: it is not merely ``compiles'' but
``compiles with an audited and minimal axiom footprint''.
\end{remark}

\section{The open frontier: M5 and M6 (Honest Ledger item 3)}\label{sec:mile-open}
%============================================================

This section states plainly what the registry records and what it does not. Neither
M5 nor M6 is \emph{lean-verified}, and --- against the roadmap's informal
``proved-on-paper'' framing --- neither is graded \emph{proved} in the registry
either. This is the third item of the dossier's Honest Ledger.

\paragraph{M5 --- enriched $\DCont=\mathbf{Cof}$.}
Registry node \texttt{m5-enriched-dcont-cof}, attributed in the registry to
Clarke--Di~Meglio, graded \textbf{\emph{unclassified}}. The registry note is
unambiguous: ``Clarke--Di Meglio 2022 state this is open. No sequel since.'' So M5
is \emph{not} proved on paper; it is an open problem inherited from the literature,
with no formalisation and no proof artifact on disk.

% registry records. Do NOT upgrade M5 to proved or lean-verified.

\paragraph{M6 --- $\infty$-$\DCont=\Cat$.}
Registry node \texttt{m6-infinity-dcont}, graded
\textbf{\emph{speculative}}. The registry records this as Kan~Chen's
Conjecture~7.2: that an
$\infty$-categorical directed-container notion recovers $\Cat$ (the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical target). It is a conjecture, not a theorem; there is no
proof file and no Lean module.

\begin{remark}[Honest Ledger item 3, stated for the record]\label{rem:mile-ledger3}
Milestones M5 and M6 are proved neither in Lean nor (uniformly) on paper: the
registry grades them \emph{unclassified} and \emph{speculative} respectively, and
the M5 source explicitly declares the enriched statement open. Any claim in the
grant narrative that ``the equivalence programme is fully formalised'' must be read
as scoped to M1--M4 (with converses M2b, M3b). The formalised core is exactly the
non-enriched, $1$-categorical chain; the enriched and $\infty$-categorical
extensions are frontier, not ledger.
\end{remark}

\section{Adjacent verified structure}\label{sec:mile-adjacent}
%============================================================

The registry carries further \emph{lean-verified} material orbiting the milestones,
useful to cite when the grant narrative reaches beyond the bare chain. The Zappa--
Sz\'ep associativity criterion (node \texttt{zs-lean}, Lean
\texttt{MatchedPair.zs\_iff\_assoc}) is \emph{lean-verified}. All four monoidal
structures on $\Cont$ --- coproduct, product, Dirichlet tensor, and the sequential
operator $\tri$ --- are machine-checked with bifunctorial action, associators,
unitors, naturality, pentagon and triangle, plus extension-functor comparison isos
(node \texttt{four-monoidal-lean}, \emph{lean-verified}), as is the comparitor
$C\otimes D\to C\tri D$ exhibiting the Dirichlet tensor as the uniform fragment of
$\tri$ (node \texttt{dirichlet-is-uniform-fragment-of-seq}, \emph{lean-verified},
with the machine-checked non-surjectivity witness \texttt{dirToSeq\_not\_surjective}).

\begin{remark}[Grades of the surrounding theory are mixed --- cite carefully]\label{rem:mile-mixed}
Not everything adjacent to the chain is verified, and the registry is deliberate
about the gap. The Day-family classification (node \texttt{day-family-classification},
Theorem~A) is graded \emph{proved} (paper, not Lean); the uniform closure formula
(\texttt{uniform-closure-formula}) is only \emph{computed} --- one hand-check plus
literature instances, no write-up artifact (\texttt{file: null}) --- and the closed
convolutional classification (\texttt{closed-convolutional-classification}) is
\emph{speculative}, missing its necessity direction. The comparitor no-go
(\texttt{comparitor-comonoid-nogo}) is \emph{speculative}. The fibrational comonoid
layer (\texttt{fibrational-comonoid-layer}) is graded \emph{peer-reviewed} in the
sense that the equivalence is an established, refereed result
(Shapiro--Spivak \cite{SS23}) --- not that anything new was proved; a full-PDF audit
found it scooped in full. When these results feed the grant, quote the registry
grade, not the ambition. Only Table~\ref{tab:milestones}'s M1--M4 rows carry the
\emph{lean-verified} guarantee.
\end{remark}
