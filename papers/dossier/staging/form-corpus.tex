\chapter{The sorry-free Lean corpus}
\label{ch:corpus}

The theory of this dossier is not only proved on paper; its load-bearing
theorems are machine-checked. The formalisation lives in a single Lean~4
package, \texttt{Containers}, comprising \textbf{66} modules that build the
category $\Cont$ of containers, the sub-theory of directed containers
$\DCont \iso \Cat$, the four monoidal structures on $\Cont$, and the
application-facing results (Zappa--Sz\'ep welds, emergent holonomy, trust and
trajectory systems). The corpus is deliberately austere: almost every module is
\emph{Type}-level Lean~4 core with \textbf{no Mathlib dependency}, so the trust
base is the Lean kernel plus, where noted, \texttt{funext}/\texttt{Quot.sound}
(propositional extensionality). The corpus is \textbf{sorry-free}: every literal
occurrence of the token \texttt{sorry} across all 66 files sits inside a
docstring asserting sorry-freeness, never in a proof term.

\section{What ``lean-verified'' means, and what it does not}

We grade this corpus \textbf{lean-verified}. The standing caveat is the usual
one and must be stated plainly: a machine-checked proof term certifies that the
\emph{stated} proposition type-checks, not that the stated proposition is the
one a reader intends by the English name of the theorem --- the statement, not
the proof, is where residual trust lives. Accordingly the table below records
each module's headline \emph{as the module's own docstring states it}, and flags
in the Notes column every caveat the sources themselves declare: a narrowed
scope, a step left informal, or a genuine axiom footprint
(\texttt{noncomputable}/\texttt{open Classical}). Several modules go further and
pin their footprint with an in-file \texttt{\#print axioms} audit; where a
docstring makes a positive axiom claim we record it. Modules with an em dash in
Notes are clean: they close by \texttt{rfl}, kernel \texttt{decide}, or
definitional proof irrelevance, with at most \texttt{funext}/\texttt{Quot.sound}.

are set in \texttt{typewriter}; the middle column paraphrases the docstring
headline.
