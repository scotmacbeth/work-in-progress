\title{Branching is not a coproduct of branches:\\
\large unfolding versus descent in the container bifibration}
\author{MacBeth \\ \small Kodamai --- internal note}
\date{9 October 2026 \\ \small\texttt{content commit 4136ecf}}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
The container bifibration $\Cont(\cod)=\Fam(\cod^\op)$ is a candidate categorical home
for the arboreal / game-comonad analysis of structure and power. In an earlier note we
matched, over the base of \emph{paths}, the arboreal relational fibration to a
\emph{one-shape} container functor, up to a fibrewise opposite. The obvious way to
extend the match from paths to branching trees is to make the branches of a tree into
the \emph{shapes} of a container. We show this is wrong, and precisely why: the branch
assignment $\Psi(T)=(\branches(T),\{\mathrm{chain}_b\})$ computes the colourings of the
\emph{branch-unfolding} of the tree --- the disjoint union of its root-to-leaf paths ---
and therefore over-counts the colourings of the tree itself by the exact factor
$2^{\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)}$, where $\lambda(v)$ is the number of branches through the
node $v$. The arboreal fibre reappears inside the container fibre as the sub-poset of
\emph{prefix-consistent} colourings: it is the object of descent data for the cover of
the tree by its branches, a limit-shaped datum that the shape-coproduct $\Fam=\Sigma$
cannot impose. The faithful encoding uses instead a single shape whose position set is
the entire node set; conditionally on a cartesian-lift property of the arboreal cover,
this reproduces the path-level match over the full tree base. A separate and higher
obstruction --- invisibility of $k$-ary relations for $k\ge 2$ --- confines the whole
correspondence to the unary fragment. The reusable principle: shared sub-trajectories in
a branching process are descent data, not a coproduct over branches.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection*{The bridge, and the natural guess}

Two compositional vocabularies have grown up side by side. On one side, \emph{containers}
and \emph{directed containers} --- equivalently, small categories \cite{ahman-dcont-cat} ---
package a system as a set of \emph{shapes} together with, for each shape, a set of
\emph{positions}; they organise into the bifibration $\Cont(\cod)=\Fam(\cod^\op)$ whose
fibres are predicates on positions and whose two reindexing legs are direct and inverse
image \cite{macbeth-contcod}. On the other side, \emph{game comonads} and the
\emph{arboreal} axiomatics \cite{arboreal-axioms} present model-comparison games (pebble,
Ehrenfeucht--Fra\"iss\'e, modal) as comonads whose coalgebras are trees of plays, with a
forgetful fibration $\Pfib\colon\Arb\to\Tree$ from coloured trees to trees.

That these two pictures should be \emph{the same picture} is a natural conjecture: both
are theories of ``a process together with a predicate on its states, pulled back along
sub-trajectories''. In an earlier note (Stage~1, \cite{macbeth-stage1}) we tested it at
the smallest honest scale --- a single unary colour, propositionally truncated, over the
base $\Path$ of \emph{paths} (chains of plays) --- and found a clean match, up to a
twist. The arboreal path fibration $\Pfib|_\Path$ is isomorphic to the \emph{fibrewise
opposite} of the one-shape container functor
\[
\Phi\colon \Path\to\Cont(\Set),\qquad [n]\mapsto (1,\{[n]\}),
\]
with arboreal relation-reflection matching the \emph{opcartesian} (pullback) leg $\rho^*$
of $\Cont(\cod)$ --- not, as one might first guess, the cartesian leg $(\Sigma_\rho)^\op$.

Stage~1 reached only paths, because a one-shape container $(1,\{[n]\})$ \emph{is} a single
chain of positions and a branching tree is not. The registered next step --- Stage~2 ---
conjectured the obvious repair: to reach the full tree base $\Tree$, make the branches of
a tree into the shapes of a container,
\[
\Psi(T)=\bigl(\branches(T),\{\mathrm{chain}_b\}_{b\in\branches(T)}\bigr),
\]
one shape per root-to-leaf path, its positions the nodes along that path. The slogan was
``the shape-coproduct $\Fam=\Sigma$ recovers the branch bookkeeping''.

\subsection*{The result}

The slogan is backwards, and the way in which it is backwards is the content of this note.

\begin{theorem}[Stage~2, the three-way dividing line]\label{thm:main}
In the unary, propositionally truncated regime, over the base $\Tree$ of finite rooted
trees and rooted-subtree embeddings:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(1)}] \textup{(Positive, one shape --- conditional.)}
The arboreal relational fibration extends its Stage~1 match to \emph{all} of $\Tree$,
branching included, via the \emph{one-shape} functor
$\Psip(T)=(1,\{\nodes(T)\})$: there is a fibration isomorphism
$\Pfib\cong(\Psip{}^*\Cont(\cod))^{\mathrm{fib\text{-}op}}$ over $\Tree$. This holds
conditionally on the cartesian morphisms of $\Pfib$ being the pathwise embeddings at
\emph{subtree} \textup{(}non-path\textup{)} embeddings; see \S\ref{sec:conditional}.
\item[\textup{(2)}] \textup{(Negative, branches-as-shapes --- proved.)}
The conjectured branch functor $\Psi$ does \emph{not} give the match. Its container fibre
is the Boolean lattice of colourings of the \emph{branch-unfolding}
$\Uf(T)=\bigsqcup_b \mathrm{chain}_b$, strictly larger than the arboreal fibre by the
factor $2^{\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)}$ with $\lambda(v)$ the number of branches through $v$;
the arboreal fibre reappears as the proper sub-poset of \emph{prefix-consistent}
colourings. The excess is nonzero exactly when $T$ branches \textup{(}first at the
three-node V-tree: $8\subsetneq 16$\textup{)}.
\item[\textup{(3)}] \textup{(Higher, arity --- proved.)}
For $k\ge 2$, a $k$-ary relation is a subset of $\nodes(T)^k$, which is not a subobject in
$\Set/\nodes(T)$; the container functor is blind to it and collapses distinct $k$-ary
structures \textup{(}on two nodes, all $16$ binary relations onto the same datum, versus
$4$ unary\textup{)}. This sits strictly above \textup{(1)--(2)} and is untouched by the
choice of shape functor.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The three clauses answer three different questions, and conflating them was the error in
the original conjecture. Clause~(2) is about the \emph{wrong} encoding of branching;
clause~(1) about the \emph{right} one; clause~(3) about a ceiling that no encoding of
the unary data can lift.

\subsection*{The mechanism: unfolding versus descent}

The heart is clause~(2), and it has a one-line diagnosis. The shape-coproduct
$\Fam=\Sigma$ is a \emph{coproduct}: applied to the branches of a tree it forms their
disjoint union $\Uf(T)$, the universal \emph{unfolding} of the tree by its paths, in which
every shared prefix is duplicated once per branch that passes through it. Colouring
$\Uf(T)$ freely lets the duplicated copies of a node disagree. The colourings of the
\emph{tree} are exactly those that agree across copies --- the \emph{prefix-consistent}
ones --- and reconstructing the tree from its branch-cover by imposing that agreement is a
\emph{gluing}: a descent/equalizer datum, limit-shaped, of the wrong variance for a
coproduct. Thus
\[
\Pfib\text{-fibre over }T
\;=\; \text{descent data for the branch-cover } \Uf(T)\twoheadrightarrow T,
\qquad
\Cont(\cod)\circ\Psi\text{-fibre}\;=\;\text{the raw cover colourings};
\]
they coincide iff the cover is trivial, i.e.\ $T$ is a path --- which is exactly why
Stage~1 saw no discrepancy. The honest encoding, clause~(1), sidesteps the unfolding
altogether: keep \emph{one} shape and put the whole node set in the positions, letting the
\emph{base} $\Tree$ carry the tree order through its subtree-embedding morphisms. One copy
of each node, no duplication, nothing to glue.

\subsection*{Why it matters}

The reusable statement is a modelling warning with teeth. Whenever a branching process ---
a supply chain forking into parallel routes, an agent-orchestration log splitting into
concurrent sub-dialogues, a provenance DAG --- is to be encoded in the container
bifibration, the temptation is to let each branch be a shape and take their coproduct. Our
count shows precisely what that costs: an over-representation by $2^{\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)}$,
one spurious bit of freedom for every extra branch through every shared node. The shared
history that distinguishes a tree from its unfolding is \emph{descent data}, and the
$\Fam=\Sigma$ coproduct structurally cannot see it. This is the container-side form of our
running theme that \emph{composition is a degree-two datum}: the sharing lives one level up
from the branches themselves, and is recovered by an explicit gluing step, never read off a
product over branches. Clause~(3) locates the ceiling of the whole arboreal--container
bridge: it is a theorem about the unary fragment, the $k$-ary relational power of game
comonads lying genuinely beyond $\Cont(\cod)$.

\section{Two fibrations and the Stage~1 match}\label{sec:prelim}

Throughout we work in the \textbf{unary, propositionally truncated} regime: a single
relation symbol, interpreted as a \emph{subset} (a subobject, not a proof-relevant family).
This is the fragment in which the two theories have a chance of agreeing; \S\ref{sec:arity}
shows it is also the ceiling.

\subsection{The container bifibration}

Recall \cite{ahman-dcont-cat,gambino-kock} that a \emph{container} is a pair
$(S,\{P_s\}_{s\in S})$ of a set $S$ of shapes and a family of position sets, and that
containers organise into $\Cont(\cod)=\Fam(\cod^\op)$, the bifibration over
$\Cont(\Set)$ whose fibre over $(S,\{P_s\})$ is $\prod_{s\in S}(\Set/P_s)^\op$. We use only
its propositional truncation, recorded in \cite{macbeth-contcod}:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Fibre.} Over $(S,\{P_s\})$ the truncated fibre is
$\prod_{s\in S}\Sub(P_s)^\op=\bigl(2^{\bigsqcup_s P_s},\supseteq\bigr)$: predicates on the
total position set, ordered by \emph{reverse} inclusion.
\item \textbf{Cartesian leg.} A container morphism carries a \emph{backward} position map
$\rho$; its cartesian reindexing is $(\Sigma_\rho)^\op$, truncated \emph{direct image}
$\rho(-)\colon\Sub\to\Sub$.
\item \textbf{Opcartesian (pullback) leg.} The same $\rho$ has an opcartesian reindexing
$\rho^*$, truncated \emph{inverse image} $\rho^{-1}$, left adjoint to the cartesian leg.
\end{itemize}

The two legs are the whole point: ``pull a predicate back along a sub-trajectory'' is
$\rho^*$, inverse image, \emph{not} the cartesian direction.

\subsection{The tree base and the arboreal relational fibration}

\begin{definition}\label{def:tree}
$\Tree$ is the category of finite rooted trees of bounded height (a tree is a finite poset
with least element $\mathrm{root}$ in which every down-set is a chain) and
\emph{rooted-subtree embeddings}: a morphism $f\colon T\to T'$ is an injection on nodes
$j=\nodes(f)\colon\nodes(T)\inpr\nodes(T')$ whose image is prefix-closed and contains the
root. $\Path\subset\Tree$ is the full subcategory of chains (single branches); on $\Path$
the only morphisms are initial-segment inclusions --- exactly the Stage~1 base.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:arb}
$\Arb$ is the category of trees carrying one unary colour $R\subseteq\nodes(T)$ --- the
unary, truncated relational lift of the arboreal coalgebra category, whose underlying
coalgebras are the copresheaves of \cite[Lemma~4.1]{macbeth-game}. The functor
$\Pfib\colon\Arb\to\Tree$ forgets the colour.
\end{definition}

Two facts about $\Pfib$ are all we use; both are elementary and recorded in Stage~1.

\begin{lemma}[arboreal fibre and reindexing]\label{lem:arbfibre}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A vertical map $R\to R'$ over $\id_T$ is a colour-preserving coalgebra homomorphism;
it exists iff $R\subseteq R'$. Hence the $\Pfib$-fibre over $T$ is the \emph{full} Boolean
lattice $(2^{\nodes(T)},\subseteq)$ --- every subset is a legal colouring, branching
imposing no constraint.
\item Along a subtree embedding $f\colon T\inpr T'$ with node injection $j$, the arboreal
reindexing restricts the colour: $f^*R'=R'\cap\nodes(T)=j^{-1}(R')$ (relation-reflection).
Inverse image is strictly functorial, so $\Pfib$ is the subobject/predicate fibration
$\Sub\to\Tree$ with reindexing $=$ pullback.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

The identification in part~(1) depends on reading a node of $T$ as the unique play from the
root to it: the shared root is a \emph{single} play, so $\Pfib$ colours $\nodes(T)$ with the
prefix shared, not the branch-disjoint unfolding. This is the sentence the branch functor
$\Psi$ will violate.

\subsection{The Stage~1 orientation}

\begin{lemma}[Stage~1, \cite{macbeth-stage1}]\label{lem:stage1}
Over the path base $\Path$, with $\Phi([n])=(1,\{[n]\})$:
the $\Pfib$-fibre $(2^{[n]},\subseteq)$ and the truncated container fibre
$\Sub([n])^\op=(2^{[n]},\supseteq)$ are \emph{opposite} posets; arboreal relation-reflection
$j^{-1}$ equals the \emph{opcartesian} leg $\rho^*$ of $\Cont(\cod)$ (and differs from the
cartesian $(\Sigma_\rho)^\op=$ direct image). Hence
$\Pfib|_\Path\cong(\Phi^*\Cont(\cod))^{\mathrm{fib\text{-}op}}$.
\end{lemma}

Everything below asks what happens to Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1} off the path base. The fibre-op
and the $\rho^*$ identification used only that $j$ is an injection of sets and that inverse
image is functorial --- never that the positions formed a chain. This is why one of the two
candidate extensions survives verbatim, and the other does not.

\section{The positive extension: one shape, positions the whole tree}\label{sec:conditional}

\begin{definition}\label{def:psiprime}
$\Psip\colon\Tree\to\Cont(\Set)$ sends $T$ to the \emph{one-shape} container
$(1,\{\nodes(T)\})$: a single shape, position set the entire node set as a \emph{flat} set,
the tree order discarded by $\Psip$ and carried by the base $\Tree$. On a subtree embedding
$f\colon T\inpr T'$, $\Psip(f)=(\id_1,\rho_f)$ with
$\rho_f\colon\nodes(T')\twoheadrightarrow\nodes(T)$ the nearest-ancestor retraction (each
node of $T'$ to its closest ancestor lying in $T$; well-defined as $T$ is prefix-closed and
contains the root). On a chain, $\Psip([n])=(1,\{[n]\})=\Phi([n])$, so $\Psip$ extends
$\Phi$; and $\Psip$ is functorial, $\rho_{g\circ f}=\rho_f\circ\rho_g$ (nearest ancestor
composes through an intermediate subtree).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[fibre and reindexing match, for every tree]\label{prop:psiprime-match}
For every $T\in\Tree$, branching or not:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the truncated container fibre over $\Psip(T)=(1,\{\nodes(T)\})$ is
$\Sub(\nodes(T))^\op=(2^{\nodes(T)},\supseteq)$, whose fibrewise opposite is the
$\Pfib$-fibre $(2^{\nodes(T)},\subseteq)$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:arbfibre}(1), on the nose
\textup{(}one shape, one copy of each node: no over-count\textup{)};
\item relation-reflection $j^{-1}$ along a subtree embedding is the opcartesian leg $\rho^*$
of $\Cont(\cod)$ for the arrow carrying $j$ backward, exactly as in Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1},
the identification being insensitive to any order on the positions.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(1) is immediate: $\bigsqcup_s P_s=\nodes(T)$ since there is one shape, and the Stage~1
fibre-op argument (Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1}) referenced only the position \emph{set}. (2) is
the Stage~1 argument of Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1}, clause by clause, with $[n]$ replaced by the
flat set $\nodes(T)$: $j^{-1}$ is functorial inverse image, which is the opcartesian leg of
$\Cont(\cod)$ along the backward map $j$. No step used the chain order.
\end{proof}

Proposition~\ref{prop:psiprime-match} matches the two fibrations \emph{fibrewise and on
reindexing}. To promote it to an isomorphism of fibrations one needs that the arboreal
\emph{cartesian lifts} along subtree embeddings are computed by this same reindexing. This
is the single external input, and it is where clause~(1) is conditional.

\begin{proposition}[one-shape extension, conditional]\label{prop:clause1}
Assume \textup{(}$\ast$\textup{)}: the $\Pfib$-cartesian morphisms are the pathwise
embeddings at \emph{subtree} embeddings as well as at path embeddings, so that the
$\Pfib$-cartesian lift of $f\colon T\inpr T'$ at a colouring $R'$ is unique and equals
$R'\cap\nodes(T)$. Then
\[
\Pfib\;\cong\;(\Psip{}^*\Cont(\cod))^{\mathrm{fib\text{-}op}}
\qquad\text{as fibrations over }\Tree,
\]
in the unary truncated regime: the branching of $T$ is encoded entirely in the base and its
subtree-embedding morphisms, while the container functor keeps one flat position set and the
opcartesian leg performs the reflection.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Given ($\ast$), Proposition~\ref{prop:psiprime-match}(1) gives the fibrewise anti-iso and
(2) gives agreement of reindexing; ($\ast$) gives agreement of cartesian lifts. The fibrewise
opposite converts the arboreal cartesian structure into the container's opcartesian leg, as
in Stage~1. Composing, $\Pfib\cong(\Psip{}^*\Cont(\cod))^{\mathrm{fib\text{-}op}}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[status of \textup{(}$\ast$\textup{)} --- honest flag]\label{rem:ast}
At a path embedding, ($\ast$) is the standard pathwise-embedding characterisation of
cartesian maps in the arboreal cover. At a \emph{subtree} embedding $f$, a pathwise
embedding of $f$ reflects the colour along \emph{each} path of $T$, and a global $R'$
restricts consistently because the paths of $T$ overlap only on shared prefixes, on which a
single $R'$ automatically agrees --- forcing the unique lift $R'\cap\nodes(T)$. I have
verified this on the V-tree by direct enumeration (every colouring $R'$ has a unique
pathwise-embedding lift, equal to the preimage; see \S\ref{sec:unfolding}), and the
derivation is independent of the tree. But the non-path case of the pathwise-embedding
characterisation is an input I take from the arboreal literature
\cite{arboreal-axioms} rather than one I have independently verified at the stated
generality; hence clause~(1) is graded \emph{computed, conditional on~($\ast$)}, not proved.
The proved content of this note --- clauses (2) and (3) --- does not use ($\ast$).
\end{remark}

\section{Branches as shapes unfolds the tree}\label{sec:unfolding}

This section proves clause~(2) of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}: the conjectured branch functor
computes the colourings of the wrong object. Nothing here is conditional.

\begin{definition}\label{def:psi}
$\Psi\colon\Tree\to\Cont(\Set)$ sends $T$ to $(\branches(T),\{\mathrm{chain}_b\}_b)$: one
shape per root-to-leaf branch $b$, with position set over $b$ the chain of nodes along $b$.
On a chain there is one branch and $\Psi=\Phi=\Psip$, so Stage~1 holds; off $\Path$ they
diverge.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:unfolding}
The \emph{branch-unfolding} of $T$ is $\Uf(T)=\bigsqcup_{b\in\branches(T)}\mathrm{chain}_b$,
the disjoint union of the branch-chains, with \emph{gluing map}
$\pi\colon\Uf(T)\twoheadrightarrow\nodes(T)$, $(b,v)\mapsto v$. For a node $v$, write
$\lambda(v)$ for the number of branches through $v$ (equivalently, the number of leaves above
$v$), so $\pi^{-1}(v)$ has size $\lambda(v)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[the exact over-count]\label{lem:overcount}
The truncated container fibre over $\Psi(T)$ is
$\prod_{b}\Sub(\mathrm{chain}_b)^\op=(2^{\Uf(T)},\supseteq)$. Its cardinality is
$2^{|\Uf(T)|}$ with
\[
|\Uf(T)|=\sum_{b}|\mathrm{chain}_b|=\sum_{v\in\nodes(T)}\lambda(v),
\qquad\text{so}\qquad
|\Uf(T)|-|\nodes(T)|=\sum_{v}(\lambda(v)-1).
\]
The pullback $\pi^*\colon 2^{\nodes(T)}\inpr 2^{\Uf(T)}$ is injective with image the
\emph{prefix-consistent} colourings --- those constant on each fibre $\pi^{-1}(v)$. Hence the
$\Pfib$-fibre $(2^{\nodes(T)},\subseteq)$ embeds as the proper sub-poset of prefix-consistent
colourings inside the $\Cont(\cod)\circ\Psi$-fibre $(2^{\Uf(T)},\supseteq)$, the excess being
the factor $2^{\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)}$. The inclusion is proper exactly when some node has
$\lambda(v)\ge 2$, i.e.\ exactly when $T$ branches.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The fibre formula is the container fibre of \S\ref{sec:prelim} with
$\bigsqcup_s P_s=\bigsqcup_b\mathrm{chain}_b=\Uf(T)$. Counting positions by the node they
glue to gives $|\Uf(T)|=\sum_v\lambda(v)$. A subset $A\subseteq\Uf(T)$ equals $\pi^*(B)$ for
some $B\subseteq\nodes(T)$ iff $A$ is a union of fibres of $\pi$, i.e.\ constant on each
$\pi^{-1}(v)$; such $B$ is then unique ($B=\{v:\pi^{-1}(v)\subseteq A\}$), so $\pi^*$ is
injective with the stated image. The complementary degrees of freedom number
$\prod_v 2^{\lambda(v)-1}=2^{\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)}$ (for each $v$, a consistent colouring
fixes all $\lambda(v)$ copies together; a free one chooses them independently). This exponent
is positive iff some $\lambda(v)\ge 2$.
\end{proof}

In particular the two fibres have different cardinalities as soon as $T$ branches, so there is
\emph{no} fibrewise equivalence $\Pfib\cong(\Psi^*\Cont(\cod))^\op$ --- not even onto the image
of $\Psi$.

\begin{example}[the V-tree]\label{ex:v}
Let $V$ have root $r$ and two leaves $a,b$. Then $|\nodes|=3$, the two branches are
$ra$ and $rb$, $|\Uf|=4$, and $\lambda(r)=2$, $\lambda(a)=\lambda(b)=1$, so
$\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)=1$. The arboreal fibre has $2^3=8$ colourings; the
$\Cont(\cod)\circ\Psi$-fibre has $2^4=16$. The $8$ excess colourings are precisely the
\emph{inconsistent} ones --- those colouring the root in branch $ra$ but not in branch $rb$,
or vice versa. The map $\pi$ identifies the two copies $(ra,r)$ and $(rb,r)$ of the root;
consistency is agreement on that single overlap. (Verified by enumeration;
\texttt{scratch/crown\_stage2\_witness.py}.)
\end{example}

\begin{example}[the Y-tree: deeper sharing]\label{ex:y}
Let $Y$ have root $r$, a single child $c$, and $c$ in turn with two children $x,y$. Then
$|\nodes|=4$, the branches are $rcx$ and $rcy$, $|\Uf|=6$, and
$\lambda(r)=\lambda(c)=2$, $\lambda(x)=\lambda(y)=1$, so $\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)=2$: the whole
shared spine $\{r,c\}$ is double-counted. The arboreal fibre has $2^4=16$ colourings, the
$\Cont(\cod)\circ\Psi$-fibre $2^6=64$, ratio $2^2=4$. (Verified;
\texttt{scratch/crown\_stage2\_more.py}.)
\end{example}

\subsection{The diagnosis: $\Fam=\Sigma$ is unfolding, gluing is descent}

Lemma~\ref{lem:overcount} is a count; here is what it means. The branch family
$\{\mathrm{chain}_b\inpr T\}_b$ is a \emph{cover} of $T$ by its paths. Its nerve is governed
by the overlaps $\mathrm{chain}_b\times_T\mathrm{chain}_{b'}=\mathrm{chain}_{b\wedge b'}$, the
shared prefix down to the meet of $b$ and $b'$. A colouring of the tree is the same as a
family of colourings of the branches that \emph{agree on overlaps}: a \emph{descent datum}
for the cover.

\begin{proposition}[descent description of the arboreal fibre]\label{prop:descent}
For every $T$, the image of $\pi^*$ --- the prefix-consistent colourings --- equals the set of
colourings of $\Uf(T)$ that agree on all pairwise overlaps of the branch cover; and this
equals $2^{\nodes(T)}$, the full arboreal fibre. Thus
\[
\Pfib\text{-fibre over }T
\;=\;\text{descent data (equalizer) for } \Uf(T)\twoheadrightarrow T,
\qquad
\Cont(\cod)\circ\Psi\text{-fibre}\;=\;\text{the raw, pre-descent cover colourings,}
\]
coinciding iff the cover is trivial, i.e.\ $T$ a path.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Two copies $(b,v)$ and $(b',v)$ of the same node $v$ lie in the overlap
$\mathrm{chain}_{b\wedge b'}$ precisely when $v\preceq b\wedge b'$, i.e.\ when both $b,b'$ pass
through $v$; as $b,b'$ range over branches through $v$ these copies exhaust $\pi^{-1}(v)$.
Agreement on all overlaps therefore says exactly that a colouring is constant on each
$\pi^{-1}(v)$ --- prefix-consistency. By Lemma~\ref{lem:overcount} these are the image of
$\pi^*$, in bijection with $2^{\nodes(T)}$. (The gluing is exact: the tree carries its
Alexandrov topology, the branch cover is a cover for it, and a sheaf of colourings glues
uniquely --- here at the elementary level of a single equalizer, verified in
\texttt{scratch/crown\_stage2\_witness.py}: \texttt{consistent = agree-on-overlap = }$2^{\nodes}$.)
\end{proof}

The conceptual point is a mismatch of variance. $\Fam=\Sigma$ is a coproduct over shapes; fed
the branches it yields the disjoint union $\Uf(T)$, the \'etale unfolding of the tree.
Reconstructing the tree is the \emph{opposite} operation --- a gluing that \emph{identifies}
shared prefixes, a coequalizer/descent equalizer, a limit-flavoured datum. A coproduct cannot
impose it. So $\Psi$ realises $\Pfib$ only as the \emph{descent completion} of
$\Cont(\cod)\circ\Psi$: an equivalence onto the descent sub-fibration of prefix-consistent
colourings, never onto the whole thing.

\begin{remark}[reconciliation with clause~(1)]\label{rem:reconcile}
$\Psi$ and $\Psip$ differ exactly by unfolding versus gluing: $\Psi(T)$ is the branch cover,
$\Psip(T)$ is the tree. $\Cont(\cod)$ colours whatever flat position set it is handed. Hand it
the unfolded cover ($\Psi$) and it colours the cover, which descend to the tree only after an
equalizer $\Fam$ cannot supply; hand it the node set ($\Psip$) and it colours the tree
directly. \textbf{Branching is not a coproduct of branches}: the faithful multi-shape encoding
is a single shape whose position set is the branching node set, with the base $\Tree$ carrying
the order.
\end{remark}

\section{The arity obstruction is independent and higher}\label{sec:arity}

Everything above is $k=1$: one unary colour. Clause~(3) concerns $k\ge 2$ and is a different,
strictly higher obstruction --- it survives \emph{either} choice of shape functor.

\begin{proposition}[non-fullness for $k\ge 2$]\label{prop:arity}
A $k$-ary relation on $T$ is a subset of $\nodes(T)^k$. For $k\ge 2$ this is not a subobject in
$\Set/\nodes(T)$, and $\Cont(\cod)$ over $\Psip(T)=(1,\{\nodes(T)\})$ has fibre
$\Sub(\nodes(T))^\op$ --- unary predicates only. Hence the container fibre ($2^{n}$ on $n$
nodes) cannot surject onto the arboreal $k$-ary fibre ($2^{n^k}$ structures): the functor
factors through the unary reduct and collapses distinct $k$-ary structures.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
$\Set/\nodes(T)$ has as objects maps into $\nodes(T)$; its subobjects are subsets of
$\nodes(T)$, not of $\nodes(T)^k$. A $k$-ary relation determines at most its unary projections,
so any two $k$-ary structures with equal unary reducts --- e.g.\ on two nodes $\{0,1\}$ the
relations $\varnothing$ and $\{(0,1)\}$, among all $2^{4}=16$ binary relations --- map to the
same container datum, of which there are only $2^{2}=4$. (Verified;
\texttt{scratch/crown\_stage2\_positive.py}.)
\end{proof}

This is the same ``the forgetful functor is blind to the higher datum'' mechanism we have met
as out-degree invisibility, as the nerve-level gap $N_1\subsetneq N_2$, and as
composition-being-a-degree-two datum. It sits strictly above the $k=1$ unfolding/descent
discussion of \S\ref{sec:unfolding} and is untouched by replacing $\Psi$ with $\Psip$. The
arboreal--container bridge, honestly, is a theorem about the \emph{unary} fragment.

\section{Scope, open problems, and placement}\label{sec:scope}

\paragraph{What is proved, and what is conditional.}
Clauses~(2) and~(3) of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} are \emph{proved}: the exact over-count
$2^{\Uf}\supsetneq 2^{\nodes}$ with factor $2^{\sum_v(\lambda(v)-1)}$ and its descent reading
(Lemmas~\ref{lem:overcount}, Prop.~\ref{prop:descent}, Examples~\ref{ex:v},~\ref{ex:y}), and
the arity collapse $2^{n^k}\supsetneq 2^{n}$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:arity}). Clause~(1) --- the
positive one-shape extension over $\Tree$ --- is \emph{computed, conditional on}~($\ast$) of
Remark~\ref{rem:ast}: the pathwise-embedding characterisation of arboreal cartesian maps at
subtree embeddings, verified on the V-tree with a tree-independent derivation but taken from
the arboreal literature at the stated generality rather than re-proved here.

\paragraph{Open problems.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Discharge~($\ast$).} Verify directly that the $\Pfib$-cartesian lift of a subtree
embedding is $R'\cap\nodes(T)$, upgrading clause~(1) from \emph{computed} to \emph{proved}. This
is the one gap between this note and a complete Stage~2.
\item \textbf{Proof-relevance.} The match is propositional. The full container fibre
$(\Set/\nodes)^\op$ is strictly richer than $\Sub(\nodes)^\op$; a proof-relevant comparison
would need an arboreal theory with witness-set colours.
\item \textbf{The descent completion as a fibration.} Proposition~\ref{prop:descent} is
elementary (a single equalizer per tree). A clean statement ``$\Pfib$ is the descent completion
of $\Cont(\cod)\circ\Psi$ as fibrations over $\Tree$'' is sketched, not proved here.
\item \textbf{Beyond embeddings.} $\Tree$ here has subtree embeddings only; open maps and
non-monic tree morphisms are untreated, inherited from the Stage~1 conventions.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Placement.}
The net correction to the registered Stage~2 conjecture is clean: it is \emph{not} ``$\Psi$
positive on the unary fragment, failing at $k\ge 2$''. It is that the branch-multishape $\Psi$
fails already at $k=1$ \emph{branching} (unfolding versus descent); the positive Stage~2 holds
over the full tree base via the one-shape $\Psip(T)=(1,\{\nodes(T)\})$ under~($\ast$); and
$k\ge 2$ is a separate, higher obstruction. The reusable principle, and the one worth carrying
into the applied side of the programme: \emph{when encoding a branching process in the container
bifibration, do not make the branches into shapes.} That unfolds the process and over-counts
shared history by one bit per extra branch through each shared node; keep one shape and put the
whole state set in the positions, because the sharing --- shared provenance, shared
sub-trajectory, composition itself --- is a descent datum that the $\Fam=\Sigma$ coproduct
structurally cannot see. This is the container-side statement of ``composition is a degree-two
datum'', and it applies verbatim to supply-chain provenance models and to agent-orchestration
logs: shared sub-trajectories must be glued by an explicit descent step, not recovered from a
product over branches.

\paragraph{Formalisation note.}
The counts of \S\ref{sec:unfolding}--\ref{sec:arity} are small finite enumerations
(\texttt{scratch/crown\_stage2\_\{witness,more,positive\}.py}) and are natural candidates for a
Lean oracle in the style of the nerve-level and gerbe engines; the registry node
\texttt{crown-stage2-multishape-branch-extension} should be created and graded
\emph{computed} to match this note. These are lean/registry tasks, flagged here, not done here.
