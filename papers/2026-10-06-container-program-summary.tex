\section{Thesis}

The claim this program defends is that the four-way equivalence
\[
  \text{Containers} \;\simeq\; \text{Directed Containers} \;\simeq\;
  \text{Polynomial Comonoids} \;\simeq\; \text{Small Categories}
\]
is the correct \emph{foundation} for formally-verified composition of heterogeneous
computational systems. A directed container is simultaneously a data shape, a comonad, a
comonoid in the composition-monoidal structure $(\Cont,\lhd,I)$, and a category; a system
built from one is therefore a system whose dynamics, data layout, and type discipline are a
single object that different tools can read differently without translation loss. Composition
of such systems is then not an engineering convenience but an algebraic question with a
yes/no answer.

The central technical tool for \emph{when} two such systems compose is the
\textbf{Zappa--Szép product} (equivalently, a distributive/matched-pair law): given directed
containers $C,D$, a global product $C \bowtie D$ exists iff two conditions hold---a freeness
condition $(\mathrm{L})$ and a global-closure condition $(\mathrm{G})$---and the obstruction to
$(\mathrm{G})$ is a second cohomology class. When that class is nonzero, the composite does
not exist on the nose, and the failure is observable: in blockchain re-entrancy it is exactly
the generator of $H^2 \cong \mathbb{F}_2$. This is the program's bridge from category theory to
economically-consequential correctness.

The work is organised against the three grant pillars.
\textbf{Theory} (\S\ref{sec:theory}): the equivalence-chain core, the tangent/Cartan arc for
the container derivative, the Zappa--Szép / $H^2$ obstruction calculus, and the
fullness/admissibility/change-of-base lane. \textbf{Applications} (\S\ref{sec:apps}): agent
orchestration, blockchain re-entrancy, supply chains, linear attention, and game comonads, each
realised as a directed container and each with its composition question answered.
\textbf{Formalisation} (\S\ref{sec:lean}): roughly $107$ sorry-free Lean~4 declarations,
including the 1-categorical core of the equivalence chain and the arithmetic oracles of the
tangent arc.

One honesty statement up front, because it governs the grant narrative. The equivalence chain
is \textbf{lean-verified only at its 1-categorical core} (milestones M1--M4). The enriched rung
(M5) is unclassified/open and the $\infty$-rung (M6) is speculative, so the \emph{full} chain
is honestly \textsf{in-progress}. The pieces that are done are done to machine-checked standard;
the chain is not yet closed end-to-end.

\section{The spine}
\label{sec:spine}

Four results carry the program. Stated crisply, with grades.

\begin{result}[Equivalence-chain core, M1--M4]\grade{lean-verified}
\label{res:chain}
The container representation functor is fully faithful (M1); comonad laws $\Leftrightarrow$
directed-container laws with both round-trips (M2); directed containers $=$ comonoids in
$(\Cont,\lhd,I)$ in both directions (M3); and $\DCont = \Cof$ as an isomorphism of categories
at the morphism level (M4). All four are sorry-free Lean, axiom footprints in
$\{\textsf{propext},\textsf{Quot.sound}\}$. The \emph{full} three-way equivalence root is
\textsf{in-progress}: M5 (enriched $\DCont = \Cof$) is unclassified and M6 (an $\infty$-level
statement, Kan--Chen Conj.\ 7.2) is speculative.
\end{result}

\begin{result}[Flagship T1: fullness $\Leftrightarrow$ unit-connectedness]\grade{proved}
\label{res:t1}
The container extension $\sem{-}\colon \Fam(C^{\mathrm{op}}) \to [C,C]$ is fully faithful
\textbf{iff the monoidal unit $I$ is connected} (i.e.\ $C(I,-)$ preserves coproducts). This
corrects the natural ``extensivity'' conjecture: $\Set \times \Set$ is lextensive yet
$\sem{-}$ is not full, while $\Set$ is classical precisely because $1$ is connected.
\end{result}

\begin{result}[Tangent/Cartan arc for $\partial$]\grade{proved; lean-verified arithmetic cores}
\label{res:tangent}
The AAGM one-hole container derivative $\partial$ \emph{is} the differential combinator of a
Cartesian differential category / first-order tangent structure on $\SPoly$, realised by
dual-number substitution $T(p) = p(y+\varepsilon v) \bmod \varepsilon^2$
\grade{proved; 0-axiom finite Lean bridge}. Among representable first-order tangent structures
there are \textbf{exactly two}---the trivial one and the dual-number one \grade{proved}. And
$\partial$ sits on a sharp \textbf{$\N$-vs-$\Z$ Cartan knife}: over the rig $\N$ there is
\emph{no} scalar ring object and no fiberwise negation, so the full Cartan calculus does not
descend \grade{proved; $[\textsf{propext},\textsf{Quot.sound}]$ core}; group-completing
$\N\to\Z$ switches it \emph{on}---$\SPoly_\Z$ is Rosický, carries a scalar ring object, and
meets the full Aintablian--Blohmann specification \grade{proved}.
\end{result}

\begin{result}[Pairwise Zappa--Szép criterion and the $(\mathrm{G})$ obstruction]\grade{proved; lean-verified instantiation}
\label{res:zs}
A global Zappa--Szép product exists iff $(\mathrm{L})$ freeness and $(\mathrm{G})$ global
closure both hold \grade{proved}; the $(\mathrm{G})$-obstruction is a class $[\omega] \in H^2$
\grade{proved}. Instantiated in agent orchestration / blockchain re-entrancy: unprotected
re-entrancy is exactly $(\mathrm{G})$-failure with $[\omega(K_\varepsilon)] = \varepsilon$ the
generator of $H^2 \cong \Z/2$ \grade{lean-verified}.
\end{result}

\section{Theory}
\label{sec:theory}

\subsection{The equivalence-chain core (Lane A)}

Beyond Result~\ref{res:chain}, the chain's infrastructure is largely machine-checked:
matched-pair associativity $\Leftrightarrow$ the four Zappa--Szép axioms ZS1--ZS4
\grade{lean-verified}; monoidal coherence (pentagon + triangle) for all four tensors
$\otimes,\times,+,\lhd$ on $\Cont$ \grade{lean-verified}; and the pairwise ZS criterion
$(\mathrm{L})\wedge(\mathrm{G})$ \grade{proved}, shared into the chain.

Supporting structural results. The free monad on a container is the free $\lhd$-monoid under
grafting, with the three monoid laws machine-checked \grade{lean-verified} (the universal
property is \emph{partial}: a multiplication-backward node and backward-uniqueness remain). The
cofree comonad is the $\lhd$-comonoid of $p$-trees with its couniversal property in container
coordinates \grade{proved}. The state object $\Delta S$ is the codiscrete category, and Workers
form a $(\Set,\times)$-graded category via the Dirichlet tensor \grade{proved}.

\begin{remark}[Honest status of the chain]
The 1-categorical core is lean-verified; the enriched (M5) and $\infty$ (M6) rungs are open.
The grant narrative should read ``1-categorical core lean-verified, enriched/$\infty$ open,''
not ``equivalence chain proved.''
\end{remark}

\subsection{The tangent / Cartan arc (Lane B)}

The arc develops Result~\ref{res:tangent} into a full dividing-line picture.

\emph{Existence.} $T = (-)\otimes\W$, base change along $k \to \DD = k[\varepsilon]/\varepsilon^2$
(Weil algebra $\W = \N[\varepsilon]/\varepsilon^2$), is a strict $\lhd$-monoidal functor whose
laxator is the AAGM chain rule, and it restricts to $\DCont$ \grade{proved}. Honest caveat: the
target base is genuinely $\DD$; over $\Set$ the tangent bundle carries no comonad structure.

\emph{Uniqueness.} Among representable first-order tangent structures there are exactly two
(trivial $\W=\N$; dual-number $\W=\N[\varepsilon]/\varepsilon^2$) \grade{proved}. \textbf{Rick
referee correction (accepted):} the ambient category is base change by the Weil algebra,
$T = (-)\otimes\W$, \emph{not} $T = (-)\lhd\DD$ on full $(\Poly,\lhd)$. The $\lhd$-reading was
refereed wrong (on full $\lhd$-$\Poly$ the honest operation collapses to $\{\mathrm{id}\}$;
$\lhd = \otimes\W$ only on the linear layer). The write-ups were relabelled accordingly, and the
former ``third structure at infinite support'' (F4) is \textbf{demoted to open}. Several
Lean oracles support the uniqueness arithmetic: solid-idempotent collapse, universality
rank-rigidity, and the two-model distinction, all $[\textsf{propext}]$.

\emph{The $\N$-vs-$\Z$ Cartan knife.} Over the rig $\N$, $\SPoly/\partial$ has no scalar ring
object and is not Rosický (no fiberwise negation), so the full Cartan calculus does \emph{not}
descend---only a rig-scalar fragment survives \grade{proved; $[\textsf{propext},\textsf{Quot.sound}]$
arithmetic core}. Group-completing to $\Z$ puts us on the far side of the knife: $\SPoly_\Z$ is
Rosický, carries scalar ring object $R=\Z$, and satisfies the entire Aintablian--Blohmann spec,
with Cartan descent a \textsf{computed} downstream consequence \grade{proved; lean-verified positive companion}.

\emph{A dividing line by refutation.} The Lanfranchi ``parallelizable $\Leftrightarrow$ Lie group
object'' port is \textbf{ill-posed} on polynomial functors ($T$ is not Cartesian there); what
survives---tangent-regular $\Leftrightarrow$ out-degree homogeneous---is \emph{blind to
composition}, hence not a groupoid criterion. Refuted both ways \grade{proved, as an impossibility result}.

\subsection{Zappa--Szép / skew-brace / $H^2$ obstruction (Lane C)}

The obstruction calculus behind Result~\ref{res:zs}. The solvability obstruction
$\onu\colon H^2_+ \to C^3/A(Z^2_\circ)$ is a well-defined homomorphism killing $B^2_+$ with
$\operatorname{im}\varphi^+ = \ker\onu$: an additive class extends to a skew-brace cocycle iff
$\onu = 0$ \grade{proved; lean-verified at $|G|=8$ slice and $|G|=16$ lock}. This is the honest
repair of the earlier (refuted) $[\nu]\perp[\Omega]$ independence claim---the residue, $\sigma$,
and $[\beta]$ are coupled.

The holonomy-composition ZS bridge is \textsf{peer-reviewed}: composite liftings compose by ZS
product, isotropy containment holds, and the aligned-abelian $[\omega]$ obstruction is proved
(the originally-stated part (b) was refuted and replaced by (b$'$)). The two $[\omega]$-sites
(handoff vs.\ stabiliser) are irreducibly distinct \grade{proved}; emergent-holonomy meeting
count $= \dim\operatorname{Ext}^0$ with the higher Ext tower vanishing \grade{proved}. The
cyclic $H^2$ engines---$H^2(C_n;\Z) = \Z/n$, $H^2(C_n;\Z/m) = \Z/\gcd(n,m)$,
$H^2(\Z/2;\Z/2) = \Z/2$ with split/nonsplit witnesses---are \grade{lean-verified} (Bézout built
from scratch). Two roots sit honestly at \textsf{computed}: the groupoid ZS obstruction (the
strong conjecture ``groupoid $\Rightarrow [\omega]=0$'' was \emph{refuted}; the correct dividing
line is cohomological dimension $\le 1$) and the quiver skew-brace / Ferri-prunability
separation (prunability $=$ descent/subfunctor, Schreier-blind; many \textsf{proved} sub-nodes,
including the \textsf{peer-reviewed} $[\Omega] =$ Rathee--Yadav identification, under a
\textsf{computed} root).

\subsection{Fullness / admissibility / closed structure (Lane D)}

Flagship T1 (Result~\ref{res:t1}) anchors this lane. Around it: the copower test yields the
writer monad and, over any extensive base, decides fullness \grade{proved} (residual $Q'$ open);
$(-)\lhd q$ has a left adjoint iff the unit is connected---T1's condition applied twice---and over
$\Vect$ the left adjoint exists iff verticality \grade{proved} (this root carries the large
Gap-S / Conj.\ 6.2 ZFC sub-program); fibredness of $(-)\lhd q$ is \emph{not} its left-closure
(a two-sided separation, answering BHM) \grade{proved}.

Closed-structure classification: Dirichlet $\otimes$ on $\Fam(C^{\mathrm{op}})$ is closed iff
$\Phi$ is familially representable (T2) \grade{proved}; $\lhd$-left-closure exists over a
tiny/rigid base via the collapse $\lhd = \otimes$ (T4-left) \grade{proved}; a Day-convolutional
tensor is left-closed iff $(-)\star B$ is polynomial \grade{proved}; $(-)\lhd q$ is a parametric
right adjoint for every $q$ \grade{proved}. Logic of containers: $\Cont(\mathrm{cod}) =
\Fam(\mathrm{cod}^{\mathrm{op}})$ is a bifibration, quantifiers are Neil's A/E liftings, and it is
a co-hyperdoctrine with one-sided Beck--Chevalley/Frobenius \grade{proved}. Comonoid
classifications: bare $\otimes$-comonoids in $\Poly$ are families of monoids (answering
Niu--Spivak Ch.\ 9 Q5), $\otimes$-monoids are monoid $+$ oplax functor, and double comonoids in
$(\Poly,\lhd,\otimes)$ are sets of commutative monoids (Eckmann--Hilton)
\grade{proved; mostly lean-verified}. The full Hedges $4\times4$ interchange table is computed with
two corrections to Hedges \grade{proved; most cells lean-verified}. Two roots remain open/computed:
the closed-convolutional classification ($\star \cong \times$ or $\vee_S$; bounded-arity proved,
infinite-arity open) is \textsf{in-progress}, and the $\ltimes/\rtimes$ Dialectica tensors
(with $\rtimes$ left-closed and the pair duoidal/LDC, both proved) sit under a \textsf{computed} root.

\subsection{Containers over $\Vect$ / M-containers / change-of-base (Lane E)}

Linear containers $\Fam(\Vect^{\mathrm{op}})$: biproduct collapse $\sem{S,P} \cong
\mathrm{Id}^N$, $\sem{-}$ is \emph{not} full over $\Vect$ (the extensivity crux again), and
$\lhd$-comonoids are families of $k$-algebras \grade{proved; several lean-verified}. M-containers:
$M\text{-}\Cont = \Fam(\Kl(M)^{\mathrm{op}})$, with $\sem{-}_M$ always faithful and full iff $M$
is codense and each $(M-)^A$ is connected (the coproduct criterion was refuted; probabilistic $D$
keeps fullness) \grade{proved; THM3 composition-polynomiality sub-tree carries many
Rick-peer-reviewed nodes}. Change-of-base: $\mathcal{CL}\cap\mathcal{UN} = \{\mathrm{Id}\}$---no
nontrivial effect monad gives a uniform fully-faithful enrichment theorem, a sharp negative answer
to Neil UID-168 \grade{peer-reviewed; lean-verified algebraic core}. Finally the monad$\to$comonad
transfer $G(S,P) = (S,M\circ P)$ (comonad laws $\Leftrightarrow$ monad laws, $=$ left
coclosure/Lan) \grade{proved}; a mixed distributive law (entwining) exists for all $M$ with the
reverse failing on branching \grade{proved}; and effect-coeffect arrows form a category iff $M$ is
non-branching, with $\lambda$ a Turi--Plotkin bialgebra for all $M$ \grade{proved; the largest tree,
$\sim$90 nodes, many lean-verified}.

\section{Applications}
\label{sec:apps}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Agent orchestration $=$ Zappa--Szép weld.} An orchestration is a directed container;
two orchestrations compose as $C \bowtie D$ iff $\varepsilon = 0$. Unprotected re-entrancy is
$(\mathrm{G})$-failure \grade{proved; peer-reviewed standalone note}.

\item \textbf{Blockchain re-entrancy $=$ failed distributive law.} The re-entrancy obstruction is
$[\omega(K_\varepsilon)] = \varepsilon$, the generator of $H^2 \cong \Z/2$ \grade{lean-verified}.
This is the program's sharpest Applications claim: a known class of economically-consequential
smart-contract bugs is exactly a nonzero second cohomology class. Security audits of such
contracts are, in this framing, searches for failed distributive laws.

\item \textbf{Supply chains $=$ directed containers.} A supply chain is a directed container; a ZS
product governs composition; inventory inconsistency is $[\omega] \in H^2(\mathrm{Sk};\Z/n)$, with
a worked $\varepsilon$-family computed and $n=2$ reusing the orchestration bit
\grade{computed}.

\item \textbf{Linear attention and GA composition.} Linear attention is the $\odot$ product in
$\mathrm{Mat}(\Vect)$ \emph{exactly at fixed context}; the strong ``stack $=$ one matrix'' claim
is \emph{refuted} (a live depth-$L$ stack has degree $3^L$; softmax is not a $\odot$)
\grade{proved}. O'Neill's stacked-linear-attention free monad is the free $\lhd$-monoid on a linear
container $=$ the tensor algebra, correcting O'Neill Thm 3.2 (an Adámek chain of partial sums, not
bare powers) \grade{proved}. The $\mathrm{Mat}(\Vect)$/attention lane is the nearest proved anchor
for GA composition; a dedicated GA/Kendall's-$W$ registry does not yet exist.

\item \textbf{Game comonads are polynomial comonads.} Ehrenfeucht--Fraïssé, pebbling, and modal
comonads factor through $\Poly$; the underlying functor is polynomial \grade{proved} and the
comonoid is a small category \grade{proved, with a 0-axiom EF-comonoid Lean witness}. The
arboreal-fibration $= \Fam(\mathrm{cod}^{\mathrm{op}})$ crown remains \textsf{speculative}; the root
is honestly \textsf{computed}.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Honest scope note: blockchain is covered via the orchestration re-entrancy result; tax
computation and a standalone GA composition have \emph{no} dedicated registry yet. Clio's
crystal-base results are off-territory and recorded only at her \textsf{peer-claimed} evidence level.

declarations whose axiom footprints are mostly $[\textsf{propext}]$ or
$[\textsf{propext},\textsf{Quot.sound}]$. Four registries are lean-verified at the \emph{root}:

\item \textbf{Game comonads:} the EF-comonoid witness (\texttt{\#print axioms} empty), linear

\item \textbf{Misc:} holonomy-triviality (full), the disjointness lemma, free/cofree UP, the

\section{Open questions \& roadmap}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Full equivalence chain.} Close M5 (enriched $\DCont = \Cof$---currently
unclassified) and M6 (the $\infty$-level $\infty\text{-}\DCont = \Cat$, Kan--Chen Conj.\ 7.2---

into ``chain proved.''

as stated; the open question is whether a composition-\emph{sensitive} invariant exists that
recovers a groupoid criterion once $T$ is honestly $\otimes\W$ rather than $\lhd\DD$.
\item \textbf{The internal replacement theorem.} A named grant deliverable; not yet a registry root.
\item \textbf{Path-5 applications.} Promote supply chains from \textsf{computed} to a worked real
directed container; build the missing GA-composition (Kendall's $W$) and tax-computation registries
rather than leaning on the attention lane as a proxy.
\item \textbf{Strathclyde mini-course (Oct 13--16).} Neil teaching; the tangent/Cartan arc is the
natural Theory-pillar lead---existence, the two-structure uniqueness, and the $\N/\Z$ Cartan knife
are a clean, mostly machine-checked narrative that also surfaces the honest open frontier (the
far-side Cartan descent and the Lie-object question).
\end{itemize}
