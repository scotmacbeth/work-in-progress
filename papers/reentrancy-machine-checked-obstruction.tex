\begin{abstract}
An orchestration of AI agents is a directed container, and welding two agents that share a
resource into one interleaved system is a Zappa--Sz\'ep product of their handoff categories ---
the distributive-law construction for directed containers being Ahman--Uustalu's. Such a product
need not exist, and its non-existence is a degree-two cohomology class
$[\omega]\in H^2(\Sk_\C;\dir)$ on the handoff category. We take the smallest faithful instance ---
a supervisor dispatching to a worker, parametrized by a one-bit turn token whose mutation bit
$\varepsilon$ marks whether the worker writes to the supervisor's shared state during a pending
call --- and package its obstruction as a finite $\FF$ linear-algebra computation. The output is a
short, self-contained Lean~4 development, sorry-free and free of external libraries, whose theorems
certify that the obstruction class equals the token-mutation bit on the nose, $[\omega]=\varepsilon$,
and hence that the joint agent exists if and only if $\varepsilon=0$. Unprotected re-entrancy --- the
DAO-class failure mode --- is thus not a heuristic smell but a machine-checked nonzero generator of
$H^2\cong\mathbb Z/2$. We state precisely what the formalisation certifies and what remains a
pen-and-paper input.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection*{A concrete failure}
In 2016 an attacker drained roughly a third of the Ethereum DAO's funds through a single structural
flaw: a contract called out to an external party \emph{before} updating its own bookkeeping, and the
callee re-entered the still-open call and spent against stale state. \emph{Unprotected re-entrancy}
--- a callee mutating the caller's shared state during a pending call --- has since become the
canonical smart-contract hazard, and the same shape recurs whenever a supervisor dispatches work to a
sub-agent that may call back before returning. The question this note answers is whether that hazard
has an \emph{invariant}: a mathematical object that is zero exactly when the composition is safe and
nonzero exactly when it is not, and which can be \emph{computed and checked} rather than argued
informally.

\subsection*{Agents are containers, composition is a Zappa--Sz\'ep product}
The setting is the polynomial-functor (container) model of interaction, due to Spivak
\cite{NiuSpivak} and developed for agent orchestration by Ghani at Kodamai. An agent that may issue
queries from a set $S$ and, for each query $s$, receive a reply from $P(s)$ is the container
$p=(S,P)$, i.e.\ the functor $\sum_{s\in S}y^{P(s)}$. A \emph{handoff topology} --- who may delegate
to whom, staying put, concatenating handoffs --- is a small category, equivalently a directed
container in the sense of Ahman--Uustalu \cite{AhmanUustalu}. Running two orchestrations that share a
resource is not composition \emph{along} one fixed topology (which a functor already models and which
therefore cannot fail); it is the \emph{interleaving} of two, and the interleaving of two directed
containers is a \emph{Zappa--Sz\'ep product} $\K=\C\bowtie\D$. That a distributive law of directed
containers assembles such a joint container --- generalising the Zappa--Sz\'ep product of monoids, its
matched pair recording the two mutual actions --- is a theorem of Ahman--Uustalu \cite{AhmanUustaluDL};
we take the construction from them and claim none of it.

\subsection*{The gap, and the invariant}
A Zappa--Sz\'ep product \emph{can fail to exist}, and Ahman--Uustalu's account --- which is our
source for the construction --- builds no measure of that failure: composition simply exists when a
distributive law does. Supplying the missing invariant is the author's contribution elsewhere: the
existence of the complementary factor is governed by a single degree-two cohomology class,
\[
  \C\bowtie\D \text{ exists}
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  [\omega]=0\in H^2(\Sk_\C;\dir),
\]
cohomology of the handoff category's skeleton with coefficients in the reply (direction) functor
$\dir$ \cite{gobstr,pairwise}. The cohomology itself is classical
\cite{baueswirsching,rw,pirashvili}; the delta over Ahman--Uustalu is the \emph{identification} of
the orchestration composite with $\C\bowtie\D$ and hence of its obstruction with this class.

\subsection*{The result, and what is new here}
The present note is not the proof that $[\omega]$ is the right invariant --- that is the author's
analytic argument \cite{analytic}, whose spine we recall only as far as needed. What is new here is a
\emph{machine-checked} certificate for the smallest faithful instance. Fix a supervisor $\Sup$
dispatching to a worker $\Wk$ that returns to $\Rt$, carrying a one-bit turn token. A single design
bit $\varepsilon\in\mathbb Z/2$ records whether the worker's re-entrant outcome \emph{mutates} the
token ($\varepsilon=1$, unprotected re-entry) or \emph{fixes} it ($\varepsilon=0$, a lock). The
analytic argument reduces the categorical obstruction to a tiny cochain complex over the two-element
field $\FF$; the class living in that complex is what we certify. Our main statement is the following,
proved in Lean~4 with no \texttt{sorry} and no external library
(Section~\ref{sec:lean}, Theorem~\ref{thm:lean}).

\begin{theorem}[machine-checked]\label{thm:main-intro}
Let $\varphi(a,b)=b-a$ be the class map on the $2$-cochains $C^2\cong\FF^2$, whose kernel is the
coboundary subgroup $B^2$, so that $\varphi$ realises $H^2(\Sk_\C;\dir)=C^2/B^2\cong\FF$. Let
$\omega_\varepsilon=(0,\varepsilon)$ be the defect cocycle of the natural transversal. Then
\[
  \varphi(\omega_\varepsilon)=\varepsilon,
  \qquad
  \omega_\varepsilon\in B^2 \iff \varepsilon=0 .
\]
Equivalently: the composition class $[\omega]$ equals the token-mutation bit, and the joint agent
$\C\bowtie\D$ exists if and only if $\varepsilon=0$.
\end{theorem}

The reader should note exactly what changes relative to the author's broad orchestration paper
\cite{orchestration}. There the identity $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ is established analytically and the
formalisation is a corollary cross-check; \emph{here the Lean development is the deliverable}, and the
note is built to state, quote, and delimit it. The verification boundary is drawn honestly in
Section~\ref{sec:disc}: the formalisation certifies the finite $\FF$ class computation --- the step
that turns ``a cochain complex'' into ``$[\omega]=\varepsilon$'' --- and takes the reduction of the
categorical obstruction \emph{to} that complex as a cited, pen-and-paper input.

\subsection*{Why the degree matters}
One structural remark orients the reader against the neighbouring literature. Multi-agent-systems work
places cellular-sheaf obstructions of degree $H^0$ (consensus, Nash equilibria) and $H^1$
(identifiability) on the agents' \emph{communication} graph, asking whether agents currently agree
\cite{sheafMAS,sheafID}. The container obstruction sits one degree up, in $H^2$, on the \emph{handoff}
category, and asks the prior question: can the agents be fused into one consistent joint agent at all?
Re-entrancy is the smallest place where the answer is no, and $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ is why.

\subsection*{Outline}
Section~\ref{sec:prelim} fixes the container / directed-container / Zappa--Sz\'ep vocabulary and the
parametrized supervisor--worker category $\K_\varepsilon$. Section~\ref{sec:complex} records the
finite $\FF$ complex that the analytic reduction produces, and the class $\omega_\varepsilon=(0,
\varepsilon)$. Section~\ref{sec:lean} presents the Lean development --- the load-bearing artifact ---
quoting its definitions and theorems verbatim and stating precisely what they certify.
Section~\ref{sec:disc} draws the verification boundary and disposes honestly of a demoted side-reading.
Section~\ref{sec:concl} closes.

\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:prelim}

We include only what the statement and its Lean certificate use. Containers, the directed-container /
small-category identification, and the Zappa--Sz\'ep construction are all prior art and are cited as
such; the parametrized category $\K_\varepsilon$ is the minimal faithful model of the
supervisor--worker pattern.

\subsection*{Containers and directed containers}
A \emph{container} $p=(S,P)$ is a set $S$ of shapes together with, for each $s\in S$, a set $P(s)$ of
positions; it represents the polynomial functor $\sum_{s\in S}y^{P(s)}$ \cite{NiuSpivak,SpivakRef}. A
\emph{directed container} equips $(S,P)$ with a root, a sub-position operation, and a
position-concatenation, and Ahman--Uustalu's theorem is that directed containers are exactly small
categories: objects are shapes, morphisms out of $s$ are positions $P(s)$, identities are roots, and
composition is concatenation \cite{AhmanUustalu}. We use the category picture throughout and call it
the \emph{handoff category}.

\subsection*{Zappa--Sz\'ep products}
Let $\K$ be a small category and $\D\subseteq\K$ a wide subcategory (same objects, some morphisms). A
\emph{Zappa--Sz\'ep product} $\K=\C\bowtie\D$ is a second wide subcategory $\C$ such that every
morphism of $\K$ factors \emph{uniquely} as a $\C$-morphism followed by a $\D$-morphism --- a strict
factorization system, equivalently a distributive law $\lambda:\D\C\Rightarrow\C\D$ satisfying the
matched-pair axioms. For directed containers this is the composition construction of Ahman--Uustalu
\cite{AhmanUustaluDL}: it generalises the Zappa--Sz\'ep product of two monoids, and its data is a
matched pair of mutual actions. The point for us is that, given $\D$, the complementary factor $\C$
\emph{may or may not exist}; that existence is what the obstruction measures.

\subsection*{The parametrized supervisor--worker category}
Fix three roles: $\Sup$ (supervisor), $\Wk$ (a worker running), $\Rt$ (a return point). The supervisor
carries a one-bit \emph{turn token}; toggling it is an involution $\tau$ with $\tau^2=\id$, so
$\End(\Sup)=\{\id_\Sup,\tau\}\cong\mathbb Z/2$. The generating handoffs are
\[
  p,\ p\tau:\Sup\to\Wk\quad(\text{dispatch in turn-state $0$ resp.\ $1$}),
  \qquad
  s,\ s_2:\Wk\to\Rt\quad(\text{two worker outcomes}),
\]
with composites $q,q\tau:\Sup\to\Rt$ and right $\mathbb Z/2$-action $p\cdot\tau=p\tau$,
$q\cdot\tau=q\tau$. The prescribed right factor is the supervisor-internal (token) part,
$\D=\{\id_\Sup,\tau,\id_\Wk,\id_\Rt\}$, a wide subcategory whose only nontrivial vertex group is
$G_\Sup\cong\mathbb Z/2$; the left factor $\C$ --- the genuine dispatch/handoff part --- is what a
Zappa--Sz\'ep product would have to supply.

\begin{definition}[the family $\K_\varepsilon$]\label{def:family}
For $\varepsilon\in\mathbb Z/2$ let $\K_\varepsilon$ be the category above with the single length-two
composite fixed by
\[
  s\circ p=q,\qquad s_2\circ p=q\cdot\tau^{\varepsilon},
\]
where $s$ is ``the worker returns normally'' and $s_2$ is ``the worker re-enters'' --- calls back into
the supervisor before returning. Thus $\varepsilon=[\,s_2\circ p=q\tau\,]$ records whether the
re-entrant outcome \emph{mutates} the token ($\varepsilon=1$, unprotected re-entry) or \emph{fixes} it
($\varepsilon=0$, state-protected re-entry / a lock).
\end{definition}

Each $\K_\varepsilon$ is a genuine category with $\D$ wide \cite{analytic}; the reading is exact, in
that re-entrancy --- the callee mutating shared caller state during a pending call --- is precisely the
$s_2$-branch dragging the token when $\varepsilon=1$.

\section{The obstruction as a finite \texorpdfstring{$\FF$}{F2} complex}\label{sec:complex}

The categorical obstruction of \cite{gobstr} is, for $\K_\varepsilon$, computed by an explicit
orbit/cochain calculation in \cite{analytic}. We do not reproduce that reduction; we record its
output, because that output is exactly what the Lean development certifies. The single fact we import
is:

\begin{proposition}[analytic reduction; {\cite{analytic}}]\label{prop:reduction}
For each $\varepsilon\in\mathbb Z/2$ the family $\K_\varepsilon$ satisfies the freeness condition
\textnormal{(L)} and the vertex-group hypothesis of the obstruction theorem, its skeleton $\Sk_\C$ is
the branch $\Sup\to\Wk\rightrightarrows\Rt$ \textup{(}independent of $\varepsilon$\textup{)}, and the
normalized cochain complex of $H^\bullet(\Sk_\C;\dir)$ is
\[
  C^1\cong\FF^2,\qquad C^2\cong\FF^2,\qquad C^3=0,
\]
with coordinates $h([p]),h([q])$ on $C^1$ and $\omega([s],[p]),\omega([s_2],[p])$ on $C^2$; the
coboundary is $\delta^1 h=(h[p]-h[q],\,h[p]-h[q])$; and the defect $2$-cocycle of the natural
transversal $T=\{p,q,s,s_2\}$ is $\omega_T(\varepsilon)=(0,\varepsilon)$.
\end{proposition}

Everything after Proposition~\ref{prop:reduction} is finite linear algebra over $\FF$, and it is this
part that we formalise. Write $C^2=\FF^2$. Since $C^3=0$ we have $Z^2=C^2$. The coboundary image is
the diagonal,
\[
  B^2=\operatorname{im}\delta^1=\{(t,t):t\in\FF\}=\langle(1,1)\rangle,
\]
so $H^2=C^2/B^2\cong\FF$, and the quotient is realised by the \emph{class map}
\[
  \varphi:\FF^2\to\FF,\qquad \varphi(a,b)=b-a=a\oplus b,
\]
whose kernel is exactly $B^2$. The defect cocycle is $\omega_\varepsilon:=\omega_T(\varepsilon)=
(0,\varepsilon)$, so $\varphi(\omega_\varepsilon)=\varepsilon$: its class is trivial for
$\varepsilon=0$ and the nonzero generator for $\varepsilon=1$. Chaining this with the obstruction
theorem gives the dichotomy
\[
  \C\bowtie\D\text{ exists}\iff [\omega_\varepsilon]=0\iff \varepsilon=0 .
\]
This is Theorem~\ref{thm:main-intro}; the next section certifies its finite core.

\begin{remark}[reading the class]
The two coordinates of $\omega$ are the two worker outcomes; the coboundary diagonal is the freedom to
relabel which token-state is ``canonical.'' The class is nonzero precisely because the two outcomes
disagree on that relabelling by one token-flip --- the re-entrant branch's side effect. This is the
categorical form of ``a flat bundle is trivial iff its holonomy vanishes'': each partial handoff
completes freely, but the completions fail to glue into a global serialized agent by a single
$\mathbb Z/2$ monodromy around the dispatch/return loop.
\end{remark}

\section{The Lean formalisation}\label{sec:lean}

This section is the note's load-bearing artifact. The finite $\FF$ computation of
Section~\ref{sec:complex} is transcribed into Lean~4 and machine-checked. The development is
sorry-free and uses no external library --- in particular no Mathlib --- matching the pure-core style
of the author's container development (\texttt{Basic.lean}, \texttt{ZappaSzep.lean}); this is possible
because the entire computation is finite, so the substantive facts close by \texttt{decide} or by case
exhaustion. The file is \texttt{Containers/Reentrancy.lean}. The listings below transcribe it faithfully, with
Unicode replaced by ASCII for typesetting: \texttt{exists} is $\exists$, \texttt{<->} is
$\leftrightarrow$, \texttt{<-} is the reverse rewrite $\leftarrow$, \texttt{x} is the product
$\times$, \texttt{e} is $\varepsilon$, and $\langle\,\cdot\,\rangle$ is the anonymous constructor.

\subsection*{The model}
The two-element field $\FF$ is modelled as \texttt{Bool} with \texttt{false}${}=0$,
\texttt{true}${}=1$, and \texttt{xor} as addition (which, in characteristic two, is also subtraction).
The objects of Section~\ref{sec:complex} become the following declarations.

\begin{lstlisting}[style=lean]
abbrev Z2 := Bool                    -- F2, with xor = + = -
abbrev C2 := Z2 x Z2                 -- the 2-cochains C2, coords omega([s],[p]), omega([s2],[p])

def d1 (t : Z2) : C2 := (t, t)       -- delta^1 reparametrised: image is the diagonal
def InB2 (x : C2) : Prop := exists t : Z2, d1 t = x   -- membership in B2 = im delta^1
def omega (e : Z2) : C2 := (false, e)                 -- transversal defect omega_T = (0, e)
def phi (x : C2) : Z2 := xor x.1 x.2                  -- class map (a,b) |-> b - a
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent Here \texttt{Z2}, \texttt{C2}, \texttt{d1}, \texttt{InB2}, \texttt{omega}, \texttt{phi} are
$\FF$, $C^2$, $\delta^1$, $B^2$, $\omega_T$, $\varphi$ respectively.

\subsection*{The certified theorems}
Three facts establish $H^2\cong\FF$ via $\varphi$: that $\varphi$ kills the coboundary, that its kernel
is \emph{exactly} the coboundary (so it descends to an injection $H^2\hookrightarrow\FF$), and that it
is surjective. In Lean:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=lean]
theorem phi_d1 (t : Z2) : phi (d1 t) = false                 -- phi kills B2
theorem phi_ker_eq_inB2 (x : C2) : phi x = false <-> InB2 x   -- ker phi = B2  (H2 injects into F2)
theorem phi_surjective (y : Z2) : exists x : C2, phi x = y    -- phi onto  (H2 = F2)
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent The main identity and the dichotomy are then two lines:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=lean]
-- Class form of [omega] = e: the class of the defect cocycle (0,e) is exactly e.
theorem phi_omega (e : Z2) : phi (omega e) = e := by
  cases e <;> rfl

-- Membership form of [omega] = 0  <->  e = 0: the defect is a coboundary iff e = 0,
-- iff the Zappa-Szep product K_e = C |><| D exists.
theorem omega_inB2_iff_zero (e : Z2) : InB2 (omega e) <-> e = false := by
  rw [<- phi_ker_eq_inB2, phi_omega]
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent and the two endpoints are recorded as sanity checks that close by \texttt{rfl}:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=lean]
example : phi (omega true) = true := rfl        -- e = 1: nonzero class, product does NOT exist
example : InB2 (omega false) := <false, rfl>    -- e = 0: trivial class, product exists
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{theorem}[the certified statement]\label{thm:lean}
In \texttt{Containers/Reentrancy.lean}, sorry-free and without external libraries, the following are
machine-checked:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{phi\_ker\_eq\_inB2} and \texttt{phi\_surjective}, together giving the isomorphism
  $H^2=C^2/B^2\cong\FF$ realised by $\varphi$;
\item \texttt{phi\_omega} : $\varphi(\omega_\varepsilon)=\varepsilon$ --- the class form of
  $[\omega]=\varepsilon$;
\item \texttt{omega\_inB2\_iff\_zero} : $\omega_\varepsilon\in B^2\iff\varepsilon=0$ --- the membership
  form of $[\omega]=0\iff\varepsilon=0$, i.e.\ the joint agent $\C\bowtie\D$ exists iff the worker fixes
  the token.
\end{itemize}
Consequently, on the finite complex of Proposition~\ref{prop:reduction}, the obstruction class equals
the token-mutation bit, and unprotected re-entrancy ($\varepsilon=1$) is the nonzero generator of
$H^2\cong\mathbb Z/2$.
\end{theorem}

The proofs are as short as they look. \texttt{phi\_omega} is a two-case split closing by
\texttt{rfl}; \texttt{omega\_inB2\_iff\_zero} rewrites by $\ker\varphi=B^2$ and then by
$\varphi(\omega_\varepsilon)=\varepsilon$; \texttt{phi\_ker\_eq\_inB2} is a four-case exhaustion over
$(a,b)\in\FF^2$. The brevity is the point: once the categorical obstruction is reduced to this
complex, the identity $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ is a finite fact that a proof assistant settles with no
room for hand-waving.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:disc}

\subsection*{The verification boundary, drawn honestly}
It matters to say exactly what is and is not certified. The Lean development checks the \emph{finite
$\FF$ class computation}: given the complex of Proposition~\ref{prop:reduction} --- $C^2\cong\FF^2$,
the coboundary equal to the diagonal, and the defect cocycle equal to $(0,\varepsilon)$ --- it proves
$H^2\cong\FF$, $\varphi(\omega_\varepsilon)=\varepsilon$, and the membership dichotomy. It does
\emph{not} certify the \emph{reduction} of the categorical obstruction to that complex: that
$\K_\varepsilon$ satisfies (L) and the vertex-group hypothesis, that $\Sk_\C$ is the stated branch,
and that the transversal defect is $(0,\varepsilon)$ are Proposition~\ref{prop:reduction}, established
by hand in \cite{analytic} and imported here as input. So the honest reading of
Theorem~\ref{thm:lean} is: \emph{the last mile --- from cochain complex to $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ ---
is machine-checked; the first mile --- from category to cochain complex --- is a cited pen-and-paper
theorem.} Formalising the first mile (the orbit category, the freeness argument, the defect
computation) is the natural next Lean target and is not attempted here.

\subsection*{A demoted side-reading}
An earlier framing of this material distinguished a ``total'' from a ``partial'' composition and
predicted that the two would be separated by a degree-\emph{one} invariant. That prediction is
withdrawn. The correct statement is the classical distinction between an \emph{absolute} and a
\emph{classified} obstruction --- Gerstenhaber's ``absolute versus classified'' dichotomy for
deformation/obstruction problems \cite{gerstenhaber} --- and it carries no novelty and no separate
$H^1$ degree here; the single degree-two class $[\omega]$ of Section~\ref{sec:complex} is the whole
invariant. We flag the point only to retire the earlier prediction cleanly.

\subsection*{Relation to the broad orchestration paper}
This note is deliberately narrow. The wider case for reading agent orchestration as directed-container
composition --- the honestly-graded dictionary, the four-regime table (independent product, the
non-abelian $S_3$ interleave, and both re-entry rows), and the degree-axis contrast with sheaf-Laplacian
multi-agent methods --- is the author's broad paper \cite{orchestration}, where $[\omega]=\varepsilon$
is proved analytically and the present formalisation appears as a cross-check. The contribution of
\emph{this} note is the inversion of emphasis: the machine-checked certificate is foregrounded and
delimited, so that a formal-methods reader can point to a sorry-free file and a category theorist can
see precisely which finite fact it settles.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:concl}

Unprotected re-entrancy --- a worker writing to the supervisor's shared state during a pending call, the
DAO-class failure --- is a nonzero degree-two cohomology class on the handoff category, and on the
minimal faithful model that class equals the token-mutation bit on the nose. The identity
$[\omega]=\varepsilon$, and with it the dichotomy that the joint agent exists if and only if the worker
fixes the token, is now a sorry-free Lean~4 theorem depending on no external library. What the
formalisation buys is not a new mathematical fact but a \emph{located} one: the composability of two
shared-resource agents is decided by a finite object a proof assistant can check, and the check is the
last mile of an argument whose first mile --- the reduction from category to complex --- is the clearly
marked pen-and-paper input and the obvious next formalisation target. Beyond that lie larger handoff
topologies, where the obstruction is a class in a genuinely larger $H^2$, and the $\infty$-analogue,
where a re-entrant tool-call is itself an orchestration.
