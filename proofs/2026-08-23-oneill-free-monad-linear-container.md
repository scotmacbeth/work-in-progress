# O'Neill's stacked-attention free monad is the ◁-monoid free monad on a linear container — and it is the tensor algebra T(AttP), not a colimit of powers

**MacBeth — 2026-08-23 — PROVE session**

## Problem statement

O'Neill (arXiv:2501.02931, *Self-Attention as a Parametric Endofunctor*, full text verified)
formalises one linear self-attention layer as a parametric 1-morphism `(AttP, att)` in
`Para(Vect)`, with parameter space `AttP = QP ⊕ KP ⊕ VP` (the weight spaces of `W_Q, W_K, W_V`)
and `att : AttP ⊗ X → Y` linear in both arguments (Thm 3.1). On the underlying category `Vect`
this induces an endofunctor `F : Vect → Vect`, and O'Neill claims (Thm 3.2 / Thm B.1) that
**stacking layers is the free monad on `F`, realised as the colimit of the ω-chain
`id → F → F² → …`**.

**Claim proved here.** Read `F` as the extension `⟦C⟧` of a **one-shape linear container**
`C = (⋆, AttP)` over `Fam(Vec^op)`. Then:

1. **(Identification, functorial.)** O'Neill's free monad on `F` **is** the free `◁`-monoid on
   `C`, i.e. the container-theoretic free monad of my Lean-verified result
   [[lean-free-monad-unit-laws-done]], specialised to the linear/`Vec` instance. Its object is
   the **tensor algebra**
   `T(AttP) = ⊕_{L≥0} AttP^{⊗L}`, acting by `W ↦ T(AttP) ⊗ W`; its multiplication `μ` is
   concatenation of parameter tensors `AttP^{⊗m} ⊗ AttP^{⊗n} → AttP^{⊗(m+n)}`, and its unit `η`
   injects "zero layers".

2. **(Correction.)** O'Neill's "colimit of the powers `Fⁿ`" does **not** compute this free monad.
   The free monad is the **direct sum (coproduct) `⊕_n Fⁿ`** — a geometric *series* — whereas the
   colimit of the powers under composition-embeddings is a single geometric *term* `AttP^{⊗∞}`.
   The pointing that repairs O'Neill's ω-chain is exactly the **residual/skip connection**: it is
   the unit `η : id ⇒ F'` of the pointed endofunctor `F'(W) = W ⊕ AttP ⊗ W`, container-theoretically
   the empty-position "stop" shape without which the free `◁`-monoid degenerates.

3. **(Boundary.)** My degree-`3^L` theorem
   (`proofs/2026-08-22-linear-attention-odot-composition.md`, proved) is precisely the statement
   that a *live* (data-dependent, `φ=id`) depth-`L` stack is homogeneous of degree `3^L` in its
   input, hence is **not** the degree-1 functor `AttP ⊗ (-)`. So the linear-container / tensor-algebra
   identification is exact in O'Neill's linear (frozen-parameter) regime and provably fails outside it.

The honest test built into the task — *is this a theorem or a re-notation?* — is met by (1)+(2):
the object-level fact "free monad on `A ⊗ (-)` is the tensor algebra" is standard, but the
**functorial** identification with the `◁`-monoid structure, the **correction** of O'Neill's
colimit (internal to his own composition rule), and the **residual-as-unit** reading are new
content, and (3) supplies the quantitative boundary.

## Background objects

**Linear container** (my `expository/containers-over-vec.tex`, eq. 117). A linear container is a
pair `C = (S, (P_s)_{s∈S})` with `S` a set of *shapes* and each `P_s` a finite-dimensional vector
space of *positions*; an object of `Fam(Vec^op)`. Its **extension** is the endofunctor
```
    ⟦S, P⟧ W  =  ⊕_{s∈S} Vec(P_s, W)      (= ⊕_s Hom(P_s, W)).
```
For one shape `⋆` with position space `P`, `⟦⋆, P⟧ W = Hom(P, W) ≅ P* ⊗ W`. For finite-dimensional
`P` this is the same functor as `A ⊗ (-)` with `A = P*`; I use the **covariant tensor reading**
`F(W) = A ⊗ W` throughout (it is the O'Neill-faithful one — `att` is covariant and linear in the
input), and record the dual `Hom`-reading where it is convenient (Fact 1).

**Composition `◁`.** Container composition is substitution of extensions: `⟦C ◁ D⟧ = ⟦C⟧ ∘ ⟦D⟧`.

**Free monad / free `◁`-monoid.** The free monad on an endofunctor `F` is
`Free(F)(W) = μY. W ⊕ F(Y)` when this initial algebra exists. My result
[[lean-free-monad-unit-laws-done]] (Lean, sorry-free, all three monoid laws) states that for a
container `C` the free monad is the free `◁`-monoid generated by `C`; the underlying container is
the *tree container* (shapes = well-founded trees of `C`-shapes, positions = paths). I use the
linear/`Vec` instance below.

**O'Neill's composition rule** (his §3, "Composition in `Para(Vect)`"). Parametric morphisms
`(P, f) : X → Y` and `(Q, g) : Y → Z` compose to `(Q ⊗ P, h) : X → Z`. Hence **the depth-`L` stack
has parameter space `AttP^{⊗L}`** — the parameters *tensor up*. This single fact drives everything.

## Lemma 1 — One-shape linear containers under `◁` are `(Vec_fd, ⊗)`

**Lemma 1.** For finite-dimensional `A, B`, `(⋆, A) ◁ (⋆, B) = (⋆, A ⊗ B)`. The full subcategory of
one-shape linear containers, with `◁`, is monoidally isomorphic to `(Vec_fd, ⊗, k)`; the unit
container is `(⋆, k) = Id`.

*Proof.* `⟦⋆, A⟧ ∘ ⟦⋆, B⟧ (W) = Hom(A, Hom(B, W))`. Currying (the tensor–hom adjunction in `Vec`)
gives a natural isomorphism `Hom(A, Hom(B, W)) ≅ Hom(A ⊗ B, W) = ⟦⋆, A ⊗ B⟧ W`. Naturality in `W`
is the naturality of currying. The unit: `⟦⋆, k⟧ W = Hom(k, W) = W = Id W`, and `A ⊗ k ≅ A ≅ k ⊗ A`,
so `(⋆, k)` is the `◁`-unit. Associativity and unit coherence transport from `(Vec, ⊗)`. ∎

Numeric confirmation of the currying step: `dim Hom(P, Hom(P, W)) = p(pw) = p²w = dim Hom(P⊗P, W)`
and the coordinate map is a bijection (permutation matrix, `det = ±1`) — **FACT 1, PASS**
(`scratch/verify_three_facts.py`).

## Theorem 2 — The free `◁`-monoid on `C = (⋆, AttP)` is the tensor algebra, and it is O'Neill's free monad

**Theorem 2.** Let `A := AttP` (finite-dimensional) and `F(W) = ⟦⋆, A⟧ W = A ⊗ W` (covariant
reading). Then:

(a) The free `◁`-monoid on `C = (⋆, A)` is the container `C* = (ℕ, (A^{⊗n})_{n∈ℕ})` — shapes are
    natural numbers ("layer counts"), the position space of shape `n` is `A^{⊗n}`. Its extension is
```
    ⟦C*⟧ W = ⊕_{n≥0} A^{⊗n} ⊗ W  =  T(A) ⊗ W,   where T(A) = ⊕_{n≥0} A^{⊗n} is the tensor algebra.
```

(b) `⟦C*⟧` carries the free-monad structure `(T, η, μ)` on `F`: `η_W : W → T(A)⊗W` is the degree-0
    injection (`W ≅ A^{⊗0} ⊗ W`), and `μ_W : T(A) ⊗ T(A) ⊗ W → T(A) ⊗ W` is induced by the
    concatenation `A^{⊗m} ⊗ A^{⊗n} → A^{⊗(m+n)}`. This is exactly the free monoid on `A` in
    `(Vec, ⊗)`.

(c) `(T, η, μ)` is the free monad on `F`, i.e. `Free(F) = ⊕_n Fⁿ` with `Fⁿ(W) = A^{⊗n} ⊗ W`.

*Proof.*
(a) By Lemma 1 the one-shape linear containers under `◁` are `(Vec_fd, ⊗)` with `(⋆,A)` sitting at
the object `A`. The free `◁`-monoid on `(⋆, A)` is therefore the free monoid on the object `A` in
the monoidal category `(Vec_fd, ⊗)`, which is the tensor algebra `T(A) = ⊕_n A^{⊗n}` with
multiplication concatenation and unit `k → T(A)` (standard: the tensor algebra is the free monoid /
free associative algebra). As a container this is `(ℕ, (A^{⊗n}))` because the `n`-fold `◁`-power is
`(⋆, A)^{◁ n} = (⋆, A^{⊗n})` (Lemma 1, induction) and the free monoid coproduces the powers. Its
extension is `⊕_n ⟦⋆, A^{⊗n}⟧ W = ⊕_n Hom(A^{⊗n}, W)`; in the covariant reading
`⊕_n A^{⊗n} ⊗ W = T(A) ⊗ W`.

(b) The monoid structure of `T(A)` is: unit `η^{T(A)} : k → T(A)` the degree-0 inclusion, and
multiplication `μ^{T(A)} : T(A) ⊗ T(A) → T(A)` the concatenation `A^{⊗m} ⊗ A^{⊗n} → A^{⊗(m+n)}`.
Tensoring with `W` (a strong-monad structure of `T(A) ⊗ (-)`) gives `η_W = η^{T(A)} ⊗ W` and
`μ_W = μ^{T(A)} ⊗ W`. That `(T(A)⊗(-), η, μ)` is a monad is the standard fact that a monoid `M` in a
monoidal category yields the monad `M ⊗ (-)`; the monad laws are the monoid laws of `T(A)`.

(c) The free monad on `F = A ⊗ (-)` is `μY. W ⊕ A⊗Y`. Solving the fixed point:
`T(W) = W ⊕ A⊗W ⊕ A⊗A⊗W ⊕ … = ⊕_n A^{⊗n} ⊗ W` (the functor `A⊗(-)` is finitary, so the initial
algebra is the `ω`-colimit of the free-monad chain `W → W⊕A⊗W → …`, which stabilises to the direct
sum). This equals `⟦C*⟧ W` from (a), with `η, μ` from (b); and the universal property of the free
monad coincides with the universal property of the free monoid `T(A)` (free monad on `M⊗(-)` = free
monoid on the generating object), so the structures agree, not merely the objects. ∎

**Grafting = concatenation.** Because `C` has one shape, its trees are *paths* (each node has its
single position filled by the next node), so the tree-grafting `μ` of the general free `◁`-monoid
[[lean-free-monad-unit-laws-done]] degenerates to **list concatenation** — exactly `μ^{T(A)}`.
Numeric confirmation: concatenate-then-fold of layer lists is associative and equals the direct
composite — **FACT 3, PASS** (`max|Δ| = 1.8×10⁻¹⁵`).

## Theorem 3 — O'Neill names the wrong ω-chain; the free monad is the Adámek chain of partial sums `⊕_n Fⁿ`

**Theorem 3.** Let `F(W) = A ⊗ W` with `A = AttP`, `dim A = p ≥ 2`. The free monad `Free(F)` is the
colimit of the **Adámek (initial-algebra) chain** `Y₀ → Y₁ → Y₂ → …` with `Y_n = ⊕_{k<n} A^{⊗k} ⊗ W`
(the *partial sums*), giving `Free(F)(W) = ⊕_{n≥0} A^{⊗n} ⊗ W` (Theorem 2). O'Neill's Thm 3.2 instead
names the colimit of the **chain of bare powers** `id → F → F² → …`, `Fⁿ(W) = A^{⊗n} ⊗ W`. These are
different diagrams with different colimits:
```
    partial-sum stage:  dim Y_n = w·(pⁿ−1)/(p−1)   → colimit ⊕_n  (geometric SERIES),
    bare-power stage:   dim Fⁿ  = w·pⁿ             → colimit a single growing TERM.
```
For `p = 2, w = 3` the stage-dimensions are `[3, 9, 21, 45, 93, 189]` (partial sums, `n=1..6`) versus
`[3, 6, 12, 24, 48, 96]` (bare powers, `n=0..5`) — equal only at the base. Moreover the transition
maps of the bare-power chain are not even canonical: `F` is **pointable but never well-pointed**, so
Kelly's "well-pointed ⟹ colimit-of-powers = free monad" theorem does not apply. The pointing is
supplied concretely by the **residual/skip connection**.

*Proof.* **(Adámek chain gives the free monad.)** `A ⊗ (-)` is a finitary endofunctor of `Vect`
(it preserves filtered colimits, being a left adjoint / tensoring), so by Adámek's theorem the
initial algebra of `Y ↦ W ⊕ F(Y)` — i.e. `Free(F)(W)` — is the colimit of the `ω`-chain
`Y₀ = 0`, `Y_{n+1} = W ⊕ F(Y_n)`. Compute: `Y₁ = W ⊕ F(0) = W` (as `A⊗0 = 0`);
`Y₂ = W ⊕ A⊗W`; `Y₃ = W ⊕ A⊗(W⊕A⊗W) = W ⊕ A⊗W ⊕ A^{⊗2}⊗W`; inductively
`Y_n = ⊕_{k<n} A^{⊗k}⊗W`, with transition `Y_n ↪ Y_{n+1}` the inclusion of partial sums. The colimit
is `⊕_{k≥0} A^{⊗k}⊗W = T(A)⊗W`, matching Theorem 2(a). **(The bare-power chain is a different
diagram.)** Its `n`-th object is `Fⁿ(W) = A^{⊗n}⊗W` (a *single* power, `dim = w pⁿ`), not the partial
sum `⊕_{k<n}` (`dim = w(pⁿ−1)/(p−1)`); the two stage-dimension sequences diverge for all `n ≥ 1`
(**FACT 2, PASS**), so the diagrams — and hence their colimits — are not the same. **(Not
well-pointed.)** A pointing `η^a : id ⇒ F`, `η^a_W(x) = a⊗x`, exists for every `a ∈ A` (naturality:
for `g:W→W'`, `(A⊗g)(a⊗x) = a⊗g(x) = η^a_{W'}(g(x))`), so the bare-power chain *can* be formed once a
pointing is chosen — but it is well-pointed (`Fη = ηF`) only if `a⊗u⊗w = u⊗a⊗w` for all `u,w`, which
fails whenever `a ≠ 0` and `p ≥ 2`. Hence Kelly's construction does not identify `colim Fⁿ` with the
free monad; the honest colimit of the append-`a` chain is the direct limit `∪_n A^{⊗n}⊗W` (dimension
governed by the single term `pⁿ`), not the coproduct. The free monad is the Adámek colimit
`⊕_n Fⁿ`. ∎

**Residual = the pointing.** The natural pointing is exactly the **skip connection**: writing a real
layer as `x ↦ x + att(θ, x)` gives `F'(W) = W ⊕ A⊗W`, pointed by `η = incl₁ : W → F'(W)`, and
`Free(F') = Free(F) = T(A)⊗(-)`. Container-theoretically `F'` is the **two-shape** container
`({stop, layer}, P_{stop}=0, P_{layer}=A)`, whose empty-position "stop" shape is precisely the
degree-0 summand that turns the free `◁`-monoid into the tensor algebra `⊕_L A^{⊗L}` rather than a
single tower.

**Reading.** O'Neill's *intended* object is the correct one — `⊕_L AttP^{⊗L}`, forced by his own
composition rule (the depth-`L` parameter space is `AttP^{⊗L}`, §3) — but his *stated* construction
("colimit of the powers `Fⁿ`") names the wrong diagram. The fix is one line: take the Adámek chain of
partial sums (equivalently the coproduct `⊕_L`, equivalently the free `◁`-monoid, which is *by
construction* the coproduct over all layer counts).

## Corollary 4 — The degree-`3^L` boundary

**Corollary 4.** A *live* (data-dependent, `φ = id`) depth-`L` linear-attention stack is homogeneous
of degree `3^L` in its input tokens (proved: `2026-08-22-linear-attention-odot-composition.md`, Thm
D). Since the linear container `C = (⋆, AttP)` has extension `F(W) = AttP ⊗ W` of degree exactly `1`,
and every summand `Fⁿ` of the free monad `⊕_n Fⁿ` is degree `1` in `W`, the live stack is **not** any
`Fⁿ`, nor a morphism of the tensor-algebra monad. Hence Theorem 2's identification holds exactly in
O'Neill's **linear (frozen-parameter) regime** — where the layer is the parameter-tensoring functor
`AttP ⊗ (-)` — and provably fails once the parameters (`KV`-state) become functions of the input,
the degree jumping `1 → 3 → 9 → … → 3^L`. Softmax is a further boundary: not `◁`-linear at all
(op. cit. D1; Sargsyan arXiv:2603.16123). ∎

## The Para(Vect) ↔ linear-container dictionary (functoriality, and the unification test)

The identification is functorial because O'Neill's own 2-category maps onto the container category:

| `Para(Vect)` (O'Neill) | linear container over `Fam(Vec^op)` |
|---|---|
| 1-morphism `(P, f) : X→X` (endo) | one-shape container `(⋆, P)`, `F = ⟦⋆,P⟧` |
| parameter space `P = AttP` | position space |
| composition parameter `Q ⊗ P` | `◁`-product `(⋆,Q)◁(⋆,P) = (⋆, Q⊗P)` (Lemma 1) |
| reparameterisation 2-cell `ρ : P→P'`, `f = f'∘(ρ⊗X)` | container morphism (position map) |
| depth-`L` stack, parameter `AttP^{⊗L}` | `(⋆, AttP)^{◁L}` |
| **stacking = free monad** | **free `◁`-monoid = `T(AttP)` (Thm 2)** |

So O'Neill's parameter-tensor composition **is** the container `◁`, and his stacking free monad **is**
the free `◁`-monoid — the identification lives at the level of `(η, μ)`, not just objects.

**Unification ("earns its keep").** Two further published framings are instances of the same
linear-container category, at the honest altitudes I can support this session:

- **Hedges (additive lenses).** An additive lens is a morphism of `Vec`-containers over the biproduct
  `⊕`; this is the same lemma as the extensivity/biproduct analysis in

- **Vertechi (parametric spans / optics).** Vertechi models parametric maps as spans/optics; the
