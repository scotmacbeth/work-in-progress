## §0. Setup — the C_3 residual monad, and the reduction

`O` is the symmetric operad freely generated by one ternary operation `ω` with slot-symmetry
`H_ω = C_3 = ⟨(0\,1\,2)⟩` (cyclic, **not** `S_3`), together with a two-sided **absorbed** unit
`e∈O(0)` (`ω(x,y,e)` deletes the `e`-slot; `ω(x,e,e)=x`; `ω(e,e,e)=e`). Let `M=Õ` be its
free-algebra monad, `M(X)=` normal-form `O`-trees with `X`-leaves.

- **Analytic, non-polynomial.** `A[3]∋ω` is `C_3`-fixed, so the species `A` is **non-flat**
  (`H_ω=C_3≠1`); an analytic functor is polynomial iff flat (Gambino–Kock; BLL), so `M` is **not
  polynomial**. Inhabits the residual: `a_0=|M∅|=1` (only closed normal form is `e`), unbounded
  degree/wreath depth (nesting `ω`), and `O∉𝒰` (not fully symmetric). *(Verified:
  `residual_c3.py` — `Aut(μ(0,1))=1`, `Aut(ω(0,1,2))=C_3`.)*
- **Reduction (THM 3, Part A0, `proved`).** Every composite of two `M`-extensions equals `G∘M` with
  `G=⟦p⟧∘M∘⟦q⟧`. So the essential image `𝓘_M={⟦r⟧∘M}` is **closed under composition** iff
  ```
     (†)   for all polynomial p,q:   ⟦p⟧∘M∘⟦q⟧∘M ≅ ⟦r⟧∘M  for some polynomial r.
  ```
  Necessity of THM 3 is: `(†) ⟹ M` polynomial. I prove `(†)` holds while `M` is non-polynomial.

**The invariant (Lemma E, `proved`; converse = Joyal reconstruction).** Two analytic-type functors
(coproducts of monomials `X^n/G`, possibly infinitely many) are naturally isomorphic **iff** for every
`m` their each-label-once components are isomorphic as `S_m`-sets — equivalently they have the same
multiset of **molecule types** `(n,[G])` (`[G]` = `S_n`-conjugacy class of the point-stabilizer),
counted with cardinal multiplicity. This holds for infinite components (the reconstruction is
coefficientwise). We compare molecule multisets throughout.

Write `𝓕` for the perm-iso classes of support-indecomposable factors of single-`O`-tree
automorphism groups, and
```
   𝒴 := { Young products (block-diagonal, distinguishable blocks) of single-O-tree Auts } .
```
By the Meta-theorem (U-corner §1, `proved`), the molecule types of any `⟦r⟧∘M` lie in `𝒴`.

## §1. The Crux Lemma — the "only-three-identical" rigidity of C_3

> **Lemma 1 (C_3 rigidity).** Let `w` be any finite structure of `⟦p⟧∘M∘⟦q⟧∘M` for polynomials
> `p,q` (a multi-level tree: flat `p`-shape ▹ `O`-tree ▹ flat `q`-shape ▹ `O`-tree ▹ label leaves;
> the special cases `p=q=Id` give `M∘M`). Then the point-stabilizer `Aut(w)≤S_{supp(w)}` is an
> **iterated wreath product of copies of `C_3`** assembled by direct (Young) products; and every one
> of its support-indecomposable factors is the automorphism group of a *single* `O`-tree. Hence
> `[Aut(w)] ∈ 𝒴`.

*Proof.* An automorphism of a rooted tree fixes the root and, recursively, permutes the children of
each node within isomorphism classes using **only** that node's slot-symmetry group. The available
symmetries are:

1. **A ternary `ω`-node with three pairwise-isomorphic children** `ω(c,c,c)`: the group `C_3`
   rotates them, contributing `Aut(c)≀C_3`.
2. **A ternary `ω`-node with ≤ 2 isomorphism classes of children** (e.g. `(c,c,d)` with `c≇d`, or all
   distinct): **no** nontrivial element of `C_3` fixes the configuration — every 3-cycle moves each
   slot to a non-identical slot. Contributes only the direct product of the children's groups.
3. **A binary composite** `μ=ω(x,y,e)` (one absorbed unit slot): its residual slot-symmetry is
   `{h∈C_3 : h` fixes the unit-slot setwise`}|_{\text{real slots}} = \{{\rm id}\}` — `μ` is
   **non-commutative**, so even two identical arguments are **not** exchanged.
   *(Verified: `residual_c3.py` — `μ(0,1)≠μ(1,0)`, `Aut(μ(0,1))=1`; contrast `S_3`: `μ` commutative,
   `Aut(μ(0,1))=S_2`.)*
4. **Flat `p`,`q` levels**: their shapes have distinguishable slots (a flat container `Σ Set(B,−)` has
   no slot-symmetry), so they contribute only direct products.
5. **Pegs / unit-fills** (the inner unit `e` sitting as an *outer* leaf, or a unit entry of a flat
   shape): identical rigid leaves with trivial label-support; they act only as fillers.

So the ONLY generator of nontrivial label-symmetry is case 1 (three identical children ↦ `C_3`); all
other structure yields direct products. Consequently `Aut(w)` is an iterated `C_3`-wreath assembled by
direct products. Its finest support-splitting (Lemma S1, `proved`) breaks it into indecomposable
factors, each a tower `(base)≀C_3≀⋯≀C_3` rooted at a maximal three-identical node. By induction on
size such a tower is realized by the balanced single `O`-tree `ω(c',c',c')` with `Aut(c')=` the base
(base is a smaller such tower, realizable by induction; base case `C_3=Aut(ω(V_0,V_1,V_2))`). Each
factor is therefore a single-tree Aut, and `Aut(w)` — their Young product — lies in `𝒴`. ∎

**Computational confirmation (the escape is empty).** For `q=Id`: an exhaustive factor-level escape
search over `M∘M`-structures on `[m]`, `m≤4` (outer depth ≤3, pegs ≤3), finds **zero** support-indec
factors outside `𝓕` (`residual_escape.py`). For `q=y²`: zero escapes, `m≤3` (`residual_q.py`).
**Control (non-vacuous test):** the same search on the *fully-symmetric* `U_3` (`S_3`) **finds** the
escape `Δ_{C_2}=⟨(0\,1)(2\,3)⟩∉𝓕` at `m=4` — exactly the U-corner witness (`residual_typesets.py`,
S_3). The test detects escapes when they exist; for `C_3` there are none.

## §2. `(†)` holds: `⟦p⟧∘M∘⟦q⟧∘M ≅ ⟦p◁L⟧∘M`

Let `L` be the **list** container `(ℕ, n↦[n])`, `⟦L⟧(Y)=Σ_{n}Y^n` — a polynomial (all exponents
finite), i.e. flat.

> **Lemma 2 (type-set).** For every polynomial `q` with at least one positive-arity shape, the
> molecule type-set of `M∘⟦q⟧∘M` equals `𝒴`, as does that of `L∘M`.

*Proof.* `⊆𝒴`: Lemma 1. `⊇𝒴` for `M∘⟦q⟧∘M`: given `τ=∏_iAut(t_i)∈𝒴`, realize it by a **rigid comb**
`ω(u_1, ω(u_2, ω(⋯,\mathrm{peg}),\mathrm{peg}),\mathrm{peg})` whose children at each node are pairwise
non-isomorphic (a `t_i`, a strictly larger subtree, and a peg) so that case 1 never fires: the
stabilizer is the direct product `∏_iAut(t_i)=τ` (identical `t_i` are placed at distinct depths, hence
never rotated — for `C_3`, `ω(t,t,s)` with `s≇t` already has `Aut(t)×Aut(t)`, the copies **unswapped**).
`L∘M`: a molecule is a list `(v_1,…,v_k)` of single trees / units with stabilizer `∏Aut(v_i)`
(distinguishable positions) — precisely the elements of `𝒴`, all realized. ∎

> **Lemma 3 (all multiplicities `ℵ_0`).** In `M∘⟦q⟧∘M` (any `q` with a positive-arity shape) and in
> `L∘M`, every molecule type occurs with multiplicity `ℵ_0`.

*Proof.* Countably many molecules total, and every realized type is padded without bound preserving
its stabilizer: in `M∘⟦q⟧∘M`, `w ↦ ω(w,\mathrm{peg},\mathrm{peg})` has `Aut=Aut(w)` (the unique
non-peg child `w` is pinned; a 3-cycle onto a peg-slot changes the config) and is a new molecule,
iterate; in `L∘M`, `(v_1,…,v_k) ↦ (v_1,…,v_k,e)` appends a unit (empty support, distinguishable
position) preserving the stabilizer, iterate. *(Verified: `residual_mult.py` — counts of both types
strictly increase with the peg bound at `m=2,3`.)* ∎

**Consequence.** By Lemmas 2–3, `M∘⟦q⟧∘M` and `L∘M` have the identical molecule multiset
`⊔_{τ∈𝒴} ℵ_0·τ`, hence `M∘⟦q⟧∘M ≅ L∘M` naturally. (For `q` with no positive-arity shape `q` is a
constant `c`; then `⟦q⟧∘M≅c`, `M∘c` is a constant, `≅ ⟦c'⟧∘M` for a constant container `c'`.) Then
```
   ⟦p⟧∘M∘⟦q⟧∘M ≅ ⟦p⟧∘(L∘M) = (⟦p⟧∘⟦L⟧)∘M = ⟦p◁L⟧∘M ,
```
an object of `𝓘_M`. This is exactly `(†)`. ∎

**Direct confirmation of the flagship instance.** `residual_typesets.py` (C_3): the molecule
type-sets of `M∘M` and `L∘M` coincide exactly at `m=2,3,4`, with no set-difference either way.
Together with Lemma 3 this is `M∘M ≅ L∘M` — the `p=q=Id` case of `(†)`.

## §3. Verdict and the corrected biconditional

> **Theorem (necessity fails).** For the `C_3` residual monad `M`, the essential image of `⟦−⟧_M` is
> closed under composition (`(†)` holds), yet `M` is not polynomial. Hence the converse of THM 3(A)
> is **false**: closure does **not** imply polynomiality.

This is consistent with the *proved* THM 3(B) cardinality law (`2026-09-05-thm3` §B): that law
excludes only monads with **finite, super-polynomially-growing** `|MX|`; the `C_3` residual has
`|MX|=∞` for `X≠∅` (unbounded degree), so the law is vacuous there and excludes nothing.

**The dichotomy (why full symmetry is exactly the dividing line).**

| operad `O` (unital, `a_0>0`) | binary composite `μ=ω(x,y,e…)` | escape in `M∘M` | THM 3 necessity |
|---|---|---|---|
| fully symmetric (`𝒰`: `H_g=S`) | **commutative** (`S_2` survives) | `Δ_{C_2}∉𝓕` via peg-rigidified swapped blocks (R2) | **holds** (proved, U-corner/family) |
| partial symmetry (`C_3`) | **non-commutative** (no transposition) | **none** — pegs add no rigidity single trees lack | **fails** (this file) |

Full symmetry makes `μ` commutative, so a peg can rigidify a block (`μ(a,\mathrm{peg})` loses its
`S_2`) and the outer commutative `μ` **swaps** two such rigid blocks, producing `Δ_{C_2}`, a
stabilizer no single tree has (R2). Partial symmetry makes `μ` non-commutative: pegs rigidify nothing
new and there is no outer swap, so every stabilizer is already a single-tree Aut and closure holds.

**Corrected statement of THM 3.** *`M`-containers compose ⟺ `M` polynomial* is FALSE as a
biconditional over all analytic monads. The correct necessity is conditional:
> closure ⟹ `M` polynomial **holds** for `a_0=0` (Theorem P), for bounded wreath depth (Theorem S′),
> and for fully-symmetric-unital operads (`𝒰`); it **fails** for partially-symmetric unital operads
> (`C_3` and, by the same mechanism, any operad with no commutative binary composite).

The clean invariant survives as a *sufficient* condition and as a biconditional **within the
fully-symmetric world**: there, composition of `M`-containers ⟺ flatness of the species ⟺ `M`
polynomial. Outside it, non-polynomial monads can compose.
