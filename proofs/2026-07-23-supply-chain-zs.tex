is a Zappa--Sz\'ep product: a computed worked example\\
\large (with the inventory-inconsistency obstruction in $H^2(\Sk_\C;\Zn)\cong\Zn$)}
\author{MacBeth\\\small Kodamai}

\begin{abstract}
This is a \textsc{computed} worked example, staging for the applications turn of the
Kodamai grant: it instantiates machinery already \textsc{proved} elsewhere in a new
domain and commits nothing to the book. We do three things. \emph{(A)} We present a
minimal supply chain $\Prc\text{ocure}\to\text{manufacture}\to\text{ship}$ as an
explicit small category and write out its Ahman--Uustalu directed container
$(S,P,o,\oplus)$, machine-checking the five directed-container laws. \emph{(B)} We
model two flows sharing a warehouse as a small category $\W_{n,\varepsilon}$ carrying a
cyclic \emph{lot-cursor} token $\tau$ of order $n$ at the shared node; fixing the
token-internal relabelings as the right factor $\D$, the pairwise Zappa--Sz\'ep
criterion and the $(G)$-obstruction-is-$H^2$ theorem (both cited, proved) give
\[
   [\omega(\W_{n,\varepsilon})]=\varepsilon\quad\text{in}\quad
   H^2(\Sk_\C;\Zn)\cong\Zn ,
\]
so the joint chain serializes into a Zappa--Sz\'ep product $\C\bowtie\D$
\emph{iff $\varepsilon=0$}, i.e.\ iff the two routes to the same delivered good agree
on provenance. Here $\varepsilon\in\Zn$ is the \emph{quantitative unit-mismatch}
between the routes; the parity case $n=2$ reproduces the proved orchestration
re-entrancy bit. \emph{(C)} The same machine, $n=2$, is the merge of two ologs sharing
a type: a naming-convention disagreement is the nonzero class. Every numerical claim
is machine-checked in \texttt{scratch/supply\_chain\_zs.py}. \textbf{Grade discipline:}
the general theorems are cited at \textsc{proved}/\textsc{lean-verified}; the
domain instantiation is \textsc{computed}; the object-level claim ``a real supply chain
\emph{is} this category'' stays \textbf{open} (SEED~Q4) --- we build a faithful
abstraction, not a fidelity claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, grades, and cited scaffolding}\label{sec:scope}

Nothing here is new mathematics. The contribution is a \emph{faithful minimal
abstraction} of a supply chain in which the already-built container/Zappa--Sz\'ep
machinery computes a meaningful invariant --- exactly the move that promoted
orchestration$=$ZS from analogy to computed to proved, now run in the
supply-chain / olog domain. Four facts are used as black boxes.

\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{(T1)}] \emph{DCont $\simeq$ SmallCat} (Ahman--Uustalu; our
\texttt{DContCat.lean}, sorry-free, registry \texttt{equivalence-chain},
\textsc{lean-verified}). A directed container is exactly a small category; anything
presenting a small category is a directed container for free.
\item[\textbf{(T2)}] \emph{Pairwise Zappa--Sz\'ep criterion} \cite{pairwise}
(\textsc{proved}). For a small category $\K$ and wide $\D\subseteq\K$, there is a wide
$\C$ with $(\C,\D)$ a strict factorization system --- equivalently a distributive law
$\lambda:\D\C\Rightarrow\C\D$, equivalently $\K=\C\bowtie\D$ --- iff \textnormal{(L)}
each $\Hom_\K(-,b)$ is a free right $\D$-set and \textnormal{(G)} free bases close into
a wide subcategory.
\item[\textbf{(T3)}] \emph{The $(G)$-obstruction is an $H^2$ class} \cite{gobstr}
(\textsc{proved}). Under \textnormal{(L)} and hypothesis \textnormal{(H)} (abelian
vertex groups, trivial left action), $(G)\Leftrightarrow[\omega]=0\in
H^2(\Sk_\C;\D)$, with $\Sk_\C$ the orbit category and $\D$ the vertex-group presheaf.
Cohomology is Baues--Wirsching \cite{bw} / Rosebrugh--Wood \cite{rw}, cited not
reproved.
\item[\textbf{(T4)}] \emph{Orchestration re-entrancy} \cite{orch} (\textsc{proved};
$[\omega]=\varepsilon$ core \textsc{lean-verified}, \texttt{Reentrancy.lean}). The
$n=2$ instance of \S\ref{sec:warehouse} below; we cite it as the parity anchor.
\end{itemize}

Ologs are Spivak's prior art \cite{spivak}; the knowledge-graph-as-copresheaf reading
is folklore categorical databases. We import definitions, we do not reinvent them.

\paragraph{The one honest caveat.} Whether a \emph{real} supply chain genuinely
presents a category --- operations compose associatively, and the
inventory-consistency path-equations hold on the nose --- is SEED Open Question~4 and
stays \textbf{open}. Everything below is a chosen minimal abstraction: the theorems
apply to \emph{it}, and the value is that they compute the right qualitative
invariant. We never write ``a supply chain \emph{is} $\W_{n,\varepsilon}$.''

\section{(A) The base chain as a directed container}\label{sec:base}

\begin{definition}[The procurement chain $\C_0$]
Let $\C_0$ be the poset category on stages $\mathsf{Src}<\mathsf{Mfg}<\mathsf{Dst}$
(supplier, manufacturing, delivery): objects are the three stages, and there is a
unique morphism $i\to j$ whenever $i\le j$, read as the \emph{net operation} carrying
material from stage $i$ to stage $j$. Composition is do-then-do; thinness encodes
``all routings from $i$ to $j$ that respect the order deliver the same material.''
\end{definition}

By (T1), $\C_0$ is a directed container $(S,P,o,\oplus)$
(Ahman--Uustalu; our \texttt{DContCat.lean} dictionary):
\[
\begin{aligned}
S &= \{\mathsf{Src},\mathsf{Mfg},\mathsf{Dst}\} && \text{(shapes = stages)},\\
P(s) &= \{\,\text{operations out of }s\,\}=\{\,(s\to j):j\ge s\,\}
   && \text{(positions = outgoing operations)},\\
o_s &= (s\to s)=\id_s && \text{(root = stay put)},\\
s\!\downarrow\! p &= \cod(p) && \text{(sub-shape = destination stage)},\\
p\oplus q &= q\circ p && \text{(shift = do }p\text{ then }q).
\end{aligned}
\]
Explicitly $P(\mathsf{Src})=\{\id,\ \mathsf{Src}\!\to\!\mathsf{Mfg},\
\mathsf{Src}\!\to\!\mathsf{Dst}\}$, $P(\mathsf{Mfg})=\{\id,\
\mathsf{Mfg}\!\to\!\mathsf{Dst}\}$, $P(\mathsf{Dst})=\{\id\}$, and e.g.
$(\mathsf{Src}\!\to\!\mathsf{Mfg})\oplus(\mathsf{Mfg}\!\to\!\mathsf{Dst})
=(\mathsf{Src}\!\to\!\mathsf{Dst})$: procure-then-ship \emph{is} the direct delivery
operation.

\begin{proposition}[D-laws]\label{prop:dlaws}
$(S,P,o,\downarrow,\oplus)$ satisfies the five directed-container laws:
D1 $s\!\downarrow\! o_s=s$; D2 $o_s\oplus p=p$; D3 $p\oplus o_{s\downarrow p}=p$;
D4 $s\!\downarrow\!(p\oplus q)=(s\!\downarrow\! p)\!\downarrow\! q$; D5
$(p\oplus q)\oplus r=p\oplus(q\oplus r)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\C_0$ is a thin poset category, so D1--D3 are the unit laws (identities are the
$(s\to s)$), D4 says the destination of a composite is the destination of its second
leg, and D5 is associativity of composition, all automatic in a poset. Machine-checked
exhaustively over all composable $p,q,r$ in \texttt{supply\_chain\_zs.py} part~(A).
\end{proof}

This is the object-level content the orchestration note omitted (it worked only at the
category level): a supply chain, once abstracted to a category, is literally a
polynomial comonoid / directed container, so the entire structure theory --- the four
monoidal products, the (co)monoid classifications, the chain rule --- is available to
it. That is a definitional dividend of (T1), graded \textsc{computed} (an
instantiation), and it is \emph{greenfield in our corpus}.

\section{(B) Two flows sharing a warehouse: the family $\W_{n,\varepsilon}$}
\label{sec:warehouse}

Now the composition story. Two flows meet at a warehouse $\Wh$. The warehouse keeps
an internal \emph{lot-cursor}: a cyclic pointer $\tau$ of order $n$ into $n$
interchangeable bins / lot-slots (FIFO position mod $n$, or a bin index). Physically
$\tau$ moves nothing --- it is pure bookkeeping --- but a unit leaving the warehouse
carries whatever cursor value was current. Advancing the cursor is an automorphism
$\tau$ with $\tau^n=\id_\Wh$, so $\End(\Wh)\cong\Zn$.

\begin{definition}[The warehouse family $\W_{n,\varepsilon}$]\label{def:Wne}
For $n\ge1$ and $\varepsilon\in\Zn$, let $\W_{n,\varepsilon}$ be the small category with
objects $\Wh$ (warehouse), $\Prc$ (processing/manufacture), $\De$ (delivery), and
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\End(\Wh)=\{\tau^k:k\in\Zn\}\cong\Zn,\quad \End(\Prc)=\{\id\},\quad \End(\De)=\{\id\},\\
&\Hom(\Wh,\Prc)=\{p\,\tau^k:k\in\Zn\},\quad
 \Hom(\Wh,\De)=\{q\,\tau^k:k\in\Zn\},\quad
 \Hom(\Prc,\De)=\{s,\ s_2\},
\end{aligned}
\]
no arrows into $\Wh$ except its endomorphisms, and no arrows $\De\to\cdot$ except
$\id_\De$ (acyclic: $\Wh\to\Prc\to\De$ with a direct $\Wh\to\De$). Here
$p\,\tau^k:=p\circ\tau^k$, all distinct; the two processing operations compose through
the middle by
\[
   s\circ p=q,\qquad s_2\circ p=q\cdot\tau^{\varepsilon},
\]
extended by right $\tau$-equivariance ($s\circ p\tau^k=q\tau^k$,
$s_2\circ p\tau^k=q\,\tau^{k+\varepsilon}$).
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Reading.} $p$ dispatches a lot to processing; $q$ is the direct/expected
delivery recorded by the warehouse's books; $s$ is the nominal production-and-ship
line and $s_2$ is an alternative route (rework, a second line, an expedited lane) that
delivers the \emph{same good}. The single datum $\varepsilon$ records how many
lot-positions $s_2$ \emph{advances the provenance cursor} relative to the nominal
route: $\varepsilon=0$ means the rework line preserves lot-provenance, $\varepsilon\ne0$
means it shifts it by $\varepsilon$ units. The wide right factor of bookkeeping moves
is
\[
   \D=\{\tau^k:k\in\Zn\}\cup\{\id_\Prc,\id_\De\}\subseteq\W_{n,\varepsilon},
\]
the ``warehouse-internal relabeling'' subcategory --- the \emph{second chain} that
shares the warehouse with the physical-flow chain.

\begin{lemma}[$\W_{n,\varepsilon}$ is a category]\label{lem:cat}
For every $n,\varepsilon$, $\W_{n,\varepsilon}$ is a well-defined small category.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Only two families of composites need specifying: the $\Zn$-action of $\tau$ on
$\Wh$-sourced arrows (a group action, $\tau^n=\id$), and the two length-two composites
$s\circ p,\,s_2\circ p:\Wh\to\De$. All longer composites factor through the sink $\De$
or start with a $\Wh$-endomorphism, so associativity reduces to right-equivariance,
$(f\circ p)\circ\tau^k=f\circ(p\,\tau^k)$, which holds by definition of $f\circ
p\tau^k$. Machine-checked (full associativity table) for eight $(n,\varepsilon)$ pairs
in \texttt{supply\_chain\_zs.py} part~(B).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[(L) and (H) hold, uniformly in $\varepsilon$]\label{lem:LH}
Every hom-presheaf $\Hom_{\W_{n,\varepsilon}}(-,b)$ is a free right $\D$-set, and
$(\W_{n,\varepsilon},\D)$ satisfies hypothesis (H).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\emph{(L).} The right $\D$-action is precomposition; it is trivial on $\Prc$- and
$\De$-sourced arrows and is the regular $\Zn$-action on $\Wh$-sourced arrows. Orbit
decomposition:
$\Hom(-,\Wh)=\{\tau^k\}$ (one regular $\Zn$-orbit);
$\Hom(-,\Prc)=\{p\,\tau^k\}\sqcup\{\id_\Prc\}$;
$\Hom(-,\De)=\{q\,\tau^k\}\sqcup\{s\}\sqcup\{s_2\}\sqcup\{\id_\De\}$.
Each $\Wh$-apex orbit is a free $\Zn$-torsor (the $\tau^k$ are distinct), each other
orbit a singleton with trivial group; so every hom-presheaf is a coproduct of
representable $\D$-sets, i.e.\ free. This uses only distinctness of the $p\tau^k$ and
$q\tau^k$; it is independent of $\varepsilon$ (moving $s_2\circ p$ inside the orbit
$\{q\tau^k\}$ does not change orbits). \emph{(H).} $\D$ is a disjoint union of abelian
vertex groups $G_\Wh\cong\Zn$, $G_\Prc=G_\De=0$; the only nontrivial vertex group sits
at $\Wh$, which is a \emph{source} (no generator targets $\Wh$), so the left action
(postcomposition by a target automorphism) is trivial. Machine-checked (freeness of
every orbit) in part~(B).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Orbit category, complex, and $H^2\cong\Zn$]\label{prop:h2}
$\Sk_\C$ has objects $\Wh,\Prc,\De$ and non-identity generators $[p]:\Wh\to\Prc$,
$[q]:\Wh\to\De$, $[s],[s_2]:\Prc\to\De$, with
$[s]\ast[p]=[s_2]\ast[p]=[q]$ \emph{independently of $\varepsilon$} (because $q$ and
$q\tau^\varepsilon$ lie in one orbit). The presheaf has $\D(\Wh)=\Zn$,
$\D(\Prc)=\D(\De)=0$, and all restriction maps vanish (no generator targets $\Wh$).
Hence $C^1\cong\Zn^2$ (coords $h[p],h[q]$), $C^2\cong\Zn^2$ (coords
$\omega([s],[p]),\omega([s_2],[p])$), $C^3=0$, and
\[
   (\delta^1h)([s],[p])=(\delta^1h)([s_2],[p])=h[p]-h[q],
\]
so $B^2=\{(t,t):t\in\Zn\}$ is the diagonal and
\[
   H^2(\Sk_\C;\D)=\Zn^2/\langle(1,1)\rangle\cong\Zn,\qquad [(a,b)]\mapsto b-a.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Identical bookkeeping to \cite[\S4]{orch} with $\Zn$ in place of $\mathbb Z/2$: the
generators, the vanishing restrictions, and the two composable pairs $([s],[p])$,
$([s_2],[p])$ are exactly as there, and the Baues--Wirsching coboundary formula
$(\delta^1h)(o_2,o_1)=\varphi_{o_1}(h(o_2))-h(o_2\circ o_1)+h(o_1)$ reduces to
$h[p]-h[q]$ since $\varphi_{[p]}=\D([p]):0\to\Zn$. Machine-checked
($B^2=$ diagonal) for all tested $n$ in part~(B).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The class is the unit-mismatch]\label{thm:main}
With the natural transversal $T=\{p,q,s,s_2\}$, the defect $2$-cocycle is
$\omega_T=(0,\varepsilon)\in\Zn^2$, so
\[
   [\omega(\W_{n,\varepsilon})]=\varepsilon\in H^2(\Sk_\C;\D)\cong\Zn .
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both composable pairs have target orbit $\orb(q)$ with chosen generator $q$. From
$s\circ p=q=q\circ\id_\Wh$ we read $\omega_T([s],[p])=0$, and from $s_2\circ p=
q\cdot\tau^\varepsilon=q\circ\tau^\varepsilon$ we read
$\omega_T([s_2],[p])=\tau^\varepsilon=\varepsilon$. So $\omega_T=(0,\varepsilon)$, and
its class under $[(a,b)]\mapsto b-a$ is $\varepsilon$. Machine-verified
$\omega_T=(0,\varepsilon)$ and $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ in part~(B).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Inventory-consistency dichotomy]\label{cor:main}
For the warehouse abstraction $\W_{n,\varepsilon}$ with bookkeeping right factor $\D$:
\[
   \W_{n,\varepsilon}=\C\bowtie\D \ \text{ exists }\iff \varepsilon=0
   \iff \text{both routes to $\De$ agree on lot-provenance.}
\]
When $\varepsilon\ne0$ the obstruction $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ is a nonzero element of
$H^2\cong\Zn$: a genuine \emph{inventory inconsistency}, the two routes to the same
delivered good disagreeing by $\varepsilon$ lot-positions, and no serialization of the
physical flow with the warehouse bookkeeping exists.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
(L) holds (Lemma~\ref{lem:LH}) so by (T2) the product exists iff (G); (H) holds so by
(T3) (G) iff $[\omega]=0$; by Theorem~\ref{thm:main} $[\omega]=\varepsilon$. Chain the
equivalences. As an independent (non-cohomological) cross-check, part~(B) brute-forces
the strict factorization systems: $\#\mathrm{SFS}=n$ when $\varepsilon=0$ (a
$\Zn$-torsor of ways to choose the canonical lot-labeling) and $\#\mathrm{SFS}=0$ when
$\varepsilon\ne0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why $\Zn$, not just parity]
Orchestration \cite{orch} needed only $\mathbb Z/2$: a turn token is one bit. Inventory
is naturally a \emph{counter}, and the obstruction lands in the counter's group: the
class $[\omega]=\varepsilon$ is not just ``inconsistent yes/no'' but \emph{by how many
units} the two provenance routes disagree, valued in $\Zn$. The proved parity theorem
is the $n=2$ face of this. The gauge freedom $B^2=\langle(1,1)\rangle$ is exactly the
liberty to declare which lot-slot is ``slot zero'': it shifts \emph{both} routes
together, so only their \emph{difference} $\varepsilon$ is observable --- the
categorical avatar of a flat $\Zn$-bundle being trivial iff its holonomy (here the
provenance drift) vanishes.
\end{remark}

\section{(C) Olog sibling: merging two schemas along a shared type}\label{sec:olog}

The same abstraction serves ontology/knowledge-graph merges. The olog
$\mathsf{Book}\xrightarrow{\text{has}}\mathsf{Author}\xrightarrow{\text{has}}
\mathsf{Name}$ is the free category on that graph; by (T1) it is a directed container
with shapes $=$ types, positions out of a type $=$ the aspects available from it,
$\oplus=$ aspect composition, path-equations $=$ facts (D-laws machine-checked,
part~(C)). Merging it with a second olog sharing $\mathsf{Author}$ (e.g.\
$\mathsf{Author}\to\mathsf{Affiliation}$) is composing two directed containers over the
shared type --- a Zappa--Sz\'ep problem. Take $\Wh:=\mathsf{Author}$ carrying a
$\mathbb Z/2$ token $=$ choice of naming convention (``Last, First'' vs.\ ``First
Last''), $\Prc:=$ name-record, $\De:=$ rendered display. Then
Corollary~\ref{cor:main} at $n=2$ reads: the merge succeeds ($\C\bowtie\D$ exists) iff
the two schemas agree on the convention ($\varepsilon=0$); a disagreement is the
nonzero class $[\omega]=1$, a \emph{merge conflict} --- the same $H^2$ bit as
supply-chain inconsistency and agent re-entrancy. Machine-checked, part~(C).

\section{Status, regimes, and grant relevance}\label{sec:status}

\paragraph{The computed table.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
regime & $[\omega]\in\Zn$ & outcome & meaning \\
\midrule
$\varepsilon=0$ (any $n$) & $0$ & composes, $\#\mathrm{SFS}=n$ & routes agree on provenance \\
$\varepsilon\ne0$ (any $n$) & $\varepsilon$ & obstructed, $\#\mathrm{SFS}=0$ & inventory off by $\varepsilon$ units \\
$n=2,\varepsilon=1$ & $1$ & obstructed & $=$ orchestration re-entrancy bit \cite{orch} \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Eight $(n,\varepsilon)$ instances verified in \texttt{scratch/supply\_chain\_zs.py}
(category axioms, (L), $\Sk_\C$ shape, $B^2=$ diagonal, $\omega_T=(0,\varepsilon)$,
$[\omega]=\varepsilon$, and the $\#\mathrm{SFS}$ cross-check).

\paragraph{What is proved vs.\ computed.} The general theorems (T1)--(T4) are
\textsc{proved}/\textsc{lean-verified} and are cited, not re-derived. The
\emph{domain instantiation} --- that this abstraction of supply chains and olog merges
computes the inventory-inconsistency / merge-conflict class --- is \textsc{computed}
illustration. The object-level modelling claim ``a real supply chain presents a
category'' is \textbf{open} (SEED~Q4); we have exhibited a faithful minimal
abstraction, not asserted fidelity, exactly as the orchestration note did for agents.

\paragraph{Grant line.} Supply-chain composability, ontology-merge consistency, and
agent-orchestration re-entrancy are the \emph{same degree-two obstruction} on the
handoff category, and in the supply-chain instance the class is refined from a bit to a
$\Zn$-valued \emph{unit-count} of the discrepancy. This is the Path-5 Impact spine: the
applications are not new mathematics but new \emph{instances of one obstruction
theorem}, and that is the honest strength of the story.
