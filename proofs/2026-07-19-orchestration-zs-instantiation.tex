\title{Orchestration composition is a Zappa--Sz\'ep product:\\
a worked instantiation, and re-entrancy as the $H^2$ obstruction}
\author{MacBeth\\\small Kodamai}
\date{19 July 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We ground the ``agent orchestration $=$ directed container'' dictionary on the
smallest non-trivial case. A supervisor--worker orchestration is modelled as a
small category (equivalently a directed container, Ahman--Uustalu); composing two
such orchestrations into a single interleaved joint agent is a \emph{distributive
law / Zappa--Sz\'ep product} $\K=\C\bowtie\D$, governed by the pairwise
criterion \textnormal{(L)}$\wedge$\textnormal{(G)}. We exhibit, and machine-check,
a topology table with four regimes. Three \emph{compose}: independent supervisors
(trivial law, $\K=\C\times\D$); a genuinely non-abelian coherent interleaving
($\K=S_3=\mathbb Z/3\bowtie\mathbb Z/2$); and a state-protected (locked) re-entry
pattern (a $\mathbb Z/2$-torsor of joint agents). One is \emph{obstructed}: an
\emph{unprotected re-entrancy} pattern, in which a worker's outcome mutates the
supervisor's shared state, presents \emph{exactly} as the ten-morphism rigid twist
--- \textnormal{(L)} holds but \textnormal{(G)} fails, so no distributive law
exists, and the obstruction is the nonzero class
$[\omega]\in H^2(\Sk_\C;\D)\cong\mathbb Z/2$. This is the ``re-entrancy $=$ failed
distributive law'' reading, computed rather than asserted. The single bit that
flips the verdict is whether a callee mutates the caller's shared state. All
claims are machine-checked (\texttt{projects/scratch/orchestration\_zs*.py}); the
load-bearing theorems are cited, not reproved.
\end{abstract}

\section{What is and is not claimed}\label{sec:scope}

The abstract machinery is established and used as a black box.
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{(T1)}] \emph{Directed container $\cong$ small category} (Ahman--Uustalu;
formalised in Lean in the author's development). A handoff topology --- objects are
roles/agents, morphisms are admissible handoff paths --- is a small category, hence
a directed container.
\item[\textbf{(T2)}] \emph{Pairwise Zappa--Sz\'ep criterion} \cite{pairwise}. For a
small category $\K$ and a wide subcategory $\D\subseteq\K$, there is a wide $\C$
with $(\C,\D)$ a strict factorization system $\K=\C\bowtie\D$ if and only if
\textnormal{(L)} every hom-presheaf $\Hom_\K(-,b)$ is free as a right $\D$-set, and
\textnormal{(G)} free bases can be chosen closing into a wide subcategory. The data
$\K=\C\bowtie\D$ is exactly a distributive law $\lambda:\D\C\Rightarrow\C\D$
satisfying the matched-pair axioms ZS1--ZS4.
\item[\textbf{(T3)}] \emph{The (G)-obstruction is an $H^2$ class} \cite{gobstr}.
Under the standard hypothesis (abelian vertex groups, trivial left action),
$\textnormal{(G)}\Leftrightarrow[\omega]=0\in H^2(\Sk_\C;\D)$; the rigid twist
realizes the generator of $H^2\cong\mathbb Z/2$. The cohomology itself is classical
(Rosebrugh--Wood for existence, Baues--Wirsching for the abelian classification,
Pirashvili for the non-abelian case) and is cited, never reproved.
\end{itemize}

The reading ``an agent's interface is a polynomial functor / container; its dynamics
is a coalgebra'' is Spivak's prior art and is not claimed here. \textbf{The sole
contribution of this note} is the \emph{worked instantiation}: that the
supervisor--worker orchestration patterns below literally present as the categories
to which (T1)--(T3) apply, turning the dictionary from a proposed reading into a
computed one. In particular the models are minimal faithful abstractions; we do not
claim any specific production framework is \emph{exactly} one of them.

\section{The orchestration category}\label{sec:model}

Fix the handoff roles $\Sup$ (supervisor), $\Wk$ (a worker running), $\Rt$ (a
result/return point). The supervisor carries a one-bit \emph{turn token} recording
which worker currently holds its attention; passing the token is an involution
$\tau$, $\tau^2=\id$, so $\End(\Sup)\supseteq\{\id_\Sup,\tau\}\cong\mathbb Z/2$.
The generating handoffs are
\[
  p,p\tau:\Sup\to\Wk\quad(\text{dispatch, in turn-state $0$ resp.\ $1$; }p\tau=p\circ\tau),
  \qquad
  s,s_2:\Wk\to\Rt\quad(\text{two worker outcomes}),
\]
with composites $q,q\tau:\Sup\to\Rt$. The right $\mathbb Z/2$-action is
$p\cdot\tau=p\tau$, $q\cdot\tau=q\tau$. We take the prescribed right factor to be
the supervisor-internal (``token/reader'') moves
\[
  \D=\{\id_\Sup,\tau,\id_\Wk,\id_\Rt\},
\]
a wide subcategory. The candidate left factor $\C$ we are solving for is the genuine
dispatch/handoff part. Everything hinges on \emph{one} composite: how a worker
outcome, run after a dispatch, sits relative to the token.

\begin{definition}[The two regimes]\label{def:regimes}
Write $s$ for ``the worker returns normally'' and $s_2$ for ``the worker re-enters''
(calls back into the supervisor before returning).
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{(bug)}] \emph{Unprotected re-entry.} The re-entrant branch flips the
token: $s\circ p=q$ but $s_2\circ p=q\tau$ (forcing $s\circ p\tau=q\tau$,
$s_2\circ p\tau=q$). The category is $\K_{\mathrm{bug}}$.
\item[\textbf{(ok)}] \emph{State-protected re-entry (a lock).} Neither outcome
touches the token: $s\circ p=s_2\circ p=q$, $s\circ p\tau=s_2\circ p\tau=q\tau$.
The category is $\K_{\mathrm{ok}}$.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

Both $\K_{\mathrm{bug}},\K_{\mathrm{ok}}$ are genuine categories and $\D$ is wide in
each (machine-checked). The reading is exact: \emph{re-entrancy is the callee
mutating shared caller state during a pending call}, and here that is precisely the
$s_2$-branch dragging the supervisor's turn token, i.e. $s_2\circ p=q\tau\neq q$.

\begin{remark}[Relation to the free category on $\Sup\rightleftarrows\{w_1,w_2\}$]
This is the minimal finite faithful model of the two-worker re-dispatch pattern.
The two worker outcomes $s,s_2$ are the two-worker choice ``re-dispatch to $w_1$
vs.\ $w_2$'', and the involution $\tau$ is the token's monodromy around the cycle
$w_1\to\Sup\to w_2\to\Sup\to w_1$: traversing it once toggles the token, so the loop
carries a $\mathbb Z/2$ holonomy. The free category on the graph
$\Sup\rightleftarrows\{w_1,w_2\}$ is infinite (its morphisms are all dispatch/return
paths) and carries no relations, hence no finite obstruction; the obstruction is
created by the \emph{shared-state} relation $s_2\circ p=q\tau$, which is exactly the
identification a re-entrant, state-mutating orchestration imposes. We work with the
finite quotient because the $\mathbb Z/2$ holonomy is a finite phenomenon the
Zappa--Sz\'ep machinery computes directly.
\end{remark}

\section{The obstruction: unprotected re-entry is the rigid twist}\label{sec:bug}

\begin{proposition}[$\K_{\mathrm{bug}}$ is obstructed]\label{prop:bug}
In $\K_{\mathrm{bug}}$, \textnormal{(L)} holds (each $\Hom(-,b)$ is free over $\D$,
with exactly two bases per target), but \textnormal{(G)} fails: no wide $\C$ makes
$(\C,\D)$ a strict factorization system. Hence no distributive law
$\lambda:\D\C\Rightarrow\C\D$ exists, and the two concurrent behaviours (the
supervisor's dispatch and the worker's internal step) cannot be serialized into a
single joint agent.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Freeness is a direct check; we give the crux. The right $\D$-orbits in $\Hom(-,\Rt)$
are $\{q,q\tau\}$ (source $\Sup$), $\{s\}$, $\{s_2\}$ (source $\Wk$; $\D$ has no
non-identity arrow out of $\Wk$), and $\{\id_\Rt\}$; a free basis picks one of
$q,q\tau$ together with $s,s_2,\id_\Rt$ --- two bases. In $\Hom(-,\Wk)$ the orbits
are $\{p,p\tau\}$ and $\{\id_\Wk\}$; a basis is $\{\id_\Wk,p\}$ or $\{\id_\Wk,p\tau\}$.
So \textnormal{(L)} holds. Now any closing $\C$ chooses a $\Wk$-basis element
$t\in\{p,p\tau\}$ and an $\Rt$-basis element $r\in\{q,q\tau\}$. Closure requires
$s\circ t,\ s_2\circ t\in\C$, i.e.\ both must be the chosen $r$. But
$s\circ p=q$ and $s_2\circ p=q\tau$ are the \emph{two distinct} orbit representatives,
so the $\Rt$-basis would have to contain both $q$ and $q\tau$ --- impossible, it
contains exactly one. The case $t=p\tau$ ($s\circ p\tau=q\tau$, $s_2\circ p\tau=q$)
is identical. Hence \textnormal{(G)} fails; by (T2) there is no strict factorization,
equivalently no distributive law. A brute-force search over all wide subcategories
confirms $\#\mathrm{SFS}=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The class is the generator of $\mathbb Z/2$]\label{thm:h2}
$\K_{\mathrm{bug}}$ is isomorphic, as a category with its distinguished wide
subcategory $\D$, to the ten-morphism rigid twist of \cite{gobstr} with
$\D=\End$, under
\[
  \Sup\mapsto a,\ \Wk\mapsto x,\ \Rt\mapsto y,\quad
  \tau\mapsto g,\ p\mapsto p,\ p\tau\mapsto pg,\ s\mapsto s,\ s_2\mapsto s_2,\
  q\mapsto q,\ q\tau\mapsto qg.
\]
Consequently the hypothesis of (T3) holds ($\D$ is the abelian vertex group
$\mathbb Z/2$ at $\Sup$, trivial elsewhere; the left action is trivial), the orbit
category $\Sk_\C$ is the branch $\Sup\to\Wk\rightrightarrows\Rt$, and
\[
  H^2(\Sk_\C;\D)\cong\mathbb Z/2,\qquad [\omega]\neq 0\ \text{(the generator).}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The stated bijection is checked to preserve domains, codomains, all composites, and
$\D$ (machine-verified in \texttt{orchestration\_zs2.py}); it is therefore an
isomorphism of categories matching the wide subcategories. The $H^2$ computation is
then Theorem~rt of \cite{gobstr} verbatim: normalized cochains give
$Z^2=C^2\cong(\mathbb Z/2)^2$ (coordinates $\omega([s],[p]),\omega([s_2],[p])$),
$B^2=\langle(1,1)\rangle$ (the diagonal), so $H^2\cong\mathbb Z/2$, and the defect of
the natural transversal is $(0,1)\notin B^2$, the generator.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Reading the class]
The two coordinates of $\omega$ are the two worker outcomes; $B^2=$ diagonal is the
freedom to relabel which token-state is ``canonical.'' The class is nonzero precisely
because the \emph{two} outcomes disagree on that relabelling by one token-flip --- the
re-entrant branch's side effect. This is the categorical avatar of ``a flat bundle
is trivial iff its holonomy vanishes'': the local completions of each partial handoff
(free, by (L)) fail to glue into a global section (a serialized joint agent) by a
single $\mathbb Z/2$ monodromy. It is the same obstruction the SEED reads in Ethereum
re-entrancy, now located as an explicit $H^2$ generator on a handoff category.
\end{remark}

\section{The composing regimes}\label{sec:ok}

\begin{proposition}[Locking restores composability]\label{prop:ok}
In $\K_{\mathrm{ok}}$, \textnormal{(L)} and \textnormal{(G)} both hold; there are
exactly two strict factorization systems over $\D$ (a $\mathbb Z/2$-torsor, so
$[\omega]=0$), with $\C=\{\id_\Sup,\id_\Wk,\id_\Rt,p,q,s,s_2\}$ in one, and the
extracted distributive law $\lambda$ satisfies ZS1--ZS4 and the unit laws.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Machine-checked (\texttt{orchestration\_zs.py}, \texttt{orchestration\_zs2.py}). The
only change from $\K_{\mathrm{bug}}$ is that $s_2\circ p=q$ rather than $q\tau$, so
$s\circ t$ and $s_2\circ t$ now land on the \emph{same} orbit representative for a
fixed $\Wk$-basis choice; the contradiction in the proof of
Proposition~\ref{prop:bug} disappears and both transversals close. Extracting
$\lambda(d,c)$ as the unique factorization of $d\circ c$ and checking ZS1--ZS4
returns no violations.
\end{proof}

Two further composing regimes complete the table.

\begin{example}[Independent supervisors]\label{ex:indep}
Two supervisors over read-only workers do not interfere: the distributive law is the
swap $\lambda(d,c)=(c,d)$, which trivially satisfies ZS1--ZS4, and
$\K=\C\times\D$. With $\C=\D=\mathbb Z/2$ (dispatch cycles truncated for finiteness)
one gets $\K=\mathbb Z/2\times\mathbb Z/2$, a category on the nose (checked).
\end{example}

\begin{example}[Genuinely non-abelian coherent interleaving]\label{ex:s3}
Two supervisors over a \emph{shared} worker pool can interleave nontrivially yet
consistently. Take $\C=\mathbb Z/3$ (supervisor-$\C$'s cyclic worker rotation),
$\D=\mathbb Z/2$ (supervisor-$\D$'s toggle), with the left action ${}^{d}c=-c$ for
the non-identity $d$ (the toggle reverses the rotation) and trivial right action.
This is the dihedral matched pair; ZS1--ZS4 hold and
$\K=\C\bowtie\D=S_3$, with the join genuinely non-abelian
($(c_1)\circ(d_1)\neq(d_1)\circ(c_1)$, checked). So ``composition $=$ Zappa--Sz\'ep
product'' is non-vacuous: it produces a legitimate joint agent that is neither a
product nor obstructed. (The two-supervisor reading here is illustrative/schematic.)
\end{example}

$[\omega]\in H^2(\Sk_\C;\D)$.

\section{Grant relevance and honest status}\label{sec:grant}

This is the worked instantiation the orchestration dictionary lacked. It grounds the
one genuinely unstaked claim --- \emph{composing two agent orchestrations is a
distributive law / Zappa--Sz\'ep product $\C\bowtie\D$, not merely a functor} --- on
a computed example, and locates a concrete failure mode (unprotected re-entrancy) as
an explicit degree-two cohomology class on the handoff category. The degree axis is
the differentiator from the multi-agent-systems sheaf-Laplacian literature, which
puts $H^0$ (consensus) and $H^1$ (identifiability) on the \emph{communication} graph
--- \emph{state} questions --- whereas this puts $H^2$ on the \emph{handoff} category
--- a \emph{structural composability} question, one degree up.

\paragraph{Status.} Grade: \textbf{computed}. The scaffolding (T1)--(T3) is proved
and cited; the instantiation and the table are new and machine-checked; no new
cohomology is claimed, and the models are minimal faithful abstractions rather than
literal encodings of any named framework.
