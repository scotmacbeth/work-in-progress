## The collision

My completed result (2026-09-02, `proved`, `lemmaS-sufficiency-extensive-pole`,
[[lemmaS-sufficient-extensive-pole-pi0]]): on an infinitary-lextensive CCC base, `Fam(C^op)` is
**◁-admissible ⟺ `π₀` preserves finite products** (Cor 3.1). The two inadmissible extensive bases
(`Set×Set`, `Gl((−)²)`) both fail exactly this (Cor 3.2), [[gl-inadmissible-pi0-multiplicativity]].

Xarez (2011) proves a reflection has **stable units** (Cassidy–Hébert–Kelly strong admissibility)
**⟺ any finite product of connected components is connected.** Read literally, that second clause is
**verbatim my π₀-multiplicativity** — arrived at 15 years earlier, in a different subfield (Galois
descent / semi-left-exact reflections), under a *different* meaning of the word "admissible"
(Janelidze-admissible reflection, NOT my ◁-admissible base).

## Why this is the crown meta-pattern in REVERSE

Usually my delta is the seam prior work *fused away* ([[contribution-is-the-delta-prior-work-fused-away]]).
Here the risk is the opposite: a **lossy vocabulary collision** — two communities gave the *same
combinatorial fact* about connected-components functors two names ("stable units" / "π₀ multiplicative"),
and my summaries could manufacture novelty by not knowing the older name. This is the FUSION-vs-COLLISION
discipline ([[fusion-versus-identification]]) applied to *citations*: check the map, not the slogan.

## What survives even if it IS the same fact — and what to do

- **My ◁-admissibility application is still novel:** Xarez's setting is a reflective-subcategory /
  semi-left-exact reflection; mine is `Fam(C^op)` under the composition product `◁`, and the *bridge*
  (Lemma S / external distributive law ⟹ the π₀ condition is what decides ◁-admissibility) is not in
  his picture. What would move is the status of the **underlying lemma**: "products of connected
  components connected" would become citable folklore, not my discovery.
- The nLab pages `extensive category` / `connected object` independently state "products of connected
  objects need not be connected" (09-14 browse) — consonant: π₀-multiplicativity IS a genuine extra
  condition, and its failure is classical. This raises the prior-probability that Xarez's clause is a
  literal match.
- **ACTION (before the admissibility paper is called `publishable-result`):** deep-read 1112.4277,
  compare Xarez's reflection setup to my Fam(C^op)/◁ setup side by side. If literal: Cor 3.1 cites
  Xarez for the connected-components fact and claims only the ◁-admissibility equivalence. If merely
  analogous (his base = a reflection, mine = a composition product; the "finite product" may live in a
  different category): cite as related and keep the lemma. Either way this is a **citation correction**,
  not a demotion of the theorem.
