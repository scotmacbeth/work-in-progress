## The convention (this was the OWED item — decoded, not asked)
Row `R` distributes over column `C`, **`R` = the outer/distributing operation** (forced by `⊗/◁=L`
being Spivak's `⊗`-outer interchanger, Ref eq. 29). Then the grid is generated by two readings:
- **`(co)cartesian columns `+`,`×`:** "R preserves the (co)product". `D` = iso, `–` = not.
- **tensor columns `⊗`,`◁`:** duoidal interchanger `(aCb)R(cCd)→(aRc)C(bRd)`. `D`=iso, `L`=genuine
  lax, `–`=none.
- **formal-gadget exclusion:** the (co)limit interchangers that exist for *any* partner (copair out of
  a coproduct source when `R=+`; pair into a product target when `C=×` — S–S Example 2.2 + dual) are
  dashed. This is exactly why the whole `+` row and `×` column are `–`.

## Two deliverables beyond "reproduce Hedges"
1. **`◁/+` and `◁/×` are LEFT-variable only.** Sequential composition distributes over choice and
   product strictly in its *outer* argument (precomposition preserves all (co)limits) and **fails** in
   the inner argument: `(y+1)◁(1+0)=2` vs `3` (Niu–Spivak Ex 6.56); `2y◁(y×y)=2y² ≇ 4y²`. So Hedges'
   bare `D` there is a *one-sided* distributive law. Only `⊗/+`, `×/+` are two-sided.
2. **`×/⊗` correction: I prove it is `–`, not `L`.** The `×`-outer/`⊗`-inner interchanger does not
   exist — witness `(a,b,c,d)=(1,y,y,1)` makes it a morphism `1→y`, impossible (source `1` has an empty
   shape, target `y` does not). The genuine `⊗`–`×` interchanger is `⊗/×` (⊗ outer, × inner; S–S
   Example 2.2 dual, *pair-into-product*), which is **formal and never iso**. So the `⊗`–`×` lax cell,
   if recorded at all, belongs at `⊗/×`; Hedges' grid has it at `×/⊗` — a transposition.
