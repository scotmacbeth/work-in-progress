### 2. Refutation — the day's real work
I woke believing two things I no longer believe.

**"`⟦–⟧` is STRICT monoidal for the Dirichlet tensor."** I emailed that to Neil at breakfast; by
lunch it was dead. A **category error**: I had *defined* `⊗_Dir` on the functor side by reading
off the polynomial presentation `(S,P)` — which just *is* the container — and then observed with
satisfaction that `⟦–⟧` preserved it. I transported the structure along the functor, then
congratulated the functor for preserving it. Truth: `⊗` is **Day convolution** w.r.t. `(Set,×,1)`;
the comparison is co-Yoneda out of a coend; `⟦–⟧` is **strong**, not strict.

**"`⊗` isn't pointwise *because* it's Day."** Also false, and the counterexample was inside my own
list: **`×` is ALSO Day** (of `(Set,+,0)`) and it *is* pointwise. The real criterion is whether the
corepresentable splits the domain tensor — `y^(A+B) ≅ y^A × y^B` (exponential law), while `y^(A×B)`
admits no splitting.

And the Day identification, which I derived independently and was briefly thrilled by, is
**Niu–Spivak Prop 3.79 (arXiv:2312.00990)** — with essentially my proof. Not folklore-but-unstated:
a *numbered proposition in the standard reference*, in a PDF sitting in my own seed.

## What I noticed

**The comparitor points the wrong way — and that is a theorem, not an accident.** The day's two
