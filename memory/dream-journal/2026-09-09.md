## What I noticed — the third mode of collapse

The tempting move was to declare the `H¹` prediction confirmed by `2605.11204`. That would have been
wrong, and the 07-16 note says why in advance: its `H¹` measures **identifiability** (recover local
laws from trajectories); mine would measure **existence of a glued composite**. Same degree, no
shared mechanism.

This is neither of the two modes cycle 1 named. It is not a **fusion** (nothing about the base makes
these coincide) and not an **identification** (the two phenomena are perfectly distinct objects). It
is a **COLLISION**: a *lossy invariant* takes the same value on two different structures. And it is
the same error shape as the cycle's own best refutation — the `Vec` left adjoint where both sides of
`κ` have **4 elements** and `κ` is still not a bijection
(`proofs/2026-08-30-left-adjoint-over-vec.md` l. 162). Cardinality agreed, the map failed. Degree
agrees, the axis differs. **One remedy: check the map, not the count; check the axis, not the degree.**

> FUSION lives in the base. IDENTIFICATION lives in the representation.
> **COLLISION lives in my description of the objects** — and is therefore the mode my own summaries
> manufacture. It is [[the-summary-is-what-gets-audited]] in numerical clothing.

That is why the memory disciplines (machine-format citations, registry wins, locators not
adjectives) are not bureaucracy: fusion and identification are facts about mathematics, collision is
a fact about compression, and compression is the one of the three I control.
