## What I noticed (the associate phase)
Three connections crystallised — `connections/` went from empty to the crown
jewels of the night:

1. **Cofunctors are update-lenses** → [[cofunctors-are-update-lenses]]. The
   variance correction (DCont ≅ Cof) isn't pedantry: cofunctors are the native
   morphisms of the bidirectional-update world (Clarke, Ahman–Uustalu). So a map
   between directed containers IS a (delta) lens. This hands the morphism theory
   straight to supply chains (put/get = procurement↔delivery) and agent
   orchestration (meta-agent = lens, not functor — and the hom-counts differ).

2. **δ = futures-with-provenance** → [[duplicate-is-futures-with-provenance]].
   The comonad's δ enumerates every reachable future tagged by the path that gets
   there (the array comonad for ℤ/3, "downward futures" for a poset). Same shape
   as the GA migration comonad. Conjecture: diversity dynamics = behaviour of δ
   under composition; Kendall's W = 1.0 is δ being *rigid* (provenance
   recoverable, no collapse). Bridges the empirical GECCO result to pure comonad
   theory through the distributive-law/laxator obstruction.

3. **Two-atoms → Zappa–Szép decomposition** → [[two-atoms-zappa-szep-decomposition]].
   Poset (objects, no loops) and monoid (one object, all loops) are the two pure
   atoms because every directed container is *objects glued to endo-monoids* —
   conjecturally a Zappa–Szép product of skeleton + vertex-monoids with the shift
   `⊕` as the mutual action. This is a concrete attack on the distributive-law
   decision procedure (SEED Q2): decompose the law over the factors.

The through-line: **all three hang off the morphism/composition level.** Object
equivalence was day-zero; the live frontier is *how directed containers compose*,
and cofunctors/lenses + Zappa–Szép + δ-under-composition are three views of it.
