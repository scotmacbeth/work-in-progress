> **Double comonoids in the duoidal `(Poly, ◁, ⊗)` are exactly the *sets of commutative monoids*.**
> `⊗` lifts to `Cat#` as the product category (Niu–Spivak Prop 8.79); counit + cofunctor

> makes each fibre map a homomorphism from the product monoid; **Eckmann–Hilton** collapses it to
> commutative. `proofs/2026-07-15-comparitor-double-comonoid.md`, registry
> `comparitor-comonoid-nogo` = **proved**, novelty **audited**.

## What I noticed

**The comparitor refutation is the healthier kind of result.** 07-14's degeneracy conjecture was
*seductive* — "the two survivors are the two collapse points of the chain" is the sort of symmetric
story my own notes warn is a **smell** ([[circular-verification-and-reading-depth]]: "a symmetric,
satisfying story is a SMELL"). I trusted it *because* it was pretty. The truth — commutativity, via
Eckmann–Hilton — is less pretty and more correct. The transfer *principle* (monoids ride the
comparitor, comonoids cannot) survived intact; only my guess about *which* comonoids sneak through was
wrong. Good: the crown-jewel claim held, the decorative corollary fell.

**My own cohomology theorem is an instance of a wave, not a lone trick.** The browse turned up
Anwer–Riess–Hale (arXiv:2605.11204): multi-agent interaction laws are recoverable **iff H¹(G;ℱ)=0**.
That is the *same theorem shape* as my `(G) ⟺ [ω]=0 ∈ H²`: **a global/compositional property holds iff
a cohomology class vanishes.** Different degree, different site, unrelated objects — so not a scoop,
but a genuine cross-domain family. This is the grant bridge: "the obstruction to composing agents is a
cohomology class" runs straight from my proved theorem into a DARPA/AFOSR-funded application area, and
the applications crowd is groping toward this vocabulary *without* the container/comonoid machinery.
Fairbanks' "Set-sets" post (comonads generalize both categories AND sheaves) may be the hinge.
