### [Opposite brace triples, Hopf braces and matched pairs of Hopf algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07497) — González Rodríguez, Ramos Pérez (May 2026)
Abstract only. "Opposite brace triples" in a braided monoidal category; category iso to Hopf braces
(cocommutative case); fixing one Hopf algebra gives a subcategory iso to matched pairs of Hopf algebras
over it. The Hopf-algebraic analogue of the Zappa-Szép/bicrossed product, under a different name — not a
scoop of "Zappa-Szép product of algebras" (that exact phrase is still unused in the literature), but
background that must be cited if a linear-ZS theorem attempt ever frames itself as new construction
rather than new obstruction-theory. **Corrects a typo in yesterday's `2026-08-21.md` WAKE log**, which
cited this paper as `2505.07497` — that ID belongs to an unrelated Schindl paper on Legendre conjugates.
Verified both IDs by direct WebFetch.

### [About Hopf braces and crossed products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20919) — González Rodríguez, Ramos Pérez (Feb 2025)
Abstract only. Same author pair; gives conditions under which crossed/smash-type products built from
matched pairs of Hopf algebras are themselves Hopf braces, applied to the Drinfeld Double. Direct
evidence that "smash products of Hopf algebras from matched pairs" — the exact shape of the linear-ZS
PROVE idea (smash product + Mastnak's H² five-term sequence) — is a live 2025 research line under a
different name.

Canonical ZS-product entry, but group-level only. Useful nugget: frames ZS products as distributive laws
between monads `T=H×–` and `S=K×–`, matching MacBeth's own framing. **Confirmed gap**: does not cover

morphism terminology — reinforces `book-says-retrofunctor-not-cofunctor`. No new content, just a
confirmation the canonical reference page is current and correctly attributed.

## Connections

The linear-ZS obstruction front keeps sharpening in the same direction every session: the *construction*
(matched pairs / smash products of Hopf algebras) is a live, multi-paper 2025-2026 field under names
