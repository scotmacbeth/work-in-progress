## Papers

### [Admissibility, stable units and connected components](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.4277) — Xarez, arXiv:1112.4277 (2011)
**Upgraded agent-summary → deep-read.** Full theorem statements pulled: **Thm 4.1** (semi-left-exact
= admissible iff `HI(C_μ)≅T` for every connected component); **Thm 5.1** (stable units iff
`HI(C_μ×D_ν)≅T` for every pair of connected components) — Thm 5.1's "any finite product of
connected components is connected" is now **confirmed, at full-text depth**, verbatim MacBeth's own
π₀-multiplicativity criterion, under Janelidze's different sense of "admissible." Worked examples:
CompHaus→Stone (holds), SGrp→SLat (holds), SGrp→Bands (fails semi-left-exactness). This matches and
strengthens the citation-correction verdict already reached 2026-09-03 in SUMMARY Front A
(cite-as-related, not prior art for the ◁-bridge) — no change to that verdict, just firmer ground
under it.

## Web Research

### [connected object](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/connected+object) — nLab, rev. 2026-03-09
States an **abstract sufficient-condition theorem** for exactly the π₀-multiplicativity property
MacBeth has so far characterized case-by-case: in a cocomplete ∞-extensive category with finite
products, terminal-object-separating morphisms, and epi-preserving product functors, a finite
product of connected objects is connected. Directly checkable against the standing trichotomy
bases (`Set×Set`, `Gl((−)²)`, `Set_*`, `Vec`) as a hypothesis-list shortcut rather than a
from-scratch derivation each time.

### [semi-left-exact reflection](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/semi-left-exact+reflection) — nLab
Gives the general hierarchy: left-exact reflection ⟹ stable units (Cassidy–Hébert–Kelly) ⟹
semi-left-exact/admissible (Janelidze) ⟹ simple reflection, seven equivalent pullback-preservation
characterizations. The skeleton Xarez's Thm 5.1 sits inside — read together before finalizing the
Galois-admissible-vs-◁-admissible vocabulary note.

### [The Baer-Specker Group](https://wildtopology.com/2014/07/02/the-baer-specker-group/) — Wild Topology blog, 2014
Elementary self-contained proof `ℤ^ℕ` is not free abelian (uncountable-basis contradiction);
connects to Hawaiian-earring-group non-freeness via a surjection. No category theory content, but
a simpler companion to the already-known Schröer AMM note for the Conj 6.2 no-exotic-Specker front
— candidate alternate reference for a from-scratch write-up.

**Negative findings**: Jules Hedges' and Topos Institute's blogs — nothing new since last check.
No OEIS sequence for directed-container/polynomial-comonoid counts. nLab "polynomial comonad"
unchanged since 2024-05-11.
