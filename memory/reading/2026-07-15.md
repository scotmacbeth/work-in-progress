## Papers

### [Multi-Agent System Identification with Nonlinear Sheaf Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11204) — Anwer, Riess, Hale (Georgia Tech, DARPA/AFOSR), May 2026
Full HTML text read. Studies recovering local interaction laws ("edge potentials") in a
multi-agent system from trajectory data, dynamics governed by a nonlinear cellular sheaf
Laplacian on a directed communication graph. **Theorem 2**: recovery of edge potentials for all
conservative, edge-separable forces is possible **iff H¹(G;ℱ) = 0** (Hodge decomposition gives
ker δ* ≅ H¹(G;ℱ)). Zero category-theory apparatus, zero citations to Spivak/Ghani/Ahman-Uustalu —
purely spectral-graph/sheaf-Laplacian. Structural sibling to MacBeth's own (G)⟺[ω]=0∈H² result
(different degree — H¹ vs H² — and different site — a graph, not Sk_C — but the same
"iff-vanishing-cohomology = recoverable/composable" theorem shape). Candidate `connections/` note,
not a scoop (different objects: dynamical identifiability vs categorical factorization).

### [Sheaf-Theoretic Planning: A Categorical Foundation for Resilient Multi-Agent Autonomous Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01879) — Hernández, Sánchez-Soto, May 2026
Full HTML text read (upgraded from agent-summary/title-only on 07-14 to deep-read today).
Defines a "Site of Time" (poset of closed intervals + Grothendieck topology) and sheaves
ℱ_World/Mem/Goal; motivates plan-incoherence as sheaf-gluing failure. **No theorem is proved** for
the cohomology-as-obstruction claim — H¹/H² are asserted qualitatively to measure "topological
obstructions to a global plan," with formalization explicitly deferred to future work. Concrete
artifacts are a Prolog/Raspberry-Pi implementation, not category theory. Zero citations to
Spivak/Ghani/Ahman/Uustalu. Weakest member of the 2026 sheaf/multi-agent cluster — a marker of
appetite for the grant's compositional-obstruction narrative, not a competitor.

### [Sketchable Infinity Categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01497) — Casacuberta, Gutiérrez, Martínez-Carpena, Nov 2025
Abstract read in full plus targeted fetch of Examples 3.7–3.15 (Segal-space/internal-category
sections) — not a full cover-to-cover read. Generalizes Gabriel–Ulmer/Lair sketch theory to
∞-categories: presentable ∞-categories = models of limit sketches, accessible = models of
arbitrary sketches. Works out sketches for complete Segal spaces (Ex. 3.8), ∞-operads, spectra.
Checked directly against MacBeth's frontier target (Kun Chen arXiv:2601.22968 Conjecture 7.2,
poly-comonads/S ≅ CSS): **zero discussion of polynomial functors, polynomial comonads, directed
containers, or Kun Chen's conjecture anywhere in the text.** Topically adjacent (both use complete
Segal spaces as a worked ∞-categorical example) but not substantively connected. Not a scoop, not
a proof tool — filed as "adjacent tooling exists" for if a general sketch-theoretic construction is
ever needed on the ∞-Segal-space side of the equivalence chain.
