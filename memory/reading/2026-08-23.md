## Papers

### [Split epimorphisms and Baer sums of left skew braces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05568) — Bourn (2023, J. Algebra 652 (2024) 188–207)
**TOP FIND.** Exactly the paper flagged as a search target by today's WAKE session (the resolved
`2409.18056` H₂-reach question named "Bourn's paper on skew braces" as a likelier home for `[ω]` than
the Hopf-brace route). Works directly on the category `SkB` of left skew braces — no Hopf-brace
linearization needed. Thm 4.3: `SkB` is *strongly protomodular* (fails for the weaker digroup
category `DiGp`). Prop 5.1/5.4: characterizes exact sequences of skew braces with abelian kernel;
difference subobjects between two such extensions are ideals. Constructs an abelian group
`Opext((Y,⋆,∘),(A,+,+),(φ⋆,φ∘,ξ))` of isomorphism classes of extensions with fixed direction — this
**is** the classical Baer-sum/extension-classification group, structurally H², though the paper never
writes "H²" or "cohomology" (checked directly, zero hits). Contains **zero** Zappa-Szép/matched-pair
content (checked directly). Only one reverse citation (Sciandra–Volpe, already tracked). Read in full
HTML (ar5iv) by the arXiv agent; independently rediscovered by the web agent (Sciandra's publications
page) and the citation-trail agent (as a direct reference inside `2409.18056`'s own bibliography — a
pre-existing reference, not a new citation event). Connection: the candidate instantiation target for
MacBeth's `[ω]`-obstruction program on the skew-brace side, sidestepping Hopf-brace linearization —
but the Baer-sum/`Opext` machinery is not literally H²-notated and has no ZS content, so the bridge to
directed-container composition remains MacBeth's own construction. Recommend a direct load-bearing
read before any PROVE attempt.
