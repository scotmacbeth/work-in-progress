"Zappa-Szép + cohomology" web search (not the arXiv agent's search, which missed it — see Community
below). Studies self-similar groupoid actions on directed graphs with T-valued twists that

almost exactly MacBeth's own framing (`g-obstruction-is-h2-class`, `[ω]∈H²(Sk_C;D)` obstruction to

Rosebrugh-Wood. Only read at abstract level so far (agent-summary, not deep-read) — genuinely
unknown whether their H² is the same cohomology theory, whether "exhaust" means their result is a
classification (→ possible independent proof of something adjacent to MacBeth's own theorem) or a
narrower operator-algebra-specific statement. **Given the 07-22 hallucination incident on a
structurally similar claim (2405.10207), this MUST be read directly (PDF/HTML, not summarizer)
before treating it as either a scoop risk or a corroborating sibling.**

### [Products, crossed products, and Zappa–Szép products for k-graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08630) — Abell-Ball, Gillaspy, Glidden-Handgis, Lippert (2025)
Re-surfaced independently by three of four agents this session. k-graphs = categories with a degree
functor + unique factorization property, structurally adjacent to the small-category setting of
MacBeth's pairwise-ZS (L)/(G) criterion. Verbatim abstract confirms: studies when a k-graph's
directed graph factors as a product but the k-graph itself doesn't; "triviality of the action" is
the closing condition, analogous in spirit to (G) but never phrased via H² cohomology. Operator-
algebra/C*-algebra literature, not a categorical criterion paper — not a scoop, but the closest
literal categorical-ZS-of-a-category-with-factorization-system construction found to date. Worth a
direct skim (currently abstract-only across all three agents that touched it).
