## Papers

### [Compositional Program Verification with Polynomial Functors in Dependent Type Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01303) — Aberlé (1 Apr 2026)
Read properly this time (prior read was agent-summary level 2026-06-11). Uses the free monad on a
polynomial functor as an off-the-shelf ingredient (`Free p C = return | bind : p.fst → (p.snd x →
Free p C) → Free p C` in Agda) inside a wiring-diagram verification framework with dependent
polynomials as pre/post-condition specs and Mealy-machine operational semantics. **Not a scoop**:
it doesn't formalise or prove the Gambino–Kock construction itself — no positions=P-trees/
directions=leaves statement, no grafting-associativity/monoid-law proof, just an ordinary
programming-language return/bind AST that happens to be extensionally the same tree structure.
Cites Niu–Spivak 2024 and Libkind–Spivak 2025, not Gambino–Kock by name. Worth citing as evidence
the free-monad-on-Poly idea has applied uptake, if/when the Lean formalisation writes up.

### [Formalising Inductive and Coinductive Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02603) — Damato, Altenkirch, Ljungström (v5 Jul 2025, no 2026 update)
Reconfirmed, read more closely this session. Formalises "containers preserve least/greatest fixed
points" in Cubical Agda **without UIP**, generalising container functors from `Set` to a
"wild category of types." Uses path types to recover bisimulation↔coinductive-equality for the
terminal-coalgebra half. Not a scoop of the free-monad/grafting target (this is about fixed-point
*preservation*, not the explicit tree/grafting monad structure) but flagged as adjacent foundation-
questioning territory: a UIP-free redo of the free-monad construction, if it ever appears, would be
worth watching for.

### [Homology and twisted C*-algebras for self-similar actions and Zappa-Szép products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09600) — Mundey, Sims (v2 Aug 2024, confirmed no 2025/2026 revision)
Reconfirmed still current: categorical homology of ZS products via a spectral sequence on a double
complex; still the closest existing "categorical homology of ZS" result; still not extended to the
Baues–Wirsching/Rosebrugh–Wood H² obstruction framing this project uses. No action needed.

### `sinhp/Poly` (Sina Hazratpour, Lean4, GitHub, not arXiv)

### [Distributive Laws](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2017/02/distributive_laws.html) — n-Category Café, Feb 2017
Liang Ze Wong on Beck distributive laws, explicitly naming **Zappa-Szép products as the general case
of a distributive law between two group monads** (direct/semidirect products are degenerate special
cases). David Jaz Myers' comment: semidirect products arise when only one factor acts — a clean
one-line example. Worth citing as the folklore source connecting ZS-as-distributive-law to the group
case.

### [Operads and Trees](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2014/03/operads_and_trees.html) — n-Category Café, Mar 2014
Joachim Kock's comment is the payload: **grafting = pushout in the category of trees**; the
Moerdijk–Weiss category Ω is generated from base trees via the free-monad construction on polynomial
endofunctors — exactly analogous to categories generated from graphs by the free-category monad.
Cisinski points to Weber's "Familial 2-functors and parametric right adjoints" (TAC 18, 2007) as the
underlying machinery. **Second independent confirmation (beyond Gambino–Kock 2009) that the
free-monad/grafting construction is old, well-trodden territory** — reinforces the "only Lean +
grafting-law write-up is defensible" verdict already banked.

### [HoTT Electronic Seminar: Garner, "Polynomial comonads and comodules"](https://groups.google.com/g/hott-electronic-seminar-talks/c/E74x-NGhImM) — Google Groups, Dec 2019
Announcement (Chris Kapulkin) for Richard Garner's talk. Abstract quoted directly: **"comonoids in
Poly(E) ... are the same as internal categories in E"** — stated for general `E`, asking what the
bicategory of comonoids-and-bicomodules in Poly(E) is ("slightly surprising"). ★ **This is a primary
source for the general comonoid=internal-category theorem, dated 2019 — four years before the
Shapiro–Spivak arXiv:2305.00167 citation currently used for the fibrational version (§10/M3).** No
paper/slides link was in the announcement itself. → flagged for a direct follow-up: find Garner's
actual paper or slides and check whether Thm 5.6 of Shapiro–Spivak should cite Garner 2019 as prior,
or whether they're independent/Garner's result is Set-level only.

### [Profunctor Optics: The Categorical View](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2020/01/profunctor_optics_the_categori.html) — n-Category Café, Jan 2020
Pillmore–Román categorize the optics zoo: lenses (cartesian-profunctor ends), **prisms as "lenses in
the opposite category"** — a different dual notion from cofunctors (op-category dual vs. Put-
direction-reversal dual). Worth a footnote distinguishing the two "duals of lens" so as not to
conflate prisms with cofunctors in future write-ups.

### [Schur Functors and Categorified Plethysm](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2021/06/schur_functors_and_categorifie.html) — n-Category Café, Jun 2021

### [Every Functor is a (Co)Reflection](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2012/09/every_functor_is_a_coreflectio.html) — n-Category Café, Sep 2012

### ★ Question 3 RESOLVED — Charles Walker, "Locally Subcartesian Closed Categories" FOUND
**arXiv:2607.10242** (July 2026, 39pp, math.CT). Defines locally subcartesian closed categories:
categories with pullbacks plus a coherent choice of subobjects of pullbacks, such that the resulting
"affine" base-change functors have right adjoints — every slice becomes monoidal closed with jointly
monic projections, a genuinely **weaker "affine"/bunched substitute** for full local cartesian
closure. Builds a bicategory of polynomials equivalent to a 2-category of **subcartesian polynomial
functors**; worked examples: Lawvere quantale, nominal sets; suggested application: extensional
dependent affine type theory with bunched contexts. **This is unusually close to MacBeth's own
objects** — a candidate framework for asking whether `Cont`/`DCont`, known to be CCC but provably
NOT LCC (Altenkirch–Levy–Staton), might carry this strictly weaker "locally subcartesian closed"
structure instead. Read at abstract level only (WebFetch on the /abs/ page) — **a full read is owed
before the next Cont-closure PROVE session**, since it could either extend or sharply bound the
`closed-day-structures` programme.
