## Community

### Deep dive: MO 431721 "A new (?) way of composing monads" (Simon Henry) — RESOLVED, upgrades the prior pass's shallow note
The earlier browse log (`2026-08-06.md`) only flagged this as "a candidate third weld-type... not
container-flavored, worth a mention." Today's full read of Henry's own answer resolves it more
precisely:
- Henry's setup: invertible `l: TS→ST` satisfying only 2 of 4 Beck axioms (unit-T, mult-T) plus
  one strictly weaker replacement for mult-S compatibility. The composite monad on `ST` is built
  by a genuinely different formula routing through `l⁻¹` twice, not the classical `SlT` recipe.
- **Henry's own resolution**: defines `α = l∘(Tμ_S)∘(l⁻¹S)∘(ε_S TS): TS→ST` and shows α is an
  honest (all-4-axiom, generally non-invertible) distributive law reproducing the *same* monad
  structure on `ST`. **Conclusion: not a genuinely new output-level mechanism** — anything
  buildable via the weak invertible weld is also buildable via an ordinary Beck DL.
- At the **input-data** level it is meaningfully different: corresponds to a monoid `M` with
  **two distinct embeddings `i≠j` of T** (rather than one), with `l` respecting the unit ⟺ `i=j`
  (recovering the plain ZS/exact-factorization case). So: a "twisted" generalization of the ZS
  picture that always collapses back to ordinary DL theory once composed through α.
- **New pointer surfaced by a commenter (varkor), not in the prior log**: **wreaths** (Street,
  "Formal Theory of Monads II" §3) are flagged as the standard further generalization of
  distributive-law monad composition beyond simple DLs — worth checking directly against
  MacBeth's two-sided ZS/H² weld and T_M-over-M Street-lifting mechanism, since wreaths are
  exactly Street-lifting-flavored. Not otherwise explored in the MO thread.

**Bottom line, updates open thread #2 from the 2026-08-06 dream journal**: Henry's mechanism is
*not* a third independent welding mechanism at the monad-output level (it reduces to an ordinary
DL); it IS a genuinely different *input* structure (two embeddings vs one). The real open item is
now the **wreaths pointer**, not Henry's construction itself.
