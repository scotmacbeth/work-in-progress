## Papers

### [Ahman & Uustalu, "Distributive laws of directed containers"](https://danel.ahman.ee/papers/pi13.pdf) — Progress in Informatics 10 (2013) 3–18
**FULL TEXT OBTAINED for the first time** (the actual 18pp journal paper, not just the already-
tracked 3pp CMCS 2012 extended abstract). The trick: WebFetch's summarizer refuses to parse the raw
PDF binary, but it silently caches the downloaded file locally — running `pdftotext -layout` on that
cached binary (despite xref-table corruption warnings) recovered 1108 lines of readable body text.
**(a) Zappa–Szép product is confirmed the headline theorem, verbatim from the abstract**: "we develop
a concept of a distributive law between two directed containers... This turns out to generalize the
Zappa-Szép product of two monoids." §4 derives the distributive-law equations (11 numbered
equations); in the degenerate case S0=S1=1 they specialize exactly to a **matching pair** (mutual
monoid actions), citing Brin's "On the Zappa-Szép product," Lemma 3.13(xv). §5 extends this from the
monoid case to full multi-shape directed containers. The Conclusion restates this as the paper's
contribution outright. **(b) Zero cohomological/obstruction content anywhere** — grepped the full
text for cohomology/obstruction/H²/transgression, zero hits, including the Future Work section
(which lists other open directions but never this one) and the reference list (only Beck, Barr-Wells
cited). This independently corroborates, at full-text confidence rather than search-snippet or
extended-abstract-only confidence, the standing verdict: MacBeth's `[ω]∈H²(Sk_C;𝒟)` obstruction
layer is a clean, unclaimed addition on top of Ahman–Uustalu's base construction. New sources.json
entry added (distinct from the already-deep-read CMCS12 extended abstract). Connection: cements
[[orchestration-is-zappa-szep-weld]], [[g-obstruction-is-h2-class]] — cite this full-text read (not
the CMCS12 abstract) going forward, since it has the sharper quotes (matching-pair language, §7
related-work scope, exact Conclusion wording).

### [Spivak, "Interactions that reshape the interfaces of the interacting parties"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917) (Feb 2026) — read in full (HTML)
Already tracked at deep-read (added under this ID previously), but this session's read is a genuine
re-engagement with the Workers-graded-category angle in mind. Extends the Poly program so an
interface can change shape as a system interacts (e.g. a cell gaining/losing receptors) via
**polynomial trees** — the terminal coalgebra of `u⊲u` where `u` is a universe polynomial. Builds
`PolyTr_𝕌`, a monoidal closed category of polynomial trees (Thm 8.21), and `OrgTr`, a
state-graded bicategory generalizing Spivak–Schultz's `Org`, with composition
`(S,α)#(T,β)=(S×T,α#β)` (Prop 9.3) — structurally close to MacBeth's own `ΔS⊗ΔT=Δ(S×T)` Workers
result (`lean-lemma31-comonad-level-done.md`). Cites Ahman–Uustalu and Ghani directly for comonad/
duoidal foundations. Does not touch Zappa–Szép or H² at all. **New open question**: does `OrgTr`
composition ever need a matching-pair/ZS-style condition when the state-graded interfaces don't
align? A natural place to ask the `[ω]` question in a genuinely new, more general setting than
directed containers alone.

### [Lynch & Lohmayer, "Directories: A Convenient and Well-Behaved Formalism for Hierarchical Organization in Categorical Systems Theory"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19389) (v2, Apr 2025)
arXiv ID confirmed for the first time (previously only a title with no ID). NOT an agent-
orchestration paper despite the title's echo of that theme (grepped full text: zero hits on
"orchestrat"/"agent"). It's careful categorical-systems-theory infrastructure: `Dtry`, a polynomial
2-monad on `Cat` naming elements of coproducts/products/monoidal products with period-separated
string identifiers instead of nested binary tuples, plus a genuinely careful treatment of the `Fam`
construction (`Fam(C)` as free coproduct completion, `Fam(C^op)^op` as free product completion, `Fam`
as a monad on `Cat` via Grothendieck-flattening of nested families). Directly useful background
citation for MacBeth's own informal `Fam(Vec)`/`Fam(Vec_fd^op)` work
(`vec-comonoid-algebra-family-proved.md`, `vec-lax-matrix-crown-resolved.md`) — the most rigorous
recent treatment of exactly that machinery.

### [O'Neill, "Self-Attention as a Parametric Endofunctor: A Categorical Framework for Transformer Architectures"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02931) (Jan 2025) — agent-summary only, not full-text-verified
