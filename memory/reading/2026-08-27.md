## Papers

### [Locally Subcartesian Closed Categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10242) — Charles Walker, arXiv:2607.10242, 2026-07-11
**Genuine depth upgrade, not a new find** — this paper was already tracked (abstract-only, since 2026-07-16). Today's arXiv agent read the full HTML text and the citation-trail agent independently flagged it as the top reverse-citation hit for Gambino–Kock 0906.4931 — two-agent convergence on the same paper via different routes is itself a signal. New detail from the full read: LSCCs equip a category with pullbacks with a coherent choice of *subpullbacks* (spans where the comparison map to the true pullback is unique-if-exists, not required to exist), giving affine base-change functors ∇_f whose right adjoints ⊠_f exist even when the full Σ⊣Δ⊣Π chain fails. Each slice's subcartesian tensor `g ⊗_Y f ≅ Σ_f∇_f(g)` is right-closed but not cartesian-closed. Motivating examples: the Lawvere quantale with tensor `A+B−X` (explicitly NOT cartesian closed — an additive, non-cartesian tensor), and the Schanuel topos of nominal sets. §5 builds a bicategory of "subcartesian polynomials," biequivalent (Thm 5.2.8) to polynomial functors with "bunched strength." Ancestry is Street's protocalibrations / Weber's distributivity pullbacks — zero engagement with Gambino–Kock/Gambino–Hyland, an independent lineage arriving at an adjacent question.
**Connection to seed:** Direct hit on **Front D**. The Lawvere-quantale tensor `A+B−X` is structurally the same shape of "additive, non-cartesian slice tensor" as Vec's `⊗/⊕`. Open question this suggests: does Fam(Vec^op) (or Vec itself) admit a subpullback structure making it *locally subcartesian closed* despite provably failing full LCC (Gambino–Kock: Vec not LCCC) — which would let a Σ-∇-⊠ polynomial semantics exist over Vec after all, sidestepping rather than confirming the extensivity/LCCC obstruction. Also worth checking Thm 5.2.8's bicategory of polynomials against MacBeth's own Fam(C^op) ⊗/◁ constructions (Street-style spans vs. family-style external ∐) for overlap or genuine disjointness. Candidate next `/expository` or `/prove` target for Front D.
sources.json: upgraded `abstract` → `agent-summary` with full note.
