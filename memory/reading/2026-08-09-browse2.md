## Papers

### [AutoBayes: A Compositional Framework for Generalized Variational Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18608) — Smithe, Perin
Actually deep-read this time (HTML full text, not abstract-only) — the paper flagged unread across
three prior sessions (08-08, 08-09 browse1 ×2). Objects = measurable spaces; 1-cells = "open models"
`p : X →∘ Y`, pairs of a latent space and a kernel; sequential/parallel composition give a
**bicategory of open models** that is **self-dual compact closed** (cups/caps, string-diagram
native). Headline result, the **Bayesian chain rule**: `(d∘c)†_π = c†_π ∘ d†_{c_*π}` — inversion of
a composite is the opposite-order composite of inversions, mirroring reverse-mode AD's chain rule.
**Bayesian lenses** (Def 3.1) pair a forward open model with a state-dependent backward inversion
map — structurally a lens in the Cont(q)/Dioptrics sense. Section 4 shows ELBO-style variational
losses decompose the same way; Section 5 separates model syntax from inference semantics
("functorial semantics"). No polynomial-functor/container vocabulary anywhere (checked directly).
**New idea flagged, not yet checked:** the chain rule reads as an associativity-type law for how
backward/inversion structure composes through forward composition — the same shape as MacBeth's
G_M/T_M entwining-via-κ pattern (`effect-coeffect-arrows-reverse-entwining.md`), with `†_π` playing
κ's role. Worth a dedicated `/expository` pass to check whether Bayesian-lens composition is a
genuine instance of the entwining/branching-obstruction machinery, which would be a new grant bullet
(compositional Bayesian inference as a directed-container/entwining instance), not just a citation.
