## Papers

### ["Fibrations of algebras"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16581) — Ahman, Reimaa, Coraglia, Castelnovo, Loregian, Martins-Ferreira (2024, math.CT)
**NEW find**, not previously indexed. Read via full-text HTML (targeted extraction of abstract +
definitions/theorems, not cover-to-cover). Bundles F-algebra categories over a parameter category
𝒜 into one fibration, which the authors *themselves* call a **"categorified semidirect product"**
𝒜⋉^F𝒳 (monadic case 𝒜⋉^T𝒳 via a parametrized monad T:𝒜→Mnd(𝒳) and Eilenberg–Moore algebras).
Main results: 𝒜⋉^T𝒳 is monadic over 𝒜×𝒳 (Prop 4.1); a bifibration criterion (Thm 4.4); a converse
(Thm 5.13) — under "pruned" hypotheses every fibration arises this way from a canonical parametrized
monad. **Double relevance**: (1) a *one-sided* structural cousin of the Zappa-Szép/orchestration
work (no H² content, no two-sided weld — cite as neighbour, not scoop); (2) directly in the
vocabulary of today's fibrational framing (`T_M` as a monad opfunctor lying over base monad `M`) —
general Σ/Π-reindexing toolkit worth citing alongside Jacobs/Hermida/Katsumata. **Author overlap
worth noting**: Danel Ahman (co-author of the foundational Ahman–Uustalu directed-containers papers)
and Fosco Loregian (profunctor/formal CT — relevant to the `Arr_M`-is-a-profunctor thread). No
mention of cartesian-morphism preservation specifically, nor of polynomial functors/containers/
graded monads — checked explicitly. Does not touch the crown TFAE result itself, just its general
setting.

### ["Examples of non-polynomial comonads on Set?"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/457580) — MathOverflow, 2023, 3 answers, score 15
Cites the AU polynomial-comonad = category correspondence directly, then gives negative-space
examples: comonads from surjective geometric morphisms (topos/localic-monoid-action comonads);
pullback-preserving comonads on Set ≅ Grothendieck toposes with enough points; and an explicit
non-pullback-preserving counterexample (Multigraph-comonadic-over-Set, formula
`X ↦ (X³+X²)/2 + X`). Useful citable negative-space characterization of "polynomial" for book Ch3.

### ["About Coreader Comonads"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/478413) — MathOverflow, 2024, 1 answer
