## Papers

### [Comonads as Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15091) — Fairbanks, Carlson, Spivak (submitted 2026-07-16, one day old)
Characterizes topological spaces as density comonads of diagrams of subsets ("subbases"/"halos"),
explicitly motivated by "Ahman and Uustalu characterized categories as precisely the polynomial
comonads on Set." Shows every comonad on Set has both an underlying space (reflection) and an
underlying small category (coreflection). States that continuous maps + comonad morphisms form a
double category which, restricted to polynomial comonads, "recovers **the double category of
functors and retrofunctors of Clarke and Di Meglio**" — the exact formalism behind MacBeth's DCont≅Cof
result. Surfaced independently by three of four agents this session (arXiv, community, citation-trail)
— strong convergent signal. Second paper in Spivak's density-comonad programme (after 2503.21974,
"Categories by Kan Extension"), confirming it is active and expanding right now. Not a scoop of any
proved theorem, but a MEDIUM watch-risk: full read owed before any future density-comonad or
comonad-as-topological-space claim. → sources.json new entry.

### [What kind of linearly distributive category do polynomial functors form?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01849) — Spivak, Srinivasan (2024) — re-read pp.1-5
Re-read as the closest-adjacent candidate for the Dialectica sweep. Proves (Poly,y,⊗,◁) is a normal
duoidal category, all normal duoidal categories are isomix LDCs (Lemma 3.3), introduces biclosed LDCs
with a ◁-co-closure matching MacBeth's own `closed-structures-are-spivaks` note. **No mention of
"Dialectica," "Chu," or "de Paiva" anywhere** — squarely about the existing ⊗/◁ pair, not a third
tensor. Does NOT scoop ⋉/⋊. Its unread §6 (linear monoids/comonoids/bialgebras in Poly, starting p.26)
is flagged for a compare against [[comparitor-points-the-wrong-way]] before that chapter closes.

### [Dialectica categories and polynomial functors (Part 1)](https://topos.institute/blog/2022-07-12-dial-and-poly-1/) — Nelson Niu, Topos Institute blog, 2022-07-12
**The key negative result of this session.** Unifies Dialectica categories and Poly under one
framework ΣΠ𝐂 (Poly = ΣΠ𝟙; de Paiva's dialectica categories = ΣΠ(walking-arrow)) — but purely at the
object/category level, no tensor product. Ends with an explicit promise of a "Part 2": *"we'll see how
each monoidal structure on 𝐂 induces several interesting monoidal structures on ΣΠ𝐂"* — precisely the
⋉/⋊ question. **That Part 2 was never published** (confirmed independently by two agents: direct
fetch of the natural next-URL 404'd, the blog listing shows no follow-up, and a targeted search for a
2023-2025 sequel found nothing). The material appears to have matured instead into Dorta-Jarvis-Niu
arXiv:2305.05655 — already MacBeth's known closest neighbour — whose §6 leaves the ⋉/⋊ tensors
uninterpreted. This is the cleanest possible outcome for a novelty sweep: the one team best-positioned
to have found this (Topos Institute, presumably Spivak/Niu) explicitly set up to write it in 2022,
and didn't.

### [All Concepts are Cat#](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02571) — Lynch, Shapiro, Spivak (2023) — read at abstract level (bumped from unread)
Studies the double category Cat# of comonoids in Poly under Dirichlet/composition structure; dual
equivalences to dynamic rewiring systems and "generalized polynomial categories." Confirms it is
exactly the Shapiro-Spivak home of cofunctor morphisms between comonoids-in-Poly, i.e. background for
DCont≅Cof, not competing. Worth a citation check in the PR #1 paper.
