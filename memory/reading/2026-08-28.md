## Papers

### [Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17191) — Purdy, Damato, CALCO 2025
Already deep-read since 2026-06 (at least 8th rediscovery this session — see Housekeeping below, no new content). For the record: monadic-side mirror of Ahman–Uustalu's directed-container distributive laws; Example 22/Section 6 shows mixed distributive laws (monadic-over-directed) correspond to "functional monoid actions" not classical matching pairs, and re-derives Zappa–Szép products from the monadic side.

### [Polynomials in categories with pullbacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1983) — Mark Weber, TAC 30:533–598 (2015)
**Top-priority unread reference, now read in full (arXiv agent, HTML v4).** Generalises Gambino–Kock from LCCC down to *mere pullbacks*, requiring only that the middle leg of a polynomial be exponentiable. Central device: **distributivity pullbacks** — a pullback around (f,g) is terminal-with-property iff a canonical comparison δ is an isomorphism; replaces GK's type-theoretic proofs with pure category theory. Thm 1/2: polynomials in E form a bicategory Poly_E, and the polynomial-functor assignment is a homomorphism Poly_E → CAT. §4 extends to 2-categories (Street's fibration 2-monads are polynomial); §5 lightens the polynomial-monad axioms via Johnstone's bagdomain data. **Directly on-target for Front D**: this is the master reference for "what survives when LCCC drops to pullbacks-only" — exactly the question behind the Vec-non-LCCC obstruction (Walker LSCC verdict already banked). Weber's distributivity pullbacks are the likely right language for the δ/closedness obstructions MacBeth keeps hitting over Vec. Extraction upgraded agent-summary → deep-read in sources.json.

### [Terms and Derivatives of Polynomial Functors via Negation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.13088) — Charles Walker, 2022 (new to sources.json)
Same author as the already-tracked LSCC paper (2607.10242), using the *same* distributivity-pullback machinery as Weber above. Builds a negation operator ¬p (an "image complement" via distributivity pullback) to define homogeneous/monomial terms and, at I=J=1, recovers **McBride's classical container derivative** as ¬δ_p acting on E⊠_B E. Gives an orthogonal factorisation system (dense-then-closed) on LCCC-with-strict-initial-objects, and shows localising by dense monos preserves local cartesian closure — so derivatives are universally available in that setting. **Directly relevant to the open ∂p↔Weihrauch-degree question** (`weihrauch-containers-frontier.md`) — worth reading Pradic–Price 2501.17250 against this negation-operator machinery next.

### [On polynomial functors and polynomial comonads over infinity groupoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22968) — Kun Chen

Worth comparing technique, not base.
