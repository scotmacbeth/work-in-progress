## Web Research

### [Distributive laws confirmed as the categorical home of Zappa–Szép, two independent citations](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2017/02/distributive_laws.html)
n-Category Café guest post (Liang Ze Wong) on Beck's distributive laws; a comment by David Jaz Myers
states explicitly that Zappa-Szép products of groups are precisely distributive laws between the monads
`G×−` and `K×−` (semidirect/direct products as special cases). The nLab [bicrossed product](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/bicrossed+product)
page gives the same statement categorically. Good, independent, textbook-adjacent citations for the
book's ZS chapter (already known in substance, but concrete new citation pins for §sec:sigma-is-comp /
the ZS-weld material).

### [double coset](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/double+coset) — nLab

pullbacks." A clean categorical/fibration-language framing of the double-coset formula behind the D₈
Ext-tower work — useful crosslink to the fibration language Neil prefers (per memory:
`neil-prefers-fibration-language-not-proof-relevance`).
