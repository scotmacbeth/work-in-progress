## Papers
### [Towards a Higher-Order Bialgebraic Denotational Semantics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18295) — Goncharov, Peressotti, Tsampas, Urbat, Volpe (2026)
Abstract-only (HTML 404s). Extends Turi-Plotkin bialgebraic abstract GSOS to higher-order languages
via a locally-final coalgebra for a behaviour bifunctor, decoupled from syntax. **Re-cleared at
abstract level**: no comonads, coeffects, distributive laws, Freyd/Hughes arrows, or branching-arity
content. Does not scoop the closed non-branching classification. Weaker than a true close-read since
full text is unreachable — stand down unless HTML/PDF becomes available.

### [A topos for extended Weihrauch degrees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08697) — Maschio, Trotta
**Genuinely new addition** (see Corrections below — initially misidentified by a subagent as already
tracked). Builds a topos for extended Weihrauch degrees via Hyland-Johnstone-Pitts tripos-to-topos;
states extended Weihrauch degrees over a filtered PCA are isomorphic to the posetal reflection of the
category of containers over partitioned assemblies pAsm(K2rec,K2). Direct continuation of the
Pradic-Price Weihrauch-containers frontier. Composition is only a quasi-bifunctor because the base is
merely weakly LCC — a concrete illustration of where MacBeth's own LCC hypothesis is load-bearing.
**Its "derivative ↔ Weihrauch-degree" row is explicitly left open** — third independent confirmation
(after Pradic-Price 2501.17250 and 2601.15420) that the ∂p↔Weihrauch-degree connection is genuinely
open literature-wide, not just internally.

### [Monads and Distributive Laws in Substructural Contexts (Extended Version)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13533) — Fujii, Tsai, Montacute, Hasuo, LICS 2026
Upgraded from "abstract" to a substantive partial read (HTML: definitions, Theorem 5.2, worked
examples). Builds monad-monad distributive laws δ:ST→TS on Set via Tronin's verbal categories;
Theorem 5.2's compatibility condition has an exceptional clause — "unless S is so lean that it is
generated by operations of arity ≤1" — a structural echo of the non-branching/arity-≤1 criterion from
the effect-coeffect arrow-category result, in a completely different (monad-monad, verbal-category)
setting. Confirmed NOT a scoop (no branching/leaf-symmetry/Freyd/coeffect content) — a citable
parallel-discovery neighbour for a future write-up of the branching criterion.

### ["Examples of non-polynomial comonads on Set?"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/457580) (MathOverflow, Oct 2023) — answered
Sharpest answer: pullback-preserving comonad on Set ≅ Grothendieck topos with enough points;
non-polynomial pullback-preserving comonads ↔ non-presheaf toposes. Clean citable boundary statement
for the book's Ch3 "which functors are containers" material: polynomial comonads = the presheaf-topos
case.

### ["About Coreader Comonads"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/478413) (MathOverflow, Sept 2024) — answered

right." Also confirms Karamlou-Shah's no-go paper circulates in community discussion (already cleared
as orthogonal, `ks-nogo-not-h2.md`).
