### [Compositional Active Inference I: Bayesian Lenses. Statistical Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04461) — Smithe (arXiv:2109.04461)
Deep-read in full (pdftotext full-text grep, not WebFetch summary). Pure Grothendieck-lens / copy-delete
category (Markov category) machinery: Bayesian lens = channel `c : X↦Y` + state-dependent Bayesian
inversion `c†_π : Y↦X`, formalized via Spivak's Grothendieck-lens categories `GrLens_F`. Statistical
games, MLE, variational Bayes as worked examples. **Correction to a prior note**: "cilia" does NOT appear
in Part I at all (zero grep hits) — that concept lives entirely in Part II. Full-text grep also confirms
zero mentions of "polynomial", "container", "Ahman", "Uustalu", "Abbott" in the body. Verdict: Part I
alone is not a close formal peer to directed containers.

### [Compositional Active Inference II: Polynomial Dynamics, Approximate Inference Doctrines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.12173) — Smithe (arXiv:2208.12173)
Deep-read in full — this is the real hit. Defines **cilia**: coalgebras/dynamical systems living over an
external-hom polynomial `⟦A^S,B^T⟧` built from a category of Bayesian lenses, "controlling optics" in
predictive-coding style. Composition lives in monoidal bicategories of hierarchical inference systems
(0-cells = polynomial interfaces, 1-cells = cilia). **Directly cites Ahman–Uustalu, "Directed Containers
as Categories" (EPTCS 207, 2016)** and uses, as a black-box fact, exactly MacBeth's core theorem:
◁-comonoids ↔ categories, comonoid homs ↔ cofunctors, `y^T≅BT` (delooping), `S·y^S` = codiscrete comonad
(= MacBeth's `ΔS`). Independent external corroboration that Ahman–Uustalu is the right foundational
citation — genuine peer validation from outside Kodamai. **No Zappa-Szép/bicrossed/semidirect-product
language anywhere in CAI I or II** (grepped both) — Smithe's composition mechanism (lens-composition-
controlled-by-coalgebras inside a bicategory) is structurally different from MacBeth's ZS weld `C⋈D`.
Cite in the grant's Applications section as evidence the Poly/directed-container toolkit is independently
recognized for compositional agent theory; contrast, don't conflate, with the ZS orchestration mechanism.
Not read: CAI II §4.3/4.4 (Laplace/Hebb-Laplace doctrines), and whether a promised CAI III (action/control)
exists — flagged for a future session if a fuller comparison is wanted.

### ["Snoc Trees: Growing Trees From the Bottom Up"](https://actconf2026.github.io/papers/ACT_2026_paper_13.pdf) — Ghani, Nordvall Forsberg, Fish (ACT 2026)
Found and read in full — **standing debt closed after 3 sessions flagged unread.** (Neil's own paper;
public PDF, no need to chase Neil/Robin for a copy.) A snoc-tree inverts ordinary tree construction
(`F* = μK.Id+F∘K`) to build bottom-up (`F_* = μK.Id+K∘F`), a "rank-2" initial algebra one level up in the
functor category. **Thm 3.5**: `F_*` is the **free ℕ-graded monad on F** — direct hit on MacBeth's own
graded-monad/Workers line ((Set,×)-graded categories, index-monoids). **Thm 4.3**: explicit container
shape/position equations for snoc-trees, building on Gambino–Kock and Abbott–Altenkirch–Ghani. **Thm 4.4**:
finitary containers admit a retraction between ordinary trees and height-graded snoc-trees. Motivating
application (§5, Agda): compositional probability sampling over inductive types — branching types need
the snoc-tree accumulator because subtree-size distributions don't factor independently across branches.
**Notable gap**: the paper never cites or engages with directed containers/Ahman-Chapman-Uustalu — open
angle for MacBeth: does the snoc-tree growth-point structure carry comonadic structure analogous to
directed containers?

work extending the exact theorem MacBeth's book classification chapter leans on. Candidate neighbour-
citation; check whether Garner's "Ionads" paper needs its own tracked entry first.
