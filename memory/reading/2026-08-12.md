## Papers

### [Skew brace extensions, second cohomology and complements](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.12371) — Rathee, Yadav (2026)
Genuinely new. Skew braces `(A,+),(A,∘)` with λ-action give a semidirect product
`Λ_A=(A,+)⋊_λ(A,∘)`. Main results: a homomorphism `Ψ: H²_Sb(H,I) → H²_Gp(Λ_H,I×I)` (Thm 1.1);
a splitting criterion (Thm 3.5, under a socle condition, skew-brace extension splits iff the
associated group extension splits); Schur–Zassenhaus-type complement theorems for coprime order
(Thm 4.3); trivial `H²` ⟹ complement exists (Cor 4.4). Same shape as MacBeth's planned
`[ω]∈H²` splitting obstruction for the ZS composition of two update monads — "H² obstructs
splitting/complementation" — but in group/skew-brace land, not monoid actions on Cat. Does NOT
discuss Zappa-Szép products itself (checked). Candidate citation + technique comparison for the
in-progress PROVE session (the Ψ-homomorphism-into-an-ordinary-H² move is worth comparing against
whatever construction the ZS-composition proof ends up using).

### [Classification of fiber sequences with a prescribed holonomy action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.01477) — Mario Fuentes (2021)
New to the index, abstract-depth only. Defines ℋ-fibration sequences (holonomy of `π₁(base)` on
fiber constrained to a subgroup `ℋ≤ℰ(F)`), classified via one universal ℋ-fibration sequence.
Topology-side instance of "fix the holonomy subgroup, get a classifying object" — structurally
close to the degree-1 update-monad theorem (liftings ≅ `Fun(𝔸(↓),Cat)`, classification by
holonomy of the position-threading action), though not the "trivial holonomy ⟹ collapse" pattern
specifically. Flag for a fuller read before citing.

### [Toposes of Monoid Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10198) — Morgan Rogers, PhD thesis (2021)
New to the index, low priority, abstract only. Classifies which presheaf toposes arise from
monoid actions on sets. Background on the classification of monoid-action categories (relevant
to `𝔸(↓)` in the abstract) but no holonomy/update-monad/ZS content.

### Noted, not added as a real find: "Gauge-invariant representation holonomy" (arXiv:2601.21653, ML)

### [Formal Theory of Monads (Following Street)](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2014/01/formal_theory_of_monads_follow.html)
Eduard Balzin's 2014 exposition of Street's 1972 paper. A distributive law `λ: ST→TS` between two
monads on the same object is an object of `Mnd(Mnd(K))` — "a distributive law is a monad in the
2-category of monads" — giving `Mnd` a lax 3-monad structure on `2-Cat`, plus the
`Mnd(K)/Mnd(K^op)/Mnd(K^co)/Mnd(K^coop)` duality square. Comment thread (Riehl, Campion, Loregian)
stays at free-algebra/representability level, no entwining/holonomy discussion. Candidate
2-categorical framing citation for Ch7's entwining/holonomy material.

under the name of mnd-containers, very different from directed containers. Mnd-containers can be

duplicated." Monad-side dual to directed containers (comonad-side) — sounds directly relevant to

### [parallel transport (nLab)](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/parallel+transport)
Clean differential-geometry mirror: the transport functor `tra: P₁(X)→G` factors through the
fundamental groupoid `Π₁(X)` precisely when the connection is flat (trivial holonomy). Same
skeleton as MacBeth's "trivial holonomy ⟹ classification collapses to `𝕊×C`" — gauge-theory
dialect of the same fact. Good analogy citation for the holonomy-triviality connections note (a
fifth flavour alongside Categorical Belief Propagation's graph-connection-Laplacian flavour).
