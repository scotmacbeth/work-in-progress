## Papers

### [Categorical Equivariant Deep Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18417) — Yoshihiro Maruyama (Nov 2025, v2 Dec 2025)
Read in full (HTML) by the arXiv agent. Defines category-equivariant neural networks as finite
compositions of continuous natural transformations between **Vect-valued presheaves** `X, Y : C^op →
Vect` (Def 10), proves a universal approximation density theorem (Thm 3.1) unifying
group/groupoid/poset/lattice/graph/sheaf-equivariant networks via naturality. Confirmed zero mention
of polynomial functors, containers, Poly, free monads, or Para(Vect), and zero citation of
Ghani/Ahman/Uustalu/Spivak/Gavranović/Niu. An independently-derived tradition that structurally rhymes
with the Vec-containers front (`Fam(Vect)`-valued containers) — presheaf-valued-in-Vect neural layers,
naturality standing in for equivariance — without container framing. Not a scoop; a genuine
near-neighbour precedent worth keeping on the radar for the Vec front.

### [Relational inductive biases on attention mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04117) — Mijangos et al. (July 2025)
Abstract-level check only. Classifies attention by permutation-subgroup equivariance, geometric-deep-
learning/rep-theory framing. Confirmed NOT categorical — no functors, no Poly, no citation of O'Neill.
Ruled out, not read further.

## Web Research

### [Generalized Transformers from Applicative Functors](https://cybercat.institute/2025/02/12/transformers-applicative-functors/) — Jules Hedges, cybercat.institute
Genuinely new find. Generalizes attention/MLP Transformer layers to arbitrary **applicative functors**
(lax monoidal functors with tensorial strength) rather than concrete matrices — instantiated on
function-valued "vectors" (Chebyshev-coefficient `CVector`), binary trees (common-refinement zip), and
probability distributions, via an abstract `pair`/`Counit`/`Ring` typeclass trio standing in for the
usual sum-product linear algebra. No mention of polynomial functors or containers — a third
independent lineage (after Vertechi's parametric spans and O'Neill's Para(Vect) free-monad paper) for
"attention-like structure generalizes beyond matrices," this time via lax-monoidal/applicative
machinery rather than container-theoretic. Worth citing as a third comparison point in the eventual
Vec-containers "uses" write-up. Author candidly flags it as not GPU-competitive without custom kernels.
