## Papers

### [Harness Engineering as Categorical Architecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12239) — Bogdan Banu, 2026-05-12
★ **The standout find of the session, independently surfaced by BOTH the arXiv agent and the
community agent** — cross-corroboration from two blind searches. Introduces **ArchAgents**, a
category of "Architecture triples" `(G, Know, Φ)`: `G` = wiring graph of modules/ports (syntactic
protocol layer), `Know` = structural invariants/certificates, `Φ` = deployment map from abstract
capability slots to concrete model/tool implementations. Maps LLM-agent-harness design's "four
pillars" onto this: **Memory as coalgebraic state** (kin to my comonad/container world), **Skills
composed via operads**, Protocols = the wiring graph, full Harness = the Architecture object.
Proves architectural-equivalence theorems (up to symmetric-monoidal/hypergraph structure) and
"compiler functors" targeting concrete runtimes (LangGraph, Swarms, DeerFlow, Ralph, Scion),
preserving certificates through compilation.

**Connection:** the closest live categorical treatment of multi-agent orchestration found to date.
Composition goes through **operads + coalgebraic state**, NOT distributive laws / polynomial
functors / Zappa-Szép products — a genuinely different axis (parallel skill-assembly vs. my
sequential/interleaved two-party interaction via `C ⋈ D`). Good news twice over: (1) confirms the
Path-5 gap (container/DCont/ZS lens on orchestration) is still open — no scoop; (2) hands a
concrete, citable differentiator for the grant's Impact section and any future write-up of
`orchestration-is-zappa-szep-weld` — "the other live CT-for-agents proposal models parallel skill
composition; mine models sequential two-party interaction and its re-entrancy obstruction." Worth
a light watch for follow-ups that might independently reach for distributive laws.

### [Cartesian Linearly Distributive Categories: Revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04435) — Kudzman-Blais, Lemay (2025-09-04, rev. 2026-02-24)
