## Papers

### [Inner post-Lie algebras and inner post-groups](arxiv.org/abs/2605.21992) — Gubarev, Li, Sheng, Wang (2026)
Defines an obstruction class [κ] ∈ H²(𝔤▷, Z(𝔤)) (Lie case) / H²((G,∘), Z(G)) (group case) built by
pulling back a central extension along the inner-derivation map; the post-Lie algebra/group is
induced by a Rota-Baxter operator iff the class vanishes (Prop 2.10, Thm 2.12, Thm 3.11).
Structurally identical in shape to MacBeth's own `g-obstruction-is-h2-class` and part (c') of
`holonomy-composition-zs-bridge-proved` — a second, fully independent instance of "H² obstructs
splitting" outside the skew-brace/container literature. Post-Lie algebras are the Lie-theoretic
sibling of skew braces. Good evidence the pattern is a recognised phenomenon in extension theory
generally, worth citing as a structural parallel if (c') gets written up formally.

### [Action accessibility in the variety of skew braces](arxiv.org/abs/2605.30097) — Albano, Stefanelli (2026)
Proves (Thm 2.13, explicit order-24 GAP counterexample) that skew braces are NOT action accessible:
no universal split-extension classifier/"actor object" exists, unlike for ordinary groups. Extends
negatively to pre-Lie/post-Lie algebras (Prop 3.5). Relevant caution for the Zappa-Szép/matched-pair
side of MacBeth's work: no general "moduli of ZS-compatible actions" can be assumed to exist as a
classifying object for skew braces (or, by structural analogy, directed containers/action
categories) — any general statement about the space of matched pairs 𝔸(↓) needs case-by-case
verification. Same Oberwolfach-workshop cluster as the already-tracked Rathee-Yadav 2601.12371.

### [Reinforcement Learning in Categorical Cybernetics](arxiv.org/abs/2404.02688) — Hedges, Rodríguez Sakamoto (Strathclyde, 2024)
Major RL algorithm families (DP, Monte Carlo, TD learning, deep RL) arise as extremal cases of one
categorical construction: Bellman operators extended to parametrised optics on action-value
functions, nested doubly (value backup + agent-environment loop). No polynomial-functor/container
content. Hedges co-authored the already-tracked "Towards Foundations of Categorical Cybernetics"
(2105.06332) — two of that paper's four authors sit in Neil Ghani's own Strathclyde department.
Concrete, citable evidence the Para/Optics categorical-cybernetics program is applications-ready —
useful grant-narrative ammunition for the orchestration/Impact section alongside MacBeth's own
ZS/holonomy result. Does not itself bridge Para↔polynomial-functors (that gap stays open, flagged
2026-07-28).

## Web Research

### [Update Monads: Cointerpreting Directed Containers](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.TYPES.2013.1) — Ahman, Uustalu (TYPES 2013)
The single-monoid source paper for update monads as reader∘writer glued by a monoid action M↷S,
arising by "cointerpreting" directed containers; the dependently-typed generalisation does NOT
factor into plain reader+writer but into two relative monads. This is the primary citation for the
base case that MacBeth's live cross-mode-bridge PROVE result (composing two update monads sharing
S via a ZS product P⋈P') generalises to two jointly-acting monoids. Previously only the
directed-container-side dual (Ahman-Uustalu CMCS12) was tracked — this fills the gap on the
update-monad side specifically.

### [group extension (nLab)](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/group+extension)
Standard citable background: central extensions classified by Ext(G,A) ≅ H²_Grp(G,A), explicit
twisted-product cocycle formula, trivial class ⟺ split. Notes general non-central abelian extension
classification needs H² *plus* the induced action, not H² alone — a clean sanity check matching why
MacBeth's (c') result restricts to *aligned* abelian actions rather than claiming a full nonabelian
statement.

### [Cybercat Institute](https://cybercat.institute/)
Non-profit successor hub (est. 2022) to the already-tracked Strathclyde ACT2021 categorical-
cybernetics paper, run by Hedges/Capucci/Gavranović. 2025-2026 output (Dependent Optics II,
Categorical Pipelines, Bidirectional Typechecking with Dependent Lenses) is the right place to
re-check in future cycles for Para+Optics work intersecting the ZS/orchestration theme — dependent
lenses are morphisms of containers, so these titles are read-next candidates, though nothing there
is a direct hit yet.

## Connections

- **The H²-obstruction pattern now has independent instances in THREE separate algebraic settings**
  outside containers: skew braces (Rathee-Yadav, already tracked), post-Lie algebras/groups
  (Gubarev-Li-Sheng-Wang, today's find), and — per nLab — ordinary group extensions (the textbook
  case). All share the same shape: pull back a central/abelian extension along a structure map,
  take the cocycle of a section, vanishing ⟺ splits. This strengthens the case that MacBeth's own
  (c') result (`holonomy-composition-zs-bridge-proved`) is an instance of a *general* phenomenon in
  extension theory, not an idiosyncrasy of the container setting — good framing for the eventual
  write-up ("directed containers exhibit the same H²-splitting-obstruction pattern found across
  Lie theory, group theory, and Hopf-algebraic matched pairs").
- **A caution surfaced against a temptation not yet acted on**: Albano-Stefanelli's negative result
  (skew braces have no universal split-extension classifier) is a warning sign for any future move
  to claim a "moduli space of ZS-compatible actions" for directed containers/action categories —
  worth remembering before reaching for that generality in a future PROVE session.
- **The categorical-cybernetics frontier (Cybercat Institute) is now a standing venue to watch**,
  not just a one-off ACT2021 citation — Hedges/Capucci/Gavranović are actively producing
  Para/Optics work (dependent lenses = container morphisms) that could eventually bridge to
  MacBeth's own polynomial-functor orchestration story, though no direct hit yet.
