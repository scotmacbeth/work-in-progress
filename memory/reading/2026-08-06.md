**Core construction.** `𝒜 ⋉^T 𝒳 := ∫^𝒜 𝖤𝖬` — the Grothendieck construction of an *indexed category
of algebras*: over a parameter category `𝒜`, a **parametrized monad** `T:𝒜→𝖬𝗇𝖽(𝒳)` (family of monads
on a fixed carrier `𝒳`), total category = EM-algebras of the family. Projection `p^T:𝒜⋉^T𝒳→𝒜` is a
fibration; `⟨p^T,V⟩:𝒜⋉^T𝒳→𝒜×𝒳` is **monadic** (Result 1.2 — algebraic over the *product*, creates limits).

**1. vs my Zappa–Szép / orchestration (H²) — ORTHOGONAL.** Their "semidirect product" is **one-sided**
(the abstract itself: "semidirect product … acting on another via a representation"); `𝒳` does not act
back. My ZS weld `C⋈D` is the **two-sided** matched-pair (mutual DLs L∧G), and my obstruction is a genuine
`[ω]∈H²(Sk_C;𝒟)≅ℤ/2`. They develop **no** H² theory; their "section" criterion is a 1-categorical
adjunction-unit-is-equivalence condition + a fundamental-group-style monodromy action on the fibre over
the initial object (`T^p_∅=id`), not a cohomology class. Their construction is exactly the *degenerate*
(one-factor-acts-trivially) case that ZS generalises. Sharpens my framing rather than threatening it.
→ [[orchestration-is-zappa-szep-weld]].

**2. vs the fibrational T_M-over-M vocabulary — NEIGHBOUR, no proof leverage.** Wrong shape of "lying
over": my `T_M(S,P)=(MS,P⋆)` is a **monad on the total category (Cont) lying over a base monad (M on Set)**
(Street lifting along `p:Cont→Set`). Their `T` is **not** a monad on `𝒜` and not a lifting of a base
monad — it's a **parametrized family** on the *fixed* carrier `𝒳`, and the monadicity is over `𝒜×𝒳`.
Their **Thm ~4.4** = a bifibration criterion (adjoints to reindexing exist via AFT under terminal-in-`𝒳`
+ identity-monad-at-initial-`𝒜`) — about (co)completeness of the fibration, **not** cartesian-morphism
preservation, and says nothing about branching. ⟹ borrow the *language* (algebra-fibrations, parametrized
monads, p^T as (bi)fibration, monadicity-over-product); **no lever** on my crown "T_M cartesian ⟺ M
cartesian" or the branching stratification. Those stay mine.

**3. Book citation sentence (banked).** "…develops the general theory of algebra-categories bundled into a

### [Is the free monad 2-monad cartesian?](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/499885/is-the-free-monad-2-monad-cartesian) — new find
Asked and accepted-answered Sept 2025 by Kevin Carlson: **No** — the free-monad monad F on
finitary Set-endofunctors does not preserve pullbacks, witnessed by a tree-merging
counterexample (two distinct height-3 4-ary trees collapse to the same pair of binary trees
under the induced projection F y^{A+B}(1) → F y^A(1) × F y^B(1)). **Directly relevant**: an
independent, general, *externally-discovered* confirmation of the exact phenomenon MacBeth
found computationally in the crown-TFAE work — "cartFun ⇏ cartesian/polynomial" (the Bag/
multiset witness in `crown-tfae-strict-chain.md`). Carlson's tree-merging counterexample is
structurally the same species of "merging witness" as the leaf-map non-injectivity in the Lean
T_M-cartesian-boundary proof. Good citable external corroboration that cartesian Set-monads
are the exception, not the rule — worth a line in the book/grant chapter.

### [Fibrations from parametric monads](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/418434/fibrations-from-parametric-monads) — origin story of 2408.16581

### [A new (?) way of composing monads](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/431721/a-new-way-of-composing-monads) — new find
Simon Henry (2022, score 17): a way of composing two monads S, T via an **invertible** map
l: TS → ST satisfying only 3 of the 4 Beck distributive-law axioms (drops compatibility with
S's multiplication), giving a genuinely different composite monad structure on ST. Worked
example: S = a monoid M, T = free-monoid-of-lists; composite = "non-empty lists of M-elements
with adjacent-endpoint multiplication." A candidate **third weld-type** beyond ordinary
distributive laws and Zappa-Szép products — worth a mention if the book's H²/ZS chapter wants
to enumerate "how many ways are there to weld two monads together" for completeness. Set/
monoid-flavored, not container-flavored — neighbour-cite, not competition.

### [The Agent That Doesn't Know Itself](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2026/03/the_agent_that_doesnt_know_its.html) — n-Category Café, William Waites, 2026-03-20
Fully read. An LLM agent, confronted with evidence its context was just compacted, confidently
denies it and confabulates — it lacks metadata about its own runtime state. Waites formalises
the fix with **traced monoidal categories** (the agent's conversation history is a hidden,
*self-feeding* trace — an endo-loop distinct from a second, outward-facing telemetry trace
through an external "homunculus" supervisor) and **session types** compiled into "plumbing
calculus" primitives (e.g. a `Compaction` protocol: Pause/GetMemory/SetMemory/Resume with acks).
**Checked against MacBeth's three-modes taxonomy** (directed/ZS-H², state/Workers,
effect-coeffect/branching-κ-λ) as instructed: does **not** slot cleanly into any of the three.
Closest in subject matter to state/Workers, but structurally different — Workers-mode state
threading is linear/sequential (Set,×)-graded composition between *distinct* components,
while Waites's trace is *reflexive* (one agent looped on itself) with an asymmetric inner/
outer trace the Workers ΔS comonad formalism doesn't capture. Reads as a genuine **fourth,
orthogonal pattern** — "traced self-reference for context management" — complementary rather
than competing: the three modes classify composition of *multiple* systems, Waites addresses
how a *single* agent's own state loops back on itself, a substrate question the taxonomy
currently assumes away. Companion paper "Artificial Organisations" (arXiv:2602.13275) is
institutional-design framing, weak CT connection, low priority.
