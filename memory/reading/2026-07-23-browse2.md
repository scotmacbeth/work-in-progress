## Papers

### [Twisted operator algebras of self-similar groupoid actions on arbitrary graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07906) — Mundey, Kwaśniewski (Nov 2025)
★ **RESOLVED — genuinely verified this time, not a hallucination, and CONFIRMED orthogonal.**
Full PDF downloaded and grepped directly (saved `~/papers/2511.07906.{pdf,txt}`). The phrase about
twists "exhausting the second cohomology group of the associated Zappa-Szép product category" is
genuinely present — unlike the 07-22 2405.10207 incident, this was not a WebFetch fabrication. But
the theorem is orthogonal to MacBeth's `(G)⟺[ω]=0∈H²` existence-obstruction theorem: their ZS
product category `E*⋊G` is built *directly by formula* from a self-similar action (Remark 4.22,
citing Mundey-Sims' matched-pair construction) — its existence is never in question, it's
definitional. What H² classifies is *twisted-representation data* (2-cocycles) layered *on top of*
an already-existing category, not an obstruction to existence. Grepped the full extracted text for
"obstruct" — zero hits anywhere. This independently re-verifies (via a completely fresh full-text
read, not a repeat of the same summary) the existing verdict in
[[mundey-kwasniewski-cleared-orthogonal]]. **Close this thread — do not re-flag.**

### [Products, Crossed Products, and Zappa-Szép Products for k-Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08630) — Abell-Ball, Gillaspy, Glidden-Handgis, Lippert (2025)
Full-text read (saved `~/papers/2503.08630.{pdf,txt}`), closing a thread that had resurfaced across

### ★★ [A Reference for Categorical Structures on Poly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00534) — Spivak (v14, Sept 2025)
**The session's real find.** This living reference document was flagged as "HIGH PRIORITY to
deep-read in full" back on 2026-07-14 and never fully read (`sources.json` extraction was
`verified-quote` for only ~10 equations on the Day-convolution/closure territory, pp. 3-4). Today's
arXiv agent read further and found — at lines 3899-3995 of the extracted text — **exactly** the two
constructions Neil's new Ch4 milestones need:
1. **Cofree comonad as an adjunction** `U ⊣ 𝔠 : Poly ⇄ Comon(Poly)`, built as a limit tower:
   `𝔠p = lim(p_k)`, `p_0 = y`, `p_{k+1} = (p ◁ p_k) × y`, with a Lambek's-lemma corollary. This is
   the DUAL of the already-Lean-verified `Free.lean` grafting construction — the exact recipe for
   the new LEAN target.
2. **Free monad universal property as an honest adjunction** `𝔪 ⊣ U : Poly ⇄ Mon(Poly)`, via an
   ordinal-indexed colimit construction — the exact gap Neil flagged as Ch4 Milestone 3's one open
   piece.
Both are presented as *established* results (consistent with the existing Gambino-Kock attribution,
not a new claim by Spivak), but this is the single most load-bearing find for the next prove/lean
cycle: a concrete, citable construction to formalize/verify rather than derive from scratch.
`sources.json` entry updated (below) with the new locators; still not a full cover-to-cover read —
the rest of the ~4000-line reference remains unread.
