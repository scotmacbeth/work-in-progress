## Papers (arXiv agent)

### Katsumata, Rivas, Uustalu, "Interaction Laws of Monads and Comonads" — arXiv:1912.13477
Already indexed since 06-11-browse2 at a one-line agent-summary; today's read (abstract page only,
richer) surfaces the actual mechanism: functor-functor interaction laws = Chu spaces over
(Endofunctors, Day convolution); monad-comonad interaction laws are exactly the monoid objects
there (Hasegawa glueing); duality result — the greatest comonad interacting with a given monad is
its Sweedler dual. **Directly answers Neil's steer with a DIFFERENT engine than arrow categories**
— Chu-space/Day-convolution, not Freyd/profunctor. Since Day convolution is already load-bearing
in MacBeth's own Dirichlet-tensor work (`dirichlet-is-day-convolution.md`), this is a genuinely
useful alternative lens for the next PROVE session, complementary to the arrow-category route Neil
suggested. sources.json entry enriched.

### Goncharov, Milius, Schröder, Tsampas, Urbat, "Higher-order bialgebraic semantics" — arXiv:2405.16708
New to index. Found via citation-trail agent, title/abstract only (not fetched). Extends
Turi–Plotkin's bialgebraic abstract-GSOS framework — first-order only in the original — to
higher-order languages via dinatural "(pointed) higher-order GSOS laws," with a compositionality
theorem on combinatory logic and λ-calculus (Abramsky applicative bisimilarity). **The strongest
direct hit on Neil's Plotkin–Turi question** — this is the modern continuation of exactly the
programme he asked about (lift a comonad to algebras / monad to coalgebras). Recommend deep-read
next session, check whether the entwining (`monad-comonad-entwining.json`) is a special case or
dual of their dinatural laws.

## Web Research

### Grodin (with Spivak), "Poly-morphic effect handlers" — Topos Institute blog, 2024-01-03
Effect-handler semantics built natively in Poly: 𝔪_p ≅ y + p◁𝔪_p as a monad on Poly, handlers as
Kleisli morphisms p → 𝔪_q, category of handlers as a Grothendieck construction with a
factorization theorem. **Monad-only — zero comonad/coeffect/arrow content.** Confirms the gap:
nobody has built the comonad-dual half of this Poly effect-handler picture, which is exactly what
MacBeth's transfer (`G(S,P)=(S,M∘P)`) and cofree-comonad UP already supply. Strong citation target
for an eventual effects+coeffects paper.

### Heunen & Jacobs, "Arrows, like Monads, are Monoids" — MFPS 2006 (Oxford-hosted PDF)
Canonical categorical characterisation: arrows = monoids in the profunctor bicategory
C^op×C→Set = monads relative to Yoneda = Freyd categories. No comonad/coeffect content — this is
exactly the standard-FP fact underlying Neil's "arrows are the FP answer" intuition, but nobody
has extended it with a coeffect/comonad side.

### Petricek, Orchard, Mycroft, "Coeffects" — ICALP 2013 / ACM FP 2014
Introduces coeffects with an *indexed comonad* semantics (strictly more general than a plain
comonad) for context-dependent computation. Almost certainly the paper Neil has in mind as prior
art for "coeffects" — flagged as the top read-in-full recommendation before scoping an
arrows/effects+coeffects paper.

## Connections

The whole session converges on one honest picture for Neil's steer: the *ingredients* for an
"arrows unify effects and coeffects" paper all exist separately in the literature —
promonads-as-arrows (Heunen–Jacobs), coeffects-as-indexed-comonads (Petricek–Orchard–Mycroft),
graded effect+coeffect distributive laws (Gaboardi et al.), a Chu-space/Day-convolution
alternative unification (Katsumata–Rivas–Uustalu), and a live modern Plotkin–Turi extension
(Goncharov et al., higher-order bialgebraic semantics) — but **nobody has assembled them into one
structure tied to containers/Poly, or explicitly framed as an arrow category**. This raises both
the difficulty and the novelty of what Neil is proposing. Two candidate technical routes now sit
on the table for the next PROVE session: (a) build the arrow category directly (Freyd-category
style, per Neil's own framing), or (b) reframe the entwining as a Chu-space monoid object over
(Endofunctors, Day), which plugs into machinery MacBeth already has. Route (b) may be more
tractable to attempt first since MacBeth already has Day convolution results to reuse.
