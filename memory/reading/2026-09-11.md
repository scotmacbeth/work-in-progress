## Headline — the QPF paper is now properly read, and its DOI was wrong in the index

**Avigad, Carneiro, Hudon, "Data Types as Quotients of Polynomial Functors"** (ITP 2019) was
sitting in `sources.json` at `agent-summary` under DOI `10.4230/LIPIcs.ITP.2019.6` — **the article
number was wrong**. The correct DOI is `10.4230/LIPIcs.ITP.2019.17` (LIPIcs vol. 141, pp.
17:1–17:19). The arXiv agent fetched the real PDF (CMU mirror,
`contrib.andrew.cmu.edu/~avigad/Papers/qpf.pdf`) and read pages 1–10 of 19 directly. Content: a
QPF is a functor `F` equipped with a polynomial functor `P = A ▷ B` (their own container notation)
and a split surjective natural transformation `abs : P ⟹ F`. They construct initial algebras
(`fix F`, a quotient of the `W`-type by an inductive `Wequiv` relation) and final coalgebras
(`cofix F`, a quotient of the `M`-type by the union of all "precongruence" relations — engineered
specifically to avoid the weak-pullback-preservation side condition Isabelle's BNF class needs),
then generalise to the multivariate case (`typevec`, `mvfunctor`) — the direct ancestor of
Mathlib's `Data.QPF.Multivariate` / `Data.PFunctor.Multivariate`. Motivation: polynomial functors
(= containers) are closed under composition and (co)initial (co)algebras but NOT under quotients —
`finset`/`multiset` are Isabelle BNFs but not polynomial functors, by a cardinality argument. Did
not reach pages 11–19 (weak pullbacks, the data-type package, future work). **Concrete Path-6
action item**: the `qpf` typeclass (`abs`/`repr`/`abs_repr`/`abs_map`) is precisely a retraction of
a container onto a functor, and `Wequiv`/`Mcongr` are a template for quotienting any of MacBeth's
◁-monoid/comonoid containers that fail to be literal polynomial functors — e.g. constructions
arising from ZS/admissibility collapse. `sources.json` entry corrected: key renamed to the right
DOI, extraction bumped `agent-summary → deep-read`, `corrections` entry added recording the wrong
article number (grepped — no proof/registry file referenced the old key, only prior reading logs).

**Companion find**: the live **Lean 4** QPF successor is `github.com/alexkeizer/QpfTypes`
(Avigad/Carneiro/Hudon's own package, `github.com/avigad/qpf`, is Lean 3 only, superseded).
`QpfTypes` implements `data`/`codata` commands via QPF, backed by Alex Keizer's MSc thesis: 300
commits, 54 stars, explicitly **work-in-progress, "not at all ready for serious use"** (no mutual
recursion, limited totality checking). Not yet Mathlib-integrated. New `sources.json` entry.
