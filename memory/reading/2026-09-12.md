### [A Convenient Fibration for Dependently-Typed Probability Theory](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.4) — Ahman, Kammar, Møgelberg (LICS 2026)
Upgraded from abstract-only to deep-read: two independent agents (arXiv, web) both fetched and read
the full DROPS PDF (still no arXiv preprint). Builds "quasi-Borel families" (qbf) — a split fibration
refining the ordinary family fibration Fam(C) with quasi-Borel (measurable) structure on each fibre
— with fibred Σ/Π types, a propositions universe, and fibred measure/probability monads constructed
via a distributive-law-style lift of a base monad through the fibration. Culminates in a fibred
Kolmogorov conditional-expectation theorem (Sec. 9). Confirmed via full-text check: zero
container/polynomial-functor/W-type vocabulary anywhere. Co-author Danel Ahman is half of
Ahman-Uustalu (directed containers ≃ small categories, MacBeth's foundational equivalence) — a live
2026 instance of serious semantics built directly on the family fibration that underlies MacBeth's
own `Cont(cod) ≅ Fam(cod^op)` bifibration result, with zero cross-reference either way. Standing
Front-D read-next item now discharged.

Asked 2026-08-31, not previously tracked. Asks whether an infinitary extensive category with finite
limits and coequalizers of kernel pairs automatically has all coequalizers. Answered (score 7) in

kernel-pair coequalizers; no direct π₀/products-of-connected-objects content though.
