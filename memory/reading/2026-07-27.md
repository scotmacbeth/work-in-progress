## Papers

### [Higher Dimensional Trees, Algebraically](https://alexhkurz.github.io/papers/CALCO-07/GK07.pdf) — Ghani, Kurz (CALCO 2007, Springer LNCS 4624)
Full 8pp read (curl-downloaded, Read-tool PDF support — WebFetch couldn't parse the stream).
Recasts Rogers' higher-**dimensional** trees (linguistics: strings→trees→nD trees) as an
initial-algebra tower `R_{n+1}X = μY. X×T_nY` (R₁=List⁺, R₂=Rose). Proves the previously
"hitherto unused quadrant" of the free-monad/cofree-comonad 2×2 table: `μY.X×FY` is **always a
comonad** (Thm 3.2); `R_n` is **also a monad** (Thm 3.3, proof omitted for space); §4 shows `T_n`,
`R_n` are **containers**. This is the strongest candidate for Neil's unsourced "Ghani-Kurz
higher-order trees" Ch4 item-1 reference — **but it's higher-DIMENSIONAL, not higher-ORDER**
(HOAS/PL-binder) trees. Flag for Robin/Neil to confirm intent before citing.

### [Contextads as Wreaths; Kleisli, Para, and Span Constructions as Wreath Products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21889) — Capucci, Myers (Oct 2024)
Independently surfaced by **three** of four agents this session (arXiv, community, citation-trail)
— the strongest convergent signal of the day. Defines contextads via categorified Lack-Street
wreaths (tricategory of spans with display maps), unifying Kleisli-of-comonad, Para, and Span
constructions; parametric-right-adjoint monads dualize to dependently-graded comonads. Lack-Street
wreaths are the standard categorical home of semidirect/Zappa-Szép products, so this could either
(a) give a much more general categorical home for MacBeth's pairwise-ZS `(L∧G)` criterion, or
(b) risk overlapping it if their machinery specializes to containers/Poly somewhere in the full
text. Full PDF fetch failed for every agent (only abstracts/secondary summaries obtained) — **not
yet resolved either way**, needs a dedicated deep-read session.

### [Free PLTL algebras and a coalgebraic extension of hyperdoctrines](https://topos.institute/blog/2025-09-26-free-pltl-algebras-and-hyperdoctrines/) — Q Le, w/ José Siqueira, Kobi Kremnitzer (Topos Institute blog, 2025-09-26)
Read in full by the web agent (byline confirmed: Q Le, Oxford summer intern; not Spivak). Builds a
linking map λ: L∘P ⇒ P∘I^op relating a modal monad L on Pos (via a hyperdoctrine P) to an
**already-existing** comonad I on the base (e.g. stream comonad on B×X) — direction and objects
differ from MacBeth's transfer, which **constructs** a new comonad (S,P)↦(S,M∘P) from an arbitrary
Set-monad M on the **positions** of an **arbitrary** container. Their "branching/degree≥2"
obstruction is a logic-semantics problem (PLTL Next/Until on branching cofree comonads), not a
category-theory gap — no counterpart in MacBeth's unconditional coordinate proof. No
Ahman/Uustalu/Ahman-Bauer citations. **Verdict: adjacent, not a scoop.**
