## Papers

### [Bicrossed Products of Fusion Categories and Exact Factorizations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10207) — Müller, Peña Pollastri, Plavnik, 2024 (v2 2025)
Lifts matched pairs / bicrossed (Zappa-Szép) products from groups to **fusion categories**.
Main theorem: exact factorizations of a fusion category E into subcategories C, D correspond to
bicrossed products C⋈D — the same *shape* of characterization as MacBeth's pairwise-ZS criterion
(K=C⋈D over wide D iff (L)∧(G)), one level up in categorical semisimplicity. The PDF-extracted
summary reports a **cohomological obstruction** (Sec 4, ~Thm 4.16) governing existence — a
potential structural parallel to `(G)⟺[ω]∈H²`. Connection to seed: Path 4 (ZS criterion) +
Path 6 (obstruction theory pattern). **Not yet verified against primary source** (HTML mirror
404'd, PDF-extraction only) — flagged HIGH PRIORITY for a real re-read before citing anything
specific; if the obstruction is genuinely H²-valued this is a good "the pattern recurs across
categorical factorization theories" citation, not a competing claim (different category).

### [Compositional Semantic Communication for Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18115) — Kurisummoottil Thomas, Saad, Calvanese Strinati, 2026
Models heterogeneous physical-AI device composition via Karoubi envelopes, lenses (get/put), a
Grothendieck topology, and fibrations, then treats device coordination as a Stackelberg game with
Nash/Pareto equilibrium theorems. No operads, no polynomial functors. Connection to seed: Path 5
(orchestration) — closer to MacBeth's lens/fibration-flavoured angle than the usual sheaf-cohomology
multi-agent hits. New cluster member, not a scoop of the ZS/H² mechanism (no distributive laws,
no cohomology). Agent-summary provenance only (PDF fetch, not independently verified).

### [Operads for Compositional Reasoning in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13634) — Bottman, Richardson, 2026
"Questions operad" Q — operations are question templates, composition is sub-answer substitution;
QA models are algebras over Q; "operadic consistency" (agreement between direct and
decomposition-mediated answers) as a compositional-reasoning diagnostic. Connection to seed:
Path 5 frontier — clearest operad+LLM hit this session, though single-agent decomposition rather
than multi-agent orchestration. Weak provenance (PDF text partially garbled) — treat as
abstract-level until re-fetched.

### [Finitary Cartesian Closed Varieties and Semigroup Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19904) — Lawson, 2026
Builds on Garner; a "matched pair" [B|M] of Boolean algebra + monoid, category-equivalent to
Boolean left restriction monoids. Inverse-semigroup/restriction-monoid tradition — same "matched
pair" word as Zappa-Szép but a genuinely different lineage (no factorization-system or ZS
language). Low priority; useful mainly as a reminder the vocabulary is overloaded across
communities. Full HTML read (deep-read provenance).

**Ruled out:** arXiv:2605.25222 "Polynomial 2-monads and delooping" (De Leger) — Batanin-Berger
higher-operad sense of "polynomial," unrelated to the Gambino-Kock/Ahman-Uustalu container
tradition. Not read further.

## Community

### [Monoidal Closed Categories and Their Deviant Relatives](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2009/02/monoidal_closed_categories_and.html) — n-Category Café, Feb 2009 — ★ best find of the session
Baez and Stay ask whether an "internally closed" monoidal category (a functor ⇒ plus a natural
currying iso, **no adjunction required**) is automatically genuinely closed. Commenter "Theo"
gives a counterexample of the same *genus* as MacBeth's collapse tensor — a degenerate ⇒ (constant
functor) that satisfies currying vacuously. Cisinski gives a second, via Bousfield localization,
that satisfies currying but fails X≅Hom(1,X). Houston's proposed fix: closure needs
M(I,A⇒B)≅M(A,B) plus an associativity-coherence condition. **This is a 17-year-old precedent for
"currying-isomorphism-without-adjunction can be satisfied vacuously."** Open question in-thread,
never resolved: does the mechanism here match collapse-tensor's `η_B` non-injectivity, or is it a
genuinely different failure mode (their examples read as "too much identification"; collapse
tensor is "not enough structure to invert")? **Needs a direct check against the collapse-tensor
proof before the result goes to /write** — either a citation is owed, or the write-up should
explicitly distinguish the mechanisms.

### [Progic II](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2007/09/progic_ii.html) — n-Category Café, Sep 2007
