## Papers

### [On the cohomology of a smash product of Hopf algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0210123) — Mitja Mastnak, math/0210123 (2002)
Proves a five-term exact sequence for the low-degree cohomology of a smash product of
cocommutative Hopf algebras, **explicitly generalizing Tahara's group-theoretic result**
for `H²` of semidirect products — the exact classical citation already anchoring MacBeth's
group-side H² work (two-omega-sites, ks-nogo-not-h2). This is the single best hit of the
session: a direct structural template, one level up (Hopf algebras vs. groups), for where a
linear-ZS obstruction class would live if the "linear-ZS = smash product" conjecture holds.
**Directly feeds PROVE idea #1.**

### [Hochschild cohomology of smash products and rank one Hopf algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608762) — Burciu, Witherspoon, math/0608762 (2006)
Ring-structure results for `HH^*(A#H)`, worked computation for finite-dim rank-one Hopf
algebras. Computational toolkit paper — useful if a concrete `HH^*` computation is ever
needed to test the linear-ZS conjecture against a small example.

### [Hochschild cohomology of a smash product in the nonsemisimple case](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05388) — Marcos, Volkov, 1511.05388 (2015)
Relates `HH^*(A)` to `HH^*(A#G)` for finite `G`, focused on extensions of cyclic `p`-groups
by `p'`-groups in char `p`. Adjacent toolkit paper for the non-semisimple case — relevant
since the k-algebras coming out of the Vec-comonoid result (`vec-comonoid-algebra-family-proved`)
are not guaranteed semisimple.

### [Cohomological Obstructions and Weak Crossed Products over Weak Hopf Algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02528) — 2105.02528
Constructs an invertible twisted-convolution 2-cocycle and shows the obstruction to lifting
it to an honest crossed product is a **degree-3 Sweedler cocycle**, vanishing in 3rd Sweedler
cohomology iff a crossed product exists. Template for a "degree-2-cocycle-goes-to-degree-3-obstruction"
pattern; weak-Hopf setting is one hop from what's needed, worth keeping in mind if the naive
H² guess for linear-ZS turns out too low a degree.

### [Products in Hochschild cohomology and Grothendieck rings of group crossed products](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0212003) — math/0212003
Hochschild cohomology *ring* formula specifically for group crossed products — the most
classical case (crossed product by a group algebra, not a general Hopf algebra), closest in
spirit to the algebroid/matrix setting MacBeth already resolved classically. Grounding
material for the algebra-side H² story before generalizing to genuinely linear ZS.

## Community

### [MathOverflow 132410 — "Cohomological dimension of knit products"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/132410)
Mark Grant asks whether `cd(A⋈B)` (knit = Zappa-Szép product) can be strictly smaller than
`cd(A×B)`, and requests a spectral-sequence tool analogous to Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre for
knit products in general. Answers give concrete examples where `cd` drops (free-group
extensions, Stallings' `H⋊ℤ`) but explicitly state **no general LHS-type spectral sequence
exists for knit/ZS products** — subadditivity `cd(A⋈B)≤cd(A)+cd(B)` is only conjectured, not
proved, in general (confirmed only for asymptotic dimension). **Directly relevant to PROVE
idea #2**: independent confirmation from group cohomology that genuine ZS/knit-product
cohomology is a known-hard, currently open gap (vs. the semidirect case, which has
Hochschild–Serre) — consistent with the intuition that MacBeth's "aligned" restriction is
doing real mathematical work, not just simplifying for convenience.

## Web Research

### [nLab: crossed product algebra](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/crossed+product+algebra)
Defines `A♯H` for a bialgebra `H` acting on algebra `A`: `(a⊗h)(a'⊗h') = Σ a(h₁▹a')⊗h₂h'`.
Discusses **cocycled crossed products** — generalizing via an invertible 2-cocycle `σ`; cleft
extensions are always isomorphic to a cocycled crossed-product algebra. The right entry point
for the multiplication-formula shape a "linear ZS product" would need; no explicit H²
statement on the page itself but links out to Sweedler cohomology.

### [Hochschild Cohomology of Smash Products and Rank One Hopf Algebras](https://people.tamu.edu/~sjw/pub/hh-smash.pdf) — S. Witherspoon (TAMU expository notes)
Expository computation of `HH^*(A#H)` via a spectral sequence for smash products; a worked
small example to test the linear-ZS conjecture against directly, if pursued.

### [Math 210B: Mackey theory and applications](http://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/210BPage/handouts/mackey.pdf) — Brian Conrad, Stanford
Standard graduate handout proving Mackey's double-coset formula for `Res_K Ind_H^G V` in
terms of `H\G/K` — literally the same double-coset object `A\U/B` as the emergent-holonomy
count `h(s)`. The representation-theoretic cousin of the group-factorization holonomy
question. **Feeds PROVE idea #2** as the standard technique (restriction of induction) to
check the "h cohomological iff aligned" conjecture against. WebFetch couldn't parse the
compressed PDF stream — flag for a direct download+read if this front is picked up.

### [nLab: double coset](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/double+coset)
Basic reference definition, `H\G/K` bijects with `H`-orbits on `G/K`. Elementary but the
canonical citation anchor for the double-coset formalism itself.
