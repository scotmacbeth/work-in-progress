## Papers
### [Tridirectional Discriminating-Power Formal Verification of Smart Contract Reentrancy Defense](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01794) — Ray Iskander (arXiv:2606.01794, June 2026)
Read full text (arXiv agent, HTML/PDF via WebFetch). First machine-checked Lean 4 correctness
proof of the OpenZeppelin reentrancy-guard pattern against a source-level state-machine model of
production Solidity (DAO/Compound/Aave), composed under a "no-retrofit discipline" into one
capstone theorem = the literal conjunction `⟨h_dao, h_compound, h_aave⟩` of three independently
sealed proofs (propext-only, zero sorry). **Connection — a genuine contrast case for
[[lean-reentrancy-omega-equals-epsilon]]:** MacBeth's own reentrancy result is [ω]=ε∈H²≅𝔽₂, a
*categorical obstruction-theoretic* account of composability. This paper achieves industry-grade
verification at real-dollar stakes with **zero** categorical machinery — no distributive law, no
entwining, no coalgebra — composability here is pure proof conjunction. Worth flagging to Neil:
either (a) the guards don't structurally interact so the obstruction is trivially absent, or (b) a
real gap the categorical account should explain but doesn't yet.

### [Polynomial Functors in π-Clans for the Semantics of Type Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05689) — Joseph Hua, Yiming Xu (arXiv:2602.05689, Feb 2026)
Read full HTML text. Develops polynomial-functor theory (P_f, f:E→B) in "π-clans" — a class of
maps stable under pullback, closed under composition, with a partial pushforward condition — which
is strictly weaker than local cartesian closure. Proves elementary (CwF) and algebraic (natural
model / polynomial-functor) presentations of MLTT semantics equivalent (Thm 5.6/Cor 5.7).
**Connection:** an admissibility-style question one level up from Gap 1/Set×Vec_fd — here the
minimal structure is on the *ambient category*, not the pole. Filed as background for a possible
future push on "what's the minimal categorical structure containers actually need."

Linton–Paré: for any comonad D on Set, D-Coalg^op → Set^op is monadic (every covariety is dual to

Set-monads vs Dirichlet/comonad duals) is exactly this question one level of generality down.

### [Parametrized initial algebras — are these definitions equivalent?](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/512584/parametrized-initial-algebras-are-these-definitions-equivalent) — MathOverflow, 2026-07-08, 0 answers
For F:A×C→C, compares "pointwise" parametrized initial algebra (object-by-object μX.F(A,X)) vs.
"global" (initial algebra of the induced endofunctor on [A,C]). Pointwise⟹global shown;
global⟹pointwise open. Same shape as [[oneill-free-monad-tensor-algebra-linear-container]]
(colim F^n) — the open direction is a clean, reusable lemma-shaped gap worth checking against that
proof's notes.
