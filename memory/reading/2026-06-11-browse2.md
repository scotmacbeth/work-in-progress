## Papers

### [Baues-Wirsching Cohomology and Švarc Genus in Small Categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00727) — Carcacía-Campos, Macías-Virgós, Mosquera-Lois (Filomat 2026)

**Key result:** Self-contained treatment of BW cohomology via the factorization category FC (morphisms of C as objects, factorizations as morphisms). Cochain groups F^n(C;D): maps on composable n-tuples with values in D(composition). Cup-length in relative BW H* gives lower bound for Švarc genus of bifibrations.

**BW coboundary on 2-cochains:**
δφ(f,g) = (f)_* φ(g) − φ(f∘g) + (g)^* φ(f)

**Critical result for my work:** H²(Sk_C; 𝒟) IS a special case of H²_BW with *source-constant* natural system D̃(f:a→b) = 𝒟(a). With source-constant coefficients (f)^*φ(f') = φ(f') and (f)_* acts via D's functoriality = identity (D constant along morphisms), so the coboundary reduces to my cocycle equation exactly. **[ω] ∈ H²_BW(Sk_C; D̃). ✓**

**H² classification:** From arXiv:1512.03250 (Schreier theory of track categories): H²_BW(C; D) classifies linear extensions of C by D = exact sequences 1 → D → E → C → 1; splits iff class = 0. This is precisely the theorem that makes [ω] the right invariant.

**H² vs H³ clarification:** H² = classification of extensions (how many). H³ = secondary obstruction (whether exists). Since my pairwise criterion (L∧G) already answers existence, [ω] is correctly H². ✓

**Historical note from nLab:** Leech (1975), Wells (1979) non-abelian version (relevant for non-abelian (G)!), Baues-Wirsching (1985) JPAA 38:187-211.

**Svarc genus dividend:** When [ω] ≠ 0, cup product length of [ω] gives a sharper stratification of the obstruction. New tool for the ZS problem.

**What the B-W community is NOT doing:** Nobody is studying ZS extensions using BW cohomology. The B-W community is doing homotopy theory and sectional category. My H²(Sk_C; 𝒟) obstruction is genuinely original territory.

**Connection:** The (G) classification is now a named theorem: "the (G) obstruction is the BW cohomology class [ω] ∈ H²_BW(Sk_C; D̃) with source-constant coefficients." Cite Baues-Wirsching 1985 for the framework, arXiv:1512.03250 for the H²-classifies-extensions theorem.

### [Pattern Runs on Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16321v3) — Libkind-Spivak (EPTCS 2025)

**Key result:** In (Poly, ◁, y):
- Free monad monad 𝔪_p: transfinite induction p_{(0)}:=y, p_{(α+1)} := y + p ◁ p_{(α)}, limit by colimit. Left adjoint to forgetful from ◁-monoids.
- Cofree comonad comonad 𝔠_q: coinductive "q-behavior trees." Right adjoint to forgetful U: Cat♯ → Poly.
- **Module structure (Thm 3.2):** Natural map Ξ_{p,q}: 𝔪_p ⊗ 𝔠_q → 𝔪_{p⊗q} making 𝔪 a left module over 𝔠. Arises from duoidality of ⊗ and ◁.

**Critical fact:** Cat♯ = DCont ≅ Cof appears explicitly — **the cofree comonad on any polynomial functor lands in my world.** The right adjoint of U: Cat♯ → Poly is the cofree construction. Directed container = cofree comonad.

**For the book chapter on free/cofree:**
- Free monad = W-type = p-labeled decision trees = 𝔪_p ≅ y + (p ◁ 𝔪_p)
- Cofree comonad = M-type = q-labeled behavior streams = 𝔠_q ≅ y × (q ◁ 𝔠_q)
- Module action = "pattern runs on matter" = protocol executing against agent

**Connection to grant narrative:** Protocol (free monad) runs on directed container (cofree comonad). The ZS product C⋈D may be expressible as 𝔠_q ◁-extending 𝔠_p; my pairwise criterion would then classify when this extension exists.

### [Structures on Categories of Polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00167) — Shapiro-Spivak

**Key result for book chapter on closed structure:**

Internal hom for ⊗ (Prop 2.8):
```
[p, q]  :=  Σ_{φ : p → q}  y^{Σ_{i : p(1)} q[φ₁(i)]}
```
Adjunction: Poly(r ⊗ p, q) ≅ Poly(r, [p, q]). Makes (Poly, y, ⊗, [−,−]) a **symmetric monoidal closed category**.

**Note:** The composition product ◁ is NOT closed; only coclosed. Duoidality between ⊗ and ◁ is the best one can do for ◁.

**Connection:** The internal hom formula is a sum over container morphisms φ: p→q, connecting cleanly to the DCont≅Cof perspective. This is the closed structure chapter of the book.

### New observation: OFS on Cof = ZS two-level criterion

The OFS (Bij^op, DOpf) on Cof:
- **Bij-on-objects** piece ↔ **(L) local freeness**: source-UF, Hom(a,b) free right End(a)-set
- **Discrete opfibration** piece ↔ **(G) global closure**: free bases close into SFS

Every cofunctor factors as A ↛ A' (Bij-on-objects) followed by A' ↛ B (discrete opfibration). In ZS terms: the morphism d∘c factors through d (in D) then c (in C). This OFS is the categorical trace of the ZS two-level criterion.

**Status:** Observation, not yet proved. Proposition to write up: "The OFS (Bij^op, DOpf) on Cof is the factorization-system shadow of the pairwise ZS criterion (L∧G)."
