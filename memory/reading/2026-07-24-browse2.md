## Papers

### [The Zappa-Szép product of twisted groupoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00466) — Duwenig, Li (2024)
Assumes a matched pair of groupoids already exists; studies when twists over the two factors
combine into a twist over the product (C*-blend characterization). Same shape as every other
sibling in the H²-obstruction-family hunt: classifies twists on an **already-given** product,
orthogonal to my `(G)⟺[ω]=0` existence obstruction. Does not touch the groupoid-invertibility
question. Abstract-depth only.

### [Formalising Inductive and Coinductive Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02603) — Damato, Altenkirch, Ljungström (ITP 2025)
Cubical Agda formalization proving containers preserve least/greatest fixed points (UIP-free,
generalizing Abbott et al. to intensional type theory). A direct check of the PDF body for
"comonad"/"cofree"/"counit"/"comultiplication" found **zero hits** — bare M-type preservation,
no comonad structure built on top. This is a genuine negative result, not a search failure: the
comonad-on-M-type step looks unformalized *anywhere*, Agda included, consistent with why the
Lean cofree-comonad work stays blocked on more than just tooling. Worth citing as "closest
existing formalization, stops one step short" in the Ch6/Lean writeup.

### [A reference for categorical structures on Poly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00534) — Spivak (v14, Sept 2025)
Re-confirmed (already known, deep-read prior session): explicit `U ⊣ 𝔠` cofree-comonad adjunction
via limit tower (`𝔠p = lim p_k`, `p_0=y`, `p_{k+1}=(p◁p_k)×y`, eqs 244-252) and `𝔪 ⊣ U` free-monad
adjunction via ordinal colimit (eqs 256-264) — both of Neil's Ch4 milestones' citable recipes live
side by side in this one document. No new content this pass; flagged again only to confirm it's
the primary citation target before the cofree-comonad Lean/prove session starts.

### [Products, crossed products, and Zappa–Szép products for k-graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08630) and [Homology and twisted C*-algebras for self-similar actions and Zappa-Szép products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09600) — re-confirmed cleared
