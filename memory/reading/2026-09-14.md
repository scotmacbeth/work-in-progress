### [Nested Inductive Types: Justified and Usable Nested Inductive Types in Lean and Rocq](https://popl26.sigplan.org/details/rocqpl-2026-papers/9/Nested-Inductive-Types-for-Rocq-and-Lean) — Lamiaux, Forster, Sozeau, Tabareau (PACMPL/OOPSLA, DOI 10.1145/3808322)
Confirmed NOT on arXiv (three independent agents checked — direct arXiv API, WebSearch, author search — all negative). Exists as a Zenodo code record, a bot-blocked HAL preprint, and an accepted RocqPL 2026 talk (POPL, Rennes, 2026-01-17). Three agents (arXiv, web, citation-trail) independently converged on the same POPL26/HAL/Semantic-Scholar abstract text, upgrading the entry from "citer-metadata only" (09-12) to a real abstract: introduces a new positivity condition for nested inductive types (e.g. RoseTree-style constructors parametrised by the type being defined) that is neither Lean's overly strict check nor Rocq's overly lax one, with a MetaRocq plugin auto-generating provably-correct eliminators. Directly relevant to Path 6 (QPF/Lean formalisation) as a sibling technique for justifying inductive-type constructions with usable recursion principles — for genuinely *nested* rather than merely parametrised shapes. Still no full text (HAL PDF blocked by bot-detection); re-check arXiv in a few weeks.

### [Admissibility, stable units and connected components](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.4277) — Xarez (2011)
Categorical Galois theory. Proves a reflection is semi-left-exact ("admissible" in Janelidze's sense) iff its connected-components functor sends connected objects to connected objects, and has **stable units** iff **any finite product of connected components is connected**. That second condition reads verbatim as MacBeth's own π₀-multiplicativity criterion (`lemmaS-sufficient-extensive-pole-pi0`, `gl-inadmissible-pi0-multiplicativity`, 2026-09-02) — under a *different* sense of "admissible" (Galois-reflection admissibility, not ◁-admissibility). **Flagged as a real terminology/possible-prior-art collision, not yet resolved** — needs a full read before any claim either way. Found by the community agent via WebSearch (not previously in the seed or sources.json).

### [Transfer of homological objects in exact categories via adjoint triples. Applications to functor categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04012) — authors unconfirmed from abstract (2024)
Studies `Add(A,R-Mod)` with an exact structure; introduces "stalk functors" (evaluation at an object of `A`) and derives cotorsion pairs induced by them, giving an *intrinsic* characterization of projective/injective objects in `(Add(A,R-Mod);E)` — no prior existence hypothesis on injectives/projectives needed. `A=Vec`, `R=k` is exactly MacBeth's Conjecture 6.2 setting (is `F=Hom(E,⊕_ℕ−)`, essentially `∏_ℕ∘⊕_ℕ`, projective in `Add(Vec,Vec)`?). **Best single find of the session** — strongly flagged for a full read before the next PROVE session on the Vec-admissibility crux.

### [Gorenstein projective objects in functor categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05493) — authors unconfirmed from abstract (2018)
Same genre as 2407.04012 (constructing/characterizing projective-type objects in a functor category `B^C`) but Gorenstein-projective (relative/Frobenius setup) rather than plain projective, and requires `C` Hom-finite/locally bounded — `Vec` (MacBeth's domain category) doesn't obviously satisfy this. Lower priority, technique-adjacent only.

### Web (non-arXiv): [Baer's Result: The Infinite Product of the Integers Has No Basis](https://www.math.uni-duesseldorf.de/~schroeer/publications_pdf/infinite_product-1.pdf) — Schröer, expository note
Clean 3pp proof that `∏_ℕ ℤ` has no basis (Baer-Specker group is not free), via the classical splitting-off-a-countable-free-subgroup + divisor-counting technique, citing **Kaplansky, "Projective modules" (Ann. Math. 68, 1958)** as the source of the general splitting-off technique for projectivity criteria in module categories. Directly on-topic technique for the "bet A (inadmissible)" direction of Conj 6.2 — worth pulling Kaplansky 1958 itself as a classical reference (not arXiv-indexable; note only, no sources.json entry).

Useful contrast case: the "weak" (distributive) version of connectedness vs. the genuine `π₀` MacBeth's characterization needs.

## Connections

- **The Xarez paper is the session's most consequential find**, if it holds up: "stable units ⟺ finite products of connected components are connected" (Xarez, categorical Galois theory, 2011) may be the *same condition*, independently discovered fifteen years earlier in a different vocabulary, as MacBeth's own π₀-multiplicativity characterization of ◁-admissibility on the extensive-CCC pole. This would not diminish MacBeth's result (the ◁-admissibility application and the Lemma-S/extensive-pole framing are still novel), but it would mean the *underlying combinatorial fact* about connected-components functors is decades-old folklore in a sister subfield, and should be cited rather than left implicit.
