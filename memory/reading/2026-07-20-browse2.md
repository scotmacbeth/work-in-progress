## Papers

### [Comonads as Spaces](arxiv.org/abs/2607.15091) — Fairbanks, Carlson, Spivak (2026-07-16)
150+pp monograph generalizing comonads-on-Set to an abstract notion of *space* (density comonad
⟨P⟩=Lan_P P, dual to monad-as-algebra). Every comonad on Set has an underlying topological space
*and* an underlying small category; topological spaces are density comonads of subbasis-diagrams;
continuous maps + comonad maps assemble into a double category **ℂmd** which, restricted to
polynomial comonads, recovers **the double category of categories, retrofunctors (cofunctors), and
functors** (Thm 3.2.7) — but this square is cited as prior literature ([HM93, Agu97, Meg21, AU17,
NS25]), not reproved. "Directed containers" never appears by name. **Not a scoop** of MacBeth's
Lean-verified DCont≅Cof — confirms Spivak's group is actively building one level up from it. Their
"bicomodules between comonads" future-work remark is a plausible eventual intersection with the
Zappa-Szép/weld programme (two-sided actions), not yet real.

### [Biological Motifs for Agentic Control](arxiv.org/abs/2607.04240) — Bogdan Banu (2026-07-05)
Second paper by the ArchAgents author (arXiv:2605.12239). Maps five systems-biology control motifs
onto agentic-software patterns via polynomial functors + wiring-diagram algebra (§3.1 "The Category
Poly", §3.3 "Promoters as Lenses", §4 "Agentic Operad": parallel ⊗, serial ∘, trace). Explicitly
checked for Zappa-Szép products, distributive laws, semidirect products: none appear. "Provable
error suppression bounds" are qualitative, not formally proved. **Not a scoop** of
orchestration-is-Zappa-Szép-weld — confirms operad/wiring-diagram algebra is this subfield's default
vocabulary, useful as an explicit contrast in the grant write-up. Banu is now a two-paper recurring
author in the categorical-agent-orchestration space, worth tracking.

### [Cartesian Linearly Distributive Categories: Revisited](arxiv.org/abs/2509.04435) / [Linearly Distributive Fox Theorem](arxiv.org/abs/2506.02180) — Kudzman-Blais, Lemay
General LDC-foundations papers (posetal-distributive/semi-additive examples; medial-LDC + duoidal
extension of the Fox theorem). Confirmed as the sole reverse-citation of Spivak-Srinivasan
arXiv:2407.01849 found this session — genuinely new but **no overlap**: no Poly, no Dialectica, no
⋉/⋊ construction. Only 1 total citer of 2407.01849 exists — low uptake, low collision risk from this
specific cluster (says nothing about independent discovery elsewhere).
