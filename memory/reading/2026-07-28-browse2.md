## Papers

### [Contextads as Wreaths; Kleisli, Para, and Span Constructions as Wreath Products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21889) — Capucci, Myers
**★★ Deep-read this session — cycle-29's own recommendation (`download_pdf` + `pdftotext -layout`
instead of WebFetch) worked on the first try**, after 2+ prior sessions stuck at abstract-only.
Appendix A.2 (Thm A.1, Def A.3, Thm A.4) proves almost exactly the shape of Neil's Workers steer: a
polynomial monad `T` (container `P:S→Type`) transposes to a dependently graded comonad
("contextad") — grade type for `X` is `X→S`; action `X⊙s = (x:X)×P(s(x))`; composition **multiplies
the grade via the monad's `seq`**: `(s⊗t)(x) := seq(s, t(x))`, matching "composition multiplies
context `D(S×T)`" close to verbatim. **Theorem A.4** (`Kl(T) ≅ Ctx(⊙)`) is explicitly **left
unproven** by the authors — "we leave this theorem unproven because we want to give it an abstract
proof in future work... for any parametric right adjoint monad." This is a genuine open theorem in
the published literature, squarely in the path of Neil's Workers construction — either MacBeth
proves/generalizes it, or explicitly cites it as the special case being subsumed. Zero
Zappa-Szép/semidirect content anywhere in the 82pp (grepped) — orthogonal to the ZS thread as
before. **Distinct from MacBeth's own proved transfer** `G(S,P)=(S,M∘P)` (arbitrary Set-monad
pushed onto *positions*) — this is a *self*-transpose of one container-monad's Kleisli category.
Surfaced citation chain, none previously indexed: Fong-Spivak-Tuyeras (Para origin), **Gavranović
"Compositional Deep Learning" (arXiv:1907.08292, 2019)**, Katsumata POPL 2014 (graded-monad term
origin), Gaboardi et al. 2016 (graded comonads/coeffects).
