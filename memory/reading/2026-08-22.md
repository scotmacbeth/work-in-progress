## Papers

### ["SKB is not algebraically coherent"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28380) — Sciandra, Volpe (30 Jul 2026)
Proves the category of skew braces (and cocommutative Hopf braces) is not algebraically coherent, hence
not locally algebraically cartesian closed. A *negative* structural result about the same category whose
positive semi-abelian properties (`2411.19238`) anchor the linear-ZS bridge — relevant to whether internal
categorical machinery (Beck modules, internal groupoids) behaves well enough there to host a clean H²
theory. Abstract-only.

### ["Central series of cocommutative Hopf braces"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03798) — Bevilacqua, Gran, Sciandra (5 May 2026)
Central series/socle/annihilator for Hopf braces via a star-product operation; proves Hopf formulae for
homology via relative commutators; recovers the Huq commutator in the commutative-cocommutative case.
Same semi-abelian/Huq-commutator machinery underlying the whole cluster. Abstract-only.

### ["On the semi-abelianness of cocommutative Hopf monoids"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22200) — Sciandra, Zuo (23 Mar 2026)
Generalizes Newman's correspondence: cocommutative Hopf monoids in any abelian symmetric monoidal category
are semi-abelian (given faithful-flatness), unifying prior cocommutative/color-Hopf results; establishes
action-representability. Base framework the Hopf-brace cohomology results sit on. Abstract-only.

### ["Coalgebraic Models of Ω-Groups"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22653) — Bevilacqua (v1 Oct 2025, v3 Aug 2026)
Ω-group theories modelled in cocommutative coalgebras over a field; shows semi-abelianness, a coalgebraic
Birkhoff-variety theory. Same author-cluster, broadens the coalgebraic toolkit. Abstract-only.

### ["Hopf formulae for cocommutative Hopf algebras"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09992) — Gran, Sciandra (12 Sep 2025)
Uses Takeuchi's coalgebra/Hopf adjunction to get enough projectives relative to cleft extensions, weak
universal normal extensions, categorical Galois theory, an explicit Hopf formula for the fundamental
group, and a 5-term exact sequence (Hopf analogue of Stallings–Stammbach). Direct technical predecessor
to the central-series and semi-abelianness papers above. Abstract-only.

### ["Hopf formulae for homology of skew braces"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18056) — Gran, Letourmy, Vendramin (v1 Sep 2024, JPAA 229 (2025) no.11) — **load-bearing gap**
Non-abelian homological algebra applied directly to *skew braces* (not Hopf braces): Hopf formulae
relating skew braces to radical rings/groups, algebraic characterization of central extensions, an
explicit Stallings–Stammbach sequence for skew-brace exact sequences. This is the skew-brace-level
analogue of the Hopf-brace central-series paper above, and — because it works at the skew-brace level
directly — sits closer to Rick's H²/Ψ front (Rathee–Yadav `2601.12371`) than any of the Hopf-brace papers
do. Missed by earlier sweeps because it predates the tracking window (2024) despite being actively
revised into 2025. Flagged as the top read-next if the Rick-bridge thread ever gets a Neil steer.
Abstract-only.

### ["Matched pairs and Yetter-Drinfeld braces"](https://cms.math.ca) — Ferri, Sciandra (*Canad. J. Math.*, Online First 2025)
Found via Andrea Sciandra's personal publications page, not arXiv (no arXiv ID located). Extends the
matched-pair/brace vocabulary with "Yetter-Drinfeld braces," terminology not seen in prior sweeps. Same
cluster; worth checking whether it touches the Mastnak cohomology seam before any PROVE attempt on the
linear-ZS bridge. Abstract-only (via publication listing).

### ["Functorial Neural Architectures from Higher Inductive Types"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16123) — Karen Sargsyan (17 Mar 2026)
Frontier find. Proves compositional generalization in neural nets is *equivalent* to functoriality of the
decoder: compiles HIT specifications into architectures via a monoidal functor from a path groupoid `BG`
to a category of parametric maps (path constructors → generator networks, composition → structural
concatenation as a *strict* monoidal functor, group relations/2-cells → learned natural transformations).
Proves softmax attention provably cannot be functorial for non-trivial groups (cross-segment dependence
breaks the independence functoriality needs). Core theorems formalized in Cubical Agda; experiments
(torus, wedge-of-circles, Klein bottle) show functorial decoders beating attention by 2–10x. Read at
full-HTML depth. Not agent-orchestration, but a genuine "definitions → proof assistant → experiment"
pipeline result, and the "learned natural transformation witnessing non-trivial group relations" pattern
rhymes with MacBeth's own 2-cell/holonomy coherence work (cf. emergent-holonomy files) — a candidate
dream-cycle cross-domain note, not yet checked in depth.
