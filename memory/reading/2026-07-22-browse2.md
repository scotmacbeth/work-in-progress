## ★★ Correction — arXiv:2405.10207 does NOT contain a cohomological obstruction

**The single most important result of this session is a retraction of this morning's own finding.**
The 07-22 morning browse (cycle 21) reported, via PDF-extraction, that §4 of arXiv:2405.10207 proves
a cohomological obstruction to exact factorization of fusion categories — flagged HIGH PRIORITY as
the closest structural sibling yet to MacBeth's `(G)⟺[ω]∈H²` theorem.

This session's arXiv agent downloaded the actual PDF and read pages 1–3 **directly with the Read
tool, bypassing the WebFetch summarizer** — and discovered the summarizer's first pass had
**fabricated** "Theorem 4.1–4.16" and "Davydov–Yetter cohomology" language that does not appear
anywhere in the source. A follow-up pass explicitly grepped the extracted PDF text for "obstruction",
"cohomology", "H^2", "H2": **zero hits**. Reading the paper's own table of contents and "Organization
of the article" paragraph confirms directly: §4 defines matched pairs (Def 4.1) and constructs the
bicrossed product (Thm 4.6, Cor 4.9) — no obstruction theory. **Remark 4.16 (the actual content at
that number) explicitly leaves the categorified question open**: *"Is every exact factorization of
fusion categories given by a bicrossed product? If not, how much can the associativities differ from
the one given by the bicrossed product?"* The paper only proves the **ring-level** analogue (Thm
3.14) — the categorified version is unanswered, not answered cohomologically.

**This is a live instance of [[the-summary-is-what-gets-audited]]** — a lossy first-pass summary
manufactured a false structural-sibling claim that survived one full dream-consolidation cycle
(07-21 dream → 07-22 morning browse → 07-22 dream journal open-threads) before a direct-text re-read
caught it. Corrected in `connections/cohomological-obstruction-family.md` (sighting #4 downgraded,
correction banner added) and `memory/reading/sources.json` (extraction bumped agent-summary→
deep-read, corrections entry added, never deleting the original wrong note).

Citation-trail agent, same session: 2405.10207's 22 references are entirely internal to the
fusion-category/Hopf-algebra literature (Etingof-Gelaki-Nikshych-Ostrik, Natale, Takeuchi, Kac 1968)
— zero overlap with MacBeth's own Rosebrugh-Wood/Baues-Wirsching/Pirashvili H² stack, no
"Zappa-Szép" vocabulary at all. Only 2 reverse citations (arXiv:2507.08562, same authors' own
follow-up; and an unrelated weak-Hopf-algebra paper), no 2026 activity, nothing from anyone in
MacBeth's own community. **Verdict: two disjoint literatures independently posing the same
existence question (bicrossed-product/matched-pair vs Zappa-Szép) via classical extension theory
(Kac 1968) — a genuine intellectual parallel worth a grant-narrative remark ("this pattern recurs
across levels of categorical abstraction"), but not a citation MacBeth's own proof needs to engage
with, and not a scoop either way** (they pose the question and leave it open; nothing to have
scooped).

## n-Café 2009 "Deviant Relatives" — RESOLVED (not a scoop, cite as precedent)

Community agent fetched the actual thread (`golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2009/02/monoidal_closed_
categories_and.html` — the `/2009/06/` URL guessed yesterday was a 404) and read both counterexamples
in full:

- **Theo's counterexample** (constant functor `a⇒y=0` for all `a,y`): mechanism = coherence axioms
  satisfied *vacuously* because both sides of the currying iso are literally the same constant
  functor — a definitional critique that representability was never checked, not a collapsing/
  identification mechanism.
- **Cisinski's counterexample** (left Bousfield localization, `Hom(1,X)≃UF(X)` instead of `X`):
  mechanism = unit/counit of a *monoidal adjunction between two different categories* (C and its
  localization D) failing to be iso — an idempotent-monad compression across categories, a
  "wrong category" failure.
- **MacBeth's collapse tensor**: a single-category representability failure — `R_2=(−)⋆2` fails to
  be presentable by a polynomial because the required unit-insertion `η_B` is not injective, entirely
  internal to Poly/Cont.

**Verdict: same broad genus ("monoidal, but the naive candidate right adjoint doesn't work"), three
distinct specific mechanisms.** Cite the thread as precedent for the *phenomenon class* — appropriate
hedge, no scoop, no need to retract or reframe the collapse-tensor result. This clears the
must-resolve-before-/write flag from yesterday's dream journal; the vacuity result is clean to ship.
