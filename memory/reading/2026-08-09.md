## Papers

### [AutoBayes: A Compositional Framework for Generalized Variational Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18608) — Toby St Clere Smithe, Marco Perin
Genuinely new (confirmed via full-text grep of `memory/` and `sources.json`). Live sequel to
Smithe's Compositional Active Inference I/II, built in a bicategory of "open models" (measure-kernel
triples of unobserved/observed/latent spaces) generalizing Bayesian networks beyond DAG structure.
Central result: a Bayesian chain rule for lens composition, `(d∘c)†_π = c†_π ∘ d†_{c_*π}`, mirroring
reverse-mode autodiff, letting approximate-posterior loss functions compose correctly across
sequential (∘) and parallel (⊗) model composition; lenses compose via a "diamond" (⋄) operator.
Polynomial functors/containers not explicit in the paper (checked directly). Bayesian lenses are a
structural cousin of directed containers' get/put shape — this is the clearest evidence yet that
the VERSES/Topos-adjacent community (Smithe, ex-Topos, now VERSES) is actively extending "agents as
compositional lens-coalgebras," relevant background for the grant's agent-orchestration Applications
narrative. Read: WebFetch HTML abstract + body extraction, not full derivations.

### [Examples of non-polynomial comonads on Set?](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/457580/examples-of-non-polynomial-comonads-on-set) — new answer, new to sources.json
Question is old (Spivak, Nov 2023) but has a substantive new answer posted 2025-01-12 by Aaron
David Fairbanks: a concrete non-polynomial comonad on undirected multigraphs, `X³ + X²/2 + X`,
built as the coequalizer of the polynomial directed-quiver comonad `X³+X` (quivers = walking-
parallel-pair category `·⇉·`). Does not preserve pullbacks; demonstrates **colimits of polynomial
comonads need not stay polynomial**. Same failure mode (pullback non-preservation) as the already-
known MO 302631 Bag-monad witness, now on the comonad side — a matched pair of "how the
Containers≃DirectedContainers≃PolyComonoids≃Cat chain fails to close under colimits/quotients"
counterexamples. Candidate citation for book Ch3 boundary material.

### [The convolution of comonads is a comonad](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/346529/the-convolution-of-comonads-is-a-comonad) — corroboration, new to sources.json
2019 thread, two answers not previously logged. Alexander Campbell: Day convolution is opmonoidal
w.r.t. composition in a duoidal category, so automatically preserves comonoids (cites Street's
duoidal notes, Garner–López Franco "Commutativity"). David Spivak's answer (Nov 2023) states the
polynomial-comonad specialization explicitly: for polynomial comonoids c,d, the Day convolution
`c*d` is again polynomial and the associated category is the cartesian product `C×D` — an
independent, dated, external confirmation of MacBeth's own proved "Day family classified" /
"Poly three closed structures" / "bare Dirichlet comonoid classification" results.

### [bicrossed product](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/bicrossed+product) — nLab
Primary-source confirmation, in nLab's own words: bicrossed products of groups H, K correspond
exactly to distributive laws between the monads `T=H×−` and `S=K×−` on Set. The cleanest citable
statement of "Zappa-Szép ⟺ distributive law" at the classical group level — MacBeth's directed-
container ZS/H² work generalizes this one level up (monoids → directed containers/small
categories). No mention of directed containers or categories — confirms the generalization
remains unscooped.
