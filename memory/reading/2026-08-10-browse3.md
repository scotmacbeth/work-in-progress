## Papers

### [Distributive Laws of Directed Containers [Extended Abstract]](https://danel.ahman.ee/papers/cmcs12.pdf) — Ahman, Uustalu, CMCS 2012 / *Progress in Informatics* 10 (2013)
Not on arXiv — hosted at Ahman's personal page, downloaded and read in full (3pp). **This is the
primary source for "composing directed containers = Zappa-Szép product."** Defines a distributive
law between two directed containers as the condition for the composite comonad to again be a
comonad; in the degenerate non-indexed case, the distributive-law equations are proved to be
exactly the knit-product/Zappa-Szép conditions for two monoids, citing Brin, "On the Zappa-Szép
product," Comm. Algebra 33(2) (2005), Lemma 3.13(xv) directly. **Citation gap**: MacBeth's book/
grant cites Rosebrugh-Wood (JPAA 175, 2002) for the general (L)∧(G)⟺SFS-exists ZS criterion but
never this paper — the one where Ahman-Uustalu themselves make the ZS connection in the directed-
container setting. No H²/cohomological language in the 3pp abstract (that framing is MacBeth's own
extension). **Flag for Robin/Neil**: add as a citation alongside Rosebrugh-Wood/Brin in the book's
ZS-product section.

### [Ionads](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1415) — Garner, J. Pure Appl. Algebra 216 (2012)
Abstract + nLab page (https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/ionad) read, not the full JPAA paper. Located
as the actual primary source Kevin Carlson invokes on MO 457580 to generalize DCont≅Cat topos-
theoretically. An ionad = a set X with a finite-limit-preserving comonad on Set^X; *bounded*
ionads (accessible comonad) correspond to Grothendieck toposes with enough points as their
coalgebra category — places Ahman-Uustalu's theorem (comonads on Set ↔ small categories) one
dimension up (comonads on Set^X ↔ toposes). nLab confirms zero overlap with directed-container/
polynomial-comonad vocabulary — independent generalization axis, not prior art. Closes the citation
gap flagged in today's dream journal (`connections/dcont-cat-is-the-convergence-hub.md` point 3).
