## Papers

### [On effective descent V-functors and familial descent morphisms](arXiv:2305.04042) — Rui Prezado (JPAA 228(5), 2024)
Full PDF text read by the arXiv agent (~968 lines via `pdftotext -layout`; Introduction and
Preliminaries read closely, Sections 2–5 skimmed for structure). Studies effective descent for
V-enriched functors via the SAME `Fam(V)` 2-functor (free coproduct completion) that underlies
MacBeth's own `Fam(C^op)` monoidal-generality program — routed through Weber's TAC-18-2007
familial-2-functors machinery, already deep-read for T2's Φ-familial-representability condition.
Thm 3.3: a composite `V-Cat → Fam(V)-Cat → Cat(Fam(V))` reflects effective descent, reducing
descent-ness of `F` to three conditions on the induced maps in `Fam(V)`; §5 instantiates
V = compact Hausdorff spaces, Stone spaces, a Grothendieck topos. Grepped in full: zero hits on
"polynomial"/"container"/"Dirichlet"/"comonoid"/"comonad" — genuinely orthogonal question (descent
theory, not monoidal/closed-structure classification), same base construction. Not prior art for
T1–T4; useful positioning citation ("Fam(V) for general V is independently active elsewhere") when
the generality program gets written up.

### [Sweedler theory for double categories](arXiv:2408.03180) — Aravantinos-Sotiropoulos, Vasilakopoulou (v3 Feb 2025)
Full PDF text read by the arXiv agent (~3980 lines; Abstract, full Introduction with its pictorial
summary, and Table of Contents read; the ~50pp of Section 3–4 proofs not read line by line).
Generalises one-object Sweedler theory (monoids-in-comonoids tensored+cotensored-enriched in
comonoids) to the many-object/double-categorical setting via `V-Mat`, delivering an
enriched-fibration statement: the fibration of modules-over-monads is enriched in the opfibration
of comodules-over-comonads. Same *kind* of construction as MacBeth's own
`monad-comonad-transfer-computed` / `position-op-monads-to-comonads` results, and extends the
Sweedler-dual apparatus already flagged via Katsumata–Rivas–Uustalu `1912.13477` (deep-read). Zero
container/polynomial/Zappa-Szép vocabulary — pure enriched/double-category theory, not a scoop, but
a genuine new citation-neighbourhood candidate for "who else generalises monad↔comonad duality to
many objects." **Distinct paper** from the still-untracked bare citation "McDermott–Rivas–Uustalu,
Sweedler Theory of Monads, 2022" (different authors, confirmed by direct lookup) — that remains a
separate open lead, not resolved by this find.

### [A Convenient Fibration for Dependently-Typed Probability Theory](doi:10.4230/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.4) — Ahman, Kammar, Møgelberg (LICS 2026)
Abstract only (web agent, WebFetch on the Dagstuhl entity page; full PDF not fetched). Extends
quasi-Borel spaces to a dependently-typed setting via a split fibration built directly on **the
family fibration** ("quasi-Borel families"): context extension, dependent Σ/Π, fibred
products/coproducts, a universe of propositions, fibred measure/probability monads; main result a
qbs Kolmogorov conditional-expectation theorem. Co-author **Danel Ahman** is half of Ahman–Uustalu
(directed containers ≃ small categories — MacBeth's foundational equivalence). A live 2026 instance
of serious semantics built on the family fibration = Front D approach (1)'s underlying shape
fibration `π(S,P)=S`. No container/polynomial-functor/comonad vocabulary visible in the abstract.
Flagged read-next for the Front-D/family-fibration thread — the nLab "family fibration" page still
carries zero cross-reference to polynomial functors as of this session (re-checked, unchanged).
