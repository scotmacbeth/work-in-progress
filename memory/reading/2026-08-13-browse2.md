### [Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17191) — Purdy, Damato, CALCO 2025, arXiv:2503.17191 — ALREADY TRACKED since 2026-06-11
Characterises distributive laws between *monadic* containers and Ahman–Uustalu's *directed*
(comonadic) containers — "completing the zoo" of container/(co)monad distributive-law
characterisations. Def 17 gives the monadic-container distributive law as four maps mirroring the
monad laws; Example 22 constructs Zappa–Szép products of monoids explicitly via matching-pair
actions; Theorem 35 ("too many constants") shows some monad pairs admit *no* distributive law at
all. Most strikingly, §6 finds mixed monadic-directed distributive laws yield "functional monoid
actions" rather than classical matching pairs — a genuinely different generalisation route than the
pure-directed ZS product MacBeth proved for update-monad composition. Cubical-Agda formalised.
**Independently surfaced by both the arXiv agent and the citation-trail agent** (as a reverse
citation of Ahman–Uustalu 1604.01187) — strong cross-confirmation this is the right next read.
Direct technical neighbour of `holonomy-composition-zs-bridge-proved` / `orchestration-is-zappa-szep-weld`.
Depth: full text (arxiv.org/html).

### [Interactions that reshape the interfaces of the interacting parties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917) — Spivak, Feb 2026, arXiv:2602.17917 — ALREADY TRACKED since 2026-06-09/10
This is what SEED.md lists as "Spivak, Coinductive Polynomial Trees, 2026" with no arXiv ID —
confirmed by matching content (a "polynomial tree" = element of the terminal `(u◁u)`-coalgebra,
exactly the seed's description) under a different public title; this ID resolution was **already
made on 2026-06-10**, re-confirmed independently today, not a new resolution. Sequel
to Pattern Runs on Matter; builds category `PolyTr_𝕌` of polynomial trees (interfaces that
co-evolve with interaction, e.g. a differentiating cell gaining/losing receptors), shows it
symmetric distributive monoidal and monoidal closed, and a bicategory `OrgTr` generalising `Org` to
let wiring *and* interfaces co-evolve. Built directly on the cofree comonad adjunction — MacBeth's
own Ch7 territory, extended to dynamic interfaces. **Cites Zwart–Marsden's "No-Go Theorems for
Distributive Laws" (2019) directly** — places it inside the same obstruction cluster as MacBeth's
`[ω]∈H²` result (see Connections below). Depth: full text (arxiv.org/html), independently confirmed
by the citation-trail agent.

### [Monoid Structures on Indexed Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25879) — De Pascalis, Uustalu, Veltrì, EPTCS 2025 (LSFA 2025), arXiv:2509.25879 — ALREADY TRACKED since 2026-06-13
Puts a monoidal category structure on I-indexed containers and gives a combinatorial
characterisation of monoids in it, proving these correspond exactly to monads on the induced
`Set^I` endofunctors. Worked examples: products of two monads, indexed state/writer monads, a
free-monad instance. Cubical-Agda formalised. Directly parallel to MacBeth's own `T^Σ_M = M◁−`
classification (Σ-monad iff ◁-monoid) but one level up, in the indexed/dependent setting — open
question whether their combinatorial characterisation specialises to or generalises MacBeth's
non-indexed result. Candidate comparison target for a future session. Depth: full text.

### [Biological Motifs for Agentic Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04240) — Banu, 2026, arXiv:2607.04240 — ALREADY TRACKED since 2026-07-20 (read in full)
Single-author, non-institutionally-affiliated preprint mapping five GRN biological motifs to
agentic-software design patterns, using Poly as the shared interface language for genes/promoters
and agent capabilities. Promoters modelled as lenses, epigenetic state as a coalgebra with
bisimulation; defines an "Agentic Operad" with error-suppression bounds. **Caveat: theorems stated
without visible complete proofs, validated by a synthetic test harness rather than proof** — treat
as a source of cross-domain examples/correspondences for the grant's agent-orchestration
application thread, not a citable theorem. Depth: sections fetched, not read end-to-end.

## Community

### [Distributive Laws (n-Category Café, 2017)](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2017/02/distributive_laws.html)
Guest post on Beck's 1969 distributive-law paper with a substantial comment thread (Riehl,
Leinster, Campbell, D.J. Myers, Staton). Explicitly frames distributive laws via **Zappa–Szép
products of groups** as a first-class example (not an aside); Myers extends with
semidirect/direct-product, group-ring, and powerset-over-monoid examples. This is the abstract
skeleton behind MacBeth's just-closed `holonomy-composition-zs-bridge-proved` result, at the plain
group level. Companion reference: [bicrossed product (nLab)](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/bicrossed+product)
states the equivalence "bicrossed product of `H,K` ⟺ distributive law between monads `H×−`, `K×−`"
as citable folklore (Kassel, Brin math/0406044) — a textbook-level anchor beyond Rosebrugh–Wood.

## Connections

**The headline finding: MacBeth's `[ω]∈H²` obstruction sits inside an actively-converging
distributive-law/no-go research cluster, independently spotted by two agents.** Timeline:
Zwart–Marsden "No-Go Theorems for Distributive Laws" (2019, LICS/LMCS, general monad⊗monad
machinery) → Karamlou–Shah "No go theorems: directed containers ⊗ distribution monads" (LICS 2024,
already known, specialises to directed containers) → Purdy–Damato "Distributive Laws of Monadic
Containers" (CALCO 2025, extends AU16 to monadic containers + mixed cases, Cubical-Agda) → Spivak
"Categories by Kan extension" (2025, distributive laws of monads over comonads) → Spivak
"Interactions that reshape..." (2026, cites Zwart–Marsden directly). All are versions of one
question in different dialects: **when does composing two (co)monadic container/polynomial
structures stay well-behaved, and what classifies the failure?** MacBeth's ZS-product +
`[ω]∈H²(B;A)` result (composing update monads, stabilizer extension) is a specific instance.
Purdy–Damato is the closest technical neighbour — Agda-formalised like MacBeth's Lean work, direct
extension of the very AU16 paper MacBeth's classification sits on.

**Secondary connection — the group-theoretic skeleton of MacBeth's own result is textbook folklore.**
The n-Category Café 2017 thread + nLab bicrossed-product page confirm "distributive law ⟺ ZS/bicrossed
product of monads `H×−`,`K×−`" is standard citable material — a clean textbook anchor for the ZS
weld's group-level base case, independent of the container-level generalisation MacBeth is proving.

**Tertiary connection — a live Strathclyde thread on compositional agency.** The categorical-
cybernetics program (Capucci/Gavranović/Hedges/Rischel, Para+Optics for controller/environment
splitting) comes out of Neil Ghani's own department and reaches for the same "compose agents,
what breaks" question as MacBeth's ZS-holonomy result, informally (Capucci's blog names the
phenomenon "non-compositional effects from long-distance correlations"). Worth a citation and
possibly an actual conversation, not just a paper reference.
