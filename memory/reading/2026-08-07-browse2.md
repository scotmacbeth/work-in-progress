### [A Category-Theoretic Framework for Dependent Effect Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14846) — Kura, Gaboardi, Sekiyama, Unno, arXiv:2601.14846 (Jan 2026)
Already tracked (agent-summary, since 07-31). Today's read added real detail: builds
**indexed graded monads** and, for the "dependent effects" instance, a **predicate
fibration refinement** (§4.3, citing Kura 2021) — a monad-lifted-along-a-fibration-morphism
construction structurally the same *move* as the □/Sub(Set) side of MacBeth's just-proved
proof-relevance boundary theorem. No Beck-style distributive-law content; near-neighbour to
the propositional/□ side specifically, not the proof-relevant T_M/Σ side. Coherence proofs
deferred to appendices, not verified in the extracted text. sources.json note updated.

### [λ_A: A Typed Lambda Calculus for LLM Agent Composition](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.11767) — Qin Liu, arXiv:2604.11767 (Apr 2026)
Genuine formal semantics for agent composition — Coq-mechanized (1,519 lines, 42
theorems) typed lambda calculus with oracle calls, bounded fixpoints, mutable
environments; shows 94.1% of 835 real GitHub agent configs are "structurally incomplete"
under the calculus; embeds LangGraph/CrewAI/AutoGen as typed fragments. **Operational
semantics, not categorical** — no monads, no Zappa-Szép-style product, no monoidal
category of agents. Citable as "formal semantics of agent composition is now active in
2026," does not compete with the ZS-weld orchestration framing. New to sources.json.

### [Harness Engineering as Categorical Architecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.12239) — Bogdan Banu, arXiv:2605.12239 (May 2026) — already tracked, re-confirmed not rigorous

### Vollmer–Paviotti–Orchard "On the Category of Graded Monads" — full CT2026 abstract recovered again, still unpublished
`curl` + `pdftotext` on the CT2026 program PDF (WebFetch's converter fails on it) gives
full text: `Gmd : MonCat × 2Cat → 2Cat`, `Gmd(I,κ) = [BI^op, κ]_lax`, itself a graded monad
on 2Cat; dual `Gcmd` for graded comonads; free graded monad = left Kan extension of the
free-monoid-monad unit; category of graded monads = 2-Grothendieck construction of
`Gmd(−,κ)`. **Checked Orchard's own publications page directly — no 2026 graded-monads
posting, arXiv or otherwise.** Standing monthly watch, unchanged. The `Gmd(−,κ)`
2-Grothendieck-construction move for reindexing is structurally close to how MacBeth's
T_M/G_M framing and the Workers (Set,×)-graded category handle reindexing — worth a
proper read once it's actually posted.

### "Powers of polynomial monads" — Topos Institute blog, 2023-09-21 (previously untracked)
`t^p` (exponentiation in Poly for monad-valued base) is a monad for any polynomial p, and
for a small category c, `t^c` is both a monad and a ×-monoid ("tactics for navigating
category c"). Confirms polynomial comonoids = small categories again, publicly. **Does
NOT touch Σ-container/coproduct-type container monads** — the open Σ-monad-coherence gap
(unit=fold, mult=reverse-total-section reindex; node `sigma-monad-coherence-open`)
remains unaddressed anywhere in Topos's public output. Genuinely open, genuinely
MacBeth's.

### Wreath product (Lack–Street, "Formal Theory of Monads II") — clarifying distinction, not a scoop
Confirms: "every distributive law is a wreath," wreaths generalize DL-composition-of-
monads at the 2-categorical level. **No mention of Zappa-Szép anywhere in the wreath
pages.** Useful negative result: the Lack–Street wreath lineage (2-categorical,
monad-composition) and the Zappa-Szép/group-factorization literature (Rosebrugh-Wood,
Aguiar-Andruskiewitsch — monoid-internal/factorization) are two distinct generalizations
of "distributive law" that don't currently cross-reference each other. Worth an explicit
disambiguating remark in the book/grant when both vocabularies are in play.
