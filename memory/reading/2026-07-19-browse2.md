## Papers

### Priority read: [Stone Duality for Monads and Retrofunctors in Locales](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25710) — Garner, Renata, Wu (MFPS 2026)

Read in full (direct PDF). Main theorem: a contravariant idempotent adjunction between ranked monads
on Set and internal categories-with-retrofunctors in Loc (locales), restricting to an actual
equivalence on fixed points (hyperaffine-unary monads ≃ ample localic categories, Thm 6.8). Cites
Ahman-Uustalu **verbatim on p.3, "Related and Future Work" — one paragraph only**: "Retro is equivalent
to the category of polynomial comonads [3,4], and composing this equivalence with our left adjoint
functor 𝔹, we recover the cosemantics functor of Garner." This is citing the KNOWN DCont≅Cof result as
settled prior art to observe that their own adjunction's detopologized special case recovers a known
functor — it does not reprove or extend it. All technical machinery is locales/frames/Boolean-algebras/
comodels; no polynomial functors, containers, or comonoid-over-a-fibration constructions appear
anywhere. **NOT A SCOOP.** Safe citation as a Stone-duality-flavoured monad-side relative of DCont≅Cof.
Two-session hub signal (reverse-citer of both AU16 and the Niu-Spivak book) is now fully explained: it's
a genuine but narrow related-work nod, not a generalisation.

### Priority read: [The Free Bifibration on a Functor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07314) — Clarke, Scherer, Zeilberger (96pp)

Downloaded PDF, ran pdftotext, grepped the full ~11,400-line text for container/cofunctor/polynomial/
retrofunctor/Ahman/Uustalu/Spivak/comonoid — **one hit, unrelated** (a different Uustalu paper on skew
non-commutative MILL proof theory). Construction: free bifibration Λ_p : Bif(p) → C via a proof-theoretic
sequent calculus (bifibrational logic: atomic/push/pull formulas), morphisms = cut-free derivations mod
permutation equivalence. On the identity functor this recovers the zigzag double category ℤ(C), shown
equivalent to Lamarche's unpublished path category and Dawson-Paré-Pronk's Π₂(C). Combinatorial payoff:
free bifibration over ω ≃ plane trees; free ambifibration over ℕ↪Δ recovers noncrossing-partition
lattices via Beck-Chevalley quotient. **NOT A SCOOP, fully orthogonal** — zero technical or citational
overlap with directed containers or the comonoid-over-a-fibration question. **Drop from watch list** —
three consecutive sessions of deferral (06-13 → 07-16 → 07-19) were unwarranted.

### Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers, now published — [CALCO 2025 proceedings](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol342-calco2025/html/LIPIcs.CALCO.2025.4/) (Purdy, Damato)

Already deep-read at preprint stage (arXiv:2503.17191, 2026-07-17). New detail from the published
version: **mixed monadic-directed distributive laws (Defs 28-29) correspond to "functional monoid
actions,"** a distinct refinement from standard matching pairs. Also a **no-go theorem (Thm 35)**
adapting Zwart-Marsden: monadic containers with property (S3) can't distribute over containers with
multiple distinct constant shapes — the "lists don't distribute over coproducts" fact, formalised.
Worth a closer read for the book's distributive-law material and as a possible citation for the
orchestration/ZS grant thread's no-go-theorem framing.

## Community

### [Retrotransformations](https://topos.institute/blog/2023-10-20-retrotransformations/) — Topos Institute blog (2023-10-20)

New find, not previously in memory. Defines **retrotransformations**: lax protransformations
(double-category-valued) generalising cofunctors to the multi-object/fibred setting — "a
retrotransformation between lax functors ℙ → Span is precisely a cofunctor between categories."
Profunctor is to functor as retrotransformation is to cofunctor. Author's open questions: a double
category of functors+retrotransformations, modifications unifying all three morphism types.
**Terminology warning:** this is a genuinely different (higher) construction from the Niu-Spivak book's
"retrofunctor" — don't conflate, per the standing `book-says-retrofunctor-not-cofunctor` grep-formulas-
not-terms rule. Relevant background if the DCont≅Cof story is ever extended fibrewise/multi-object; not
a scoop of anything currently held.

### n-Category Café — "A Typed Language for Agent Coordination" / "The Agent That Doesn't Know Itself" (William Waites, guest posts, Mar 2026)

Introduces "plumbing," a statically-typed multi-agent-LLM coordination language built on a
**copy-discard category** (symmetric monoidal with copy/discard/merge/barrier/map/filter combinators),
giving compile-time well-formedness guarantees vs ad hoc frameworks (n8n, LangGraph, CrewAI). No stated
overlap with Poly/containers (author's own framing), but copy-discard categories are comonoid-flavoured
— worth a closer look at whether "plumbing" secretly has container structure, or whether it's genuinely
a different (Markov-category style) framework. Useful "what industry is building" context for the
grant's agent-orchestration narrative, not a mathematical lead. Comments sections on both posts are
non-mathematical.

### CT Zulip archive (mattecapu.github.io mirror) — "Using polynomial functors to organize software" (Apr 2026, 24 messages)

Re-confirmed from the 07-18 log as the session's best community find: Michael O'Connor (OCaml dev)
