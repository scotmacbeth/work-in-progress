## Papers

### [Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17191) — Purdy, Damato, arXiv:2503.17191, CALCO 2025
**The Purdy-Damato lead RESOLVED — but see Connections: it resolves to an already-known paper, not a new
one.** §6 constructs *mixed* distributive laws (directed-over-monadic containers, monadic-over-directed) —
the monad-side dual of Ahman-Uustalu, completing the "zoo" of container (co)monad distributive-law
characterisations. Formalised in Cubical Agda, explicitly fighting "transport hell" — a methodological
sibling to MacBeth's own Lean dependent-cast pain (`dcont-laws-need-dependent-casts`,
`lean-dirichlet-comonoid-forward`). Full HTML read, cross-checked against the abstract page. Already in
sources.json since 2026-06-11/07-17 as "Purdy-Damato ZS mirror" — this session just re-confirms it and
resolves it as *the* answer to the dream journal's "chase the arXiv ID" TODO, closing that thread.

### [Multi-Agent System Identification with Nonlinear Sheaf Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11204) — Anwer, Riess, Hale, arXiv:2605.11204, May 2026
Already in sources.json at deep-read (2026-07-15). Re-surfaced independently this session by both the
arXiv and community agents, both flagging it as the strongest member of the cohomology/multi-agent
cluster — a real theorem (recovery of interaction laws ⟺ H¹(G;𝓕)=0), not just an analogy. Reconfirms the
prior verdict: structural sibling to `g-obstruction-is-h2-class`, not a scoop (different degree — H¹ vs
H² — different site — graph vs Sk_C).

### [What kind of linearly distributive category do polynomial functors form?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01849) — Spivak, Srinivasan, arXiv:2407.01849, Jul 2024
Re-checked for a 2025/2026 revision — none found. Confirms (Poly,⊗,◁) is a bi-closed isomix LDC (closed
for ⊗, co-closed for ◁) — **does not touch the ⋉/⋊ Dialectica tensors at all**. This is the paper behind
`closed-structures-are-spivaks`; consistent with the standing verdict that ⋊-left-closedness sits outside
existing Day/LDC machinery and remains genuinely open.

### [Compositional Dynamics in Learning and Mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28984) — Spivak, submitted 27 Jun 2026 — **genuinely new**
New operad "Arr" (smooth adaptive arrangements) with two functors into the 2-category of polynomial
coalgebras, unifying gradient-based learning and Hamiltonian mechanics as functorial semantics; systems
"wired from smaller ones... assemble functorially." Abstract-level read only (community agent). Frontier
relevance to the grant's agent-orchestration pillar — another Spivak paper building compositional
dynamics directly on Poly coalgebras, adjacent to OrgTr (2602.17917). Not yet checked for overlap with
DCont≅Cof or the free-monad work — flag for a future deep-read before citing.

### [Lenses for Composable Servers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15633) — Videla, Capucci, 2022 — **title correction**
This is NOT "containers as RESTful APIs" as previously (mis)recorded — see Connections. Implements HTTP
server semantics via (dependent) parameterised lenses; lens laws mimic HTTP properties. Still a usable
cheap grant-narrative citation for "APIs as compositional structures" (close kin to directed containers /
delta lenses), but must be cited as a lens paper, not a container paper.

### [Distributive Laws](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2017/02/distributive_laws.html) — n-Category Café, Feb 2017
Revisited. David Jaz Myers' 2017 comment thread works out ZS products of groups/monoids as the
distributive-law special case, and raises two still-open questions: (1) are modules of the
powerset-monad ⊗ monoid-monad composite genuinely quantales (unchecked); (2) a characterisation of all
categories of algebras embedding into Mnd (open). Ze's comment connects distributive laws to strict
factorization systems on small-categories-as-monads-in-Span — underdeveloped in-thread. Direct ancestor
of the ZS/directed-container line MacBeth already works; the "categories of algebras embedding into Mnd"
question is worth a look if the ZS-at-category-level programme ever needs a categorical (not
monoid-level) universal property.

### [Dialectica and Poly](https://mattecapu.github.io/ct-zulip-archive/stream/323208-deprecated.3A-Dialectica/topic/Dialectica.20and.20Poly.html) — CT Zulip archive, ~Mar–Jun 2022 — **new find**
Valeria de Paiva, in-thread, raises a closure concern for the dependent Dialectica construction: "I don't

specific technical worry worth checking against MacBeth's own non-closure proof

activity in this stream; superseded by the published Spivak-Srinivasan LDC paper.

## Connections

**★ Caught and fixed a real citation-index error today.** `sources.json`'s entry for arXiv:1604.01187 had
the **wrong title** — labelled "When Is a Container a Comonad?" when the paper at that ID is actually

page). The two papers are genuinely distinct: "When Is a Container a Comonad?" (Ahman, Chapman, Uustalu,
LMCS 10(3), 2014) is the *earlier* paper stating the D1-D5 directed-container laws; "Directed Containers
as Categories" (2016) is the *sequel* establishing directed-container ≅ small-category and the
cofunctor-morphism structure. The locators already on file (Sec 3.2/3.3, Def 3.1 "relative split
pre-opcleavage") were always correctly about the 2016 paper — only the title label was swapped, so no
proof or claim built on those locators is affected. Found the correct arXiv ID for the 2014 paper
(1408.5809) via WebSearch and added it as its own sources.json entry (abstract-level extraction — its
actual D1-D5 content has been absorbed second-hand via SEED.md's summary, not yet primary-read at this
ID; flagged as a TODO). Also corrected arXiv:2203.15633's title/author (was a wrong guess "Containers as
RESTful APIs" / "Knispel"; actually "Lenses for Composable Servers," Videla & Capucci). **The lesson,
consistent with `the-summary-is-what-gets-audited`: index metadata rots exactly like proof summaries do
— a citation ID with the wrong title attached is a landmine for the next session that trusts it without
re-checking.**
