## Papers

### Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers — Purdy, Damato (arXiv:2503.17191, CALCO 2025)
Re-surfaced independently by THREE of the four agents (arXiv, community, and implicitly web)
as directly relevant to the parked Zappa-Szép front. **Already tracked at `deep-read` in
sources.json, read across 7 prior sessions** (2026-06-09 through 2026-08-21-browse2) —
confirmed via direct grep before writing this log, so NOT logged as new. Content recap for
convenience: extends Ahman-Uustalu's directed-container (comonadic) distributive laws to
the monadic side; "monadic container distributive law" = matched tuple `(u₁,u₂,v₁,v₂)`;
proves writer-monadic distributive laws correspond to matching pairs / Zappa-Szép products,
plus a "too many constants" no-go theorem; a Cubical-Agda-formalized correspondence table
distinguishes writer-over-writer / directed-over-monadic (both = matching pairs of monoid
actions, Ahman-Uustalu) from monadic-over-directed ("functional monoid actions" — a new
construction whose relation to the others the authors themselves call unclear). Connection:
sits exactly where MacBeth's `[ω]∈H²` obstruction-class work would need to check for overlap
if the ZS front reopens; not yet compared against MacBeth's own [ω] result.

### Polynomial Functors over Infinity-Groupoids — Kun Chen (arXiv:2601.22968, Jan 2026)
**Already tracked at `deep-read`** (5 prior reads, 2026-06-11 through 2026-07-19) — confirmed
via grep, not new. Re-surfaced by the arXiv agent as a live "generalize the base of Poly"
effort, but along the homotopical (∞-groupoid) axis rather than MacBeth's enrichment
(Fam(C^op)/Vec) axis — single-variable polynomial functors over ∞-groupoids as colimits of
