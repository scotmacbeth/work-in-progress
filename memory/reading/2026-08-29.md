## Community

### [Closure of polynomial monads under colimits](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/266335) — NEW to tracking
Open since 2017, 0 answers. Asks whether polynomial monads on an LCCC are
closed under the algebraic colimits used to freely generate monads with
prescribed algebra categories. Relevant background to the free-monad-as-◁-monoid
work (`free-monad-grafting-laws-done`, `oneill-free-monad-tensor-algebra-linear-container`).

### [Are the string diagrams for the Frobenius Algebra an example of a Polynomial Functor?](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/298988) — NEW to tracking
Open since 2018, 0 answers. Asks whether Frobenius-algebra string diagrams
(trees with symmetric crossings) model as a polynomial monad, generalising
Kock's tree-polynomial correspondence. Speculative link to the Dirichlet
comonoid-classification work (`vec-comonoid-algebra-family-proved`).

### [List is a monad, but is it a comonad with these natural transformations?](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/237692) — NEW to tracking
Answered 2016 (score 22, still sole answer). Concrete worked construction of
`L⁺(X) = ⨿_{n≥1} Xⁿ` carrying both monad and comonad structure — the standard
non-empty-list directed-container example spelled out explicitly, with a Todd
Trimble comment on a subtlety in which such structures are possible. Useful
citable computation for any future writeup of the TG/GT entwining result.

### [What is the monoidal equivalent of a locally cartesian closed category?](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/205902) — follow-up activity, existing entry updated
Already logged (Walker LSCC answer, 2026-07-28). New: a 2026-07-29 exchange
between Tim Campion and user varkor about whether ωCat with the Gray tensor
product is subcartesian/"locally closed" in Walker's sense for the
non-symmetric case — quiet since. Appended to the existing `mo:205902` entry
rather than duplicated.
