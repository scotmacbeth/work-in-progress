## Papers

### [2207.05702](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.05702) "Locally-finite extensive categories, their semi-rings, and decomposition to connected objects" — Fujino, Matsumoto (TAC 38(8), 2022)
Read in full via ar5iv. Locally-finite extensive category = finite Hom-sets + extensive. Builds
the semi-ring Dec(C) of iso-classes (coproduct=+, product=×) with counting maps h_D. **Thm 1.10**:
every object decomposes uniquely as a finite coproduct of connected objects, and conversely a
category with finite coproducts where every object so decomposes is extensive. Generalizes
Lovász's embedding of the finite-graph semiring. **States the distributivity axiom but never
analyzes whether products of connected objects stay connected** — no overlap with MacBeth's
π₀-multiplicativity work; complementary formalization of the *other* direction (unique
decomposition), not competing.

### [1112.4277-followup citation trail] Xarez 2025, "Very-Well-Behaved Epireflections for Categories of Models of Sketches" (arXiv:2509.07241)
Xarez's newest paper, continuing the stable-units/admissibility line from 1112.4277 (2011).
Extends to monads, prefactorization systems, pseudo-filtered index categories, and sketches;
exhibits the known reflection of n-categories into n-preorders as an instance. Abstract-level
only. Relevant if MacBeth's admissibility circle of ideas ever touches sketch/presheaf
reflections — flagged, not urgent.

### arXiv:2303.12549 (Trlifaj, "Flat Mittag-Leffler modules...") and math/9309204 (Brendle, "Evasion and prediction — the Specker phenomenon and Gross spaces")
Neither directly answers the (Q) crux. Trlifaj's survey places flat Mittag-Leffler modules
(Eklof–Mekler "1-projective") as the correct infinite-dimensional replacement for "projective" —
**the recommended reframing for pushing Conj 6.2 forward**, rather than continuing to search for
a literal field-level Baer–Specker paper (which appears to exist only in Göbel–Trlifaj's book,
not on arXiv). Brendle's paper is a methodological cousin — same genre of ZFC-independence
argument for a Specker-type phenomenon, template not source.

### arXiv:2111.10968 "Functorial Aggregation" (Spivak, JPAA 2025) — reread through the Cat-case lens
Already known (existing sources.json entry), but reread today specifically against Neil's
"Cat case" ask: constructs a framed bicategory of categories, retrofunctors, and parametric right
adjoints — categories serve as positions/variables instead of sets, proving "polynomial comonads
amount to categories" and "polynomial bicomodules between categories amount to parametric right
adjoints" (data-migration functors). This is very likely the concrete source underlying
`book-says-retrofunctor-not-cofunctor.md`. **Direct raw material for the Cat-case sketch Neil
asked for** — reread recommended before drafting that future-work section.

## Community

### [Free Objects in Functor Categories](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/114040/) (MO, 2012)
`[C,Ab]` (additive functors from small abelian C to Ab) has a forgetful-monadic functor to
`[Ob C, Set]`; free objects = coproducts of representables; "free" is explicitly non-Morita-
invariant (Eric Wofsey). Right general machinery for framing "is F a retract of ⊕h_W" but nobody
addresses infinite-object C / continuum-sized index sets — MacBeth's specific question stays open.

### [Extensions of an infinite product of copies of Z by Z](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/40499/) + [Cohomology of the Baer-Specker group](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/372820/) (MO)
**Most load-bearing community find.** Nunke, "Slender groups" (Bull. AMS 1961, Thm 5):
Ext¹(∏_ℕZ, Z) ≅ (⊕_{2^𝔠}Q) ⊕ (⊕_{2^𝔠}Q/Z) — enormous, non-canonical. Also Fuchs *Infinite Abelian
Groups* Vol II §99. This is the classical abelian-group analogue of exactly the obstruction
MacBeth is chasing (Ext¹(Q,F_fin) for the non-split 0→F_fin→F→Q→0). Strong outside corroboration
that obstruction groups of this shape tend to be large and set-theoretically delicate — supports
the "Conj 6.2 likely independent of ZFC" read (THM C, 2026-09-03 PROVE). No thread addresses the
field/Vec-level analogue directly — genuinely open territory, not prior art to check against.

### [Zero divisors in connected distributive categories](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/491968/) (MO, 2025)
Self-answered: in a connected distributive category, X×Y=0 does NOT imply X or Y is 0 (covering-
space counterexample: disjoint opens X,Y in a base A, X×Y=∅ despite ambient connectivity). Clean
citable corroboration of the Gl((−)²)/Weichsel finding that connectedness does not imply
π₀-multiplicativity. Motivated by an Artin–Mazur *Étale Homotopy* §9.1 claim — worth reading
directly if this thread resurfaces.

### [Internal categories in an endofunctor category](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/179798/) (MO, 2014/2020)
Confirms `Cat = Comon(Poly,∘,y)` was floated on MO in 2020 (citing Spivak 2005.01894 §2.5) —
community-known years before MacBeth's own more complete, Lean-verified proof. No priority
conflict. Raises a small unresolved side-question (does a compatible monad structure on the
comonoid-category correspond to a known enrichment?) — low priority.
