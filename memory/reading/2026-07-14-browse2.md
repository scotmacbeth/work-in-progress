## Papers

### [Structures on Categories of Polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00167) — Shapiro, Spivak, arXiv:2305.00167 (2023)
**★ Most important find of the session — potential gap-closer, unread in full.** Three of four
browse agents converged on this independently. Abstract states: **"◁-comonoids in `Poly_E` are
precisely the internal categories in `E` whose source morphism is exponentiable,"** generalizing
Ahman–Uustalu's directed-container/comonad theorem to *any* category `E` with finite limits —
this is exactly the shape of the open §10 question ("directed container = internal category,
fibrationally"), though phrased over a general base category with an exponentiability condition
rather than explicitly via von Glehn's `Cont(q) = ∫_B q^op` fibration construction. The paper also
builds `(Poly_E, ⊗, ◁)`, both cartesian and vertical morphisms, duoidality, closure of `⊗`,
coclosure of `◁`, and identifies internal copresheaves as coalgebras.
**Complication: this arXiv ID is already in memory, but only for a different result.**
`SUMMARY.md` §4 cites `2305.00167 Cor 5.12` as the foundational proof for `DCont ≅ Cof`. Nobody
extracted the comonoid/internal-category result from the same paper. This is not a fresh "read
poly before claiming" miss — it is a *worse* variant: the paper was already read once, at
`agent-summary` depth, for a narrow purpose, and the rest of its content was never mined.
**Action, not resolved here:** read `arxiv.org/html/2305.00167` in full before any claim about
the §10 gap being open, closed, or partially closed. Also check whether it cites von Glehn TAC 33
(2018) — none of today's agents could confirm this from the abstract page alone.
All reads today were abstract-level (WebFetch on `/abs`), not full HTML text.
