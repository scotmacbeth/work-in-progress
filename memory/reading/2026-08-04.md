## Papers

### [Closed Freyd- and κ-Categories](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?repid=rep1&type=pdf&doi=5167626d28f929326b2428ac2806ba7dcb11e889) — Power, Thielecke, ICALP 1999
Read in full via CiteSeerX mirror (Springer requires login; the old cs.bham.ac.uk PostScript
mirror is dead, domain repurposed). Gives four equivalent presentations of λc-models: strong monad
w/ Kleisli exponentials on cartesian C; **closed Freyd-category** J:C→K (J(A⊗−) has a right
adjoint); a monad on the indexed category s(C) with a closedness condition; **closed κ-category**
H:C^op→Cat — an indexed category where every object has a "generic map" apply:A→B in the fibre
over [A→B], with inc:s(C)→H having an indexed right adjoint. This is the primary source, surfaced
via nLab's Freyd-category bibliography, for the "closed indexed Freyd category" vocabulary flagged
as a paper-polish lead since 07-31 ([[branching-obstruction-is-atkeys-index]]). **Load-bearing
false-friend flag:** the letter κ here originates in Hasegawa's stack-passing κ-calculus
(push/apply/mkthunk, §3.2), historically UNRELATED to MacBeth's own compositor `κ:GT⇒TG` in the
effect–coeffect entwining work. Same symbol, different 1990s PL-theory lineage — must disambiguate
explicitly if the book uses both. Added to `sources.json` (deep-read).
