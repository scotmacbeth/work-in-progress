## Papers

### [From Semantics to Syntax: A Type Theory for Comprehension Categories](arxiv.org/abs/2503.10868) — Najmaei, van der Weide, Ahrens, North (2025, v2 Nov 2025)
Full-HTML read by the arXiv agent. A comprehension category = base `C`, Grothendieck fibration
`p:T→C`, functor `χ:T→C^→` preserving cartesian arrows over `cod` — deliberately dropping the
usual Jacobs full/split requirement (Def 2.6). Thm 3.10: every comprehension category models the
structural rules of a new explicit-substitution type theory ("CCTT"). Thm 4.13: comprehension
categories with functorial Π/Σ/Id-types model the extended rules. Examples come from algebraic
weak factorization systems via Eilenberg-Moore on the arrow category. **Connection:** exactly the
semantic substrate Gambino-Hyland/Kock-style dependent polynomial functors normally live in
(fibration of types + comprehension functor), approached from the AWFS/non-full/non-split side —
never mentions polynomial functors or cites Gambino-Hyland/Kock. Adjacent infrastructure for Front
D, not a scoop: a source of non-full/non-split comprehension categories, relevant if the survey
ever wants `Fam(C^op)`-style bases outside the strict Jacobs fibration mold.

### [Weber, "Polynomials in categories with pullbacks"](arXiv:1106.1983) — Weber, M. (2011)
Found via Walker 2607.10242's own reference list (citation-trail agent), independently flagged by
the web agent too. Not read beyond title/citation-context this session — 54 citations, a
foundational hub generalizing Gambino-Kock's dependent-polynomial-functor picture from LCCC down
to mere categories-with-pullbacks. **This is surprisingly exactly Front D's open question** ("what
survives the fibrational Σ-Π-Δ picture when the base isn't LCCC") and was absent from
`sources.json` until today. Top read-next, alongside its companion Street-Verity "The
comprehensive factorization and torsors" (2010, TAC, no arXiv ID) — also new to tracking, the
classical bridge between fibrations and the comprehension-scheme/Grothendieck-construction story.

### Four more Front-D-adjacent references from Walker's bibliography (title/citation-context only)
Arkor & Fiore, "Algebraic models of simple type theories: A polynomial approach" (2006.16949,
2020); Street, "Polynomials as spans" (1903.03890, 2019); Walker's own earlier "Universal
properties of bicategories of polynomials" (1806.10477, 2018); Curien, Garner & Hofmann,

## Connections

The session's four independent agents converged on the **same cluster** from three different
routes (arXiv keyword search, web search, and Walker's own bibliography) — Weber's
"Polynomials in categories with pullbacks," Street's "Polynomials as spans," Street-Verity's

weakening-of-LCCC construction from the same intellectual neighbourhood, which strengthens the

than three cleanly disjoint approaches. The MO 205902 answer is independent, non-MacBeth-sourced
