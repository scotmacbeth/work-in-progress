### [arXiv:2607.23228 — "Directed Homotopy, Sectional Invariants, and Functorial Databases"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23228) — Carcacía-Campos
**★ Priority close-read, loop closed.** Full HTML read (852KB → 110K chars stripped
text), every definition/proposition/bibliography entry cross-checked directly. Headline:
this is a **solo follow-up by the same author as arXiv:2508.00727** ("Baues-Wirsching
Cohomology and Švarc Genus in Small Categories", already tracked) — porting his own
LS-category toolkit into a covering-number setting for functorial databases, with the
cohomology machinery dropped entirely. Defines nested invariants `secat`/`dsecat`/
`csecat`/`Sg(P)` (Defs 6.1, 6.2, 6.9, 6.16) — all minimum-covering-number invariants;
`dsecat(P)=0 ⟺ global section exists` (Cor 6.5), same "iff" shape as MacBeth's
`[ω]=0 ⟺ ZS product exists` but **carrying no cohomological content of its own**
(grepped the full body: "cohomology" appears exactly once, only in a bibliography
title). Zero citation-graph overlap with Zappa-Szép/wreath/distributive-law/Ahman/
Uustalu/Ghani/directed-container/polynomial-functor literature anywhere. **Verdict:
genuinely orthogonal, not a competing characterization** — a covering-number invariant
that a cohomology class would be used to *lower-bound* (in the companion paper), not a
rival to `[ω]∈H²`. §7 has a nice structural neighbour result (Prop 7.1 database
decomposition into indecomposables; Thm 7.11 combinatorial global-section guarantee for
acyclic schemas) for the applications/DCont-database thread. Cite alongside 2508.00727
as "global-section-existence obstruction theory is a live area via covering numbers,
orthogonal to our H² criterion." Closes the "strongest new lead" flag from 07-29 wake-2
— safe neighbour, no action needed beyond the citation-index update (done).

### ★ [Uustalu, "Grading monads, comonads, distributive laws" (CLaP Scotland, 2016, slides)](https://conferences.inf.ed.ac.uk/clapscotland/uustalu.pdf)
Genuinely new find (via community-agent's broader search route; not on arXiv, not
previously indexed). At the **graded-monoid level**, proves two bijections: matched
pairs of actions ⟺ Zappa-Szép product structures, and graded distributive laws ⟺
compatible graded-monad structures for the ZS product. Structurally the same shape as
MacBeth's proved pairwise-ZS criterion but one level down and **with no
obstruction/cohomology content** — the bijection is unconditional at this grading. Good
citable precursor for the book's ZS chapter. Does NOT build a distributive law between a
graded monad and a graded comonad, so irrelevant to the effect–coeffect thread. Full
deep-read via `pdftotext -layout` (WebFetch failed on raw PDF bytes).

## Web Research

### [nLab: premonoidal category](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/premonoidal+category)
**★ Possibly the categorical shadow of the branching obstruction.** For a strong monad
`T`, the Kleisli category `C_T` is automatically premonoidal, and collapses to fully
monoidal only when `T` is **commutative**. Flagged for direct comparison: does "M
non-branching" (MacBeth's condition for the biKleisli arrow category to exist) coincide
with, or merely resemble, "T_M commutative"? If they coincide, this is a ready-made
categorical name for the obstruction and a shortcut into the Freyd-category proof.

### [nLab: Freyd category](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/Freyd+category)
New reference beyond Heunen–Jacobs: **Staton, "Freyd categories are Enriched Lawvere
Theories" (2014)** — recasts Freyd categories as algebraic theories with side-effecting
operations; possibly a cleaner route into the T1/T2/T3 PROVE targets than the
profunctor/Prof-bicategory framing. Also surfaces "Freyd multicategory" and "duploid" as
unchecked related concepts.

### [nLab: sectional category / Lusternik–Schnirelmann category](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/sectional+category)

opfibrations or database language anywhere** — arXiv:2607.23228's transplant (above) is
genuinely new territory, not a rediscovery of known folklore. Raises confidence that
today's close-read verdict (orthogonal, not a threat) stands on solid ground.

### Topos Institute blog — ["Poly-morphic effect handlers"](https://topos.institute/blog/2024-01-03-algebraic-effect-handlers/) and ["Algebras are promonads"](https://topos.institute/blog/2024-01-29-algebras-are-promonads/)

category would be new. Background citation target for the eventual arrows paper.
