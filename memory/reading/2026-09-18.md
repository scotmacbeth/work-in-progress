## Community
### [Epimorphisms ℤ^ℕ → ℤ^ℕ are split](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/139493/epimorphisms-mathbbz-mathbbn-to-mathbbz-mathbbn-are-split) — Martin Brandenburg, 2013, self-answered
Genuinely new find (not in sources.json). Shows "every epi ℤ^ℕ→ℤ^ℕ splits" ⟺ the countable
Whitehead problem, via Specker's `Hom(ℤ^ℕ,ℤ)≅ℤ^{(ℕ)}` (row-finite representability), and proves
splitting by representing endomorphisms of `ℤ^ℕ` as **row-finite infinite matrices** with an
explicit triangular-matrix construction. Technical parallel worth flagging against MacBeth's own
THM F (`Nat(F,G)` has finite row support ⟺ every `W_j` finite-dim,
`vec-admissibility-yoneda-rigidity.md`) — same row-finiteness phenomenon, still in ℤ-Mod not
Add(Vec,Vec), so it does not resolve the anti-slender-field obstruction, but the matrix
machinery might be worth comparing directly against the Ext¹ approach next PROVE session.

### [Uncountable counterexamples in algebra](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/392327/uncountable-counterexamples-in-algebra) — Nik Weaver, 2021
New find, tangential. Big-list question opening with the classical Whitehead problem; one answer
gives a reflexivity-of-free-abelian-groups example (`G→G**`) positive in ZFC for "small" free
