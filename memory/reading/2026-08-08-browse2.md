## Papers

### [Hofmann–Streicher Lifting of Fibred Categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09520) — Slattery & Sterling, LMCS 22(2), Jun 2026
Read in full for pp 1–10 of 35 (abstract, §1 intro, §2 technical overview, §3 existence of
oplax base change) via a cached-PDF Read-tool trick (WebFetch's own PDF-text extraction
garbled, but its auto-cached download decodes cleanly with the Read tool). Relativises
Hofmann–Streicher (1997) universe lifting: for *any* fibration `p:A↠B` (not just `p:A↠1`),
builds the lifted fibration category `Fib_B → Fib_A` as the **right pseudo-adjoint
`Δ_p^lax`** to postcomposition-with-`p`, via a 2-categorical adjoint-lifting theorem through
Emmenegger–Rosolini et al.'s presentation of `Fib_A` as pseudo-coalgebras for a lax-idempotent
pseudocomonad. Makes universe-lifting iterable (nested internal presheaf models).
**Verdict: orthogonal, not a scoop or a sharpening.** Zero mention of containers, polynomial
functors, Set-monads, or the Sub(Set)-vs-Cont codomain-fibration split in the 10 pages read;
no cartesian-ness gate analogous to "M cartesian ⟺ T_M a monad." This closes last session's
top read-next item — cleared as a background fibration-machinery reference for the book, not
load-bearing for the boundary theorem. Bumped sources.json to `deep-read` (partial).
