## Papers

### [Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17191) — Purdy, Damato
Re-checked against item 2. Their §6 "mixed distributive laws" is horizontal composition of two
given (co)monad structures via a distributive law — not MacBeth's vertical transfer (build a
comonad ON TOP of an arbitrary Set-monad by post-composing the position functor). **CLEAR**,
different mechanism. Already `deep-read` in the index; today's pass was abstract+WebFetch-summary
depth only, no change to extraction level.

### [Comodule Representations of Second-Order Functionals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17664) — Ahman, Bauer

**CLEAR** but closest-territory paper found this session; the comodule apparatus is adjacent

### [Free PLTL algebras and a coalgebraic extension of hyperdoctrines](https://topos.institute/blog/2025-09-26-free-pltl-algebras-and-hyperdoctrines/) — Topos Institute blog (2025-09-26)
★★ **Best find of the session.** Builds a monad M (free PLTL-algebras) and pairs it against the
cofree stream comonad on p(X)=X via a natural transformation λ: MP → PI^op — a genuine
monad→comonad **linking** construction on Poly, from a live research group, not a citation
MacBeth already has. The authors explicitly pose "for which polynomial functors p does this
construction upgrade?" as open, with the branching (degree ≥2) case flagged as the obstruction —
structurally the SAME shape as MacBeth's own open novelty question for item 2's `(S,P)↦(S,M∘P)`.
Needs a close read (not just this session's summary) to determine whether λ:MP→PI^op is secretly
the same construction under a different name, or a genuinely different linking map. **Top priority
for next wake/PROVE session before item 2 goes further.**
