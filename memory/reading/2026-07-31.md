## Papers

### [Monoidal categories graded by partial commutative monoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16375) — Earnshaw, Nester, Román (2026-03-17)
Full-text read. Defines PCM-graded monoidal categories (morphisms in grade-indexed hom-categories,
partial monoidal product defined only when grades are PCM-compatible). Effectful categories recover
as the 2-element-PCM case; **cartesian PCM-graded monoidal categories ≅ Freyd categories** (Thm 4.23).
Directly checked against MacBeth's forthcoming effects⊗coeffects paper: **zero mention of coeffects,
comonads, arrow/Hughes categories, containers, polynomial functors, or bialgebras** — effects-only,
one-sided. Orthogonal neighbour, not a scoop; cite as "the field's other current vocabulary for
organizing Freyd categories." No arity/commutativity/affineness classification either — doesn't
pre-empt the 2×2 independence table.

### [On the Centre of Strong Graded Monads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09780) — Breuvart, Long, Zamdzhiev (2026-02-10)
Generalizes the "centre" of a strong monad (maximal commutative sub-effect) to pomonoid-graded and
duoidally-graded strong monads. Near-neighbour to the Workers/(Set,×)-graded-category theme, but
classifies graded monads by *commutativity-via-centre*, not by arity/branching/affineness — does not
address or pre-empt MacBeth's independent 2×2 table. No container/polynomial-functor/arrow content.
New to tracker, cite as graded-monad-theory neighbour for the Workers thread.

## Community

### ["Burrito Monads, Arrow Kitchens, and Freyd Category Recipes"](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2025/08/burrito_monads_arrow_kitchens.html) — Komalan & Krenz, n-Category Café guest post (2025-08-28)
Expository walkthrough of Robert Atkey's *What is a Categorical Model of Arrows?* (MSFP 2008 / ENTCS
229, 2011) — fetched and read Atkey's primary source in full (18pp) rather than trusting the summary.
Atkey's result: Hughes Arrows are **strictly more general** than Freyd categories (Arrows admit a
second, possibly comonad-structured input; Freyd categories only have one) — folklore equivalence is
false, resolved via **indexed Freyd categories**: Arrows ≅ closed indexed Freyd categories. Section 2
gives the exact **BiKleisli Arrow**: comonad `W`, monad `T`, distributive law `λ:WT→TW` ⟹
`Ar(x,y)=[Wx→Ty]` is an Arrow, citing Uustalu–Vene. **Cross-checked against MacBeth's own
`effect-coeffect-arrows` proofs: this is exactly the `Arr_M`(`Gp→Tq`) construction, and both Atkey
ENTCS 229 (2011) and Uustalu–Vene are already correctly cited there by name.** Zero scoop risk —
independent verification that the existing attribution is accurate. New value: signals active current
interest (Adjoint School 2025) in this exact territory, extending toward quantum-computing effects via
relative monads — a different, non-overlapping application. Possible missing vocabulary for MacBeth's
book/paper: "closed indexed Freyd category" as the precise categorical home of the branching-obstructed
arrow construction — worth checking whether stating the non-branching theorem in that language sharpens
it (Atkey's Fig. 1 diagram of the Freyd/indexed-Freyd/Arrow/monad landscape could serve as a pedagogical
reference).

### ["On the Category of Graded Monads"](https://ct2026.com/assets/pdf/ct2026-program.pdf) — Vollmer, Paviotti, Orchard, CT2026 contributed talk
Abstract only (not yet on arXiv). Builds `Gmd: MonCat × 2Cat → 2Cat`, `Gmd(I,κ)=[BI^op,κ]_lax`, itself
a graded monad on 2Cat — so distributive laws between graded monads/comonads become instances of a
general 2-categorical machine, contrasted explicitly with Gaboardi et al.'s graded distributive laws
(ICFP 2016) and framed "in the style of Power–Watanabe." Sits squarely on the Workers/graded-category
thread (`workers-graded-category-proved`, `effect-coeffect-arrows-*`) — potentially a general answer
to the κ:GT⇒TG question MacBeth has been computing by hand instance-by-instance. **Follow up once
posted to arXiv** — full-text pass owed.
