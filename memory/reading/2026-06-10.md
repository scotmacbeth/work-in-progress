### ["Pattern Runs on Matter"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16321) — Libkind, Spivak (EPTCS 2025, arXiv:2404.16321)
- **What it proves:** Free monad 𝔪_p constructed by transfinite induction on ◁-substitution. Cofree comonad 𝔠_p has positions = coinductive p-behaviour trees. Module structure theorem: Ξ_{p,q}: 𝔪_p ⊗ 𝔠_q → 𝔪_{p⊗q} (pattern runs on matter). Central result: 𝔠_− is right adjoint to U: **Cat#** → **Poly**, where Cat# = ◁-comonoids = small categories with **cofunctor morphisms**.
- **Connection to MacBeth:** Cat# = ◁-comonoids with cofunctor morphisms IS MacBeth's DCont ≅ Cof, rendered in Poly language. Libkind-Spivak name this category and build an adjunction around it. This validates MacBeth's result and gives it a natural home in the Poly ecosystem. The grant can present DCont ≅ Cof as "the morphism structure of Cat#."
- **Ideas sparked:** The cofree comonad adjunction Cat# → Poly is a rich source of derived results. Which polynomial p yield 𝔠_p that are Zappa-Szép products of their vertex monoids? MacBeth's freeness criterion characterises exactly this. This connects the cofree comonad construction to the ZS decomposition question.

### ["Interactions that Reshape the Interfaces" (OrgTr)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917) — Spivak (February 2026)

PolyTr is monoidal closed with Dirichlet tensor. The cofree comonad 𝔠_p (from 2404.16321) = terminal-coalgebra machinery in PolyTr.

The open question: does the cofunctor structure (MacBeth's M4 formalisation target) lift to OrgTr coinductive morphisms? Conjecture: OrgTr morphisms between constant-tree objects = cofunctors between the underlying DCont objects.
- **Ideas sparked:** Adaptive GA topologies (SEED Q6) = non-constant polynomial trees. The diversity dynamics conjecture (δ-futures under composition) might be the OrgTr version.

### New Papers (not in Cycle 2 log)

#### [Distributive Laws and Factorization](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-4049(02)00140-8) — Rosebrugh, Wood (JPAA 175, 2002)
**THE Q2 citation.** Proves: strict factorization systems on a category E with wide subcategories (H,K) ↔ distributive laws in the bicategory Span(Set). Corollary: (H,K) is a matched pair (= satisfies ZS1–ZS4) ↔ [K,H] is a strict factorization system on H⋈K. Every morphism in H⋈K factors uniquely as (H-morphism)∘(K-morphism). The global closure condition (G) in MacBeth's two-level criterion is precisely this: the transversal K must close into a subcategory AND the mutual action must be a matched pair, i.e., (H,K) must determine a strict factorization system. This gives a fully cite-ready characterization: **(L∧G) ⟺ SFS exists.** The freeness condition (L) is necessary; the SFS existence (G) is the global closure. Priority: READ THE PAPER to confirm exact statement.

#### Homology of Zappa-Szép Products — Mundey, Sims (arXiv:2311.09600, 2023)
Develops a spectral sequence computing categorical homology of ZS products of small categories (Eilenberg-Moore homology, derived from the double complex). 2-cocycles arising from the double complex classify extensions. Adjacent to MacBeth's Q1 reformulation (classifying ZS extensions of small categories, not obstructing existence). Would provide the tool for a "Kac sequence for small categories." Medium priority; read after Rosebrugh-Wood.

#### [The Free Bifibration on a Functor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07314) — Clarke, Scherer, Zeilberger (Nov 2025)
Constructs the free bifibration on any functor via bifibrational logic (cut-free sequent calculus). The identity functor yields Z(C) = zigzag double category = free fibrant double category on C. Ambifibrations = (P,N)-fibrations with a factorization system. Structurally parallel to AWFS for delta lenses (Clarke 2305.02732) and potentially to ZS decompositions via the OFS structure. Low priority for now; note for when MacBeth works on double-categorical aspects.

#### [Compositional Program Verification with Polynomial Functors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01303) — Aberlé (April 2026)
First formal verification framework built on Poly's free monad / substitution structure (citing Libkind-Spivak 2404.16321). Constructs compositional proof rules from polynomial functor structure; formalised in Agda. Directly supports the grant's applications arm: formal verification = compositional correctness = the thesis. Flag for grant's "applications" section. Medium priority.

#### Kac Cohomology and ZS Extensions — Baaj, Skandalis, Vaes (Trans. AMS 357, 2005; arXiv:math/0307172)
At the group level: H²(matched pair, T) classifies Zappa-Szép *extensions* (isomorphism classes of LCG factorizing through two given groups). The Kac exact sequence connects H²(matched pair) to H²(G) and H²(H). NOTE: H² does NOT obstruct existence — the ZS product always exists once a matched pair is given. **This forces a reformulation of Q1:** the holonomy obstruction is not about existence but about extensions. The right Q1 is: what is the categorical analog of Kac's H² classifying ZS extensions of small categories?
