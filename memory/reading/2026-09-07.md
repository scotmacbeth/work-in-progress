## Papers

### [Fujii, Lack — "The familial nature of enrichment over virtual double categories"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05529)
**Upgraded abstract → deep-read** (full PDF, pp.1-6,10-13,21-26). Their Def 4.4 ("familial") is
Weber's Def 5.2 **verbatim** — same split-fibration factorization through `Spl(R1)` — not a
generalization or cleaner statement; MacBeth had hoped for the latter. One genuinely new
technical wrinkle: a non-representable "discrete opfibration" notion for virtual double
categories (Def 5.1), proved always powerful/exponentiable (Prop 5.5), used to manufacture new
familial 2-functors (Prop 4.7) — a new *source* of examples, not a new *definition*. Main
theorem (7.10): `Enr: VDBL → 2-CAT` is familial. **Zero container/Poly/Zappa-Szép content**,
confirmed by checking intro + §4/6/8 + full bibliography — "polynomial" here is Weber's older
2-categorical sense (powerful/exponentiable 1-cells), a false friend to MacBeth's
container-theoretic sense. Their §8 `Fam: VDBL → VDBL` (families over VDBL via a universal
discrete opfibration on spans) is conceptually adjacent to MacBeth's `Fam(C^op)` program but one
categorical level up, no technical overlap. **Verdict: closes this candidate** — corroborates
Weber's Def 5.2 is still the standard as of 2025, delivers nothing new for T2/the crown method.

### [Arkor, McDermott — "The nerve theorem for relative monads"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01281)
**Upgraded abstract → deep-read** (raw HTML, 333K chars, exhaustive grep). `grep -i` for
"familial", "parametric right adjoint", "p.r.a." across the *entire* text: **zero hits** —
familiality is not discussed at all, confirmed rather than merely under-discussed. Their
generalizing axis is orthogonal: a *dense root* `j:A→E` for a relative monad in an exact virtual
equipment (density = nerve is fully faithful, Def 2.5), not split-fibration factorization. Weber
2007 appears only once, in Example 2.17, citing his separate *arities* Def 4.1 (a companion
notion in the same paper, never his p.r.a. Def 2.3 or familial Def 5.2) as one recovered special
case of their Theorem 2.6/7.14/8.8 tower. `grep` for "container"/"polynomial"/"Poly": zero hits.
**Verdict: closes this candidate too** — does not give a cleaner familial-representability
statement; confirms instead that the "monads with arities / nerve theorem" lineage
(density-of-root as an alternative sufficient condition) is separately active.
