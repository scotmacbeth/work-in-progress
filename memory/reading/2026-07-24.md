### [Homology and twisted C*-algebras for self-similar actions and Zappa-Szép products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09600) — Mundey, Sims (v1 2023, v2 2024; *Results in Mathematics* 2025) — ★★ crown find
**Full-text read** (PDF downloaded, 3221 lines, grepped for "obstruct" — one irrelevant hit). This is
`[MuS25a]`, the paper underlying 2511.07906's matched-pair construction, and the last live unread candidate
in the H²-obstruction-family sibling hunt running since 2026-07-22. **Definition 3.1** ("matched pair"
`(C,D,⊲,⊳)`): compatible actions `⊲,⊳` are **assumed given as global data by fiat** (axioms MP1-MP3), no
notion of only-locally-defined matching data needing to glue. **Lemma 3.5**: given ANY matched pair,
`µ⋈((d₁,c₁),(d₂,c₂)) := (d₁(c₁⊲d₂),(c₁⊳d₂)c₂)` is **unconditionally associative** — the ZS product category
`C⋈D` exists automatically, full stop, no cohomological or further global condition anywhere in the
construction. Sections 4+ then build categorical homology/cohomology **on top of** an already-constructed
matched pair, used (per 2511.07906) to classify 2-cocycle twists — same shape as the already-cleared
2511.07906. **Verdict: CLEAR, orthogonal.** MacBeth's (G) obstruction is nontrivial precisely because
MacBeth's setting starts from only *locally* compatible data that must be checked to glue to a global
matched-pair structure; Mundey-Sims simply postulate the global structure and never ask the gluing question.
**This closes the entire H²-obstruction sibling-hunt thread** — Mundey-Kwaśniewski 2511.07906, fusion-category
2405.10207 (retracted, was a hallucination), k-graphs 2503.08630, and now Mundey-Sims 2311.09600 itself are
all confirmed orthogonal via direct full-text reads. → sources.json upgraded agent-summary→deep-read.
