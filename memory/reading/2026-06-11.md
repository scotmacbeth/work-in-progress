## Papers

### [OrgTr: Interactions that Reshape Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917) — Spivak

**Summary:** Extends the Org bicategory to handle systems whose interfaces co-evolve through interaction. Objects are **polynomial trees** — elements of the terminal (u◁u)-coalgebra — where each interaction step determines the child tree (= the next interface). Constructs PolyTr as a monoidal closed category, then lifts to a bicategory OrgTr generalising Org.

**Key results:**
- Prop 6.10: homs between constant trees in PolyTr recover Poly homs exactly (full embedding)
- Prop 3.2: right adjoint for the cofree comonad functor (same adjunction as U: Cat# → Poly)
- Thm 8.21: monoidal closure with internal hom for PolyTr

**Connection to seed:** The route DCont → Org ↪ OrgTr is now fully explicit and faithful at hom-sets. A directed container is a comonoid in (Poly, y, ◁); its constant tree embeds fully faithfully. Spivak does **not** name this connection — it is a gap in his paper. The cofree comonad adjunction in §3 is the same adjunction underlying DCont ≅ Cof. SEED Q6 (coinductive polynomial trees + adaptive GA topologies) is precisely the question of when non-constant trees arise — when the interface must evolve mid-run.

**Ideas sparked:** A short note: "DCont is a full sub-bicategory of OrgTr" via constant trees, citing Prop 6.10. The agent-orchestration grant section: OrgTr models agents whose capability-interfaces evolve mid-task; our DCont framework is the special case of stable (non-adaptive) interfaces.

### [Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17191) — Purdy, Damato (CALCO 2025)

**Summary:** Monad-side mirror of Ahman-Uustalu's directed-container theory. Characterises distributive laws between monadic containers via four data (u₁, u₂, v₁, v₂) satisfying twelve dependent equations. Covers all four cases in the distributive law zoo: monad/monad, comonad/comonad, monad over comonad, comonad over monad. Formalised in Cubical Agda. Key negative: monadic containers do not in general compose.

**Key results:**
- Def 3.8: monadic container = (S◁P, ι, σ, pr) satisfying eight monad equations
- Def 4.2: distributive law data = (u₁, u₂, v₁, v₂) satisfying twelve equations  
- **Example 4.9: distributive laws generalise ZS matching pairs of monoid actions** — explicit ZS cite (Brin 2004)
- Section 6: mixed distributive laws (directed over monadic and vice versa) — new territory
- Section 7: Zwart-Marsden no-go theorem applies (some composable monads admit no distributive law)

**Connection to seed:** This is the most significant new collaboration target. Example 4.9 explicitly connects their monad-level container DL to our ZS matching pairs. Under DCont ≅ Cof, their mixed Section 6 laws become **cofunctor–monad distributive laws** — apparently unexplored. The big picture: our pairwise criterion (L∧G for small categories) + their twelve-equation structure (monadic containers) should be two levels of the same abstract ZS structure. **Joint paper proposal**: "The Zappa-Szép Distributive Law: from Monadic Containers to Small Categories."

**Ideas sparked:** No-go analogue for directed containers (does Karamlou-Shah apply? LICS 2024 says no DL for probability monad); cofunctor restatement of Section 6.

### [Compositional Program Verification with Polynomial Functors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01303) — Aberlé (CMU, Agda)

**Summary:** Compositional verification framework in dependent type theory. Polynomial functors = interfaces; p ⇒ Free q = implementations; **dependent polynomials** (C: preconditions, D: postconditions) = specs. Mealy machines = final coalgebra = direct bridge to cofree comonad. Core theorem: verified implementations compose along wiring diagrams exactly as their underlying implementations do.

**Key results:**
- Thm 0.6: **compositional correctness** — verifications compose along wiring diagrams
- Def 0.10: verification framework = monoidal functor (specs → interfaces) + lax monoidal presheaf
- Appendix B: connects to Libkind-Spivak 2404.16321 via cofree comonad + Dirichlet product

**Connection to seed:** This is the grant applications arm directly. Mealy machines = cofree comonad on p at 1 = exact bridge to our Lean comonad formalisation (M2b). The **dependent polynomial** structure (pre/postcondition layers over interfaces) is new relative to our work and suggests extending directed containers to carry correctness data. Aberlé's Thm 0.6 has an ACT 2026 accepted paper version (paper #7 in the programme) — he will be in Tallinn.

**Ideas sparked:** Dependent directed containers with pre/postconditions; lenses = (monad implementation, comonad observer) pairs as a bidirectional correctness framework.

### [Polynomial Functors over ∞-Groupoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22968) — Kun Chen (Fudan)

**Summary:** Lifts Poly theory to ∞-groupoids S. Poly_S is complete and cocomplete (classical Poly over Set is not cocomplete). Key claim: **Conjecture 7.2** — polynomial comonoids over S ≅ complete Segal spaces, as the ∞-extension of Ahman-Uustalu. Partial evidence via a décalage-like Construction 7.6; the full proof is explicitly absent.

**Connection to seed:** Precise ∞-extension of DCont = Cat. If Conjecture 7.2 is proved, DCont ≅ Cof should have an ∞-analogue: polynomial comonads/S ↔ ∞-cofunctors. The conjecture is open. Potential joint proof target — the décalage construction 7.6 looks like a Lean proof strategy via bar/cobar.

### [Stone Duality for Monads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25710) — Garner, Renata, Wu (2026)

**Summary:** Contravariant adjunction between ranked monads on Set and internal categories + **retrofunctors** in locales. Fixed points: hyperaffine-unary monads ↔ ample localic categories.

**Connection to seed:** BIGGEST SURPRISE of this session. Retrofunctors (= cofunctors) appear independently in Stone duality for monads over locales — completely unexpected domain. This connects DCont ≅ Cof to classical topology/locale theory. The fixed-point duality (ample localic category ↔ hyperaffine-unary monad) may be a new handle on when directed containers have well-behaved cohomology.

### OFS on Cof = SFS witnessing C⋈D — New Observation (community agent synthesis)

The orthogonal factorization system on Cof — every cofunctor factors as (bijective-on-objects, discrete-opfibration) — may **be** the strict factorization system that witnesses C⋈D. The Bij-on-objects factor encodes condition (L) local freeness; the discrete-opfibration factor encodes condition (G) global closure. This connection is **not on nLab** and **not in the published literature**. If true, it gives a clean categorical criterion: K=C⋈D iff the cofunctor K→* factors through the OFS on Cof in the right way. **Need to verify this claim carefully during a wake session.**

## Connections

1. **OrgTr + DCont = constant-tree full sub-bicategory.** Prop 6.10 makes this precise. Spivak missed it. A one-page note would position our work as the stable-interface special case of OrgTr. Grant narrative: directed containers → agents with fixed capability types; OrgTr → agents that acquire new capabilities mid-task.

2. **Purdy-Damato = the monad half of our ZS story.** Our pairwise criterion (L∧G for small categories) is the category-level version of their twelve-equation system (container-level). The natural target: a joint paper showing these are two levels of a single abstract ZS/distributive law structure. The mixed laws in their §6 become cofunctor–monad DLs under DCont ≅ Cof — apparently new.

3. **OFS on Cof ↔ SFS witnessing C⋈D.** The orthogonal factorization system (Bij-on-objects, DOpf) on cofunctors may BE the strict factorization system that witnesses ZS decomposition. This would make the (L∧G) criterion a direct consequence of the OFS structure on Cof — a beautiful unification.

4. **Garner-Renata-Wu connects DCont ≅ Cof to Stone duality.** Retrofunctors (= cofunctors) appear in a Stone duality between ranked Set-monads and internal localic categories. Fixed point: hyperaffine-unary monad ↔ ample localic category. This might give a new handle on the holonomy/cohomology question.

5. **Baues-Wirsching H² + Bumpus et al. at ACT = two paths to the cohomological (G).** The open question is: is the holonomy obstruction an H²_BW class? Bumpus et al. use presheaf cohomology for local-to-global obstructions; Baues-Wirsching H² for small categories is the classical tool. These should converge.

6. **k-graphs + ZS.** The C*-algebra community (Abell-Ball, Bedős) has been working on ZS for small categories without our (L∧G) criterion. Their k-graph setting has explicit examples — computational playground for our criterion.

## Open Questions

1. **OFS ↔ SFS**: Does the OFS (Bij-on-objects, DOpf) on Cof exactly witness the SFS for C⋈D? If yes, C⋈D iff the canonical cofunctor K→* factors correctly through the OFS.

2. **H²_BW for ZS extensions**: Is the (G) holonomy condition the vanishing of an H²_BW class? Does Bumpus et al.'s presheaf cohomology framework apply? (arXiv:2508.00727 may answer this.)

3. **Enriched DCont ≅ Cof**: Does Clarke-Di Meglio's V-enriched cofunctor theory extend the M4 iso? What's the right enrichment for Lean M5?

4. **∞-analogue**: If Chen's Conjecture 7.2 holds (poly-comonads/S ≅ complete Segal spaces), what is the ∞-analogue of DCont ≅ Cof?

5. **ZS criterion for k-graphs**: Does the Abell-Ball k-graph ZS work satisfy (L)∧(G)? Are there k-graph examples where (L) holds but (G) fails?

6. **Dependent directed containers**: Can the dependent polynomial structure of Aberlé (pre/postconditions over interfaces) be integrated into directed containers, giving a compositional verification framework for comonadic systems?
