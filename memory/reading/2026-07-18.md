## Papers

### [Comonads as Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15091) — Fairbanks, Carlson, Spivak (submitted 2026-07-16)
Full-text read (arXiv agent, 152pp) plus independent abstract confirmation (citation-trail agent). Generalizes *topological spaces* to arbitrary comonads on arbitrary categories via "density comonads" (left Kan extension of a diagram along itself). Main results: topological spaces = density comonads of subset-diagrams (Thm 1.2.6); every Set-comonad has an underlying space (reflection) and underlying small category (coreflection); a double category of comonad-maps-and-continuous-maps that, restricted to polynomial comonads, recovers **both** the Clarke–Di Meglio functors-and-retrofunctors double category and the Clarke/Di Meglio cofunctor formalism (Thm 3.2.7). Ahman–Uustalu is cited as settled prior art, quoted verbatim: *"Translating from ionads to comonads on Set then recovers the result of [AU16] that categories are identified with certain comonads on Set, namely those carried by polynomial functors."* Never touches directed-container axioms (D1–D5) by name, never mentions Zappa-Szép or comonoids-in-Poly — works one level up (comonads-on-Set / ionads / topology), treating AU16 as a black box. **Verdict: NOT a scoop, confirmed independently by two agents.** Territory is orthogonal (topology + ionads + "halos"), but the retrofunctor double category showing up as a special case is worth citing as related work in the DCont≅Cof writeup — see companion "Set-sets" blog post below.

### [All Concepts are Cat#](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02571) — Lynch, Shapiro, Spivak (2023/2024)
Full-text read this session (previously only search-snippet level). Confirms the existing prior-art registry entry (`cat-hash-is-dcont-cof`): Cat# = comonoids-in-Poly with cofunctor vertical morphisms and prafunctor (bicomodule) horizontal morphisms, citing Spivak 2021a / AU / Garner for the base correspondence — nothing new for MacBeth's DCont≅Cof territory. The paper's actual novelty is showing Cat# subsumes Org (dynamic rewiring), Poly_ℰ (generalized polynomials internal to any finite-limit category, via a faithful embedding Thm 4.3), and effects handlers. Extraction bumped abstract → deep-read.

### [A Sheaf Framework for Strategic Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01663) — Hernández, Sánchez-Soto (2026)
Re-read for detail (already deep-read 07-15, verdict unchanged: NOT a scoop of (G)⟺[ω]=0∈H²). New detail this pass: a "game sheaf" over a time×agent-graph site; Theorem 1 states sheaf-theoretic Nash equilibria = global sections of a best-response sheaf, existing iff H¹=0; a Künneth-type decomposition of the obstruction into geometric/logical/strategic parts. Writing register (informal section titles, thin proof sketches, "immunological parable" worked example) suggests exploratory/demo-level rigor, not refereed-strength — treat claims as plausible, not verified. Still on-theme for the grant's agent-orchestration narrative as an independent H¹-obstruction pattern, structurally parallel (different degree, different category) to MacBeth's own H² Zappa-Szép result.

### [Topos Institute blog: "Set-sets"](https://topos.institute/blog/2025-11-21-set-sets/) — Aaron Fairbanks, 2025-11-21
Explicitly the accessible primer for "Comonads as Spaces" (2607.15091), posted 8 months before the paper. Frames polynomial functors as "freely generated Set-sets, presented by generators and no relations" — a special/free case of general comonads-on-Set. States "topological spaces play the same role in relation to general comonads on Set as preorders do to categories." Motivating examples only; no technical development of containers/directed containers despite naming them.
