## Papers

### [The Cofree Functor on G-Sets and Its Approximations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23866) — Murphy-Hernandez (26 Aug 2026)
New, not previously tracked. Studies the comonad `K_G(X)=X^G` on Set — the ordinary Reader/costate
comonad for a group `G`, i.e. the representable container `y^G`. Main result: `G ↦ K_G` is a fully
faithful embedding `Grp^op ↪ Comonads(Set)` — the group is recovered up to isomorphism from its
comonad structure alone. Never uses container/polynomial-functor language, cites none of the
Ahman-Uustalu/Niu-Spivak literature. Sits structurally next to MacBeth's own "comonad on `y^E` =
category on `E`" line but approached from the opposite direction (classify the group FROM the
comonad, not build the comonad from structure on `E`). Read at abstract + key-theorem level only.
Not urgent; a candidate citable independent example.

## Web Research

### [Polynomials and fibrations](http://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/volumes/33/36/33-36.pdf) — von Glehn, TAC 33 (2018) — PROVENANCE DEBT CLOSED
Full PDF fetched and read. This is the exact source for MacBeth's oft-cited-from-memory
"contravariance = fibrewise op" claim, which had **zero `sources.json` entry** despite being cited
in the shipped survey §5 — a blocking gap for any arXiv submission, now closed. Verbatim (p.1132):
"Given a two-sided fibration M, we can freely add products to M by taking the opposite fibration,
adding sums, and then taking the opposite again" = **Prop 6.9** (`Π_B := (Σ_B)^op`). **Thm 7.1**: a
pseudo-distributive law of `Π_B` over `Σ_B` exists iff the base is locally cartesian closed;
composite = `Poly_B` (Thm 7.2). Exact page/theorem numbers now available for citation.

### [Substructural Type Theories Modelled by Polynomial Functors](https://actconf2026.github.io/papers/ACT_2026_paper_91.pdf) — Braithwaite, Hedges, Mihejevs, ACT 2026 — FULL TEXT (was title-only)
Extends von Glehn's ITT-in-Poly model with three fragments: (1) adjoint modalities `disc ⊣
Poly(よ,−) ⊣ codisc` (flat/sharp) plus response types and perform/handle syntax for `E◁A`; (2) a
**graded linear type theory** — Benton-style LNL model with `×` as cartesian product and Dirichlet
`⊗` as the linear multiplicative product, built entirely inside Poly's monoidal zoo MacBeth already
works in; (3) a bunched type theory combining both, conjectured complete over a syntactic base.
Genuine finding: their composition-product graded monad `T_P(X)=X◁P` (detailed in the companion
Polylang paper below) generalises MacBeth's own `workers-graded-category-proved` grading — from a
state-object `S` to an **arbitrary polynomial `P`** — a generalisation MacBeth hasn't considered.

### [Polylang: Programming with polynomial functors and lenses](https://actconf2026.github.io/papers/ACT_2026_paper_85.pdf) — Braithwaite, Hedges, Mihejevs, ACT 2026 — FULL TEXT (was title-only)
Implements Poly's "intrinsic simple type theory" (types = polynomials, functions = lenses).
Composition product `▷` modelled as the noncommutative graded monad `T_P(X)=X▷P` (stack push/pop).
Concrete, citable statement relevant to MacBeth's own fibration work: `▷` is **"not fibred in its
left variable"** — the explicit reason they avoid Pradic-Price's fibred-endofunctor fixpoint
machinery (`2601.15420`, already tracked). Cites Spivak-Srinivasan's isomix LDC result (`2407.01849`,
already deep-read) as the sequent-calculus backbone. One open incompleteness the authors flag: they
cannot derive `A×(B⊗C)→(A×B)⊗C` without a full bunched-context calculus.

### [Sequents for sequence](https://julesh.com/posts/2026-03-13-sequents-sequence.html) + [Part II](https://julesh.com/posts/2026-03-23-sequents-sequence-ii.html) — Jules Hedges blog, March 2026 — NEW
