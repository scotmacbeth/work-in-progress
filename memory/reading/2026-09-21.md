## Papers

### [Schur Functors and Categorified Plethysm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00190) — Baez, Moeller, Trimble, arXiv:2106.00190 (2021, v3 2023)
Full HTML text read (arXiv agent). Shows **Schur** (Schur functors / FinVect-valued species) is a
**2-plethory**: a pseudomonoid in `(2-Birig, ⊙)` where `⊙` is the plethysm tensor
`ρ∙τ = ⊕ₙ ρ(n) ⊗_{k[Sₙ]} τ^⊗n` — categorifying "symmetric functions form a ring-plethory."
Generalizes to **M-plethories for arbitrary monads M on Set** (§5): M-bialgebras form a
substitution monoidal category, M-plethories are monoids therein. **Directly checked against the
Plethysm Lemma** (does a composite `F∘M` factor imply `M` decomposes?) — no such recognition/
decomposition theorem exists anywhere in the paper; every substantial result is closure/
construction (Sch closed under `⊙`, Sch is free on one generator), never "recover a factor from a
composite." This is a *third*, structurally independent confirmation (after Gambino–Kock and
Carboni–Johnstone) that the polynomial/analytic-functor literature only ever proves
closure-under-composition, never decomposition — now well-triangulated, not just unsearched.
Tangential: the "M-plethory for any monad" framework is vocabulary-adjacent to Neil's M-container
idea ([[neil-k-container-monad-lift-is-fam-kleisli]]) — both build monoidal categories from
M-(co)algebras.

### [Operadic 2-Rigs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12705) — Anel, Fiore, Gambino, arXiv:2607.12705 (July 2026)
Full HTML text read (arXiv agent). By a Gambino–Kock co-author, so upstream of MacBeth's core
polynomial-functor citation. A **2-rig** = presentable category with cocontinuous symmetric
monoidal structure; **operadic 2-rig** = essential image of `Psh∘Env: Opd → 2-Rig`. **Thm A:**
operadic 2-rigs ≃ operads-with-bimodules (biequivalence). **Thm B:** operadic 2-rigs = closure of
free 2-rigs under Eilenberg–Moore object formation ("EMK-completeness" of 2-Rig). Checked directly
against the Plethysm Lemma: the only recognition criterion is bicategorical-image-based ("operadic"
:= "in the image of"), the wrong shape for an intrinsic property of `M∘M` implying one of `M`; zero
mention of polynomial functors/species/plethysm anywhere (works in general presentable categories,
not Set). Not a resource for Lemma N, but flags a **companion paper worth reading next cycle**:
**2607.12683**, "Symmetric 2-rigs: coexponentiability and cartesian closure" — closed-structure
classification on 2-rigs, directly parallel to MacBeth's own
[[closed-convolutional-tensors-classified]] / `poly-three-closed-structures.md` work.

### [A Double Categorical Framework for Multi-Stage Portfolio Construction and Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12301) — Wesley Phoa, arXiv:2603.12301 (March 2026)
Full HTML text read (arXiv agent). Thin double category of portfolio weight-spaces (objects),
re-implementation maps (horizontal), compliance relations (vertical). Four theorems: compositional
alignment, pushforward-pullback adjunction, Beck–Chevalley, Frobenius reciprocity; extended via
Libkind–Myers' Double Operadic Theory of Systems (DOTS) in §17. Author's own honesty flag: "no
novel category theory required — the contribution is the modelling claim." **Verdict:** a genuine
economically-consequential compositional system (good citable sibling for the grant's Impact
section, same spirit as [[applications-are-directed-containers]]) but mechanism is plain
double-category composition, zero Poly/Dirichlet/ZS content — not competition, not prior art for
any MacBeth theorem. Reinforces the standing "two schools of compositional applications" observation.

## Community

### [The category-theoretic origin of the arithmetic product (of species)](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/461268) — MathOverflow, score 14, answered — **NEW**
The arithmetic product `⊡` on species (Maia–Méndez) is a *second* symmetric monoidal structure
alongside substitution, satisfying `D_{M⊡N} = D_M · D_N` for Dirichlet series. Accepted answer
traces: Dwyer–Hess (arXiv:1302.3711, `⊡` = Day convolution from multiplication on ℕ, the "matrix
monoidal structure"); Bremner–Dotsenko (1705.04573); **Garner–López Franco, "Commutativity"
(arXiv:1507.08710) §6 — proves substitution ⊗ arithmetic product form a normal duoidal structure
on Species**; Gambino–Garner–Vasilakopoulou (arXiv:2206.06858) — modern derivation via Kleisli
bicategories, recovering Maia–Méndez as a special case. **This is a second, independently
discovered instance of MacBeth's own (⋉,⋊)-duoidal pattern**
([[ltimes-rtimes-duoidal-ldc-proved]]) and the "Dirichlet ⊗ IS Day convolution" note
([[dirichlet-is-day-convolution]]) — worth reading Garner–López Franco §6 directly to compare proof
technique and check whether the arithmetic-product unit matches MacBeth's `y`-unit shape.

### [Is there a nice formula for the "non-crossing substitution" of linear combinatorial species?](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/210266) — MathOverflow, 2015, score 15, **0 answers, still fully open**

theme: substitution = composition of species = analytic-functor composition, so "what does
