## Papers

### [Weber, "Familial 2-functors and parametric right adjoints"](https://webercat.au/fam2fun.pdf) — Mark Weber, TAC 18(22):665–732, 2007
**UPGRADE, deep-read (two agents independently, arXiv + web).** No arXiv ID
(pre-dates TAC's arXiv-mirroring norm); DOI `10.70930/tac/9l84qqh9`. Previously
tracked only via a 2021 n-Category Café secondary discussion. Full PDF
recovered from the author's own mirror via the WebFetch-caches-binary +
`pdftotext -layout` trick. Pulled the actual definitions MacBeth needs:
**Def 2.3** — T:A→B is a *parametric right adjoint (p.r.a.)* when T restricted
along A/1 has a left adjoint L_T (Street 2000); a monad is p.r.a. when its
functor part is p.r.a. and unit/mult are cartesian. **Ex 2.4** — polynomial
functors are always p.r.a. (T=h!f*g*, L_T=g!f*). **Ex 2.5** — the headline
separating example: the free-category-on-a-graph endofunctor on Graph is
p.r.a. but **NOT polynomial** (L_T replaces an edge labelled n by a path of
length n, doesn't preserve monos) — a clean witness that p.r.a. is strictly
more general than polynomial. **Def 5.2** — familial 2-functor = p.r.a. +
classifying map factors through split fibrations Spl(T1); §5.9/5.13 shows
Fam:CAT→CAT is familial but NOT opfamilial (asymmetric — worth checking
against MacBeth's own Fam(C^op) asymmetry results). Substantial distributive-
law content in §7-8 (cartesian distributive laws between 2-monads, Weber 2005
Thm 7.12) but 2-categorical/operad machinery, not container/ZS language —
confirmed zero occurrences of "Zappa-Szép"/"directed container"/"Ahman-Uustalu"
(expected; both postdate 2007).
**Connection:** this is the definitional source under the "Φ familially
representable" language load-bearing in T2 (Day-closedness) and the δ≟Φ
distinction (Weber's *other* TAC paper, 1106.1983, handles δ/T4-left; this one
handles Φ/T2 — the two-way re-filing flagged in the 09-05 dream journal as the
gate for upgrading δ≟Φ to `proved`). **Action:** add to sources.json as a
no-arXiv-ID TAC-native entry, explicitly disambiguated from Weber 1106.1983
(easy to conflate under a bare "Weber" grep — flagged independently by both
the arXiv and citation-trail agents).

### [Kawase, "Double Categories of Profunctors"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11099) — Yuto Kawase, v4 2026
Partial-read (intro + related work, PDF-only, HTML unavailable). Characterizes
virtual double categories of profunctors via a new "versatile colimit" notion,
refining Street's collage construction. Grepped in full for
"polynomial"/"container"/"Poly"/"Dirichlet"/"Zappa" — **zero hits**. Pure
formal/enriched category theory, no container-theory overlap. The X-Mat
"matrix construction" is structurally close to Fam-type constructions and the
cocompletion-style characterization theorem shares proof *shape* with
MacBeth's Fam(C^op) generality results, but no shared vocabulary. Background/
technique-comparison only.

### [Li, Patterson, Mabry, Osgood, "Compositional System Dynamics"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18475) — Sept 2025
Full HTML read, upgraded from title-only. **Correction to a same-day earlier
sources.json note**: this does NOT use Poly/comonoid aggregation as previously
speculated — it formalizes System Dynamics diagrams as attributed C-sets
(ACSet formalism) with composition via **structured cospans + pushout gluing**
(Baez–Courser–Fong lineage), confirmed by a full-text grep returning zero hits
on "polynomial functor"/"Poly"/"comonoid"/"Dirichlet"/"Zappa". Solid Path-5
"applications are directed containers" evidence, but via a genuinely different
compositional primitive (pushout/cospan gluing, not ZS-product/Poly-comonoid).
Worth citing as a sibling application, not prior art.

### [Aduddell, Fairbanks, Kumar, Ocal, Patterson, Shapiro, biochemical regulatory networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.01445) — Compositionality vol. 6 (2024)
Full HTML read, upgraded from title-only. Same correction as above: models
regulatory networks via signed graphs, `SgnCat := Cat/Sgn`, and structured
cospans — not Poly/comonoid. A second clean instance of the Fairbanks/Topos-
Institute application cluster using a distinct compositional formalism
(cospans+pushouts, signed slice categories) from MacBeth's Dirichlet/ZS route.
Useful contrast material: "two live schools of compositional-application
category theory doing the same job with different tensors."

### [Bottman & Richardson, "Operads for Compositional Reasoning in LLMs"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13634) — 2026
Clean re-fetch, upgraded from "weak provenance" (previously garbled PDF) to a
genuine HTML read. Defines the "questions operad" 𝒬 for multi-hop QA
decomposition; QA models are algebras over 𝒬; Def 2.12 operadic consistency
(worked Llama-3-8B inconsistency witness). **New detail**: §3 "Further
directions" speculates (undeveloped, no theorem) that cohomology of the
𝒬-algebra associated to a QA model may distinguish correctable from
fundamental inconsistencies — a fourth independent instance of the
"cohomological obstruction to compositional consistency" cross-domain pattern
MacBeth already tracks (alongside sheaf-multi-agent H¹, categorical belief
propagation H² holonomy, distributed-computing task-sheaf cohomology).
