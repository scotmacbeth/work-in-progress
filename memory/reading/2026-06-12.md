## Papers

### [Polynomial Functors: A Mathematical Theory of Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00990) — Nelson Niu, David I. Spivak (arXiv:2312.00990, 2023)

**Key result (Theorem 5.31, Section 5.3):** **(Poly, ×) IS cartesian closed.** The exponential is:

```
r^q  :=  ∏_{j ∈ q(1)}  r ∘ (y + q[j])
```

with natural isomorphism Poly(p, r^q) ≅ Poly(p × q, r). The proof is a chain of natural bijections via Yoneda. This RESOLVES my active PROVE target — it is a citation, not a new theorem.

Additionally, **three distinct closed structures** coexist on Poly without conflict:
| Structure | Unit | Closure | Internal hom |
|-----------|------|---------|-------------|
| × (cartesian) | 1 | YES, Thm 5.31 | r^q = ∏_{j∈q(1)} r∘(y+q[j]) |
| ⊗ (Dirichlet) | y | YES, §4.5 | [p,q] |
| (⊗, ◁) bi-closed LDC | — | YES (Spivak-Srinivasan 2407.01849) | left/right co-closures |
| ◁ alone | y | NOT closed; has right-coclosure | Poly([q◁p],p')≅Poly(p,p'◁q) |

The ◁ right-coclosure is the structurally relevant fact for directed containers: DCont morphisms are ◁-comonoid maps, which live in the coclosure, NOT in an internal hom. This is a new observation that clarifies the morphism story.

**Connection:** Closes the Ch3 Open/TODO on cartesian closedness. WRITE task reduced to citing Niu-Spivak Thm 5.31 and Spivak-Srinivasan arXiv:2407.01849.

### [An Introduction to Enriched Cofunctors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.01144) — Bryce Clarke, Matthew Di Meglio (arXiv:2209.01144, 2022)

**Key result:** A V-enriched cofunctor Φ: A⇸B (for V a distributive monoidal category) has lifting maps Φ_{a,b}: B(Φa,b) → ∐_{x∈Φ^{-1}{b}} A(a,x) in V, satisfying enriched unitality and associativity. V must be **distributive monoidal** for the composition axiom to type-check (one must distribute ⊗ over ∐). Main structure: double category **V-Cof** with V-Cat horizontal and V-Cat⁺ vertical. Enriched lenses = compatible (functor, cofunctor) pairs. Weighted lenses = lenses enriched in ([0,∞],+).

**What's open:** The enriched DCont ≅ Cof theorem is NOT proved — explicitly stated as future work. No sequel has appeared since 2022; the enriched cofunctor programme appears stalled. Lean: enriched cofunctors are not in Mathlib.

**Connection:** The V-enriched setting generalises my M4 result. If we want to formalise enriched DCont ≅ Cof in Lean, the first step is V=Set (recovering ordinary cofunctors), then try V a preorder or a category with products.

### [Schreier Theory of Track Categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03250) — Teimuraz Pirashvili (arXiv:1512.03250, 2015)

**Key result (Theorem 7):** For a pre-track category (π: K→C, G) with G a centralised natural system:

```
Tracks(π, G)  ≅  H²(π, G)
```

This is the non-abelian Schreier theorem for track categories. The abelian case (G abelian, natural system) is Baues-Wirsching 1985 (J. Pure Appl. Algebra 38, 187-211): H²_BW(C;D) classifies **linear extensions** of the small category C by D. Both results hold for ALL small categories, not just groupoids.

**Connection to ZS:** A ZS product C⋈D_• with abelian fiber (D_c an abelian group, C acting on D) IS a linear extension of C by D. Therefore [ω]∈H²_BW(Sk_C;𝒟) iff condition (G) fails — this is established theory. My claim that "(G)⟺[ω]=0" is a consequence of the BW classification theorem. **This is a citation, not a conjecture.** The remark in the pairwise ZS paper should cite Baues-Wirsching 1985.

**Additional papers confirming BW as the canonical framework:**
- arXiv:2510.00488 (Ikebuchi 2025): BW cohomology = Quillen cohomology for small CCCs
- arXiv:2512.15835 (Gerstenhaber-Schack, Dec 2025): another identification of BW

### [The Grothendieck Construction for Delta Lenses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21288) — Bryce Clarke (arXiv:2502.21288, 2025; to appear *Higher Structures*)

**Key result:** Delta lenses A→B correspond exactly to lax double functors B → DblSMF (double category of sets, functions, split multivalued functions). DblSMF has a universal property as a certain limit, inheriting structure from the double category of spans.

**Connection:** Extends the structural chain to: **DCont ↔ Categories ↔ Cofunctors ↔ Delta lenses ↔ Lax double functors into DblSMF.** This is one step longer than what I had. The geometric interpretation: a directed container morphism (cofunctor) is a section-selection of a lax double functor, i.e., a way to "consistently choose images in DblSMF."

nLab delta lens page last revised Aug 2023 — this canonical 2025 result is not yet cited there.
