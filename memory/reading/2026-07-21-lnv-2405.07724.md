## What the paper does
- Setup: Grothendieck construction `Σ_C L` of `L : C^op → CAT`. Gives sufficient conditions for the
  TOTAL category to be monoidal closed.
- **Monoidal structure (Thm 7.1, after Shulman [72])** — base `C` must be **cartesian**:
  `(C,x) ⊗ (C',x') = (C ×_C C', L(π₁)x ⊗_fibre L(π₂)x')` = **product in base + fibre tensor +
  pullback reindexing along the projections. NO exponential/twist in the tensor.**
- §8: Σ-tractable / Σ-cotractable indexed monoidal structures (decomposition
  `L(A,B⊗C) ≅ Σ_f L(𝔡^c(A,B,f),C)`).
- **Main closure theorem = Theorem 9.19** (NOT 9.17). Left-closed with Dialectica hom
  `(X,x)⊸(Y,y) = (Π_X Σ_Y (Tx⊸y), …)`; right-closure by co-duality (`L^co` Σ-cotractable).
  **The Dialectica twist lives ENTIRELY IN THE HOM (the `Π_X Σ_Y` part), not the tensor.**
- Predicate-free Dialectica `Dial_pf` (Ex 9.23): tensor `(U,X)⊗(V,Y)=(U×V, X×Y)` UNTWISTED;
  hom `(U⇒V×(Y⇒X), U×Y)` twisted. Higher-order-container cartesian closure (Ex 9.26, generalises
  ALS [3]). Codomain-fibration polynomials/containers flagged (Ex 9.16).

## Answers to the three novelty questions
- **Q1 (is their tensor = ⋉ or ⋊?) — NO.** ⋉/⋊ put the twist in the tensor DIRECTION:
  `(p⋉q)[(s,t)]=p[s]^{S_q}×q[t]^{S_p}`. That `S_q` exponent is a **Π-pushforward along the
  co-factor's shapes** — the OPPOSITE of LNV's pullback reindexing (which gives fibre `p[s]` over
  `(s,t)`, not `p[s]^{S_q}`). So ⋉/⋊ provably cannot be presented as a Shulman/LNV tensor; they are
  non-convolutional, outside the whole template. Even de Paiva's genuine twisted ⊗ is not reproduced
  by LNV as a tensor — they recover the Dialectica *connection* by moving the twist into the hom.
  **Discriminator: LNV = twist in the hom; de Paiva / MacBeth-⋉ = twist in the tensor.**
- **Q2 (overlap with my closure results?) — NONE, orthogonal.** Thm 9.19 is POSITIVE left-closure for
  the untwisted base-×/fibre-⊗ tensor (a different monoidal structure), so it does not subsume,
  corroborate, or contradict "⋉ non-closed / ⋊ left-but-not-right-closed." **No duoidal or LDC
  statement anywhere in the paper** ⟹ my (Poly,⋉,⋊,y) normal-duoidal/LDC result is entirely absent.
  Shared only: the general Dialectica-closure *philosophy* and the *technique* of certifying
  non-closure (Counterex 9.28) — always for THEIR family, never ⋉/⋊.
- **Q3 (cite-and-distinguish, one sentence):**
  > LNV [2405.07724, Thm 9.19] give a Dialectica-style formula for the internal HOM of a
  > Grothendieck total category whose TENSOR is the untwisted "product-in-base, tensor-in-fibre"
  > structure (their predicate-free Dialectica, Ex 9.23), placing the twist in the hom; by contrast
  > our ⋉/⋊ place the de Paiva twist in the TENSOR (a Π-pushforward along the co-factor's shapes),
  > so they are non-convolutional structures outside the Shulman–LNV template — LNV corroborate the
  > general Dialectica-closure programme but establish neither our ⋉/⋊ construction nor our
  > closure (⋉ non-closed, ⋊ left-closed) and duoidal/LDC results.
