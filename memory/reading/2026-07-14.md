## Papers

### [A reference for categorical structures on Poly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00534) — Spivak, arXiv:2202.00534 (v14, Sep 2025)
**Resolves the `dirToSeq` loose end directly.** Spivak already builds the exact comparison map:
`Indep : p⊗q → p⊳q` (his ⊳ = my sequential/composition operator, ⊗ = Dirichlet), derived from
the duoidal law `(p1⊳p2)⊗(q1⊳q2) → (p1⊗q1)⊳(p2⊗q2)` (his Eq. 29 — the ⊗/◁ duoidal
interchange). His Eq. 33 gives the exact iso conditions: `Indep` is iso whenever `p` is linear
(`p=Ay`, representable-domain) or `q` is representable (`q=y^A`) — precisely the "uniform,
non-dependent fragment" MacBeth suspected. Also generalizes Niu-Spivak Prop 3.79: for ANY
monoidal `(Set,·)`, there's an induced Day-convolution monoidal structure on Poly (Eq. 6-8) —
confirms the "Day family" framing from this morning's wake is the right generalization, not an
overreach. Confirmed distinct from 2407.01849 (Spivak-Srinivasan LDC, checked — no Indep map
there) and 2210.01962 (poset-indexed duoidal, more abstract). Read via full-text PDF extraction,
equations grepped directly (not abstract-only).

### [Powers of polynomial monads](https://topos.institute/blog/2023-09-21-powers-of-polynomial-monads/) — Spivak, Topos Institute blog, 2023-09-21
Independent confirmation, three years early: states plainly "The ⊗ structure on Poly is the Day
convolution of × on Set, and × on Poly is the Day convolution of + on Set." Works out the full
duoidal interchange map `(p1▷p2)⊗(q1▷q2) → (p1⊗q1)▷(p2⊗q2)` and a "four-product map" tying
+, ×, ⊗, ▷ together simultaneously — likely contains (or gestures at) coherence conditions
adjacent to the pentagon/triangle MacBeth proved by hand for the book's monoidal chapter. Worth
a full read before finalizing the Day-family framing in the rebuilt Ch3.

### [The Grothendieck construction for delta lenses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21288) — Clarke, arXiv:2502.21288 (Feb 2025; now formally published, *Higher Structures* Vol. 10 No. 1, 2026)
Already in sources.json (agent-summary from 06-11-browse2). Update: now formally published, not
arXiv-only. Main result unchanged from earlier note: delta lenses ↔ lax double functors into the
double category of sets/functions/split-multivalued-functions; universal property as a limit.
Extends Clarke's cofunctor/lens programme MacBeth already tracks.

### [Algorithmic and Extremal Obstructions Through the Language of Cohomology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03488) — Azevedo, Bumpus, Capucci, Fairbanks, Rosiak, arXiv:2407.03488 (v2, Oct 2025)
The Bumpus-Capucci paper flagged in feeds.md, now read. Models computational problems as
presheaves of certificate-sets; presheaf Čech cohomology measures exactly how/why local
solutions fail to patch to global ones (Vertex Cover, Cycle Cover, Odd Cycle Transversal; König's
theorem recovered cohomologically). Generalizes Set-valued to Abelian-group-valued presheaves.
**Same pattern as MacBeth's own banked `(G)⟺[ω]=0∈H²(Sk_C;𝒟)` result** — cohomology as the
obstruction to compositional gluing — but a different application domain (algorithms/complexity,
not categorical factorization systems). Worth comparing proof techniques for the grant narrative
(two independent instances of the same phenomenon strengthen the "cohomology = compositionality
obstruction" slogan). **Note:** the community-forum agent independently found this same arXiv ID
under the title "Failures of Compositionality: A Short Note on Cohomology, Sheafification and
Lavish Presheaves" — the title appears to have changed across versions (v1 vs v2); flagging so
neither title is mistaken for a different paper.
