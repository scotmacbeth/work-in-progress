## Genuinely new (added to sources.json, agent-summary level)

### [Djament, Touzé — "Homology of Strict Polynomial Functors over F_p-linear Additive Categories"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10502)
Distinct arXiv ID from the already-tracked `2407.10522` (same authors, easy to conflate — confirmed
different papers). Generalises the Franjou–Friedlander–Scorichenko–Suslin strong comparison theorem
from Fp-vector-space-valued strict polynomial functors to strict polynomial functors over general
Fp-linear additive categories; applications to stable group homology, algebraic K-theory, and
cohomology of classical algebraic groups over infinite perfect fields. On-territory for the linear-
containers-over-Vec front (exactly "polynomial functors over an additive base") but Ext/homology-
computation-flavoured rather than about representability/classification — doesn't visibly address
the `∐` vs `⊕` extensivity-collapse question directly. Abstract-only; full-text read recommended
before treating it as background citation for the crown target.

### [nLab — "extensive category"](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/extensive+category)
Definitional source (Carboni–Lack–Walters 1993). Explicitly states **Vect is not even finitely
extensive** — a direct, citable, textbook confirmation of the crux fact underlying the whole
linear-containers front (biproducts break disjointness). Fills a citation gap flagged at the end of
the 2026-08-18 dream session ("Diers/Gambino–Kock entries agent-summary, deep-read before citing")
— this page is the primary source for the extensivity *definition* itself, distinct from the Diers
familial-representability side. Also states extensive + finite products ⟹ automatically
distributive, a fact not yet used but potentially load-bearing for the comonoid-law verification
open thread.

### [nLab — "multirepresentable functor"](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/multirepresentable+functor)
Confirms Diers' criterion precisely (Diers, *Catégories localisables*, 1977, Prop 2.1.2: `F` is
multirepresentable ⟺ every connected component of its category of elements has an initial object);
Carboni–Johnstone (1995) "familially representable" as the synonym. This is the page cited (but not
previously indexed in `sources.json`) in `connections/extensivity-is-the-container-boundary.md` —
adding it closes that file's own flagged gap. Does not itself connect to polynomial functors; that
bridge lives only in citing papers (confirms the extensivity↔familial-representability synthesis is
MacBeth's own, not lifted from nLab).

### [n-Category Café — "Parametric adjoints = multi-adjoints" (2021)](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2021/03/parametric_adjoints_multiadjoi.html)
Blog-level discussion of Diers' multi-adjoints and the equivalence with local right adjoints —
a more accessible entry point than the primary sources (Diers 1977, Walker). Candidate citation for
an expository framing of "why does anyone outside the Diers literature care about familial
representability" if the extensivity material ever gets written up for a wider audience.

### [MathOverflow 491968 — "Zero divisors in connected distributive categories"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/491968/zero-divisors-in-connected-distributive-categories)
Live 2025 thread on connected distributive categories (Artin–Mazur sense: initial object, ×
distributes over ∐, terminal object connected) — asks whether `X×Y=0` with `X,Y≠0` is possible
(yes, via covering-space/sheaf counterexamples). Same axiomatic neighbourhood as the Vec-biproduct-
collapse diagnosis (coproduct/product interaction, connectedness) though not about Vec/biproducts
specifically. Low priority, worth a skim if the extensivity write-up is revisited.

### [MathOverflow 510540 — "Primes as connected objects?"](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/510540/primes-as-connected-objects)
April 2026, score 20. Seeks a natural category on positive integers whose connected objects
(extensive-category sense) are exactly the primes; answer uses Spec of finite products of cyclic
rings. Tangential — general-interest illustration that "connected object of an extensive category"
is an actively discussed notion, no direct container content. Logged for completeness, low priority.

### [math.StackExchange 4510090 — "Definition of the Zappa–Szép product of groups in categorical terms"](https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/4510090/definition-of-the-zappa-sz%C3%A9p-product-of-groups-in-categorical-terms)
2022, score 5. The accepted answer argues there is **no clean intrinsic categorical/universal-
property definition of the external ZS product inside Group** — attempts via Sub(G) fail because
`H⨿K=G` (in the subobject poset) doesn't force `HK=G`; the construction is fundamentally concrete,
needing the forgetful functor to Set. Useful corroboration: MacBeth's route to ZS/directed-container
composition (via Cat morphisms / container structure, not a bare categorical limit in Group) is
validated as the right level of generality by an independent community consensus, not a workaround.
