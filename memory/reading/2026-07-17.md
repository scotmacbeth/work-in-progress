## Papers

### [Distributive Laws of Monadic Containers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17191) — Purdy, Damato (CALCO 2025)
Full text read (HTML) this session. Extends containers to the monadic side (dual to Ahman-Uustalu's
directed-container-as-comonad) and characterizes distributive laws between monadic containers;
§6 does the mixed monadic/directed case, which the authors say "completes the zoo" of container
(co)monad distributive laws. **Example 4.9** is a container-theoretic Zappa-Szép product for a
matching pair of monoid actions — the terminal-shape, single-monoid special case of MacBeth's
pairwise ZS criterion (K=C⋈D over a wide D ⟺ (L)∧(G)). **§7 explicitly flags this as "a starting
point for no-go theorems (incomposability results)"** — the same shape of question as the (G)=H²
result, phrased via distributive laws rather than group/monoid cohomology, no citation to
Rosebrugh-Wood/Baues-Wirsching/Pirashvili. Not a scoop — MacBeth's criterion is strictly more
general (arbitrary wide category vs one monoid pair) — but this is the closest published neighbour
found yet, from exactly the group flagged as most likely to converge independently. **Action:**
re-read Ex 4.9 + §7 against the pairwise-ZS proof before the next ZS `/write` session; cite and
stake the generality claim explicitly. → bumped to `deep-read` in sources.json.

### [Categories by Kan Extension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21974) — Spivak
Not re-read in full today (still `agent-summary`), but a citation-trail search on Ahman-Uustalu
independently flagged this as "the single most important new hit": it reproves the
directed-container/small-category correspondence via **density comonads** — categories = polynomial
comonads on Set up to isomorphism, as a left Kan extension of a functor along itself — citing
Ahman-Uustalu directly. This is close enough to MacBeth's M2b/M3b comonad-converse theorems
(machine-checked both round trips) that a full read-and-compare is owed before the book's comonad
chapter is finalised — a different route (density comonad vs directed-container) to what may be the
same folklore correspondence. Second independent flag on this paper in a week (first was 07-14, for
SEED Q5).

### [The Free Bifibration on a Functor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07314) — Clarke, Scherer, Zeilberger
Already known (logged 06-13), re-surfaced by the web agent with new detail: constructs bifibrations
via proof theory (cut-free sequent calculus mod permutation), and applied to the identity functor
produces the free double category with companions/conjoints as a category of **rooted plane trees**
generated freely over ω. Structurally adjacent to the free-monad-on-a-container / grafting-monoid
work (different formalism — proof-theoretic double categories, not polynomial functors), not an
obvious scoop, but the plane-tree-over-ω construction deserves a closer read to rule out overlap
with the grafting tree-of-positions picture.

### [A Sheaf Framework for Strategic Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01663) — Hernández, Sánchez-Soto
Re-confirmed (full text, second read): Nash equilibria = global sections of a "game sheaf" ℬ,
obstruction is **H¹(𝒮;ℬ)** (existence of a compatible global best-response, not the H²
composition-existence MacBeth's (G)=H² tracks). Genuinely an existence/gluing result, so sits closer
to MacBeth's axis than the earlier-flagged pure-consensus cluster, but different degree, different
coefficient category (best-response sets, not group/monoid cohomology), no classical-cohomology
citations. Not a scoop — citable as differentiated related work for the grant's agent-orchestration
narrative if a contrast is wanted.

## Community

### [Distributive Laws](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2017/02/distributive_laws.html) — n-Category Café, Feb 2017
David Myers and Ze Wong, in comments, identify Zappa-Szép products of groups as arising from a
distributive law between the monads H×− and K×− on Set (semidirect when one side acts trivially,
direct when both are normal). Myers's phrasing — "we only need to twirl the middle two instances...
so we only need to act on that copy of H that moved" — is an informal but exact description of the
ZS interchange law in string-diagram terms. Also: an unresolved conjecture (Myers) that a
distributive law of a monoid monad over the powerset monad yields quantale structure on the
algebras. **This is the earliest clean public "ZS = distributive law" folklore source found** —
worth a citation-completeness check against the pairwise-ZS paper (PR #9) and Lean ZS-assoc (PR #8).

### [Doubles for Monoidal Categories](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2019/11/doubles_for_monoidal_categorie.html) — n-Category Café, Nov 2019
Fosco Loregian and Bryce Clarke: Tamb(𝒞) ≃ [Doub(𝒞), Set]; for (Set,1,×) the double's hom-sets are
exactly lens get/put data, tying delta lenses to Tambara-module/optics machinery. A comment thread
(Staton/Román) flags a coordinate-swap between Doub(𝒞) and Pastro-Street's original formulation —
relevant if citing both. Structural neighbour to the cofunctors-are-update-lenses thread, doesn't
touch containers directly.
