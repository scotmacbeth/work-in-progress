### ["What kind of linearly distributive category do polynomial functors form?"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01849) — Spivak, Srinivasan, arXiv:2407.01849
**Read in full (finally — closes a 5-session-old flag).** Its duoidal structure is the *classical*
pair **(Poly, ⊗, ◁)** (Dirichlet product, substitution) — Lemma 3.4 (normal duoidal) is cited to
Spivak 2022, not proved fresh here. The paper's real content is LDC-theoretic: biclosed structure
(closure for ⊗, co-closure for ◁), linear duals (Ay ⊣⊢ y^A), left/right cores, linear
monoids/comonoids/bialgebras. **Zero mention of Dialectica, de Paiva, or anything resembling ⋉/⋊**
anywhere in the paper or its 11-item bibliography — confirmed by reading intro, §2 (formulas for ⊗
and ◁ only), §3, §4 opening, §6.2-6.3, and the full reference list. **Does NOT scoop
`ltimes-rtimes-duoidal-ldc-proved`.** It DOES sit directly on top of the closure-vacuity /
`closed-structures-are-spivaks` thread — Lemma 3.3 (normal duoidal ⟹ automatically isomix LDC) is a
general machine worth checking against whether (Poly,⋉,⋊) is isomix, and the bi-closed-LDC framing
+ cores/duals should be diffed against the closure-vacuity notes. **This full-read task is now
CLOSED — stop re-flagging it.**

### ["Interactions that reshape the interfaces of the interacting parties"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917) — David I. Spivak, arXiv:2602.17917 (Feb 2026)
**New find.** Introduces **PolyTr_U**, the terminal coalgebra for `u◁u` (u = universe polynomial) —
"polynomial trees" whose interface reshapes coinductively with each interaction (motivating example:
progressive GANs). Main theorem: (PolyTr_U, ⊗, [-,-]) is symmetric monoidal closed (Dirichlet ⊗,
standard internal hom); finite coproducts distribute over ⊗. Builds **OrgTr**, a Cat-enriched
bicategory generalizing Spivak-Schultz's Org to evolving interfaces, with a duoidal-interchange
remark — again the classical (⊗,◁) pair, not ⋉/⋊. Close cousin of MacBeth's cofree-comonoid /
behaviour-tree work (terminal-coalgebra-of-u◁u ~ cofree comonoid construction). **Not a scoop** of
`orchestration-is-zappa-szep-weld` — its mechanism for interface change is coinductive tree growth,
not a Zappa-Szép weld with an H² obstruction. **Open cross-reference to resolve next session:**
memory already has an "OrgTr embedding (Prop 6.10)" note from 06-11 browsing — unclear whether that
is *this same* OrgTr or an earlier/different bicategory of the same name.

## Community (n-Category Café)

### [William Waites, "A Typed Language for Agent Coordination"](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2026/03/a_typed_language_for_agent_coordination.html) and ["The Agent That Doesn't Know Itself"](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2026/03/the_agent_that_doesnt_know_its.html) (Mar 2026)
**Both read in full (owed since 07-19/07-20).** A "plumbing" language on a symmetric monoidal
copy-discard category (streams `!A`, morphisms copy/discard/merge/barrier/map/filter/single),
extended to traced monoidal categories for nested feedback (inner = conversation history, outer =
control loop to a supervisory "homunculus"). Session types compile functorially onto plumbing
morphisms. The agent/homunculus split is architecturally supervisor/worker-like but is **never
formalized as a distributive law, Zappa-Szép/semidirect product, or category factorization** — no
cohomology, no obstruction class anywhere. Its failure mode is context-drift/memory-compaction,
handled by a pragmatic mutex ("serialisation gate"), not a re-entrancy bug traced to a nonzero class
in H². **Verdict: genuinely different mechanism — cite as related-but-different, does not
anticipate or overlap MacBeth's `C⋈D` + `[ω]∈H²` result.**

### [n-Category Café, "Distributive Laws"](https://golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2017/02/distributive_laws.html) (Liang Ze Wong, Feb 2017)
Comment thread has David Jaz Myers stating explicitly: "Zappa-Szép products of groups H,K
correspond precisely to distributive laws between the monads T=H×– and S=K×–" (direct product =
twist-map law, semidirect = law via φ:K→Aut(H)); also notes group-ring formation as a distributive
law of group-monad over ring-monad. Clean citable folklore precedent for the "orchestration = ZS
weld" framing at the (higher) category level — group-level analogue of MacBeth's category-level
construction with a cohomological existence obstruction added on top.
