### [Comonads as Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15091) — Fairbanks, Carlson, Spivak
Already tracked (deep-read since 2026-07-17/18). This pass re-read with the general-M frontier
specifically in mind and surfaced a genuinely new angle not in the existing note: **Appendix B**
generalizes "continuous map" to comonads on base categories other than Set (the base-category
generalization MacBeth's own project also needs), and the **density-comonad/basis machinery** (§2,
Background I.3) is a candidate general technology for "what happens when the aggregator P varies with
shape" — for arbitrary (non-polynomial) comonads on Set, the answer is a *coreflected small category*
plus a separate *reflected topological space* (two orthogonal shadows), not one clean classification.
Suggestive, not a direct answer — worth a dedicated close-read of the density-comonad construction
before the next general-M PROVE attempt. **sources.json note appended, extraction unchanged
(deep-read).**

### Cross-domain holonomy-triviality search
No fully independent third domain confirmed. Gauge theory (bundle gerbe holonomy) and algebraic
geometry (trivial monodromy ⟺ constant local system) both confirm the pattern but are the classical,
textbook instances — not new. Best candidate: **["A cubical formalisation of topos causal models:
intervention, sheaf gluing, and the intuitionistic do-calculus"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15629)**
(2026) — explicitly names a "contextuality obstruction: pairwise-consistent local causal data with no
global model, detected by a degree-one holonomy class." Same skeleton, different application (causal
inference / quantum contextuality), but shares direct lineage with CBP itself (both descend from
Abramsky-Brandenburger sheaf-theoretic contextuality) — so this corroborates one shared cohomological
ancestor recurring across applications, not three causally-independent rediscoveries. Distributed
systems / CRDT literature: zero "holonomy" hits — genuinely open territory, nobody has framed eventual
consistency this way yet. Added arXiv:2607.15629 to sources.json at agent-summary.
