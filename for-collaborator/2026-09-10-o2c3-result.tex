\section{What you asked}

Your referee report (2026-09-10, on the $M$-container paper, commit \texttt{70becd9},
accept-with-minor-revisions) proposed in \S4 a hybrid-operad test to decide the
\emph{open middle} of Problem~5.23. The corrected Theorem~3 established a dichotomy at two
endpoints: in the fully-symmetric-unital world $\mathcal{U}$ commutativity of $\mu$
forces necessity to \emph{hold} (the U-corner / family theorem), whereas the partially
symmetric $\Cthree$-residual operad --- which has \emph{no} commutative binary composite ---
makes necessity \emph{fail} ($M$-containers compose while $M$ is non-polynomial). The
headline ``commutativity of $\mu$ is the exact dividing line'' was thus proved only at the
endpoints. Problem~5.23 asks about the middle: a partially symmetric operad that \emph{does}
possess a commutative binary composite. Your $\mathcal{O}_{2,\Cthree}$ proposal supplies the
sharpest such probe --- a binary $\mu$ with full $S_2$ slot-symmetry (hence
$\mu(x,y)=\mu(y,x)$ commutative), a ternary $\omega$ carrying only $\Cthree=\langle(0\,1\,2)\rangle$
(not $S_3$), and an absorbed nullary unit $e$. Is the dividing line really $\mu$-commutativity,
strictly weaker than full symmetry, or does a co-inhabitant refute the headline?

\section{Verdict: necessity HOLDS}

\begin{verdict}
The dividing line is ``$\mu$ has a commutative binary composite,'' strictly weaker than
``fully symmetric.'' The free-algebra monad $M=\widetilde{\mathcal{O}_{2,\Cthree}}$ is fully
symmetric in \emph{neither} generator, yet $M\circ M \not\cong \bidx{r}\circ M$ for every
polynomial $r$: necessity holds. This confirms your $85\%$ prediction.
\end{verdict}

\noindent Here $M$ is analytic with $a_0=1$ and unbounded wreath depth $\mathfrak{h}=\infty$
(balanced binary $\mu$-trees give $S_2\wr\cdots\wr S_2$ towers), so it sits squarely in the
open middle. The structural key, verified in \texttt{o2c3-model.py}, is that
$\omega(x,y,e)$ reduces under $\Cthree$-symmetry and unit absorption to the \emph{commutative}
$\mu$ --- the precise structural difference from the pure-$\Cthree$ operad, whose binary
composite was rigid and non-commutative.

\section{The computation}

\paragraph{(a) The witness and its stabiliser.}
Take the U-corner witness verbatim,
\[
  w \;=\; \mu\bigl(\, \mu(V_0,\mu(V_2,E')),\;\; \mu(V_1,\mu(V_3,E')) \,\bigr),
\]
with $E'$ a rigid outer-leaf peg and labels $\{0,1,2,3\}$. Full brute force over $S_4$ gives
\[
  \Stab(w) \;=\; \langle (0\,1)(2\,3)\rangle \;=\; \DeltaC,
  \qquad |\Stab(w)| = 2 \ \text{exactly}
\]
(equality, not containment); $w$ is support-indecomposable, a single block on all four labels.

\paragraph{(b) The escape.}
Over all $35$ single $\mathcal{O}_{2,\Cthree}$-trees on $\le 4$ labels (depth $\le 4$), none has
$\Aut = \langle(0\,1)(2\,3)\rangle$: any single-tree automorphism group containing the element
$(0\,1)(2\,3)$ is forced to contain $(0\,1)$ and $(2\,3)$ separately, hence has order $8$. So the
order-$2$ group $\DeltaC$ is neither a single-tree $\Aut$ nor a Young product of such; that is,
$\DeltaC \notin \mathcal{Y}$. By the molecule-multiset invariant (Lemma~E) this forces
\[
  \Mcont\circ \Mcont \;\not\cong\; \bidx{r}\circ \Mcont
  \qquad \text{for every polynomial } r,
\]
which is exactly necessity.

\paragraph{(c) Type-set corroboration.}
Comparing the type-sets of $\Mcont\circ\Mcont$ against $\Lin\circ\Mcont$ ($\Lin$ the linear /
polynomial approximant): at $m=2,3$ the sets coincide (no escapes). At $m=4$,
\[
  |T_{\Mcont\circ\Mcont}| = 6, \qquad |T_{\Lin\circ\Mcont}| = 5,
\]
exactly one escape --- the cycle-type $(2,2)$ order-$2$ type, present in $\Mcont\circ\Mcont$ and
absent from $\Lin\circ\Mcont$, with $\textsf{onlyL}=0$ (no truncation artifact in the deciding
direction). This is the $S_3$-\emph{control} pattern, not the pure-$\Cthree$ pattern in which the
two type-sets coincided.

\section{Your $15\%$ risk, resolved}

You worried that a $\Cthree \wr S_m$ factor at an internal $\omega$-node could interfere with the
outer-$\mu$ swap. It does not (\texttt{o2c3-robustness.py}).

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{Interior mixing preserves the swap.} Building the inner blocks from $\omega$ rather
than $\mu$, e.g.\ $\mu(\omega(0,2,P),\omega(1,3,P))$ and nested variants, still yields
$\Stab = \langle(0\,1)(2\,3)\rangle$ exactly.
\item \textbf{Stress test.} On six labels, $\mu(\omega(0,1,2),\omega(3,4,5))$ has
$\Stab$ of order $18 = \Cthree \wr \Ctwo$: the two $\Cthree$ factors and the top swap
\emph{coexist}: the swap is not destroyed.
\item \textbf{The swap is governed by the top node.} It dies only when the outer node is $\omega$
($\Cthree$ cannot transpose two blocks) or when the two blocks differ in shape ($\mu$ swaps only
relabeling-isomorphic blocks) --- both expected and orthogonal to the risk. Toggling $\mu$ to
non-commutative (pure-$\Cthree$ behaviour) collapses $\Stab(w)$ to the identity and the escape
vanishes.
\end{itemize}
\noindent The escape is robust: it is controlled entirely by the top node being the commutative
$\mu$; interior $\omega/\Cthree$ structure only builds towers underneath.

\section{Consequence for the paper}

The headline ``commutativity of $\mu$ is the exact dividing line'' is \textbf{corroborated, not
refuted}. Theorem~5.24 becomes a genuine biconditional throughout the entire $\mu$-commutative
world, not merely the fully-symmetric $\mathcal{U}$; the $\Cthree$ reversal is confined to operads
with \emph{no} commutative binary composite. The U-corner meta-theorem mechanism ---
peg-rigidified swap of two non-isomorphic blocks --- is exactly what fires here, instantiated at
the smallest nontrivial arity for a non-fully-symmetric operad.

Honestly graded, this is \texttt{computed}: it decides the specific witness exactly (full
$\Stab$ over $S_4$) and the type-set escape at $m=4$ ($m=2,3$ as warm-up). A proof of necessity
for \emph{all} $m$ is the separate inductive step --- generalising
\texttt{theorem-general-stabilizer-necessity} from ``fully-symmetric-unital'' to ``unital operad
with a commutative binary composite'' --- which I am now attempting.
